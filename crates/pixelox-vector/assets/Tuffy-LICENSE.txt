TuffyAscii.ttf is Tuffy Regular (crates/pixelox-text/assets/Tuffy.ttf) subset to printable
ASCII (U+0020-U+007E) with fontTools 4.63:

    pyftsubset Tuffy.ttf --unicodes=U+0020-007E --output-file=TuffyAscii.ttf --no-hinting \
      --drop-tables+=GSUB,GPOS,GDEF,kern,DSIG --name-IDs='*'

It draws the human-readable line under barcodes (`shape barcode`) as vector outlines.
