# i026: Bulb hub with icon nodes on circuit arcs

## 1. Concept

A square concept map on a near-white ground. A light bulb with a thick black outline sits in
the middle, its glass filled with overlapping translucent discs in yellow, orange, and red; a
swarm of small dots in the same colors sparks around it. Eight branches leave the bulb's
neighborhood like tracks on a circuit board: thin colored lines made of arcs that share the
bulb's center, joined by short radial pieces and ending in dots. On the branches sit fifteen
white icon discs with colored rings, and beside each branch a small titled note. The message
is "one idea, eight directions, the tools for each". It works because every line bends around
one center, so fifteen scattered icons still read as one system, and because a three-step warm
scale (yellow, orange, red-orange) is the only color on the page.

## 2. Data story

No numbers. A topic with facets and no order (relationship: one hub, many related things):

* 1 hub (the topic), shown as the bulb, with no label of its own.
* 8 branches. Each has one title of 2 words and one note of about 19 words on 4 lines.
* 15 icon nodes: 7 branches carry two icons (a larger and a smaller one), 1 branch carries
  one. An icon has no label; it illustrates its branch's note.
* 1 two-line header (one word, then two words) beside a small round mark.

The original's copy is filler text; only the pattern above matters.

Placeholder content in the recipe (invented, about developing a product idea): branches
INSIGHT, PLANNING, GOALS, LAUNCH, STRATEGY, TEAM TIME, BUDGET, RESEARCH, each with a note of 9
to 13 words on 2 or 3 lines; 16 icons (two per branch).

## 3. Information architecture

1. **Header**, top left: a round dark mark, a thin title, a bold tracked second line, and a
   rainbow hairline. Small on purpose: it labels the sheet and gets out of the way.
2. **Hub**, center: the bulb. It answers "what is this about" without a word.
3. **Sparks**: 16 dots around the glass. They pass the bulb's colors outward to the branches.
4. **Eight branches**, clockwise from the top: each a line in one hue that starts at a dot
   near the bulb, runs along one or two arcs, and reaches its icon discs. The reader follows
   a line outward from the hub to an icon and then to the note beside it.
5. **Eight notes**, near the edges, each next to a free end of its branch and with a title in
   the branch's hue. Notes on the left half are right-aligned, notes on the right half
   left-aligned, so every note's straight edge faces the diagram.

There is no reading order among the branches; the eye circles the hub once.

## 4. Format and grid

* Square. Recipe canvas 1080 x 1080. Outer margin 3% W; notes come within 5% W of the edges.
* A polar grid, not columns. Center of everything: 50% W, 47% H (the middle of the bulb's
  glass). The lower 6% H stays lighter than the top, which lifts the diagram.
* Bulb: glass radius 10.5% W; whole bulb `39.5%,37%,21% x 30.7%` (top of the glass at 37% H,
  bottom of the screw base at 67.7% H).
* Sparks: between 13.5% and 15% W from the center, on the upper 240 degrees only (nothing
  under the base).
* Connector arcs: radii between 18% and 39% W from the center.
* Icon nodes: centers between 25% and 47% W from the center; three sizes: 9.5 to 10% W (six
  large), 7 to 8.5% W (eight medium), 5.5% W (one small). Larger nodes sit farther out.
* Notes: eight blocks of 11% W x 4% H, at 57%,8% (top); 84%,20%; 84%,56%; 78%,79% (right
  half, left-aligned); 37 to 48%,88% (bottom); 5 to 16%,66%; 3 to 14%,25%; 16 to 27%,14% (left
  half, right-aligned).
* Header: mark 5% W across at 6.5%,6.4%; text from 11% to 28% W, 4.3% to 8.8% H.
* The recipe regularizes the scatter into two rings: 8 large nodes on a ring of radius 36% W
  starting 22.5 degrees right of the top, and 8 medium nodes on a ring of radius 25.5% W
  starting at the top, so each branch owns a 45 degree sector.

## 5. Layer stack

1. Ground: `#f7f7f7`, a hair lighter at the top left and darker (`#ededed`) at the bottom
   right, with a few faint white diagonal streaks running from the lower left to the upper
   right.
2. Connectors: eight lines, 0.4% W thick (4 px), round joins, no fill: red-orange `#fd4f00`
   (top and bottom branches), orange `#fe9c01` (left and right), golden yellow `#fece01` and
   lemon `#feed00` (the four diagonal branches). Each is built from arcs centered on the hub,
   radial pieces, and an occasional 45 degree elbow. Two branches close into the outline of a
   ring sector (40 to 50 degrees wide, between radii of 18 to 21% and 35 to 38% W).
3. End dots: one filled dot of radius 0.7% W, in the line's color, on every free line end
   (13 in all, most of them near a note's title).
4. Icon nodes: white discs with a ring 0.4% W thick in the branch's color and a soft gray
   shadow falling to the lower right; inside, a black line icon about half the disc wide with
   one small part filled in the branch's hue.
5. Bulb: the glass is a circle running into a neck, outlined in black `#111111` 0.9% W thick;
   under it four black bars with round ends, 7.5% W wide at the top and 3.5% W at the bottom.
6. Discs in the glass: six to eight circles of radius 4 to 6% W in red-orange, orange, and
   yellow at 75 to 85% opacity, overlapping so that new tones appear where they cross,
   clipped to the glass.
7. Sparks: 16 dots of radius 0.8 to 1.3% W around the glass: 6 yellow, 6 orange, 4 red-orange.
8. Notes: title in the branch's color, four lines of gray `#555555` text.
9. Header: dark gray `#333333` mark, thin title `#333333`, tracked gray line `#777777`, and a
   hairline that runs through the spectrum from magenta to blue.

Analogous warm scale, four steps from lemon to red-orange, on a neutral ground with black as
the only dark. A hue identifies a branch (line, ring, title, and note belong together); the
order of the hues around the hub is red, yellow, orange, yellow, red, yellow, orange, yellow,
so neighbors always differ. No single accent: the bulb is the accent by size and by its black
outline.

## 8. Charts and diagrams

* **C-hub-spokes** is the family, but the original is not a plain hub: it has no straight
  spokes into the center. Its connectors are concentric arc pieces with radial stubs, elbows,
  and end dots, the nodes sit at 15 different angles and radii, and two branches are closed
  sector outlines. No script draws that (a gap). The recipe builds the nearest regular form:
  * two runs of `scripts/cycle.js` with `style=spokes`, `line=none` (no spokes),
    `nodefill='#ffffff'` (white discs with colored rings), `badges=none`, and eight empty
    labels (`labels='|||||||'` with `labelwidth=0`, so no room is kept for text): an outer
    ring with `box=9.26,6.3,81.48,81.48 start=22.5 node=100` (radius 390 px) and an inner ring
    with `box=19.6,16.6,60.8,60.8 node=72` (radius 275 px). Both share the center 540,508;
  * eight connectors with `shape path`, all the same form turned by 45 degrees: from the
    outer node straight in to the inner ring's radius, counter-clockwise along that radius
    (an arc `A 275 275 0 0 0`) through the inner node to a point 11.25 degrees past it, then
    straight in to a radius of 205 px, where the end dot sits;
  * the points on the circles are anchors too: run `scripts/cycle.js` with 32 empty labels
    and `node=2` at `box=22.84,19.88,54.32,54.32` (radius 275) and
    `box=29.32,26.36,41.36,41.36` (radius 205): the anchors are 11.25 degrees apart.
* **C-bulb**, the line-art form: `scripts/object.js`, `kind=bulb outline=1 shade=0`, box
  `38,33.3,24,31`, `colors='#111111'` (the outline, 12 px at this size), `dark='#111111'`
  (the base bars). It is rendered twice: once with `glass='#fece01'` (a filled glass, the
  layer the discs are clipped to) and once without `glass=` (the outline and the base only),
  placed on top so the discs never cover the line. The discs are one `scripts/blobs.js`
  image (`soft=0.05`, `alpha=0.8`, six fixed centers) clipped to the filled bulb with
  `layer clip`.
* No axis, no legend, no values anywhere: positions and sizes of nodes encode nothing.
* Simplified: the scatter becomes two regular rings (16 nodes, not 15); every branch has the
  same shape; node rings are 9 px, not 4 px (the script's ring is 9% of the diameter); icons
  have no colored detail.

## 9. Icons and illustration

* Node icons are Lucide line icons in `#1c1c1c`, half the disc wide: 52 px in the large
  nodes, 38 px in the medium ones. Outer ring, clockwise from the upper right: `lightbulb`,
  `notebook-text`, `trophy`, `rocket`, `target`, `clock`, `hand-coins`, `microscope`. Inner
  ring, clockwise from the top: `brain`, `mail`, `flag`, `footprints`, `chess-knight`,
  `users`, `briefcase`, `search`.
* The original's set, for reference: microscope, magnifier, money bag, briefcase, three
  people, clock with a filled quarter, chess knight, target with an arrow, running figure,
  rocket, trophy cup, envelope with a letter, spiral notebook, bulb, brain. Each is a 2 px
  black outline with one detail filled in the branch's hue (the knight's base, the clock's
  quarter, the envelope's letter).
* The bulb is `scripts/object.js`; the original's is a little rounder, with a shorter neck.
* Header mark: a dark disc with `ruler` in white, 32 px. The original shows a round dark
  emblem with a ruler shape and a crescent; treat it as a place for `logo.png` ("your image
  here") and leave it out when there is no mark.

## 10. Effects and finish

* Node shadows: `fx drop-shadow` on each placed ring image, offset 8,10 (6,8 for the inner
  ring), blur 14 (12), black at 16%. They lift the discs off the lines.
* Bulb outline: drawn by the script (`outline=1`), not by a layer style. The filled render
  lies under the clipped discs; the unfilled render lies over them, so the black line stays
  whole and the four base bars stay separate.
* Translucent discs: `alpha=0.8` in `scripts/blobs.js`, so overlaps make extra tones.
* Ground streaks: `scripts/pattern.js`, `kind=stripes angle=45 cell=230 weight=0.03`, white
  at 75%: barely visible. The original adds a slight diagonal gradient (lighter top left).
* Rainbow hairline: a 3 px rect with `fx overlay --kind gradient` and five stops.
* Everything else is flat: no gradients inside marks, no texture.

## 11. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The bulb: the largest shape, the only black, dead center.
2. The sparks, which lead outward to the nearest end dots.
3. Along each line to the icon discs (white on pale gray, lifted by shadows).
4. The colored note titles around the edge, then the gray notes.
5. The header, last: it is small and gray.

Bulb 31% of the height; large nodes a third of that; note titles a fifth of a node. Type
never competes with the picture.

Notes for reuse:

* **Change first:** the eight titles and notes (`T1` to `T8`, `B1` to `B8`), the 16 icon
  names, and the header. Nothing else depends on the copy.
* **Where the numbers come from.** Icons sit on the `node1` to `node8` anchors of the two
  ring renders (outer ring: node1 is the upper right node, then clockwise; inner ring: node1
  is the top node). Each connector is `M outer node L P1 A 275 275 0 0 0 P2 L P3`: P1 is the
  point on the inner ring's circle at the outer node's angle, P2 the point on the same circle
  11.25 degrees counter-clockwise of the inner node, P3 the point at radius 205 at P2's
  angle, and the end dot (radius 9) is centered on P3. Read P1, P2, and P3 from the 32-point
  anchor runs described in section 8; never work them out by hand.
* **Notes** sit in the eight gaps of the outer ring: the top and bottom ones centered on 540,
  the left and right ones against the inner node (x = 208 right-aligned, x = 872), the four
  corner ones 150 px from the top or 290 px from the bottom, 274 px (right-aligned) or 806 px
  from the left.
* **Other counts:** for 6 branches use six empty labels (`'|||||'`), `start=30` on the outer

## 13. Variations and pairings

* **A plain hub** when time is short: one `scripts/cycle.js` run with `style=spokes`, real
  `labels=` and `notes=`, and `sub=' '` so the spokes stop short of the bulb (C-hub-spokes as
  documented). You lose the circuit look and keep the idea.
* **Other hubs:** a rocket (`kind=rocket`, short and upright) for a launch, a `brain` or
  `target` icon at 220 px in a black ring, or `logo.png` in a white disc.
* **Cool palette:** the same four-step scale in blues (`#0b3c6f`, `#1263a8`, `#2b8fd8`,
  `#7cc0f0`) with a navy outline; or one hue per branch from a categorical set when the
  branches are departments or products that already have colors.
* **With numbers:** replace each inner icon with a figure ("42%") in the node (22 px, bold):
  the sheet becomes eight key numbers around a topic. Add a source line at the bottom then.
* **Relatives:** i036 is the other bulb design (a lying outline bulb with a fan of five solid
  panels: fewer items, more text each). i038 is the object-with-callouts idea for ordered
  steps. Pairs with C-icon-grid on a second page that explains each branch in full.

## 14. Pitfalls

* **Lines that ignore the center.** Free curves and diagonals turn the map into spaghetti.
  Every arc must share the hub's center and every straight piece must point at it (or be a
  short 45 degree elbow): take the points from the anchor runs.
* **Lemon yellow text on a pale ground.** The original's yellow titles are close to
  unreadable. Keep lemon for lines and rings; set yellow titles in `#e0b400` or in the gray
  of the notes.
* **Notes too small.** Fifteen icons leave little room, and the original shrinks its notes
  to 10 px. Cut words, not size: 16 px minimum at 1080 wide, 2 or 3 lines.
* **Size and distance read as importance (data integrity).** Large outer nodes look more
  important than small inner ones, and a branch with two icons looks bigger than one with a
  single icon. If the branches are equal, give each the same number and sizes of nodes, as
  the recipe does; if they are not equal, say what the size means in a note. Never let the
  layout rank items that the data does not rank.
* **Icons without words.** An icon alone is a riddle. Each branch needs its title; do not
  add a ninth branch with icons only.
* **A fifth hue.** Green or blue for one branch reads as "this one is different". Stay inside
  the warm scale, or recolor all branches.
