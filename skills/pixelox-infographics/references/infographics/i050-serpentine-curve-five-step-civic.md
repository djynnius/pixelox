# i050: Five steps on a serpentine curve in red, white, and blue

## 1. Concept

A tall civic explainer: five steps of an official procedure hang on one thick line that
swings right, left, right, left, right down the page like a slalom. Each half circle of the
line cradles a small spot illustration; the step's name runs along the outside of the curve,
a giant pale numeral stands beside it, and the explanation sits on the open side. Star bands
close the page at the top and bottom. It works because the line never breaks: a reader's eye
is carried from step to step without arrows, and every swing gives one step a room of its
own, with picture, name, number, and text always in the same four places.

## 2. Data story

No numbers. An ordered process:

* 5 steps. Each has a name of 2 to 3 words (caps, on the curve), a body of 12 to 30 words
  (3 to 6 lines of about 28 characters), a number, and one illustration.
* A three-part title: a lead-in of 3 words, a connector of 2 short words, the subject in 2
  words.
* A web address in the footer.

Placeholder content in the recipe (invented, neutral: how a city budget is made):
1 Departments ask, 2 Mayor drafts, 3 Public hearing, 4 Council votes, 5 Budget takes effect,
each with one sentence.

## 3. Information architecture

1. **Star band** (top edge): eight stars in three colors; it sets the civic tone.
2. **Header panel** with a wavy lower edge: the title in three lines, light, red, heavy.
3. **Five steps** on the serpentine curve. Odd steps bulge to the right: text at the left
   (right-aligned), picture in the bowl, name on the outside of the curve, numeral at the
   far right. Even steps mirror this. The reader zig-zags: text, picture, number; number,
   picture, text.
4. **Footer panel** with a wavy upper edge: the web address in widely spaced caps.
5. **Star band** (bottom edge).

The curve changes color at every junction (light blue, red, navy, black, red): the color
change marks where one step ends and the next begins.

## 4. Format and grid

* Long portrait, 1 : 2.49. Recipe canvas 1080 x 2690.
* Star bands: 0 to 2.4% H and 97.6% to 100% H, full width; stars centered every 11.7% W
  from 9.3% W.
* Header panel: 2.4% to 14% H (wavy edge between 13.4% and 14.7% H). Title lines centered,
  cap tops at 4%, 6.5%, and 9.1% H.
* Serpentine curve: axis at 50% W. Five half circles of radius 17.6% W (to the middle of the line),
  line 2.25% W thick, joined end to end from 16.9% H to 87.5% H; centers at 23.9%, 38.1%,
  52.2%, 66.3%, and 80.5% H (one every 14.1% H = 2 radii).
* Per step, on the bulge side: name on an arc of radius 21.5% W; numeral centered at 85% W
  (odd steps) or 16.5% W (even steps), on the half circle's center height.
* Per step, on the open side: body text with its inner edge at 35% W (right-aligned, odd
  steps) or 65.5% W (left-aligned, even steps), vertically centered on the half circle.
* Illustration: 16 to 22% W across, centered in the bowl, 1.5% W toward the bulge.
* Footer panel 90.7% to 97.6% H; address centered at 94.4% H.

## 5. Layer stack

1. Ground: warm off-white `#fbf6f8`.
2. Header panel: pale gray-blue `#ced2db` with a gentle wave along its lower edge; footer
   panel the same, mirrored.
3. Star bands: navy `#3b4b7f` strips; eight five-point stars each, in the order white, red,
   light blue, repeated.
4. Serpentine curve: five half rings in light blue `#a7b7da`, red `#dd181a`, navy `#3b4b7f`, black,
   red; flat ends that meet exactly on the axis.
5. Giant numerals 1 to 5 in the panel's gray-blue `#ced3da` (they read as watermarks).
6. Step names: tiny, widely tracked black caps along the outside of each half ring, tops
   pointing outward, reading clockwise (down the right side, up the left side).
7. Illustrations: flat spot drawings in the page's five colors with small stars and
   squiggles around them (a magnifier over a raised hand, a rosette with ribbons, a group of
   raised hands, a box with a slip of paper, a striped top hat).
8. Body texts in dark gray `#444444`.
9. Title: lead-in in slate blue `#4a5a8a`, connector in red, subject in black.
10. Web address in near-black.

## 8. Charts and diagrams

* **The serpentine curve** is five half rings drawn by `scripts/donut.js` (C-half-donut with a single
  slice): `values=1`, one color, `labelpos=none sep=none`, `sweep=180`, placed by center
  with `cx=50`, `r=18.7`, `inner=0.881` (a 24 px line whose middle is 190 px from the
  axis). Odd steps use `start=0` (the right half), even steps `start=180` (the left half).
  Centers are one diameter of the middle line apart (380 px), so the ends meet on the axis.
* **C-arc-label** for the step names: a half circle of radius 232 px on the same center,
  drawn clockwise (top to bottom for the right side: `M 540,cy-232 A 232 232 0 0 1
  540,cy+232`; bottom to top for the left side: `M 540,cy+232 A 232 232 0 0 1 540,cy-232`),
  text centered at 50% of the path.
* **Why not C-snake-steps:** `scripts/steps.js style=snake` draws one S-shaped ribbon in a
  single color with a node at every swing (five steps get four half swings, starting and
  ending on a node) and sets the text outside the nodes. This design needs one half circle
  per step that begins and ends on the axis, a color change at every junction, and an
  empty bowl for the picture, so the half-ring build stays. C-snake-steps is the quick
  relative (section 13); C-process-spine (`dir=v side=alt`) is the straight fallback for 6
  or more steps.
* Not built: the rounded cap on the first segment's free end, and the wavy hand-drawn
  squiggles around the pictures.

## 9. Icons and illustration

* One two-tone line icon per step, 170 px, black outline 9 px, light blue fill `#a7b7da`:
  `clipboard-list`, `pen-tool`, `megaphone`, `vote`, `calendar-check`. The originals are
  flat spot illustrations about 20% W across, each built from the same five colors.
* Two small stars beside every icon (red, outer radius 12 px; light blue, 15 px), inside
  the bowl, never touching the curve.
* Star bands: eight `shape star` marks, outer radius 24 px, per band.
* No photos. To use your own drawings, place `illustration.png` 220 px wide on each bowl's
  center.

## 10. Effects and finish

None: flat color, no shadows or gradients. The only soft shapes are the wavy edges of the
two panels (one `shape path` each).

## 11. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The black subject line of the title.
2. The curve: its thick colored line pulls the eye down the whole page at once.
3. Numerals 1 to 5, alternating right and left.
4. Per step: picture, then the body text; the curved name is read last, as a label.
5. The footer address.

Numerals 180 px (pale), subject 83 (black), lead-in 62, body 30, names 19.

## 13. Variations and pairings

* **Simpler spines:** C-snake-steps (`scripts/steps.js` with `style=snake connector=line`,
  `line='#3b4b7f'`, the five segment colors as `colors=`) builds a quick relative in one
  command: one navy ribbon, a numbered disc per step, names and bodies set by the script;
  it gives up the bowls, the curved names, and the color change per step. For 6 to 8 steps
  use C-process-spine (`dir=v side=alt`) with the same header, star bands, and palette; the
  curve only pays off up to about 6 swings.
* **Other themes:** drop the star bands and recolor the segments in one hue from light to
  dark (a journey that deepens), or in a brand's two colors alternating.
* **Dates instead of numerals:** put a year or a month where the numeral is: the serpentine curve
  becomes a timeline (compare C-timeline with `dir=v`).
* **Square carousel:** one swing per 1080 x 1080 slide, with the curve entering at the top
  center and leaving at the bottom center of every slide.
* Pairs with C-stat-row in the footer panel (three facts about the process) and with
  C-icon-badge when the pictures should sit in filled discs.

## 14. Pitfalls

* **Half circles that do not meet.** The centers must be exactly one diameter of the line's
  middle apart, and the canvas height must match the `cy` percentages; after changing the
  canvas height recompute every `cy` (center y divided by the height). A gap or overlap at a
  junction is the first thing a reader sees.
* **Order lost in the zig-zag.** Text jumps from left to right at every step; the numerals
  and the unbroken line carry the order. Never drop the numerals, and never let two
  segments in a row share a color.
* **Segment colors read as categories.** Red appears twice here without meaning; if your
  steps do fall into phases, color by phase and say so, otherwise keep the colors plainly
  decorative. A reader should not have to wonder whether steps 2 and 5 belong together:
  this is the sheet's integrity risk, since there is no data to check.
* **Curved names that are sentences.** At 19 px with wide tracking an arc holds about 22
  characters; longer names wrap around the junctions and collide with the next step.
* **Body text inside the bowl.** The open side looks roomy, but the picture needs the
  bowl; keep text outside the circle's radius (inner edge 162 px from the axis).
* **Pale numerals on a pale panel.** The numerals share the panel's gray-blue; if a
  numeral ever overlaps the header or footer panel it disappears. Keep steps on the
  off-white ground.
