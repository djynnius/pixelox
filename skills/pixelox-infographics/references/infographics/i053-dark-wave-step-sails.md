# i053: Dark wave of five gradient step tabs and sails

## 1. Concept

A landscape five-step graphic on dark navy. Each step is a pair of bright gradient shapes: a
narrow pointed tab with STEP and a big number, and beside it a wider wing-shaped "sail" with
a white line icon and a short note. Three pairs stand in an upper row with their points up
(steps 01, 03, 05); two hang in a lower row with their points down (steps 02, 04), shifted
half a pair so they lock into the gaps like teeth. Together they read as one wave. A white
pill with the step's name floats above or below each tab, tied to a dot on the tab's tip by
a thin line. The message is "five steps that interlock". It works because the dark ground
makes five saturated hue families glow, the two rows give every step the same two parts in
mirror image, and the zig-zag order (up, down, up, down, up) is carried by the numbers.

## 2. Data story

No numbers except the step numbers. Text only, as an ordered list:

* 5 steps. Each: a two-digit number under the word STEP, a name of one caps word (5 to 8
  letters) in a pill, a note of 9 to 14 words on 4 lines (the original: 7 very small lines),
  and one icon.
* No title. The piece is a module for a slide or a page section.
* Order: 01 upper left, 02 lower, 03 upper middle, 04 lower, 05 upper right. (The original
  repeats "01" on the last tab: a numbering slip; see Pitfalls.)

Placeholder content in the recipe (invented, making a printed piece): BRIEF, RESEARCH,
DESIGN, PROOF, DELIVER, each with a note of 9 to 11 words.

## 3. Information architecture

1. **Upper row**, left to right: step 01 (tab, then sail), step 03, step 05. Tab on the left
   of its sail; pill above the tab, a little to the left.
2. **Lower row**: step 02 and step 04, each turned half a turn: sail on the left, tab on
   the right, pointing down; pill below the tab, a little to the right.
3. **Columns** line up across the rows: a lower sail hangs directly under an upper sail, a
   lower tab under the next upper tab. So the eye drops from 01's sail to 02's sail, slides
   right to 02's tab, and rises to 03's tab: a continuous wave.

In an upper sail the icon is on top and the note below; in a lower sail the note is on top
and the icon below, so icons sit at the outer edges and notes face the middle seam.

  sails 10.5% W (168 px), gutters 1.6% W (26 px). One pair (tab, gutter, sail) is 20.6% W;
  pairs repeat every 22.25% W (356 px).

* Lower row from 51.2% to 78.6% H (y 546 to 838): pairs start at x = 500 and 856 (the sail

* Tab: straight sides, a pointed arch at the outer end (tip 252 px from the flat end). Sail:
  as wide as its column at the flat end, growing a horn that leans over the neighboring
  tab's shoulder; its outer edge is one long convex arc (radii 244 x 150 px) from the horn's

* Pills 172 x 50 px, centered 100 px left of the tab's axis at 13.8% H (upper) or 102 px

* Margins: 21% W left, 13.6% W right (the figure sits right of center because the pills of
  the upper row lean left); 11% H above the pills, 11.6% H below.

## 13. Variations and pairings

* **Library version:** C-process-row with `side=alt`, five colors, and `style=circle`
  gives the same up-down rhythm with round nodes and automatic text; use it when time is
  short or the notes are long.
* **Light ground:** ground `#f4f6f8`, shadows at 20% opacity, pills outlined in `#d5dbe1`;
  the gradients stay, the texture goes.
* **One hue:** five steps of blue from `#7cc0f0` to `#0b3c6f` for a single-brand look; keep
  each sail one step lighter than its tab.
* **With a title:** canvas 1600 x 1200, the figure moved down 130 px, a C-title-block in
  white on top.
* Pairs with C-timeline underneath when each step has a date, and with C-stat-row (white
  numbers on the navy) to show what the process delivers.

## 14. Pitfalls

* **Wrong or repeated numbers.** The original prints "01" on the fifth tab. With a
  zig-zag layout the numbers are the only statement of order: check them against the pills
  before export. A wrong step number is a factual error (the data-integrity rule here).
* **Reading the rows separately.** Without clear numbers a reader takes the upper row as
  steps 1 to 3 and the lower as 4 and 5. Keep the numbers at 70 px and never drop them for
  icons.
* **Notes longer than the sail.** A sail is 168 px wide and loses its outer corner to the
  curve: four lines of 18 characters at 16 px is the limit. The original's seven lines at 9
  px cannot be read.
* **Gradients running the wrong way.** Every sail brightens toward its outer side (left to
  right); a mirrored gradient on one sail makes it look bent. Keep `--angle 0` on all sails.
* **Shadows copied after the gradients.** `fx copy` copies every effect: copy the shadow
  first, add each shape's own gradient afterwards, or all ten shapes turn the first color.
* **Pills over the shapes.** The pills belong on the dark ground, clear of the tips by at
  least 60 px; on top of a sail they lose their contrast and hide the icon.
