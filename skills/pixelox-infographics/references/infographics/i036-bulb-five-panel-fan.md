# i036: Outline bulb with a fan of five panels

## 1. Concept

A landscape concept graphic on white. A light bulb drawn as one thick amber outline lies
tilted at 45 degrees, its screw base pointing to the lower left; the title is written inside
the glass. Five solid panels in five colors fan around the glass from the left, over the top,
to the lower right, like the blades of a hand fan, and each holds a white line icon, a short
title, and a note. The message is "one idea, five parts". It works because the panels are
slices of one ring that shares its center with the bulb, so they read as light given off by
the idea, and because the base fills the one gap the fan leaves open.

## 2. Data story

No numbers. A topic with five facets of equal weight, in no strict order (they are read
clockwise because that is how a fan opens):

* 1 hub title of 2 words on 2 lines (11 and 7 letters).
* 5 facets. Each: a title of 2 words (about 11 letters) and a note. The first three notes
  have about 13 words on 4 lines; the last two about 20 words on 6 lines, and their panels
  are deeper to hold them.
* 1 tiny four-bar pictogram above the title; it shows no data and repeats four of the panel
  colors.

The original's copy is filler text; only the pattern matters.

Placeholder content in the recipe (invented, about habits of a remote team): STAY IN TOUCH,
WRITE IT DOWN, DEEP FOCUS, MIND THE CLOCK, BRIGHT IDEAS, with notes of 11 to 22 words.

## 3. Information architecture

1. **Hub**: the bulb with the two-line title in the glass. Line one is dark, line two teal;
   the four small bars above them say "this is an information graphic".
2. **Five panels**, clockwise from 8 o'clock to 4 o'clock: amber, red, teal, dark slate,
   plum. Each answers "what is part N of the idea" with icon, title, note stacked and
   centered. Text stays upright in every panel; only the panel shapes turn.
3. **The open sector**, lower left: the bulb's base. Nothing else is there.

The eye starts in the glass (the only dark type on white), jumps to the nearest saturated
panel on the left, and sweeps clockwise to the two larger panels on the right, where the
longer notes are.

## 4. Format and grid

* Landscape, about 3 : 2. Recipe canvas 1600 x 1066. No margins as such: the fan comes within
  1 to 2% of the top, right, and bottom edges and leaves 11% W free on the left.
* One center for everything: 47% W, 57.3% H.
* Fan: a ring sector that starts 102 degrees left of the top (about 8:30 on a clock) and
  sweeps 235 degrees clockwise to 133 degrees (about 4:30). Inner radius 17.5% W. Outer
  radius grows clockwise: 35.5% W for panels 1 and 2, 38% for panel 3, 41% for panels 4 and
  5. Each panel spans 41 degrees; the gaps are white, about 2% W wide and parallel-sided.
* Bulb: the glass is a circle of radius 14.7% W (to the outside of the line) on the fan's
  center, so a white ring of 2.8% W separates glass and panels. The neck and base run to the
  lower left at 45 degrees; the base ends at 23% W, 93% H.
* Title block inside the glass, right-aligned at 57.2% W: bars from 41% to 51% W, 41% to 47%
  H; line one cap top at 50% H; line two at 58% H.
* Panel text is centered on each panel's middle: 20%,53% (amber); 31%,23% (red); 53%,16%
  (teal); 74%,37% (slate); 76%,74% (plum).

## 5. Layer stack

1. Ground: white `#ffffff`, flat.
2. Five panels, each a slice of the ring with corners rounded by about 2% W: amber `#f7a83d`,
   red `#da4d44`, teal `#50abb2`, dark slate `#464254`, plum `#874578`.
3. Bulb outline: one amber `#f7a83d` line 2% W thick: a circle that runs into a short neck.
   The inside of the glass is white.
4. Screw base: brown `#8b6239` (the original: an outlined pointed cap with three diagonal
   bars; the recipe: four solid brown bars), 15% W long.
5. Filament: two amber lines 0.9% W thick that rise from the neck, cross, and end in two
   loops of radius 2% W, in the lower left of the glass.
6. Four small bars (amber, red, teal, slate), each 1.6% W wide, heights 1.9% to 5% H, on one
   baseline.
7. Title: line one slate `#464254`, line two teal `#50abb2`.
8. Per panel: a white line icon 4.5% W across, a white title, a white note.

## 8. Charts and diagrams

* **C-petal-wheel** draws the fan: one `scripts/donut.js` render placed by center
  (`cx=47 cy=57.3 r=41`) with `values=1,1,1,1,1` (equal angles), `sweep=235 start=-102`,
  `inner=0.427` (inner radius 280 px, 17.5% W), `gappx=32` (parallel white gaps), `corner=30`
  (rounded corners), `sep=none`, `labelpos=none`, and `radii=0.766,0.766,0.872,1,1` for the
  three depths: outer radii of 568, 608, and 656 px (35.5%, 38%, 41% W). A `radii=` value is
  the share of the ring's depth, from the inner radius to the full outer radius, that the
  slice takes. The script logs the middle of every slice (`slice1` ...): the icon, title,
  and note are centered there.
* No axis, no legend, no numbers: slice angle and depth encode nothing (depth only makes room
  for longer notes).
* **C-bulb**, the line-art form: `scripts/object.js`, `kind=bulb outline=1 bands=1 rotate=45
  shade=0`, `colors='#f7a83d'` (the line around glass and neck, 24 px at this size),
  `dark='#8b6239'` (the base bars, not outlined), box `17.46,27.75,49.23,73.88`: a square of
  788 px whose center lies 79 px left of and 79 px below the fan's center. That puts the
  glass circle (235 px to the outside of the line) exactly on the fan's center.
* **C-columns** as a pictogram: `scripts/bars.js` in a box of 9.9% W x 5.3% H with four
  values, four colors, `axis=0 gridlines=0 vlabels=0`. It is decoration; give it no labels.
* Simplified: the original's bulb line is thicker (2% W against 1.5% W here); its bulb base
  is an outlined cap with diagonal bars.

## 9. Icons and illustration

* Panel icons: white Lucide line icons, 64 px, 3 px line, centered 78 px above each slice
  anchor: `smartphone`, `file-badge`, `brain-cog`, `watch`, `lightbulb`. The original shows a
  phone sending signal waves, a certificate with a seal, a head with a gear inside, a wrist
  watch, and a bulb with a clock face and rays: thin 2 px outlines, about 4.5% W across.
* The bulb is the illustration: `scripts/object.js` for the shape, `shape path` and two
  `shape ellipse` rings for the filament. The original's filament is one wire curled into two
  loops, like a pretzel.
* No photos, no logo.

## 10. Effects and finish

* None on the panels: flat color, no shadows, no gradients.
* Bulb line: drawn by the script (`outline=1`), one render, no layer style: the line runs
  around the glass and the neck only, so the brown base is not outlined in amber.
* Rounded panel corners (about 30 px at 1600 wide) and gaps of one width from the inner to
  the outer edge: `corner=30` and `gappx=32` in the fan render.

## 11. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The title in the glass: the largest type, dark on white, ringed by the amber line.
2. The panels as one fan of color; the eye enters at the amber panel (it shares the bulb's
   color) and sweeps clockwise.
3. Inside each panel: icon, title, note, top to bottom.
4. The base, last: it anchors the picture and closes the ring.

Title 65 px against 18 px panel titles: 3.6 : 1. The panels win by area (35% of the canvas),
the title by size and contrast.

Notes for reuse:

* **Change first:** the two title words, the five panel titles and notes, the five icon
  names. Keep line one of the title to 11 letters at 56 px (it must end 30 px inside the
  glass on the left); shrink to 48 px for longer words.
* **Where the numbers come from.** Panel text hangs on the slice anchors of the fan render
  (336,526; 530,249; 871,183; 1167,395; 1193,767): icon center 78 px above the anchor, title
  17 px above, note starting 7 px below (for the 6-line notes: icon 102 px above, title 41
  px above, note 17 px above). Run the `render js` line with `--json` again when you change
  `r`, `sweep`, `radii`, or the number of values.
* **Other counts:** 4 panels: `values=1,1,1,1` and four `radii=` values; 6 panels:
  `sweep=240` and notes of 3 lines. Leave `radii=` out for panels of one depth. Keep the
  open sector at least 110 degrees wide for the base.
* **The bulb**: the center of its glass is the center of its box moved right and up by
  0.177 x `scale` px each (the script logs `scale`: 445.5 here, so 79 px). Choose the box so
  that this point is the fan's center, and keep the box square for `rotate=45`. The
  filament's four points move with the bulb.
* The text is white on every panel. On the amber panel white reaches a lightness difference
  of only about 30 points: keep those titles bold, or darken the amber to `#e08a1a`.

## 13. Variations and pairings

* **Upright bulb, fan on top:** `rotate=0`, fan `start=-110 sweep=220`, a portrait canvas;
  the title moves under the base.
* **Numbers in the panels:** replace each icon with a figure at 64 px ("42%", "3x") and keep
  title and note: five key numbers around one topic. Say the source under the fan.
* **One hue:** five steps of one color from light to dark, clockwise, when the facets are
  stages; then number them 01 to 05 in place of the icons.
* **Other hubs:** a `target`, `globe`, or `brain` icon at 300 px inside a plain amber ring
  when a bulb says the wrong thing.
* **Relatives:** i026 is the other bulb design (a filled bulb with many small icon nodes on
  thin lines: more items, less text each). i038 is the object-with-callouts idea for ordered
  steps. Pairs with C-icon-grid or C-stat-row in a strip under the fan on a taller canvas.

## 14. Pitfalls

* **Text that follows the panels.** Rotating each block to its panel's angle makes five
  reading directions. Keep all text upright and centered on the slice anchors.
* **Notes that touch the panel edges.** A slice narrows toward the center: lines must get
  no wider than 60% of the panel's depth (about 250 px here). Shorten the lower lines first.
* **Unequal slices read as data (data integrity).** A wider or deeper panel looks like a
  bigger share. Here all angles are equal on purpose (`values=1,1,1,1,1`); the deeper panels
  exist only to hold longer notes, which a reader may still take for importance. If your
  facets are equal, leave `radii=` out; if they have sizes, show the numbers in a
  real chart (C-donut, C-bars), never by stretching panels by eye.
* **The fan and the bulb on different centers.** The white ring between glass and panels
  must have one width all round; put the center of the glass on the fan's `cx`, `cy` (the
  reuse notes say how to find it from the bulb's box).
* **An amber outline around the base.** A layer stroke (`fx stroke`) on a filled bulb also
  outlines the screw base and turns it into a blob. Use the script's `outline=1`: it draws
  the line around the glass and the neck only.
* **Six colors of type.** All type on the panels is white; only the two title words are
  colored. Do not tint the notes.
