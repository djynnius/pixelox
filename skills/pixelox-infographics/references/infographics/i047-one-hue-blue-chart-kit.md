# i047: One-hue blue chart and diagram kit

## 1. Concept

A parts kit of about thirty charts, timelines, and diagrams drawn in a single blue, from pale
sky to navy, on a light gray ground, shown as three tall panels side by side. Nothing has a
real title and the text is filler: the kit teaches a system, not a story. The system is one
hue in six steps used as an ordered series (first item lightest, last item darkest), gray for
tracks and everything that is "not yet", condensed caps for every label, and no frames or
cards. It works because six tints of one color can never clash, so any combination of its
parts looks designed. The recipe builds one long sheet from nine of them.

## 2. Data story

The kit shows chart **forms**; its numbers are filler. The forms and their data shapes:

| Element | Data shape |
| --- | --- |
| Stepped donut | 6 parts, lettered, light to dark; each slice also a little thicker than the last |
| Half-circle fan | 6 values (40 to 90%) as wedges of equal angle and different radius |
| Layer stack, sliced sphere | 6 levels, text only |
| Row timeline | 5 numbered nodes with years, text alternating above and below |
| Vertical timelines | 5 or 6 years down a segmented bar, a snaking ribbon, or a line with numbered bubbles |
| Circle flower | 5 independent percentages (35 to 90%) |
| Ring row | 5 independent percentages, rising from about 25 to 80%, one ring each |
| Segmented pill | 5 shares of one whole (each about a fifth) with a value badge per segment |
| Sliders, segmented bars | 4 percentages (40 to 90%) on gray tracks |
| Test tubes | 3 wholes, each split into 3 or 4 layers with a small key |
| Grouped columns, grouped bars | 3 series x 4 categories; 2 series x 3 categories |
| Areas, lines | 2 or 3 series over 5 to 12 points on a gray grid |
| Columns with a line | 8 columns in two alternating tints and one trend line with dots |
| People rows | 4 years, each a count out of 10 figures and a big percentage |
| Two-sided bars | 4 rows, each split between two groups (women and men) |
| Circle network | 16 nodes in 3 sizes joined by dashed links; 5 node kinds in a key |
| Dot world map | 5 numbered pins with a 5-entry key |

Placeholder data in the recipe (example data, invented, a sports club's membership): donut
9, 12, 24, 15, 22, 18%; five milestones from 2017 to 2025; rings 28, 34, 71, 78, 85%;
columns of 3 series over 4 quarters (35 to 76%); a fan of six weekdays (48, 62, 79, 93, 71,
55%); sliders 58, 91, 74, 43%; two-sided rows 62 and 38, 48 and 52, 35 and 65, 71 and 29;
people rows 3, 7, 4, 10 of 10; five map pins.

## 3. Information architecture

The kit has no reading order. Each panel is two columns of unrelated elements separated by
space alone; the only heading in the whole kit is one two-word section title (a bold word
and a light word) with a two-line note. As a catalog:

1. **Panel 1:** stepped donut with a six-entry text key; a text block beside a half-circle
   fan; a layer stack and a sliced sphere with leader labels; the section title; a row
   timeline beside a vertical segmented timeline; a circle flower beside five numbered
   blocks; a row of five rings with keys.
2. **Panel 2:** five ribbons twisting round an arrow, with notes; a segmented pill with value
   badges; a serpentine timeline beside a bubble timeline; four sliders beside four segmented
   bars; three test tubes; a gear holding a four-line list; grouped columns beside areas.
3. **Panel 3:** grouped bars beside areas on a grid; columns with a trend line beside two
   lines on a grid; four hexagon-topped banners beside four numbered rows; people rows
   beside two-sided bars; a circle network with a key; a dot world map with numbered pins.

The recipe's sheet gives nine elements an order: head; donut and key; row timeline; ring
row; columns beside the fan; sliders beside two-sided bars; people rows beside the map;
source.

## 5. Layer stack

1. Ground `#f4f4f4`, flat. No cards. Thin dividers `#d4d4d4` only between panels.
2. Gray furniture: tracks and unfilled ring parts `#d5d5d5`; chart gridlines and axes
   `#c9ced2`, 1 px; connector bands and leaders `#cfd6db`; "empty" people figures `#dfe3e6`.
3. Marks in the six-step blue ramp: `#a5d6ef`, `#6dc5e5`, `#2f9fd1`, `#018fcc`, `#146ea7`,
   `#295576`. In any ordered set the first item takes the lightest step and the last the
   darkest. Two-series charts use `#6dc5e5` with `#295576`; three use `#6dc5e5`, `#295576`,
   `#018fcc`.
4. White details on marks: numbers and letters inside nodes and slices, the knob on each
   slider, the hexagons that hold icons, the inner disc of pins and bubbles.
5. Type: condensed caps in slate `#4a6275` for titles and keys; condensed numerals in gray
   `#5b6770` for years and ring values; filler notes in gray `#8a949c`.

## 6. Palette

| Hex | Role | Area | Encodes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `#f4f4f4` | ground | 70% | nothing |
| `#a5d6ef` | ramp step 1 | 3% | first item of an ordered set |
| `#6dc5e5` | ramp step 2; series 1 in two-series charts | 5% | second item; series A |
| `#2f9fd1` | ramp step 3 | 5% | third item |
| `#018fcc` | ramp step 4; map pins | 4% | fourth item |
| `#146ea7` | ramp step 5 | 3% | fifth item |
| `#295576` | ramp step 6; series 2 in two-series charts; headings (`#4a6275`) | 4% | last item; series B |

Plus gray `#d5d5d5` (6%) for tracks, grids, and "the rest". The scheme is a sequential ramp
used as an ordinal series: position in the list, not size, picks the step. There is no
accent color at all; emphasis comes from the darkest step.

## 8. Charts and diagrams

Built in the recipe (all at `--size 1080x2400`; condensed labels use
`font='Avenir Next Condensed, Oswald, Barlow Condensed, Arial Narrow, sans-serif'`):

* **C-donut**: `scripts/donut.js`, `cx=26 cy=17 r=15.5`, `inner=0.45`, 6 values in the ramp
  order, `labelpos=in show=pct`, `sep` = the ground, `center=` and `sub=` for the two-line hub
  text, and `radii=0.7,0.76,0.82,0.88,0.94,1`: each slice's outer radius is a little longer
  than the last, the original's spiral (the C-polar-area option on a donut). The shares
  are still the angles and are printed in the slices; the steps only mark the order. The
  original writes a letter in each slice instead of its share.
* **C-legend**: `dir=column`, `bold=1`, `fs=26`, six ramp squares beside the donut.
* **C-process-row** as a timeline: `scripts/steps.js`, box `6,27.6,88,11.6`, `side=alt`,
  `badges='01,02,03,04,05'`, years as `labels=`, one short note each, five ramp colors,
  `line='#cfd6db'`, `titlesize=30`. The kit's connector is a 14 px gray band behind the nodes.
* **C-progress-ring** x5: `kind=ring`, `cy=44.6 r=6.6`, `cx=14, 32, 50, 68, 86`,
  `inner=0.83`, `round=0`, `trackcolor='#d5d5d5'`, values rising with the ramp, gray
  condensed number inside (`centersize=38`). A caps name under each.
* **C-grouped-columns**: `scripts/bars.js`, box `6,54,42,13.4`, 3 series x 4 categories,
  `max=100 ticks=5`, `vlabels=0`, `legend=none`, axis and gridlines on.
* **C-polar-area** as the half-circle fan: `scripts/donut.js`, box `49,54,48,11.6`,
  `sweep=180`, six equal `values=1,1,1,1,1,1` (six wedges of 30 degrees),
  `radii=0.48,0.62,0.79,0.93,0.71,0.55` (a wedge's radius is its value divided by 100),
  `inner=0.16`, `gappx=3`, ramp colors, and `labelpos=out show=none` with the six values as
  `labels=`, which the script writes at the wedge ends on short leaders. A six-key C-legend
  row under it names the wedges. The original sets the fan on a pale half disc with tick
  marks, writes the percentages along the wedge ends, and runs elbow leaders from the hub
  down to six letters.
* **C-progress-bars** (sliders): box `6,72.6,40,8.6`, `dir=h track=1 max=100`, `gap=0.45`,
  `trackcolor='#d5d5d5'`, value at the track's right end; a white 18 px knob (`shape ellipse`)
  14 px inside each bar's logged end anchor.
* **C-butterfly-bars** (two-sided bars): `scripts/bars.js`, box `52,72.2,42,9.6`,
  `mode=diverge track=1 max=100`, 2 series x 4 rows, `trackcolor='#d5d5d5'`: the two groups
  grow left and right from a middle column of row names, each on a gray track, with the
  group names on top in their colors and the values at the bar ends. Both sides share one
  scale (`max=100`), so lengths compare across the middle. The kit numbers the rows 01 to 04
  and puts a round avatar over each side.
* **C-icon-array** x4: `scripts/waffle.js`, `shape=person`, `value=N total=10`, one row per
  year, 22% W x 2.7% H each, the row's ramp step for the filled figures, `empty='#dfe3e6'`.
  The kit leaves out the unfilled figures and turns the year labels 90 degrees.
* **C-dot-map**: `scripts/worldmap.js`, box `52,85.2,42,12`, `shape=square`,
  `dot='#b3bcc4'`, five `points=` labeled 01 to 05, `pointcolor='#018fcc'`, `halo` = the
  ground. The stylized map is kept on purpose: the kit's own map is a dot matrix. **Map
  spec:** region set world land without Antarctica, drawn as a matrix of small square dots
  (about 110 columns across in the kit; the script's grid is coarser), no borders;
  projection as it appears: equirectangular or Miller (straight parallels, flat top);
  encoding: five locations as numbered pins (a ringed drop 5.5% W wide with a white disc and
  the number 01 to 05, one ramp step per pin) in North America, South America, central
  Africa, northern Asia, and Australia; land dots `#b3bcc4`; legend: a row of five small
  pin marks with caps names under the map; no region labels, no insets. Outline
  alternative (pixelox 0.6.0 or later): C-world-map, `scripts/geo.js` run with `render d3`,
  the same box, `points=`, `pointcolor=`, and `halo=`, and `land='#b3bcc4'`.

Described only (not in the recipe):

* **Areas on a grid**: two or three areas over 5 to 12 points, each drawn from zero with
  the darker one in front, so the back series are partly hidden: C-stacked-area
  (`scripts/line.js` with `area=stack`) keeps every series visible.
* **Segmented pill with drop badges**: one vertical pill cut into five ramp segments, a
  value in a teardrop badge beside each: C-percent-bar turned upright (`mode=percent` with
  one category) plus C-speech-bubble badges.
* **Serpentine timeline**: five gray numbered discs on a ribbon that swings left and right
  between them, years on the outer side: C-process-spine with `side=alt` is the straight
  version.
* **Bubble timeline**: a vertical line with ringed dots and years, numbered bubbles with a
  square tail alternating sides: C-timeline with `dir=v`.
* **Circle flower**: five overlapping discs (radius 7.5% W) on a ring of radius 10.8% W, a
  white percentage in each: C-venn draws up to four; for five use a C-progress-ring row.
* **Segmented bars**: a solid part, then ticks, on a gray track: C-progress-bars.
* **Test tubes**: outlined round-bottom tubes with three or four stacked liquid layers and a
  small key with a share per layer: C-stacked-columns with `mode=percent` under a tube
  outline, or C-picture-gauge for one level.
* **Columns with a line**: C-combo. **Lines on a grid**: C-multi-line. **Grouped bars with
  a gray comparison bar**: C-bars with two series.
* **Numbered rows and hexagon banners**: C-ranked-list and C-icon-grid in the ramp colors;
  each banner is a pointed strip with a white hexagon holding a line icon.
* **Circle network**: sixteen discs in three sizes holding white service marks, linked by
  dashed gray lines, with a five-entry key: the user's own marks only; nearest C-hub-spokes.
* **Twisting arrows**: five ribbons winding round one vertical arrow, each ending in an
  arrowhead and a note: an illustration of "one start, several outcomes", not a chart.
* **3D pieces:** the layer stack is six rhombus plates in isometric view (elevation about
  30 degrees, turned 45 degrees), each a flat top face in its ramp step with a darker edge
  (about 12% darker) on the two front sides, light from above; the sliced sphere is a ball
  cut into six horizontal slabs seen from about 15 degrees above, each slab one ramp step
  with a darker rim. Depth encodes nothing in either: both are six ordered levels. Flat
  equivalent: C-pyramid or six rows of a C-ranked-list.

## 9. Icons and illustration

* The kit's icons are thin line icons in white hexagons or blue discs: `lightbulb`, `trophy`,
  `shopping-cart`, `user` (banners and avatars), and a large `settings` gear as a holder for
  a short list. All one line weight, never filled.
* People figures: the standard standing pictogram (`shape=person`), one per unit.
* Pins: `map-pin` shape with a white disc for a number.
* Service marks in the network belong to their owners: place the user's own `logo.png`
  files in the discs; never redraw a mark.
* The recipe uses no icons besides the people figures.

## 10. Effects and finish

None: flat color. Depth appears only in the two isometric pieces and as a faint darker edge
on plates. Rounded ends on pills, sliders, and tubes; square ends on ring arcs and columns.
No shadows, no gradients, no outlines except the tube walls and chart frames.

## 11. Hierarchy and eye path

In the recipe's sheet:

1. The head (the only 50 px type) and the donut under it, the largest dark shape.
2. The row of five numbered nodes, then the five rings: two full-width rows that read as
   "steps" and "status".
3. The columns and the fan, the sliders, the two-sided bars.
4. The people rows with their 46 px shares, and the map.

Because every mark is the same hue, weight comes only from size and darkness: put the item
that matters last in its set, where the navy is.

## 13. Variations and pairings

* **Another hue:** the system works in any single hue. Teal: `#bfe9e2`, `#7fd3c6`,
  `#35b3a2`, `#0f9488`, `#0b6f66`, `#0a4f4a`. Violet: see the palette "Violet report" in
  [../palettes.md](../palettes.md). Keep the gray furniture.
* **With one accent:** add a single warm color (`#f2a541`) for the one item the title talks
  about (`highlight=` in `scripts/bars.js`), and leave everything else blue.
* **Slide or card:** any two neighbors of the sheet (donut and key; columns and areas) make
  a 1600 x 900 slide with the section title on top.
* **Process sheet:** the row timeline, the ring row, and a C-process-spine in the same ramp
  make a three-part "plan, progress, next steps" page.
* Pairs with C-table (navy header row, pale blue zebra rows) and with C-funnel in the ramp
  colors.

## 14. Pitfalls

* **A ramp that looks like a scale.** Light-to-dark reads as "less to more". Use the ramp
  only for ordered sets (steps, years, ranks) or sort the categories so the darkest is the
  largest; never give random categories random tints.
* **Radius and angle both changing.** The stepped donut and the fan change a slice's radius
  as well as (or instead of) its angle, so area no longer follows the value: a wedge twice
  as long looks four times as big. Print every number (the recipe does), keep a donut's
  steps small, never let length carry a second measure, and use equal radii or C-bars when
  the values must be compared.
* **Six blues too close together.** Neighboring steps need a lightness difference of at
  least 10 points or slices merge; with more than six items, group them.
* **Overlapping areas.** The kit's area charts hide the back series behind the front one.
  Stack them, or draw lines.
* **Percent rows without a base.** People rows ("7 of 10") and split rows need what the 10
  or the 100% stands for in the element title.
* **Filler left in.** Every element in the kit has three lines of note under it; in a real
  sheet most elements need none. Keep the title, cut the note.
