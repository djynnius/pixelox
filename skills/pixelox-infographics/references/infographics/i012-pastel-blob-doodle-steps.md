# i012: Long pastel blob sheet with five doodle steps

## 1. Concept

A tall classroom-style process sheet. Five huge pastel circles (sky blue, cream yellow, pink,
pale teal, peach) overlap down a white page and each one holds one step: a heading on a white
paint-stroke label, a centered paragraph, a number in a white disc, and a cartoon doodle (a
bulb, a magnifier, a megaphone, a pencil, a target). Steps zig-zag: text left and doodle
right, then the reverse. The title mixes fat outlined caps with one hand-lettered word on a
green spot. The message is "a project has five friendly stages". It works because the blobs
do the layout: every step gets its own colored room without a single box or rule, and all
type and outlines are black, so five colors and five drawings still read as one page.

## 2. Data story

No numbers except the step numbers. Text only, as an ordered list:

* 5 steps. Each: a heading of one caps word (4 to 11 letters), a note of about 20 words set
  on 6 to 7 short centered lines, and a single digit.
* 1 title of two words: one in caps (7 to 8 letters), one in lower-case hand lettering (4 to
  8 letters).
* 5 pictures, one per step, that show the step's idea as an object.

Placeholder content in the recipe (invented, a school science fair): QUESTION, READING,
GUESS, TEST, REPORT, each with a note of 21 to 24 words.

## 3. Information architecture

1. **Title**, top: caps word on the blue blob at the left, the hand-lettered word on a green
   spot at the right, a hand-drawn arrow curving from the first word to the second.
2. **Step 1** (blue blob): label and note left, bulb right, disc "1" where blue meets yellow.
3. **Step 2** (yellow blob): magnifier left, label and note right, disc "2" at the right.
4. **Step 3** (pink blob): label and note left, megaphone right, disc "3" in the middle.
5. **Step 4** (teal blob): pencil left, label and note right, disc "4" at the right.
6. **Step 5** (peach blob): label and note left, target right, disc "5" in the middle.

The numbered discs are the stepping stones: they sit on the seams between two blobs, so the
eye crosses from one color into the next at exactly that point. Labels tilt a few degrees,
all the same way, which makes the page feel hand-made without hurting the reading order.

## 4. Format and grid

* Long portrait, 1 : 2.5. Recipe canvas 1080 x 2700. No hard margins: blobs bleed off both
  sides; text keeps 5% W from the edges.
* Blobs (center, radius in %W): blue 25% W, 14.3% H, 47% W wide by 20% H tall radius
  (slightly taller than wide); yellow 83% W, 32.6% H, r 44.6% W; pink 12.5% W, 53.2% H,
  r 48.2% W; teal 84.8% W, 70.4% H, r 50.9% W; peach 33.9% W, 93.6% H, r 46.8% W. Each blob
  is about one screen (40% of the page height) tall and overlaps the next by a third.
* Title: caps word `6%,3%,45% x 5%`; hand-lettered word centered on 71% W with its baseline
  at 10.2% H; green spot `48%,8.6%,46% x 2.7%`.
* Two text columns: left centered on 27% to 29% W, right on 56% to 72% W; each 27% W wide.
* Step rows (heading center, in %H): 19.9, 36, 49.8, 67.1, 81.6. A step takes 14 to 17% H.
* Labels 42% W wide, 5% H tall with their tilt. Discs 13.3% W across.
* Pictures 26 to 30% W wide, in the column opposite the text.

## 5. Layer stack

1. Ground: white.
2. Five pastel blobs, back to front: blue `#8ecdec`, yellow `#ffecb3`, peach `#fed9a8`, teal
   `#bee7e9`, pink `#fbe7f1`. White ground shows in three corners and in slivers between
   blobs.
3. Green spot `#6aa84f`: a flat ellipse under the title's second word.
4. Title: the caps word in black with a white outline 9 px wide all round (a white copy with
   a thick stroke, behind the black one); the second word in black marker lettering.
5. A black hand-drawn arrow (one curve and a two-stroke head, 5 px, round ends).
6. Five white labels: slanted four-sided strokes with rounded corners, each dropping about
   55 px from left to right (7 degrees).
7. Five headings in black caps, horizontal, centered on the labels.
8. Five notes in near-black `#1c1c1c`, centered.
9. Five white discs with black digits.
10. Five pictures with black outlines and one flat fill each: yellow bulb `#ffc20e`, blue
    lens, teal megaphone `#49b3b0`, yellow pencil, pink target `#f08ac0`; small doodle accents
    (sparkles in magenta `#e85bb4` and blue `#4f6fe8`, a violet squiggle `#7a5cd6`).

## 8. Charts and diagrams

* No data chart. The "diagram" is the zig-zag of five numbered discs; no script draws a
  free zig-zag path, and none is needed: the discs are native `shape ellipse` layers placed
  on the blob seams (C-icon-badge with a digit instead of an icon).
* **C-bulb** (step 1): `scripts/object.js kind=bulb bands=1 badges=none rays=1`, one yellow,
  `dark='#1c1c1c'`, `box=58,13.2,29.5,12.9`.
* **C-pencil** (step 4): `kind=pencil bands=1 badges=none rotate=35` (tip down and to the
  left, as if writing), `box=9,64.6,32,12.6`.
* The nearest library chart for the same content without the blobs is C-process-spine with
  `side=alt` (see Variations).
* Simplified: the original's doodles are brush-inked cartoons with wobbly outlines and
  off-register fills; the recipe uses clean vector objects and filled line icons.

## 9. Icons and illustration

* Step 1: the script bulb, 318 px wide (the original: a yellow bulb with a coiled filament,
  a blue screw base, and seven short black rays).
* Step 2: `search`, 300 px, black 16 px line, lens filled `#8ecdec`, with `sparkles` 90 px in
  magenta beside it (the original: a magnifier with a yellow handle, pink sparkles, yellow
  dashes, a pink scribble).
* Step 3: `megaphone`, 300 px, black line, fill `#49b3b0` (the original: a hand holding a
  teal megaphone with black lightning marks).
* Step 4: the script pencil, leaning 35 degrees, with a violet squiggle under its tip (the
  original: a yellow pencil with a pink eraser drawing a violet line).
* Step 5: `target`, 280 px, black line, fill `#f08ac0`, with `sparkles` 80 px in blue (the
  original: a pink and cream target hit by a yellow arrow, blue marks around it).
* For the true hand-drawn look supply five doodles as transparent PNGs and place each with
  `comp place` at 28% W wide in the same spots; keep black outlines of one weight.

## 10. Effects and finish

* None: flat color everywhere, no shadows, no gradients.
* The white outline of the title's first word: a white text layer with `--stroke '#ffffff'
  --stroke-width 18` under the black one, both placed at the same box position (not
  `--anchor-to ink`, or the stroked copy shifts).
* Label corners are rounded by stroking the four-point path in its own color: `--stroke
  '#ffffff' --stroke-width 16 --join round`.

## 11. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The title: the darkest, largest thing, top left; the arrow carries the eye to the second
   word.
2. The bulb, the largest saturated spot, then disc 1 just under it.
3. Disc to disc down the page, 1 to 5; at each disc the reader takes the heading (white
   label) and the picture on the other side.
4. The notes, last: small, black, centered.

Type steps 150 : 86 : 46 : 24, about 6 : 3.6 : 1.9 : 1. The pictures (up to 30% W) outweigh
all type except the title.

middle), note (its top 80 px under the heading center), disc and digit, picture. To add a
sixth step extend the canvas by 440 px, add one more blob (radius 48% W, center on the side

canvas: `box=58,13.2,29.5,12.9` means left 58% W, top 13.2% H.

## 13. Variations and pairings

* **Plain process:** the same five headings and notes on C-process-spine (`dir=v side=alt`,
  five pastel node colors, black `ink`) for a faster, more formal sheet.
* **Fewer steps:** three blobs on a 1080 x 1620 canvas (blue, yellow, pink) make a 2 : 3
  poster.
* **Other pastel sets:** mint `#cdeedd`, lilac `#e4dcf7`, butter `#fff1b8`, blush `#fde0dc`,
  sky `#cfe8fb`; keep all five above 85% lightness and keep the type black.
* **Objects from the library:** `kind=rocket` for a launch stage, `kind=thermometer` with
  `value=` for a progress stage (C-picture-gauge).
* Pairs with a C-icon-grid footer (materials needed) under the last blob, and with
  C-title-block when the title must be a plain sentence.

## 14. Pitfalls

* **A stroked halo that drifts.** Placing the white outlined copy and the black word with
  `--anchor-to ink` puts them at different spots, because the stroke enlarges the ink. Place
  both by the text box.
* **Blobs of different lightness.** One darker blob (a mid blue under black notes) breaks
  contrast and makes its step look more important. Check every blob against the text color.
* **Text across a seam.** A note that straddles two blobs reads as belonging to both. Keep
  each note inside one color; only discs and pictures may cross seams.
* **Numbers that skip or repeat.** The discs are the only statement of order (the original's
  last disc repeats an earlier digit). Count them against the headings before export: a
  wrong step number is a factual error, the data-integrity rule for step sheets.
* **Mixed drawing styles.** A filled line icon beside a photo-real or gradient picture ends
  the hand-made feel. One outline weight, flat fills, on all five.
* **Long notes at 20 px.** Seven lines only work with short lines and 140% leading. With
  more than 24 words, split the step.
