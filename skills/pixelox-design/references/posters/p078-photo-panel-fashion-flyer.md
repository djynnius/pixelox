# p078: Fashion flyer with tall photo panel breakout

## 1. Concept

A stylist's promo flyer: a tall, narrow color photo panel with a white keyline stands
right of center on a gray, blurred, black-and-white version of the same studio scene, and
the seated model's knee and trouser leg step out of the panel onto the gray. A heavy
two-line title and a column of small white text are all right-aligned against the panel's
left edge. It works because color exists in one place only (the burgundy outfit on a red
backdrop), so the panel reads as a window, and the breakout turns a plain crop into depth.
Presented on a dark red mat `#5d1919` about 8% W wide; the mat is not part of the flyer.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait A-series. One strong vertical: the panel's left edge at 50.3% W. Everything
  typographic ends 2.9% W to its left, on a right-aligned edge at 47.4% W. Left margin
  4.6% W.
* Panel: `50.3%,6.5%,27.7% x 85.5%` (aspect about 1 : 4.4). Right of the panel, 78 to 100%
  W, is empty gray except for the model's leg (to 87% W).
* Ground: split by a soft horizon at 43% H: dark gray wall above, light gray floor below.
  The title sits on the dark part, the body text and contacts on the light part with the
  dark ghost behind them.
* Text rows: title 14 to 44.3% H; role and date on one row at 46% H; body 54.5 to 67% H;
  contacts 75.5 to 81% H.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: blurred monochrome studio: dark gray `#383838` to `#4a4a4a` from 0 to 41% H,
   a soft transition, then light gray `#a4a4a4` to `#b8b8b8` to the bottom.
2. Ghost: a large, heavily blurred, dark gray copy of the model (legs and chair) at
   28 to 48% W, 45 to 88% H, about 80% opacity. It darkens the area behind the body text.
3. Panel backdrop: deep red `#7c040d` at the top to `#a01d22` mid-height, a hard floor
   line at 80% of the panel's height, pale warm gray `#c1af9e` below it.
4. Model cutout in full color: seated on a stool, head at 55 to 66% W, 18 to 28% H, feet
   at 86% H. The cutout is trimmed by the panel's left edge and runs past its right edge
   by 9% W.
5. Keyline: white, 0.4% W, on the panel's box; the model's leg passes over it on the
   right side.
6. Title line 1: 3 letters, white, heavy condensed caps, cap height 14% H, right edge
   47.4% W, cap top 14% H.
7. Title line 2: 4 letters, same size, 42.7% W wide (4.7 to 47.4% W), cap top 30.7% H.
   The gap between the lines is 3% H.
8. Role (left, at 4.6% W) and date (right, ending at 47.4% W) on one row at 46% H: white,
   extended bold caps, cap height 0.95% H.
9. Body: 8 right-aligned lines of small bold white text, 35 to 45 characters each, tops
   from 54.5% H, line pitch 1.8% H.
10. Contacts: 3 right-aligned bold lines (handle, city, phone), cap height 1.05% H, pitch
    2.4% H, from 75.5% H, each with a small white icon at its left (the phone icon is a
    white disc 2.7% W).

Neutral grays plus one red. The grays have no color cast at all, which makes the red look
richer than it is. The mat repeats the red at a darker value.

The title is 15x the size of the next level; there is no mid-size text at all, which is
what gives the flyer its editorial calm.

## 6. Imagery

One studio fashion photograph used three ways. Sharp and in color inside the panel: a
woman seated sideways on a wooden-legged stool, one knee raised, forearms crossed on the
knee, chin lifted; a burgundy wide-leg suit, white shirt, patterned tie, headwrap, and
narrow sunglasses, against a red wall and a pale floor. As a full-color cutout so the
knee and leg can leave the panel. And as the ground: the same frame enlarged about 1.6x,
converted to gray, and blurred until only the wall, the floor, and a dark smear of the
figure remain. Any seated or standing full-length pose with one limb pointing sideways
works; the outfit should be the only strong color in the shot.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-frame-border**, keyline form: a 4 px white unfilled rect on the panel's box, drawn
  after the cutout's body but crossed by the limb (here the cutout sits below the keyline
  and its spill to the right reads as breaking out).
* Panel breakout: the cutout is placed over the panel and masked with
  `mask add --from-path` to a rectangle that starts at the panel's left edge and runs to
  the canvas edge, so it is trimmed on the left and free on the right.
* Blurred ghost ground: a second copy of the cutout, `adjust bw`, `adjust levels
  --out-white 120` (so it stays dark), `filter gaussian --sigma 26`, 80% opacity, over a
  four-stop vertical gray gradient that fakes the wall and floor.
* Panel backdrop: `fx overlay` at 90 degrees with a hard step (stops at 0.16 and 0.2) for
  the floor line.
* **T-icon-row**, contact form: three `shape icon` outlines (`at-sign`, `map-pin`,
  `phone`) at 28 px with `--stroke-width 3` to match the bold text, each anchored by its
  right side 10 px left of its line. The lines are right-aligned, so every icon has its
  own X.
* Title lines: `text add --at 47.4%,Y --anchor tr --anchor-to ink` puts the top-right of
  the letters exactly on the shared right edge.
* No grain, no shadows on type.
* **T-shape-crop** in [techniques.md](../techniques.md) is the tested library version of the photo shown through a drawn shape (arch, panel, tiles, or letter); start from it when reusing this idea.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The red panel and the face in it (the only color, the sharpest detail).
2. Left to the title, whose right edge touches the same vertical.
3. Down the right-aligned edge: role and date, body, contacts.
4. The leg outside the panel is noticed last and rewards a second look.

Cap heights: title 14 : contacts 1.05 : role 0.95 : body 0.85, about 16 : 1.2 : 1.1 : 1.

## 9. Build recipe

`subject.png` is your image here: a cutout of the model. The recipe sizes a bust at
`--size x840`; for a seated or standing full-length figure use `--size x1040` and
`--at 68%,86%` so the head sits near 18% H. Move the first number in the mask path
(543 = the panel's left edge in px) if you move the panel.

```bash
pixelox new -o flyer.pxo --size 1080x1528 --fill '#444444'
pixelox layer add flyer.pxo --in-place --name Room
pixelox fill gradient flyer.pxo --in-place --layer Room --from 0,0 --to 0,100% --stops '#383838@0,#4a4a4a@0.4,#a4a4a4@0.46,#b8b8b8@1'
pixelox comp place flyer.pxo subject.png --in-place --as Ghost --size x1000 --at 31%,100% --anchor b
pixelox adjust bw flyer.pxo --in-place --layer Ghost
pixelox adjust levels flyer.pxo --in-place --layer Ghost --out-white 120
pixelox filter gaussian flyer.pxo --in-place --layer Ghost --sigma 26
pixelox layer opacity flyer.pxo --in-place --layer Ghost --value 80
pixelox shape rect flyer.pxo --in-place --name Panel --rect 50.3%,6.5%,27.7%x85.5% --fill '#8f1a20'
pixelox fx overlay flyer.pxo --in-place --layer Panel --stops '#cdbfb2@0,#c1af9e@0.16,#8f1a20@0.2,#a01d22@0.6,#7c040d@1' --angle 90
pixelox comp place flyer.pxo subject.png --in-place --as Subject --size x840 --at 66%,86% --anchor b
pixelox mask add flyer.pxo --in-place --layer Subject --from-path 'M 543 0 H 1080 V 1528 H 543 Z'
pixelox shape rect flyer.pxo --in-place --name Keyline --rect 50.3%,6.5%,27.7%x85.5% --fill none --stroke '#ffffff' --stroke-width 4
pixelox text add flyer.pxo --in-place --name Title1 --content "NEW" --font @condensed-heavy --size 15.3% --color '#ffffff' --at 47.4%,15.5% --anchor tr --anchor-to ink
pixelox text add flyer.pxo --in-place --name Title2 --content "FORM" --font @condensed-heavy --size 15.3% --color '#ffffff' --at 47.4%,30.7% --anchor tr --anchor-to ink
pixelox text add flyer.pxo --in-place --name Role --content "STYLE DIRECTOR" --font @heavy-wide --size 1.3% --color '#ffffff' --at 4.6%,46.1% --anchor l
pixelox text add flyer.pxo --in-place --name Date --content "14.03.31" --font @heavy-wide --size 1.3% --color '#ffffff' --at 47.4%,46.1% --anchor r
pixelox text add flyer.pxo --in-place --name Body --content $'Meet a new season of tailoring made for\nlong days and late evenings. Soft shoulders,\nwide legs, one deep color from head to toe.\nEvery piece is cut to order in our studio\nand fitted in person, so it moves the way\nyou do. Book a session, bring a mood,\nand leave with a wardrobe that\nfeels like you on your best day.' --font @geometric-sans --weight 700 --size 1.2% --leading 150% --align right --color '#ffffff' --at 47.4%,54.4% --anchor tr
pixelox text add flyer.pxo --in-place --name Contact --content $'YOURHANDLE\nYOUR CITY\n+00 000 000 000' --font @geometric-sans --weight 800 --size 1.45% --leading 165% --align right --color '#ffffff' --at 47%,75.3% --anchor tr
pixelox shape icon flyer.pxo at-sign --in-place --name IconHandle --size 28 --at 30.9%,76.2% --anchor r --color '#ffffff' --stroke-width 3
pixelox shape icon flyer.pxo map-pin --in-place --name IconCity --size 28 --at 34.4%,78.6% --anchor r --color '#ffffff' --stroke-width 3
pixelox shape icon flyer.pxo phone --in-place --name IconPhone --size 28 --at 27.5%,81% --anchor r --color '#ffffff' --stroke-width 3
pixelox export flyer.pxo -o flyer.png
```

Both title lines share one font size, so the longer word sets it: lower `--size 15.3%`
until the longer line starts at or right of 4.6% W. Change first: the two title words,
the panel's red (take it from the outfit), then the body copy (break lines by hand, 35 to
45 characters). When a contact line changes length, move its icon: the icon's `--at` X
is the line's left end minus 10 px. To add the mat, start from a 1300 px wide `#5d1919`
canvas and place the exported flyer in its center.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Other single colors: emerald outfit and backdrop `#0f5c3a`, cobalt `#1c3fa8`, mustard
  `#c8961e`; keep the ground strictly gray.
* Mirror it: panel at 22 to 50% W, text left-aligned from 52.6% W, the limb breaking out
  to the left.
* Two panels (22% W each) for a duo or a before-and-after, title above both.
* Product version: a bottle or shoe in the panel on a colored backdrop, its shadow or a
  splash leaving the panel; body text becomes three short feature lines.
* Pairs with T-duotone on the ground instead of gray (ground color to its lighter tint),
  T-paper-grain at amount 10, and T-vignette on the ground only.

## 11. Pitfalls

* Color in the ground. A tinted or sharp background competes with the panel; convert it
  to gray and blur it until no edge is sharper than the title.
* The cutout spilling on both sides. Trim one side with the mask; a figure that overflows
  left as well covers the title and kills the window effect.
* Text aligned to the canvas margin instead of the panel. The right-aligned edge at
  2.9% W from the panel is the spine of the layout; left-aligned or centered text falls
  apart.
* White text on the light floor without the ghost behind it. Keep the dark blurred shape
  under the body copy, or darken the ground's lower half to `#8a8a8a`.
* A wide panel. Above 35% W it becomes a normal photo; keep it between 24 and 30% W and
  at least 80% H tall.
