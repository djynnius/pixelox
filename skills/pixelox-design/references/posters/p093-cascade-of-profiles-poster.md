# p093: Cascade of painted profiles poster

## 1. Concept

A comedy-thriller one-sheet painted like a 1970s film poster: seven heads in profile, all
facing left, tumble diagonally from the top-left corner to the bottom-right, each larger
than the one before and overlapping it, sharing one great cloud of black hair, on a flat
mustard-yellow ground with fold creases. The first three are different people; the rest
are the same man again and again, bigger each time, which is the plot (copies) told as a
zoom. All text hangs in the yellow triangle at the lower left in a fat black slab serif.
It works because the diagonal gives a static stack of faces real motion, and because the
picture uses only the ground's own family: yellow, orange, brown, black.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait 2:3 (close to it: 1 : 1.48). One left margin at 4.8% W for every text block.
* A diagonal splits the sheet: heads above and to the right of a line from about 2% W,
  50% H to 45% W, 100% H; text below and to the left of it. The top-left corner above the
  first head (0 to 14% H) is a second, small text spot.
* Heads, smallest to largest (face heights): 23% H at the left edge (the only one turned
  three-quarters toward us), 23% H, 35% H, 44% H, 51% H, 54% H, and a last one about 65%
  H of which only the forehead, eye, and nose remain inside the bottom-right corner. Each
  head's nose is 9 to 14% W to the right of and 5 to 15% H below the previous one's.
* Text column, 4.8 to 33% W: cast 52.7 to 57.7% H; title 59.6 to 72% H; credits 74.5% H;
  rating box 75.5 to 77.5% H. Streaming line along the bottom at 96% H, 4.8 to 54.5% W.
* Exclamation top left at 4.8% W, 7.3% H.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: mustard `#e6a404`, full canvas, a little darker toward the corners.
2. Hair mass: one continuous black-brown cloud `#0b0904` across the top of all the heads,
   15 to 36% H at the left rising to 13% H and running off the right edge, with stray
   curls breaking its outline.
3. Heads: painted portraits (visible brushwork, glossy highlights on nose, lips, and
   cheekbone) in browns from `#2f1807` through `#8d4210` to orange `#bb6c1f`. They are
   stacked back to front from left to right, so every face is complete and the back of
   each head is hidden behind the next, larger face. One has teal lipstick (the only cool
   note).
4. Exclamation: one word with an ellipsis in black slab serif, top left.
5. Cast: 3 names on 3 lines (with a tiny "and" before the last) in bold black slab serif.
6. Title: 3 lines of heavy black slab-serif caps, tightly leaded, flush left.
7. Credits: one tiny condensed line in dark brown; a small outlined rating box under it.
8. Streaming line: 2 small bold white words, a brand wordmark in red condensed caps (cap
   height twice the white words), a thin red vertical divider, a date in bold white.
9. Paper wear: white fold creases (one horizontal near 50% H, verticals at 33% and 66% W),
   scuffs along the edges, fine specks.
10. Grain.

wide): the cascade of faces is the headline, and the title is its caption.

## 6. Imagery

A painted group portrait. For a build with photographs: profile (or three-quarter)
cutouts with strong side light, one per person, or the same cutout repeated; each copy
scaled about 1.3 times the previous one and shifted right and down so that it covers the
back of the previous head. Then unify them: merge the copies, apply `filter oil-paint`
for brushwork, and pull every tone into the ground's hue family (warm color balance, or a
gradient map from black through `#5a270a` and `#bb6c1f` to a pale yellow). Hair that
merges into one dark mass across the top is what binds the stack; with short-haired
subjects, add a dark shape behind the heads.

## 7. Effects and textures

* Cascade: six `comp place` calls, small to large, then `layer merge`.
* Painted look: `filter oil-paint --brush 7 --sharpness 60 --impasto 35` on the merged
  layer, then `adjust gradient-map` with warm stops at 85% strength (stops with alpha
  blend over the original, keeping some natural color).
* **T-grunge**: white creases and specks (`grunge.js` with `color='#fff6dc'`,
  `scratches=40`, few specks), 45%, plus three straight crease lines drawn with **T-rules**
  hairlines at 35%.
* **T-vignette** in brown `#7a4a00`, light.
* **T-paper-grain**, amount 16, overlay 35%.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The biggest readable face (fifth head, center right), then up the diagonal to the small
   heads at top left: the zoom is read backward first.
2. The exclamation in the corner the diagonal points to.
3. Down the left margin: cast, title, credits.
4. The bottom line, where red and white appear for the first time.

Cap heights: title 3.6 : brand 2.6 : exclamation 2.4 : date 1.9 : cast 1.2 : credits 0.8.

## 9. Build recipe

`subject.png` is your image here: a head cutout, ideally in profile facing left. Use
different cutouts for the first copies if you have them. The stand-in is a frontal bust,
so the build shows shoulders where the poster shows only heads.

```bash
pixelox render js scripts/grunge.js -o creases.png --size 1080x1620 --seed 9 --param color='#fff6dc' --param specks=1800 --param blotches=12 --param scratches=40 --param clump=0.6
pixelox new -o cascade.pxo --size 1080x1620 --fill '#e6a404'
pixelox comp place cascade.pxo subject.png --in-place --as C1 --size x380 --at 150,270 --anchor t
pixelox comp place cascade.pxo subject.png --in-place --as C2 --size x500 --at 330,290 --anchor t
pixelox comp place cascade.pxo subject.png --in-place --as C3 --size x660 --at 540,340 --anchor t
pixelox comp place cascade.pxo subject.png --in-place --as C4 --size x880 --at 740,440 --anchor t
pixelox comp place cascade.pxo subject.png --in-place --as C5 --size x1180 --at 930,600 --anchor t
pixelox comp place cascade.pxo subject.png --in-place --as C6 --size x1560 --at 1040,860 --anchor t
pixelox layer merge cascade.pxo --in-place --layers C1,C2,C3,C4,C5,C6 --name Heads
pixelox filter oil-paint cascade.pxo --in-place --layer Heads --brush 7 --sharpness 60 --impasto 35
pixelox adjust gradient-map cascade.pxo --in-place --layer Heads --stops '#0b0904d9@0,#5a270ad9@0.35,#bb6c1fd9@0.7,#f3c86ad9@1'
pixelox text add cascade.pxo --in-place --name Exclaim --content "Hold up." --font Superclarendon --weight 900 --size 3.2% --color '#0b0904' --at 4.8%,7.3% --anchor l
pixelox text add cascade.pxo --in-place --name Cast --content $'First Actor\nSecond Actor\nand Third Actor' --font Superclarendon --weight 700 --size 1.6% --tracking 20 --leading 150% --color '#0b0904' --at 5%,55.2% --anchor l
pixelox text add cascade.pxo --in-place --name Title --content $'SIX\nTIMES\nMARCUS' --font Superclarendon --weight 900 --size 4.8% --tracking -10 --leading 90% --color '#0b0904'
pixelox transform move cascade.pxo --in-place --layer Title --to 4.8%,59.6% --anchor tl
pixelox text add cascade.pxo --in-place --name Credits --content "WRITTEN BY WRITER NAME   DIRECTED BY DIRECTOR NAME" --font "Avenir Next Condensed" --weight 500 --size 1.1% --color '#3a1a05' --at 5%,74.5% --anchor l
pixelox shape rect cascade.pxo --in-place --name RatingBox --rect 5%,75.6%,7%x1.8% --fill none --stroke '#3a1a05' --stroke-width 2
pixelox text add cascade.pxo --in-place --name Label --content "ONLY ON" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 800 --size 1.8% --tracking 20 --color '#ffffff' --at 4.8%,96% --anchor l
pixelox text add cascade.pxo --in-place --name Brand --content "STREAMER" --font Impact --size 3.3% --tracking 20 --color '#e31c2a' --at 19.5%,96% --anchor l
pixelox shape line cascade.pxo --in-place --name Divider --from 40.5%,94.6% --to 40.5%,97.4% --stroke '#e31c2a' --stroke-width 3
pixelox text add cascade.pxo --in-place --name Date --content "MAY 9" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 800 --size 2.6% --tracking 20 --color '#ffffff' --at 43%,96% --anchor l
pixelox comp place cascade.pxo creases.png --in-place --as Creases --fit cover
pixelox layer opacity cascade.pxo --in-place --layer Creases --value 45
pixelox shape path cascade.pxo --in-place --name Folds --d 'M 0 812 H 1080 M 358 0 V 1620 M 716 0 V 1620' --fill none --stroke '#fff6dc' --stroke-width 2 --opacity 35
pixelox layer add cascade.pxo --in-place --name Vignette
pixelox fill gradient cascade.pxo --in-place --layer Vignette --kind radial --from 50%,48% --to 50%,125% --stops '#7a4a0000@0.5,#7a4a0080@1'
pixelox layer add cascade.pxo --in-place --name Grain --fill '#808080'
pixelox filter add-noise cascade.pxo --in-place --layer Grain --amount 16 --monochrome
pixelox layer blend cascade.pxo --in-place --layer Grain --mode overlay
pixelox layer opacity cascade.pxo --in-place --layer Grain --value 35
pixelox export cascade.pxo -o cascade.png
```

Change first: the cutouts and the six `--at` points (keep each copy's face clear of the
next copy; keep the lower-left triangle empty), then the title lines, then the ground
color with a matching gradient map.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Other cascades: the same face aging from child to elder; a band's members; one athlete
  in six phases of a movement; a product in six sizes. Small and far at the top left, huge
  and cropped at the bottom right.
* Grounds with their own darkened family: teal `#1f8a8a` with navy-to-aqua faces; brick
  `#c8452c` with maroon-to-peach; pale pink `#f3c9c0` with plum-to-rose.
* Mirror it (heads from the top right to the bottom left, text column on the right) for
  a sequel or a second poster in a set.
* Landscape: cascade along the bottom from left to right, title block top left.
* Pairs with T-halftone-portrait (dots instead of oil paint), T-stencil-poster on each
  head, T-block-shadow on the title for a louder retro look.

## 11. Pitfalls

* Copies of equal size in a row: a lineup, not a zoom. Each copy at least 1.25 times the
  previous one, the last cropped by two edges.
* Natural photo colors on a strong ground: the heads look pasted in. Map every head into
  the ground's hue family and merge before filtering, so brushwork crosses the joins.
* Text over the faces. All type stays in the empty triangle and the top corner, on flat
  ground color.
* A light or thin slab for the title: it cannot hold its place beside the faces. Use the
  blackest weight, tight leading, and keep the block under one third of the width.
* Perfectly clean flat yellow. The creases, scuffs, and grain are what make it read as a
  painted print rather than a slide.
