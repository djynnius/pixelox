# p031: Black type on newsprint and orange paint

## 1. Concept

A faith statement set as a street poster: a wall of pasted newspaper scraps, three broad
sweeps of orange paint, and on top a five-word sentence in huge, worn black capitals where
the first and last words fill the page and the three connecting words shrink between
them. It works because of three clean layers of contrast: gray paper (texture), orange
(color), black (message). The size jump between the giant words and the tiny ones makes
the sentence read as two shouted words with a whisper in the middle.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait, close to 5:6 in the original; build it at 4:5. No margins for the texture; the
  type keeps 14% W at the left and 13% W at the right.
* Three type rows: row 1 (one 3-letter word) 12 to 44% H; row 2 (three words) 46 to 56% H;
  row 3 (one 4-letter word) 58 to 89% H. Rows are separated by only 2% H.
* Two hairline-high strips of small caps at the very top (1.8% H) and bottom (98.3% H),
  edge to edge (3 to 97% W), each with three short phrases separated by long, hollow
  capsule shapes.
* The big words are not aligned to each other: row 1 spans 14 to 74% W, row 3 spans 17 to
  87% W, row 2 sits between at 18 to 83% W. The block leans slightly right as it descends.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Base paper: warm gray `#c7bcb5`.
2. Newsprint: columns of fine dark text lines and scraps of headlines, pasted at several
   angles (0, 90, about -20 and +15 degrees), covering everything; darker gray-brown
   `#ad9a8f` where scraps overlap. Individual scraps are 30 to 70% W wide with torn, paler
   edges.
3. Orange paint, three dry-brush sweeps, each 35 to 45% H thick: one from the top center
   to the top right (30 to 100% W, 0 to 30% H), one across the middle from the left edge
   (0 to 75% W, 28 to 62% H, sloping down to the right), one in the lower right (40 to
   100% W, 55 to 95% H). Color `#f3840b`, ragged ends, paper showing through the bristle
   gaps; printed text stays visible on top of the paint in places.
4. Row 1: 3-letter word, black `#0d0c07`, cap height 31.8% H, 60% W wide, top at 11.9% H.
5. Row 2: a 4-letter word in a wider heavy face, cap height 9.6% H, 24.5 to 67% W, cap
   top 46% H; before it, hanging from its top left, a 2-letter word at cap height 3.6% H;
   after it, sitting on its baseline, a 4-letter word at the same small size.
6. Row 3: 4-letter word, black, cap height 31% H, 70% W wide, top at 57.9% H.
7. Wear: creases, scuffs, and speckles in the paper color over the black letters (the
   ink looks cracked and thin in places) and over the paint.
8. Top strip: three bold caps phrases at 3%, 56%, and 82.6% W with two hollow capsules
   (1.4% H tall, 2.5 px black outline, fully round ends) filling the gaps at 20.4 to 54% W
   and 61.4 to 80.4% W.
9. Bottom strip: three phrases at 3%, 33%, and 61.4% W; capsules at 19.7 to 30.2% W and
   47.8 to 59% W.

## 6. Imagery

No photograph. The ground is a collage of old newspaper: body columns, bold headline
fragments, a few engraved illustrations, some upside down or sideways, all in gray. Real
newsprint has to come from your own scans; the recipe suggests it with fine line patterns
(rows of "text") at two angles under and over torn paper scraps. The paint is three dry
brush strokes in one orange.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-torn-collage**: scraps from `torn.js` in two paper tones, rotated -7 and 5 degrees,
  each with a soft short shadow. Stand-in for print: `pattern.js kind=stripes angle=90
  cell=8 weight=0.38` in dark brown at 70% alpha (rows of "text"), once at full size
  under the scraps, then as two smaller patches (placed at 700 and 780 px wide, so the
  rows get finer) rotated 90 and -14 degrees on top of the scraps and the paint.
* **T-brush-swash**: `brush.js` at 1500x520 and 1300x560, `bristles` 90 to 110, `dry`
  0.5, three placements rotated 24, -18, and -32 degrees, in `normal` mode. On gray paper
  `multiply` turns this orange brown; keep the paint opaque and let the print patches
  above it do the "showing through".
* **T-fit-width** for rows 1 and 3 (each fitted to its own width: 60% and 70% W).
* **T-distress-type** and **T-grunge**: one `grunge.js` layer in the paper color over
  type and paint (specks 12000, blotches 30, scratches 120, `size=0.8`), not clipped, so
  the same scratches cross letters and paint. Many fine specks and scratches, few
  blotches: big pale blotches on black letters look like holes.
* **T-newsprint** in [techniques.md](../techniques.md) is the tested library version of the newspaper texture (real columns of type instead of stripe rows); start from it when reusing this idea.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. Row 1, then row 3: the two giant words (60% of the page height between them).
2. Row 2's middle word, then its two small companions, which complete the sentence.
3. The top strip, left to right along the capsules; the bottom strip last.

The capsules are spacers: they turn three scattered words into one justified line and
echo the round counters of the giant letters.

## 9. Build recipe

Nothing to supply (replace the line patterns with scanned newsprint if you have it).
Canvas 1080x1350.

```bash
pixelox render js scripts/pattern.js -o lines.png --size 1400x1400 --param kind=stripes --param angle=90 --param cell=8 --param weight=0.38 --param color='#2f2823' --param alpha=0.7
pixelox render js scripts/torn.js -o scrap1.png --size 820x560 --seed 3 --param color='#d6cdc5' --param edges=tblr --param rough=0.7
pixelox render js scripts/torn.js -o scrap2.png --size 700x480 --seed 7 --param color='#b3a69c' --param edges=tblr --param rough=0.8
pixelox render js scripts/brush.js -o paint1.png --size 1500x520 --seed 2 --param color='#f3840b' --param bristles=110 --param dry=0.5 --param passes=3 --param rough=0.7
pixelox render js scripts/brush.js -o paint2.png --size 1300x560 --seed 6 --param color='#f3840b' --param bristles=90 --param dry=0.5 --param passes=3 --param rough=0.7
pixelox render js scripts/grunge.js -o wear.png --size 1080x1350 --seed 14 --param color='#c7bcb5' --param specks=12000 --param blotches=30 --param scratches=120 --param clump=0.5 --param size=0.8
pixelox new -o wall.pxo --size 1080x1350 --fill '#c7bcb5'
pixelox comp place wall.pxo lines.png --in-place --as Columns --at 50%,50% --anchor c
pixelox comp place wall.pxo scrap1.png --in-place --as Scrap1 --at 72%,22% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate wall.pxo --in-place --layer Scrap1 --degrees=-7
pixelox fx drop-shadow wall.pxo --in-place --layer Scrap1 --offset 4,8 --blur 14 --opacity 35
pixelox comp place wall.pxo scrap2.png --in-place --as Scrap2 --at 24%,80% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate wall.pxo --in-place --layer Scrap2 --degrees 5
pixelox fx copy wall.pxo --in-place --from Scrap1 --to Scrap2
pixelox comp place wall.pxo paint1.png --in-place --as Paint1 --at 38%,45% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate wall.pxo --in-place --layer Paint1 --degrees 24
pixelox comp place wall.pxo paint2.png --in-place --as Paint2 --at 70%,12% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate wall.pxo --in-place --layer Paint2 --degrees=-18
pixelox comp place wall.pxo paint2.png --in-place --as Paint3 --at 78%,78% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate wall.pxo --in-place --layer Paint3 --degrees=-32
pixelox comp place wall.pxo lines.png --in-place --as PrintA --size 700x --at 76%,36% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate wall.pxo --in-place --layer PrintA --degrees 90
pixelox comp place wall.pxo lines.png --in-place --as PrintB --size 780x --at 28%,74% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate wall.pxo --in-place --layer PrintB --degrees=-14
pixelox text add wall.pxo --in-place --name Row1 --content "JOY" --font Impact --size 30% --tracking=-10 --color '#0d0c07'
pixelox transform scale wall.pxo --in-place --layer Row1 --size 648x
pixelox transform move wall.pxo --in-place --layer Row1 --to 14.3%,11.9% --anchor tl
pixelox text add wall.pxo --in-place --name Row3 --content "LOUD" --font Impact --size 30% --tracking=-10 --color '#0d0c07'
pixelox transform scale wall.pxo --in-place --layer Row3 --size 756x
pixelox transform move wall.pxo --in-place --layer Row3 --to 17%,57.9% --anchor tl
pixelox text add wall.pxo --in-place --name Mid --content "SUNG" --font "Arial Black" --size 13% --tracking=-30 --color '#0d0c07'
pixelox transform scale wall.pxo --in-place --layer Mid --size 462x
pixelox transform move wall.pxo --in-place --layer Mid --to 24.5%,46% --anchor tl
pixelox text add wall.pxo --in-place --name LeadIn --content "Is" --font "Helvetica Neue" --weight 700 --size 5% --tracking=-20 --color '#0d0c07' --at 24%,45.6% --anchor tr
pixelox text add wall.pxo --in-place --name LeadOut --content "Best" --font "Helvetica Neue" --weight 700 --size 5% --tracking=-20 --color '#0d0c07' --at 68%,56.7% --anchor bl
pixelox comp place wall.pxo wear.png --in-place --as Wear --fit cover
pixelox text add wall.pxo --in-place --name TopL --content "EVERY" --font "Helvetica Neue" --weight 700 --size 2.05% --color '#0d0c07' --at 3%,1.8% --anchor l
pixelox text add wall.pxo --in-place --name TopC --content "DAY" --font "Helvetica Neue" --weight 700 --size 2.05% --color '#0d0c07' --at 57.5%,1.8% --anchor c
pixelox text add wall.pxo --in-place --name TopR --content "WE SING" --font "Helvetica Neue" --weight 700 --size 2.05% --color '#0d0c07' --at 97%,1.8% --anchor r
pixelox text add wall.pxo --in-place --name BotL --content "COME AS YOU ARE" --font "Helvetica Neue" --weight 700 --size 2.05% --color '#0d0c07' --at 3%,98.3% --anchor l
pixelox text add wall.pxo --in-place --name BotC --content "BRING A FRIEND" --font "Helvetica Neue" --weight 700 --size 2.05% --color '#0d0c07' --at 50%,98.3% --anchor c
pixelox text add wall.pxo --in-place --name BotR --content "THE DOORS ARE OPEN" --font "Helvetica Neue" --weight 700 --size 2.05% --color '#0d0c07' --at 97%,98.3% --anchor r
pixelox shape path wall.pxo --in-place --name Capsules --d 'M 150 16 H 568 a 9 9 0 0 1 0 18 H 150 a 9 9 0 0 1 0 -18 Z M 676 16 H 903 a 9 9 0 0 1 0 18 H 676 a 9 9 0 0 1 0 -18 Z M 319 1318 H 403 a 9 9 0 0 1 0 18 H 319 a 9 9 0 0 1 0 -18 Z M 678 1318 H 707 a 9 9 0 0 1 0 18 H 678 a 9 9 0 0 1 0 -18 Z' --fill none --stroke '#0d0c07' --stroke-width 2.5
pixelox export wall.pxo -o wall.png
```

Change first: the five words (3 letters, 2, 4, 4, 4 work as given; a longer first or last
word gets shorter when fitted), then the paint color, then the strip phrases. Each
strip is three text layers (left, center, right anchored); after changing them, read each
layer's `bounds` with `--json` and redraw the capsules so that each starts 16 px after
one phrase and ends 16 px before the next (`M x1+9 y H x2-9 a 9 9 0 0 1 0 18 H x1+9 a 9 9
0 0 1 0 -18 Z` draws a capsule from x1 to x2, 18 px tall).

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Paint color swaps: signal red `#e2231a`, acid yellow `#f4d21f`, cobalt `#1f4fd6`; keep
  paper gray and type black.
* Reverse it: black-painted wall (`#141210` base, white dirt in `screen` mode), type in
  the paper color, one white paint sweep.
* Replace the line patterns with real scans (book pages, maps, sheet music, receipts)
  placed and clipped to the scraps (`layer clip`) for a themed collage.
* Other genres: gig posters, protest and campaign statements, sports slogans, sale
  announcements with the price as the giant word.
* Pairs with T-halftone-portrait (a halftone face among the scraps), T-splatter in the
  paint color, and T-label-box for a date tag.

## 11. Pitfalls

* Clean, sharp black type on a dirty wall looks pasted on afterwards. Put the wear layer
  above the type so the paper color eats into the letters.
* Paint that looks like a flat sticker: render with `dry` 0.4 or more so bristle gaps
  show, let it run off the canvas, and put print and wear on top of it. Paint in
  `multiply` over mid-gray paper goes brown; use it only on near-white paper.
* Giant words that do not fit: do not shrink the font size; shorten the word or fit it
  with `transform scale`. A 3-letter word fitted to 60% W gives the 30% H cap height.
* Too many colors of paint. One paint color; the collage supplies the variety.
* Small strips set over paint or over busy scraps become unreadable; keep them within
  2.5% H of the top and bottom edges, on paper, and bold.
