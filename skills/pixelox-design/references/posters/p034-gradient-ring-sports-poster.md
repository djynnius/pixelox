# p034: Rainbow gradient ring sports poster

## 1. Concept

A community sports-day poster on pure white: one giant ring, wider than the page and
cut by three edges, built from segments in candy gradients (pink to orange, blue to cyan,
yellow, mint) with patches of black-and-white stripes and white dots; a compact black
condensed headline sits inside the ring's hollow. It works because white space and black
type keep the page calm while the ring supplies all the color and movement, and because
the ring literally wraps the headline, so the eye circles back to it.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait A-series (1 : 1.414). Left margin 14% W; top margin 7.5% H; right text edge
  73 to 90% W.
* Main ring: center 28.7% W, 61.2% H; middle radius 45.6% W; band 18% W wide (outer
  radius 54.6% W, inner 36.5% W). It enters at the left edge around 36% H, peaks at
  22.5% H, touches 83% W on the right, and ends at the bottom around 40% W, 92% H.
* A second ring fragment hangs from the top right corner: center 90.5% W, -6% H, outer
  radius 46.7% W, band 14% W wide; visible from the top edge at 52% W round to the right
  edge at 21% H.
* Type lives where the rings are not: two small label blocks in the top corners (14% W
  and 73% W, 8 to 11% H), the headline inside the main ring's hole (14 to 50% W, 46 to
  70% H), two numbered notes in the lower left (14 to 34% W, 78 to 92% H).
* All type is left-aligned.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: white `#ffffff`.
2. Main ring, six butt-ended segments drawn clockwise, each with its own linear
   gradient: pink `#fd4e9b` to orange `#f9a13a` (left, from the 8 o'clock to the 11 o'clock position); blue `#2b87eb` to cyan `#22c7dd` (top left); yellow `#fadc5a` to
   orange `#f9a13a` with white dots (top right); mint `#07ecb0` to teal `#1fd0c0` (right);
   pink `#f74f79` to orange `#f09151` with white diagonal stripes (lower right); lime
   yellow `#e9ed01` to orange (bottom).
3. Top-right ring fragment, three segments: blue to cyan, mint to lime `#c8e616`, yellow
   `#f1de16`.
4. Striped discs: three circles filled with black-and-white 45 degree stripes (two of
   radius 6.4% W stacked at 65% W, 53% and 62% H, overlapping the ring's inner edge like
   a figure eight; one of radius 4% W at 55.5% W, 83% H).
5. Two small gradient discs on the ring's outer edge: yellow (radius 2.1% W) at 83.4% W,
   46% H; blue to mint (radius 3.7% W) at 83.4% W, 64% H.
6. Top-left label: two lines of small bold condensed caps; the first word hot pink
   `#ff0a78`, the rest black.
7. Top-right label: two lines, black, same style, at 73% W.
8. Headline: three lines of black condensed caps at 14% W, cap tops 45.8%, 54.5%, 63% H.
9. Date: hot pink italic condensed numerals to the right of headline line 1, at 35% W,
   cap top 45.8% H.
10. Two numbered notes: gray numerals "01" and "02" with a short pink underline, each
    followed by five lines of tiny gray text.

## 6. Imagery

No photograph. The image is the ring: think of a running track or a hoop seen from above,
cut into colored laps. Segments change color every 30 to 40 degrees. Patterns (stripes,
dots, small triangles in the original) appear on about a third of the segments, always in
white on color or black on white.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-geo-shapes**: each ring segment is an open arc, `shape path` with `--fill none`
  and a thick stroke (196 px = the band width). For a ring with center (cx, cy) and
  middle radius r, a segment from angle a1 to a2 (degrees clockwise from 3 o'clock) is
  `M x1 y1 A r r 0 0 1 x2 y2` with x = cx + r cos a, y = cy + r sin a. Neighbors overlap
  by 1 degree so no white hairline shows.
* Gradients: `fx overlay --stops` on each segment; the angle is chosen to run along the
  band (0 for segments near the top and bottom, 90 for the sides).
* **T-pattern**: `pattern.js` dots (white, cell 34) and stripes (white, angle 45, cell 44)
  placed right above one segment each and clipped to it with `layer clip`; black stripes
  (cell 20) clipped to a white path of three circles for the striped discs.
* **T-rules**: the two pink underlines.
* Flat: no shadows, no grain, no blur.
* **T-ring-arcs** in [techniques.md](../techniques.md) is the tested library version of the ring, halo, or arc shapes; start from it when reusing this idea.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The ring: 35% of the page, the only color.
2. The headline inside it (black, heavy, in the white hole), then the pink date.
3. Around the ring clockwise to the top-right fragment and its venue label.
4. Top-left activities list; numbered notes last.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Two-color version for a brand: every segment a tint or shade of one hue (for example
  `#0b2a6f`, `#1557c0`, `#3aa0ff`, `#9fd0ff`) plus one accent segment.
* Dark ground `#0d0d12` with the same ring, white headline, and the stripes inverted
  (white on black).
* Move the ring's center to the right edge and hang the text from the right margin for a
  mirrored layout; or use a full ring centered on the page with the headline inside it
  (then A-centered-title).
* Suits school sports days, fun runs, summer camps, music festivals, kids' events.
* Pairs with T-waves (bands instead of a ring), T-outline-type for the date, and
  T-pill-button for registration.

## 11. Pitfalls

* Gaps between segments: start each arc 1 degree before the previous one ends, and use
  butt caps (the default); round caps make sausage links.
* Gradients between opposite hues (pink to green, blue to orange) go gray in the middle.
  Stay within 60 degrees on the color wheel inside one segment.
* The ring fully inside the page looks like a logo. Make it wider than the page so at
  least two edges cut it.
* Patterns on every segment: keep them to a third of the ring, or it turns to noise.
* A wide headline font collides with the ring. A condensed face at cap height 7% H keeps
  a 7-character line inside 36% W.
