# p098: Neon-stripe training club poster

## 1. Concept

A membership poster for an athletic training club: a black-and-white sprinter charges out
of a black ground on the right, a left-hung column carries the club mark, a four-line
condensed headline, a paragraph, and a call-to-action tag, and two acid-lime stripes cut
across the whole sheet at shallow angles. It works because the poster is monochrome
except for one color used five times (two stripes, two arm bands, one tag), and because
the stripes tilt the same way the runner leans, so a static column of text feels like it
is moving. Presented as a print on a gray surface; the surface is not part of the design.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait 2:3. Text margin 7.8% W on the left; top margin 4.5% H; bottom margin 4.3% H.
* Two unequal columns. Text column: x 7.8 to 54% W, everything left-aligned to one edge.
  Subject column: x 49 to 100% W, bleeding off the right edge. They overlap by 5% W at
  the runner's leading arm, which is what joins them.
* Vertical rhythm of the text column: club mark 4.5 to 16.2% H; headline 21.2 to 40% H;
  upper stripe crossing at 41 to 47% H; paragraph 51.5 to 78% H; lower stripe at 88.6% H
  on the left; tag and wordmark 92.3 to 95.7% H.
* Two diagonals. Upper stripe: a chevron that drops from the left edge at 40.8% H to a
  vertex at 21.1% W, 46.2% H (21 degrees down), then climbs to the right edge at 31% H
  (16 degrees up). Lower stripe: a straight bar from the left edge at 88.6% H to the
  right edge at 77.6% H (9 degrees up). Both are 1.6% H thick (26 px at 1620 high).

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: near-black `#07050a` with a violet cast.
2. Haze: gray dust at the left edge around 44 to 52% H (behind the chevron's vertex) and
   at the right edge around 70 to 78% H, 20 to 30% brightness, soft.
3. Upper stripe (chevron), lime `#c2ea0b`, behind the runner.
4. Runner: black-and-white cutout, x 49 to 100% W, head top at 12.6% H, head centered at
   75% W, torso and leading arm to 60% H, legs dissolving into the ground by 83% H.
5. Arm bands: two lime bands painted over the runner's upper arms at 43 to 45% H, each
   about 5% W by 1.8% H, following the arm's angle. The only color on the figure.
6. Lower stripe, lime, in front of the runner's fading legs.
7. Club mark, top left, white: a tiny caps line (4.8% H), a rule 18% W long (6.3% H), a
   three-letter monogram (cap top 7.5% H, cap height 5.1% H, 18% W wide), and an open
   chevron line under it (13.8 to 16.2% H): a shield built from type and two strokes.
8. Headline: four white condensed lines, left at 7.8% W, cap tops 21.2, 26.4, 31.5,
   36.4% H, cap height 3.7% H; the widest line ends at 54.3% W.
9. Paragraph: ten lines, left at 7.4% W, line centers 52.4 to 77.1% H (pitch 2.74% H),
   light gray; lines 5 and 6 are bold white; widest line 44% W.
10. Tag: `7.1%,92.3%,42.1% x 3.4%`. Left part (to 30.1% W): dark, lime outline, white
    caps, ending in an arrow point. Right part: solid lime, dark caps.
11. Wordmark: white italic lowercase, x 64 to 92.7% W, centered on 94% H.

## 6. Imagery

One studio action photograph: a woman sprinting straight at the camera, braid over one
shoulder, mesh sports top, arms pumping, lit hard from both sides so the muscles are
edged with white and the middle of the body falls to black. Converted to black and white
with crushed shadows (**T-mono-on-color** settings on a black ground: `adjust bw`, then
`adjust levels` with the black point at 30), so the cutout has no visible edge: the dark
side of the body is the ground. Her legs fade out (**T-photo-fade**, 58 to 82% H) before
the lower stripe. The lime arm bands are painted on top of the photo as flat shapes.

For a build: any cutout athlete or worker in motion, rim-lit, facing or moving toward the
text column. A bust works; a figure with a leading arm or leg that crosses into the text
column works better.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-mono-on-color** on black: preset `high-contrast-red`, levels 30 / 215, gamma 0.9.
* **T-photo-fade**: a gradient mask on the subject (`mask add --gradient linear`), whole
  at 58% H and gone at 82% H, so the ground shows through the legs.
* Haze (a reduced **T-smoke**): two blurred gray blobs at the left and right edges at
  60% opacity, with a `fill noise` cloud layer clipped to them in multiply so the glow
  breaks into dust.
* Stripes: `shape path` open lines, stroke 26 px, butt caps, miter join, starting and
  ending 20 px outside the canvas so the ends are cut by the edge.
* **T-label-box**: two-part tag; the dark half is a path with an arrow point, laid over a
  lime rectangle so 3 px of lime show as its outline.
* **T-rules**: the club mark's rule and chevron are strokes, 4 px and 9 px.
* No grain, no shadow on type.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The runner's lit face and shoulders (the only photograph, the brightest area).
2. Along the upper stripe to its vertex, which points down at the paragraph and sits
   directly under the headline: the headline is read next.
3. Club mark above; paragraph below, its two bold lines first.
4. The lower stripe sweeps left to the tag; the wordmark last.

Cap heights: monogram 5.1 : headline 3.7 : tag 1.9 : paragraph 1.4 : club line 0.5,
about 3.6 : 2.6 : 1.4 : 1 : 0.35.

## 9. Build recipe

`subject.png` is your image here: a cutout figure, ideally rim-lit. `BandLeft` and
`BandRight` are placed for the stand-in; move them onto your subject's upper arms,
wrists, or shoes, or delete them.

```bash
pixelox render js scripts/blobs.js -o haze.png --size 1080x1620 --param bg=transparent --param colors='#8c8c8c,#6e6e6e' --param at='0.14,0.47;0.92,0.74' --param size=0.34 --param vary=0 --param alpha=0.55
pixelox new -o club.pxo --size 1080x1620 --fill '#07050a'
pixelox comp place club.pxo haze.png --in-place --as Haze --fit cover
pixelox layer opacity club.pxo --in-place --layer Haze --value 60
pixelox fill noise club.pxo --in-place --as-layer --name Dust --seed 8 --scale 20% --octaves 5 --warp 48 --contrast 10 --stops '#000000,#ffffff' --blend multiply
pixelox layer clip club.pxo --in-place --layer Dust
pixelox shape path club.pxo --in-place --name Chevron --d 'M -20 653 L 228 748 L 1100 496' --fill none --stroke '#c2ea0b' --stroke-width 26
pixelox comp place club.pxo subject.png --in-place --as Subject --size x1250 --at 79%,12.5% --anchor t
pixelox adjust bw club.pxo --in-place --layer Subject --preset high-contrast-red
pixelox adjust levels club.pxo --in-place --layer Subject --in-black 30 --in-white 215 --gamma 0.9
pixelox mask add club.pxo --in-place --layer Subject --gradient linear --from 0,58% --to 0,82%
pixelox shape path club.pxo --in-place --name BandLeft --d 'M 743 1000 L 795 950 L 815 972 L 763 1022 Z' --fill '#c2ea0b'
pixelox shape path club.pxo --in-place --name BandRight --d 'M 1040 948 L 1080 982 L 1080 1040 L 1022 970 Z' --fill '#c2ea0b'
pixelox shape path club.pxo --in-place --name Stripe --d 'M -20 1438 L 1100 1254' --fill none --stroke '#c2ea0b' --stroke-width 26
pixelox text add club.pxo --in-place --name ClubName --content "KINETIC RUN CLUB" --font @geometric-sans --weight 700 --size 0.8% --tracking 60 --color '#ffffff' --at 8.2%,4.8% --anchor l
pixelox shape line club.pxo --in-place --name LogoRule --from 8.2%,6.3% --to 26.2%,6.3% --stroke '#ffffff' --stroke-width 4

pixelox text add club.pxo --in-place --name Headline --content $'RAISING\nFASTER, CALMER,\nINJURY-PROOF\nRUNNERS' --font @condensed --weight 700 --size 5.1% --leading 100% --tracking 10 --color '#ffffff' --at 7.8%,21.2% --anchor tl --anchor-to ink
pixelox text add club.pxo --in-place --name Body1 --content $'A first-of-its-kind membership,\nthe Kinetic Run Club brings\ntogether the people who keep\nyou moving: our in-house' --font @geometric-sans --weight 400 --size 1.95% --leading 140% --color '#cfcfcf' --at 7.4%,50.9% --anchor tl

pixelox shape rect club.pxo --in-place --name LabelLime --rect 77,1495,454x56 --fill '#c2ea0b'
pixelox shape path club.pxo --in-place --name LabelDark --d 'M 80 1498 H 316 L 334 1523 L 316 1548 H 80 Z' --fill '#07050a'
pixelox text add club.pxo --in-place --name LabelLeft --content "MEMBERSHIP" --font "DIN Condensed" --weight 700 --size 2.6% --tracking 40 --color '#ffffff' --at 198,1527 --anchor c

```

Change first: the accent (every `#c2ea0b`), then the headline (keep the longest line
under 16 characters so it stops before the subject), then the chevron's vertex: it should
sit under the headline's left third and point at the paragraph. A `shape path` takes a
point left of the canvas as written (`M -20 1438`); in `shape line` write it as a sum
(`--from 0-20,1438`), because a bare negative coordinate there counts from the far edge.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Accent swaps on the same black: signal orange `#ff5a1f`, cyan `#19d3ff`, hot pink
  `#ff2e88`. Keep one accent and keep it off the text.
* Light version: ground `#f2f2f0`, photo in multiply, type `#111111`, stripes unchanged.
* Gym opening, race registration, sports-shop sale, team tryouts, physiotherapy clinic,
  e-sports roster: swap the tag text and the paragraph; the structure holds.
* Square post: mark, headline, one stripe, the subject cropped at the chest, tag; drop the
  paragraph.
* Pairs with T-motion-streak on the subject (streak trailing away from the text),
  T-vertical-type for a date up the right edge, and T-paper-grain at amount 10 to bind
  the photo to the flat black.

## 11. Pitfalls

* Accent overdose. More than five lime elements, or lime headline type, and the poster
  turns into a warning sign. Count them.
* Stripes at steep or unrelated angles. Keep both under 22 degrees and leaning the way
  the subject moves; the upper one must pass behind the subject, the lower one in front.
* A cutout with a visible edge on black. Crush the shadows until the dark side of the
  figure merges with the ground, and fade the bottom; never outline or glow it.
* Paragraph lines longer than 32 characters run under the subject. Break lines by hand
  and keep the column inside 44% W.
* A gray haze over the whole sheet lifts the black and kills the accent. Confine it to two
  edge patches under 30% brightness.
