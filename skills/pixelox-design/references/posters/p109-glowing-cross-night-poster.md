# p109: Glowing cross on a night hill poster

## 1. Concept

An Easter-season church poster staged like a film one-sheet: a wooden cross on a pile of
rocks stands against a blue-black storm sky with a burst of amber light behind it and an
orange lens streak cutting diagonally through it; the scene sinks into pure black, and
out of the black comes a three-part title: a white script article between two thin rules,
one huge condensed word that seems lit from above by the cross (peach at its center, dark
brown at its ends), and a thin, widely spaced line of small caps. Details follow in tiny
centered serif lines. It works because there is exactly one light on the sheet and the
main word of the title is colored by it.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait A-series. One center axis at 50% W; everything is centered on it.
* Zones: tagline at 5.6% H; scene 0 to 57% H (cross 12.7 to 31.8% H, rocks 31 to 54% H);
  title block 51 to 75% H; details 80.5 to 85% H; fine print 86.4 to 92% H; the last 8% is
  empty black.
* Title block is a column 54% W wide (23 to 77% W): the script line with its two rules at
  54% H, the big word from 58.2 to 69.6% H, the spaced line at 72 to 74.6% H. The big
  word and the spaced line are fitted to exactly the same width.
* The cross's vertical beam is on the axis, so cross, script word, and the middle letter
  of the big word line up.
* Side margins of the scene are just darkness; nothing sits closer than 23% W to an edge
  except the tagline (26 to 74% W).

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: black.
2. Scene photo, top 57%: night sky in blue-black `#1a1b24` with heavy cloud, lighter mist
   `#433738` around the hill; a mound of large brown boulders rising to a point at 50% W,
   31% H; orange rim light on the rocks' upper faces.
3. Fade: black from alpha 0 at 40% H to solid at 58% H, so the lower rocks dissolve.
4. Glow: a soft amber burst `#e3a45a` behind the cross, centered at 50.5% W, 17% H, about
   40% W across, hottest (near white-yellow) right behind the crossing.
5. Cross: a rough wooden cross, dark against the glow, its edges burning orange: beam
   3.2% W thick, 19% H tall, crossbar 17% W wide at one third of its height.
6. Light streak: a thin orange anamorphic flare through the cross's center, running from
   18% W, 6.5% H down to 81% W, 33.5% H (about 31 degrees), with faint parallel echoes.
7. Mist: pale gray-blue cloud drifting across the sky and around the rocks, 30% (in the
   recipe it sits under the fade so it sinks into black with the scene).
8. Tagline: one line of tiny, very widely tracked serif caps in pale gray-blue at the top.
9. Rules: two white 2 px lines at 54% H, from 23 to 40% W and from 59 to 78% W.
10. Script word: a white bold script article between the rules, 4.5% H tall.
11. Big word: 5 condensed capitals, cap height 11.4% H, 54% W wide, with wide letter
    gaps; colored by a soft radial fade, peach `#f0c29a` in the upper middle falling to
    dark brown `#4a3327` at the first and last letters and along the bottom edge.
12. Spaced line: 3 short words in thin condensed white caps, cap height 2.5% H, tracked
    out to the same 54% W.
13. Details: church name, city, then date and time split by a short vertical bar, in
    small white tracked serif caps.
14. Fine print: a centered 9-line paragraph in tiny white caps, 21% W wide.
15. Fine grain.

## 6. Imagery

One night photograph or render with a single backlit object on a rise: a cross on rocks
here; equally a lighthouse, a lone tree, a figure on a ridge, a steeple. The light is
behind the object, so the object is a silhouette with glowing edges, and the foreground
falls to black by itself. If the photo has no such light, paint it: a radial glow in
screen mode behind the object, `fx outer-glow` on the object, and **T-lens-flare** with
the `movie` lens for the streak. Darken and cool the rest first (`adjust levels --gamma
0.45 --out-white 170`, cold color balance) so the warm light is the only warm thing.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-photo-fade** to black, ramp 40 to 58% H: here a black gradient layer above the scene
  and the mist, so both sink into the ground together.
* Glow: `fill gradient --kind radial`, `#ffe2b0` to `#e3883a` at alpha 0, screen.
* Cross: one `shape path` (two bars) in dark brown with `fx outer-glow` (orange, blur 46)
  and a 2 px orange `fx stroke` for the burning edge.
* **T-lens-flare**: `--lens movie`, tinted `#ffb060`, then the flare layer rotated 31
  degrees about the light's center.
* **T-smoke** as low cloud: one `fill noise --as-layer` line (clouds kind, `--scale 33%`,
  `--warp 56`, `--stretch 0.6` so it drifts sideways, `--bias=-36` to thin it), cool gray
  `#c9d2e0`, screen, 30%.
* **T-script-overlap**, without the overlap: script word centered between two **T-rules**
  lines.
* **T-gradient-type** on the big word: `fx overlay --style radial`, peach center to dark
  brown edge; **T-fit-width** makes the big word and the spaced line equally wide.
* **T-paper-grain**: `filter grain --as-layer --amount 8`, added last.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The glow and the cross (the brightest point, top center).
2. Straight down the axis to the script word, then the big word, whose bright middle
   repeats the glow.
3. The spaced line; then the details.
4. Tagline and fine print last.

## 9. Build recipe

`photo.png` is your image here: a dark landscape with a rise in the middle (the stand-in
is a mountain dusk). The cross is drawn; replace it with your own object or delete it if
the photo already has one.

```bash
pixelox new -o night.pxo --size 1080x1528 --fill '#000000'
pixelox comp place night.pxo photo.png --in-place --as Scene --size 1080x --at 0,0
pixelox adjust levels night.pxo --in-place --layer Scene --gamma 0.45 --out-white 170
pixelox adjust color-balance night.pxo --in-place --layer Scene --shadows=-8,0,14 --midtones=-6,0,8
pixelox fill noise night.pxo --in-place --as-layer --name Mist --seed 8 --scale 33% --warp 56 --stretch 0.6 --bias=-36 --stops 'transparent,#c9d2e0' --blend screen --opacity 30
pixelox layer add night.pxo --in-place --name Fade
pixelox fill gradient night.pxo --in-place --layer Fade --from 0,40% --to 0,58% --stops '#00000000@0,#000000ff@1'
pixelox layer add night.pxo --in-place --name Glow
pixelox fill gradient night.pxo --in-place --layer Glow --kind radial --from 50.5%,17% --to 50.5%,34% --stops '#ffe2b0ff@0,#e3a45acc@0.3,#e3883a00@1'
pixelox layer blend night.pxo --in-place --layer Glow --mode screen
pixelox shape path night.pxo --in-place --name Cross --d 'M 523 194 H 557 V 268 H 627 V 298 H 557 V 486 H 523 V 298 H 447 V 268 H 523 Z' --fill '#2a1a10'
pixelox fx outer-glow night.pxo --in-place --layer Cross --blur 46 --color '#ff9d3c' --opacity 85
pixelox fx stroke night.pxo --in-place --layer Cross --width 2 --color '#ffb968' --position outside
pixelox render lens-flare night.pxo --in-place --at 50%,18.4% --lens movie --brightness 90 --color '#ff9a3c' --name Flare
pixelox transform rotate night.pxo --in-place --layer Flare --degrees 31 --around 540,281
pixelox layer opacity night.pxo --in-place --layer Flare --value 75
pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name Tagline --content "A NIGHT TO WATCH AND HOPE" --font @oldstyle --weight 400 --size 1.25% --tracking 400 --color '#b9c0cc' --at 50%,5.6% --anchor c
pixelox shape line night.pxo --in-place --name RuleL --from 23%,54% --to 40%,54% --stroke '#ffffff' --stroke-width 2
pixelox shape line night.pxo --in-place --name RuleR --from 59%,54% --to 78%,54% --stroke '#ffffff' --stroke-width 2
pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name Script --content "The" --font @script-formal --weight 900 --size 6.4% --color '#ffffff' --at 49.7%,53.6% --anchor c
pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name Big --content "RISEN" --font "DIN Condensed" --weight 700 --fit-width 583 --tracking 180 --color '#f0c29a' --at 50%,58.2% --anchor t --anchor-to ink
pixelox fx overlay night.pxo --in-place --layer Big --style radial --stops '#f6cfa6@0,#d99a66@0.45,#4a3327@1'
pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name Spaced --content "FOR ALL OF US" --font @condensed --weight 400 --fit-width 583 --tracking 350 --color '#ffffff' --at 50%,73.4% --anchor c --anchor-to ink
pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name Org --content $'YOUR CHURCH NAME\nCITY NAME' --font @oldstyle --weight 400 --size 1.4% --tracking 200 --leading 150% --align center --color '#ffffff' --at 50%,81.4% --anchor c
pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name Date --content "APRIL 9, 2031" --font @oldstyle --weight 400 --size 1.65% --tracking 200 --color '#ffffff' --at 49%,84.8% --anchor r
pixelox shape line night.pxo --in-place --name Bar --from 50.6%,84.1% --to 50.6%,85.5% --stroke '#ffffff' --stroke-width 2
pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name Time --content "8AM" --font @oldstyle --weight 400 --size 1.65% --tracking 200 --color '#ffffff' --at 52.4%,84.8% --anchor l
pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name FinePrint --content "ALL ARE WELCOME. DOORS OPEN HALF AN HOUR BEFORE THE SERVICE. PARKING IS FREE ON THE NORTH SIDE. CHILDREN MEET IN THE HALL. COFFEE AND BREAD ARE SHARED AFTERWARD." --font @oldstyle --weight 400 --size 0.6% --tracking 40 --leading 140% --align center --box 230x110 --color '#e6e6e6' --at 50%,87.2% --anchor t
pixelox filter grain night.pxo --in-place --as-layer --name Grain --amount 8
pixelox export night.pxo -o night.png
```

Change first: the photo, then the glow and cross position (keep them on the axis), then
the three title parts. The big word wants 4 to 6 letters. Change the mist's `--seed` if a
bright patch lands behind the tagline.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Other lights, same staging: dawn (peach and pale blue, big word in white-to-peach);
  candle (deep red-brown night, gold light); moonlight (blue-black with a cold white
  glow and a silver big word using the chrome stops of T-gradient-type).
* Concert or film night: swap the cross for a stage silhouette or a figure, the script
  word for "Live" or "A Night of".
* Square post: scene in the top 55%, script and big word only, date in the spaced line.
* Story 9:16: same order, more black between title and details.
* Pairs with T-sunburst (soft light shafts from the glow, `uneven=0.8`), T-bokeh (embers
  around the light), and T-vignette.

## 11. Pitfalls

* A second warm area (sunset horizon, warm type everywhere): the cross stops being the
  source. Cool and darken everything but the glow, and let only the big word borrow its
  color.
* Big word in flat white or flat orange: it sits on top of the picture rather than in
  its light. Use the radial fade, bright in the middle, dark at the ends.
* Fade too short: a visible photo edge at 57% H. The ramp needs 15 to 20% H and must
  reach solid black before the rules.
* Streak drawn level through empty sky: a flare must pass exactly through the light
  source, and tilting it 25 to 35 degrees keeps it from underlining the tagline.
* Fine print larger than the details: keep the three-step ladder (facts 1 to 1.2% H,
  fine print 0.4% H) or drop the fine print.
