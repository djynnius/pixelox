# p059: White studio flyer with red italic headline

## 1. Concept

A midweek-service flyer built like a sneaker ad: an all-white studio, a two-line headline
in fat, wide, slanted red capitals, and a speaker in a black suit standing full length on a
red target painted on the floor. It works because there are only three colors (white, one
red, one black) and each has one job: white is the room, red is every word that matters
and the spot he stands on, black is the man.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait 4:5. Center axis at 50% W; margins 5.6% W left and right, 4% H at the bottom.
* Zones: ticker strip 4 to 7.5% H; theme line 15.4% H; headline 17.4 to 38.3% H; figure 35
  to 87% H on the axis; floor target 79 to 90.5% H; footer 91.5 to 97% H.
* The room has a horizon: the wall meets the floor at 58% H, drawn only as a soft gray
  shadow line. The figure stands below it, so he reads as standing in a space.
* Side notes sit left and right of the figure at the height of his thighs: logo badge at
  25.5% W, 68% H; time at 81.5% W, 69% H. Footer: venue hung from the left margin,
  streaming note ending at the right margin.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: near-white `#fafafb`.
2. Wall texture: very faint vertical drape folds (2 to 4% darker), top 58% only.
3. Horizon shadow: a soft gray band `#e3e5e9`, fading in from 46% H, darkest at 58% H,
   ending sharply into the white floor.
4. Ticker: a black strip 2.4% H thick across the full width, tilted 2.5 degrees (left end
   at 7% H, right end at 4% H), filled with one short phrase in tiny white italic caps
   repeated five times with dashes between.
5. Theme line: three words in black wide italic caps, centered at 15.4% H, 41% W wide.
6. Headline line 1: 4 letters, red `#e7070f`, 23 to 82% W, cap top 17.4% H, cap height 9% H,
   slanted 12 degrees.
7. Headline line 2: 7 letters, same red, 11.8 to 89% W, cap top 28.4% H, cap height 9.9% H.
   The gap between the lines is 2% H.
8. Floor target: three concentric red-pink rings seen in perspective (ellipses 5.9 times
   wider than tall), outer ring 15 to 83% W, 79 to 90.5% H, centered at 49% W, 84.7% H; the
   rings are pale (`#f8dee1`, `#ffbbc0`) with slightly soft edges, strongest in the middle
   ring; ring and gap widths are about equal (6 to 7% W at the sides).
9. Contact shadow: a small soft dark-red ellipse under the shoes.
10. Figure cutout: a man in a black suit and glasses, full length, microphone in the right
    hand, left hand raised mid-gesture; 39 to 62% W, head top 35% H, shoes at 87% H. The
    head covers the lower middle of headline line 2.
11. Logo badge: a thin dark ring (11% W) with a small emblem, at 25.5% W, 68% H.
12. Time: red wide caps at 81.5% W, 69% H; time zone in small gray caps below at 72% H.
13. Venue name: red wide caps, left at 5.6% W, 92.6% H; a two-line address in tiny dark
    gray caps below it at 94.5 and 96.3% H.
14. Streaming note: two tiny right-aligned lines with a small video icon, ending at 94% W,
    94.3 and 96.3% H.

the ticker; upright for time and venue); one neutral grotesque does the fine print. The
headline letters are wider than they are tall, which is what gives the sportswear feel.

## 6. Imagery

A full-length studio cutout of a standing speaker in dark clothing, mid-gesture, lit evenly
from the front, with a clean edge and no outline. He is small for a hero (52% H tall, 23% W
wide): the white space around him is the luxury. The only other image is a small round
logo badge. The floor target replaces a cast shadow as the thing that anchors him; any
full-length cutout works (a singer, an athlete, a product on a stand), and dark clothing
is needed against the white.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-text-behind-subject**: the figure is placed after the headline and covers 2 letters
  in the middle of line 2.
* **T-fit-width** with **T-stretch-type**: each headline line is set in the widest heavy
  face, widened with `--scale-x` (126% and 98.6%), slanted with `--style italic`, and
  fitted to its width with `--fit-width`, to stand in for an extended italic face. The
  face has no italic, so pixelox slants it 12 degrees (warning PXW4002, expected). Both
  lines stay live text.
* Floor target: five nested `shape ellipse` layers (pink, floor white, stronger pink,
  white, pale pink), each about 6 times wider than tall, so the rings narrow at the
  front and back as a circle on the floor would; plus a **T-spotlight** contact shadow.
* Ticker: a slanted black bar (a `shape path` that runs past both canvas edges) with a
  rotated line of repeated text: a **T-label-box** stretched into a tape.
* Studio wall: cloud noise stretched vertically (`fill noise --as-layer --stretch 3 --angle
  90`), multiply at 30%, and a gray gradient for the horizon. No grain: the cleanliness is
  the look.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The red headline (largest, the only saturated color, top third).
2. The black figure, whose head breaks into line 2, down to the red target at his feet.
3. The time at the right (red again), then the venue at the bottom left (red again).
4. Theme line, logo, fine print, ticker.

Cap heights: headline 9 to 9.9 : time 2.8 : venue 2 : theme 1.4 : fine print 0.8, about
12 : 3.5 : 2.5 : 1.75 : 1. Red marks exactly the four facts: who, which day, what time,
where.

## 9. Build recipe

`subject.png` (a full-length cutout in dark clothing) and `logo.png` are your images here.

```bash
pixelox new -o midweek.pxo --size 1080x1350 --fill '#fafafb'
pixelox fill noise midweek.pxo --in-place --as-layer --name Drape --seed 6 --scale 40% --stretch 3 --angle 90 --warp 28 --stops '#d5d8de,#ffffff' --blend multiply --opacity 30
pixelox layer add midweek.pxo --in-place --name Horizon
pixelox fill gradient midweek.pxo --in-place --layer Horizon --from 0,44% --to 0,58.5% --stops '#fafafb00@0,#dfe2e7ff@0.97,#fafafbff@1'
pixelox shape rect midweek.pxo --in-place --name Floor --rect 0,58.5%,100%x41.5% --fill '#fbfbfc'
pixelox shape path midweek.pxo --in-place --name Tape --d 'M -20 68 L 1100 20 L 1100 52 L -20 100 Z' --fill '#0c0c0e'
pixelox text add midweek.pxo --in-place --name Ticker --content "SEE YOU THIS FRIDAY   -   SEE YOU THIS FRIDAY   -   SEE YOU THIS FRIDAY   -   SEE YOU THIS FRIDAY   -   SEE YOU THIS FRIDAY" --font @heavy-wide --style italic --size 1% --tracking 40 --color '#ffffff' --at 50%,4.5% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate midweek.pxo --in-place --layer Ticker --degrees -2.45
pixelox text add midweek.pxo --in-place --name Theme --content "RESTORING YOUR HOPE" --font @heavy-wide --style italic --size 1.95% --tracking 20 --color '#1d1d1e' --at 52%,15.4% --anchor c
pixelox text add midweek.pxo --in-place --name H1 --content "HOPE" --font @heavy-wide --style italic --scale-x 126% --fit-width 635 --tracking -10 --color '#e7070f' --at 52.5%,17.4% --anchor t --anchor-to ink
pixelox text add midweek.pxo --in-place --name H2 --content "FRIDAYS" --font @heavy-wide --style italic --scale-x 98.6% --fit-width 823 --tracking -10 --color '#e7070f' --at 50.4%,28.4% --anchor t --anchor-to ink
pixelox shape ellipse midweek.pxo --in-place --name Ring1 --rect 15%,79%,68%x11.5% --fill '#fbdadd'
pixelox shape ellipse midweek.pxo --in-place --name Gap1 --rect 22%,80.15%,54%x9.1% --fill '#fbfbfc'
pixelox shape ellipse midweek.pxo --in-place --name Ring2 --rect 28%,81.15%,42%x7.1% --fill '#ffb3b9'
pixelox shape ellipse midweek.pxo --in-place --name Gap2 --rect 35%,82.35%,28%x4.7% --fill '#fbfbfc'
pixelox shape ellipse midweek.pxo --in-place --name Ring3 --rect 41%,83.35%,16%x2.7% --fill '#f9ccd0'
pixelox shape ellipse midweek.pxo --in-place --name Contact --rect 39%,85.4%,22%x2.4% --fill '#8a1418'
pixelox filter gaussian midweek.pxo --in-place --layer Contact --sigma 9
pixelox comp place midweek.pxo subject.png --in-place --as Speaker --size x702 --at 50.5%,87% --anchor b
pixelox comp place midweek.pxo logo.png --in-place --as Logo --size 118x --at 25.5%,68% --anchor c
pixelox text add midweek.pxo --in-place --name Time --content "7PM" --font @heavy-wide --size 3.9% --color '#e7070f' --at 81.5%,69% --anchor c
pixelox text add midweek.pxo --in-place --name Zone --content "(GMT)" --font @neo-grotesque --weight 700 --size 1.5% --tracking 100 --color '#9a9a9e' --at 81.5%,72% --anchor c
pixelox text add midweek.pxo --in-place --name Venue --content "CITY AUDITORIUM" --font @heavy-wide --size 2.8% --color '#e7070f' --at 5.6%,92.6% --anchor l
pixelox text add midweek.pxo --in-place --name Address --content $'12 EXAMPLE ROAD, EAST DISTRICT,\nYOUR CITY 00000' --font @neo-grotesque --weight 700 --size 1.1% --tracking 60 --leading 135% --color '#55555a' --at 5.6%,95.4% --anchor l
pixelox text add midweek.pxo --in-place --name Stream --content $'Streaming live on @yourchannel\nAlso on the app and on satellite TV' --font @neo-grotesque --weight 500 --size 1.1% --leading 135% --align right --color '#3a3a3e' --at 94%,95.3% --anchor r
pixelox export midweek.pxo -o midweek.png
```

Change first: the two headline words and their `--fit-width` values. Change `--scale-x`
until each line is 4.5 to 6.5 times wider than tall, and keep it under 150% or the stretch
becomes a distortion. Then the red. With a real extended italic face installed, drop
`--scale-x` and keep `--style italic` and `--fit-width`.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Swap the signal color and keep white and black: cobalt `#1238e6`, emerald `#0a9b5a`,
  orange `#ff5a00`. Recolor the target with the same hex.
* Inverted: ground `#0c0c0e`, headline white, target and time in red, figure in light
  clothing; the horizon band becomes a slightly lighter gray.
* Series of weekdays: one layout, a different color per day, same figure position.
* Sneaker or gadget launch (product on the target), fitness class, guest-speaker night,
  job-fair post. Story format: add 20% H of white above and move the ticker to the top.
* Pairs with T-outline-type (a ghost copy of line 2 in red outline behind the solid one)
  and T-block-shadow in black on the headline for a louder version.

## 11. Pitfalls

* A gray or cream ground. The white must be nearly pure (`#fafafb`) and the wall shading
  under 5%, or the red loses its snap.
* Red used for decoration (lines, icons, borders). Reserve it for the four facts and the
  target; everything else is black or gray.
* A bust or half-length cutout: the target needs feet. With a cropped subject, drop the
  target and let the figure run off the bottom edge instead.
* Stretching type more than 150% makes the vertical strokes fat and the horizontals thin.
  Use the widest heavy face you have and stretch as little as possible.
* The figure too large. Above 60% H he covers the headline and the side notes; keep him at
  50 to 55% H with empty white on both sides.
