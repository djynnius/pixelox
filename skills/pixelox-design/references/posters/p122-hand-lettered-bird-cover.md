# p122: Hand-lettered red cover with diving bird

## 1. Concept

A novel cover that looks painted by one hand: a flat coral-red ground, a
black bird diving head-first from the top left toward two dark cherries at the bottom
right, and every word hand-lettered in wobbly white brush capitals that bounce along
their own crooked baselines. It works because of three flat inks (red, black, white) and
because nothing is straight: the bird is on a diagonal, the title sags and climbs, the
tagline is stacked down the side, and a flat dark-red shadow slides out from under the
bird like a misregistered print.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait 2:3. Margins about 4% W; lettering and wing tips come within 4 to 7% of the
  edges on purpose.
* No columns. The structure is one diagonal from the top-left corner to the bottom-right
  corner: wing tip (7%, 17%), body (52%, 48%), head (78%, 67%), cherries (69 to 94% W,
  80 to 93% H).
* The lettering fills the two triangles the diagonal leaves free: author name top left
  (7.5 to 59% W, 5.5 to 18.5% H) with a small note top right (73 to 94% W, 7.5 to 17.5%
  H); title bottom left and across the bottom (4 to 94% W, 52 to 94% H).
* A stacked tagline runs down the right edge (88 to 95% W, 41 to 65% H), filling the gap
  between the right wing and the cherries.
* Depth order, back to front: shadow, title, bird, cherries.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: flat coral red `#ea3b3d`.
2. Paper flecks: a few dozen tan and brown specks (`#b9824a`), 0.3 to 1.5% W, scattered
   unevenly.
3. Bird shadow: the bird's whole silhouette as one flat dark red `#9e3b37`, shifted 4% W
   left and 6.5% H down from the bird. No blur.
4. Author name: two 4-letter words set as one line of white brush capitals, letters 8 to
   10% H tall, baseline bouncing by 1.5% H, some letters tilted up to 8 degrees.
5. Note, top right: five short centered lines of small white hand-lettered capitals (the
   first line slanted), centered on 83% W, lines at 8.3, 10.5, 12.6, 14.4, 16.5% H.
6. Title initial: the first letter of the title word on its own, 18% H tall, 14% W wide,
   at 4 to 18% W, 51.5 to 69.5% H, tilted 8 degrees counter-clockwise.
7. Title, remaining seven letters: white brush capitals 12 to 17% H tall on a sagging
   baseline that starts under the initial at 85% H, drops to 94% H at 40% W, and climbs
   to 82% H at the right edge; each letter leans its own way.
8. Article: a 3-letter word in warm gray `#b5a6a4`, letters 5% H tall, tilted 15 degrees
   clockwise, centered at 35%, 65.5%: to the right of the initial, on top of the shadow.
9. Bird: a black songbird with a forked tail seen from above, wings spread, 82% W by
   69% H (6 to 88% W, 6 to 75% H); solid black `#030202` with white hand-drawn feather
   strokes and a small cream face patch. It covers parts of the title's last letters.
10. Cherry stems: two black curved lines, 0.9% W thick, from the beak (82%, 72%) to the
    cherries.
11. Cherries: two dark red `#901c1b` discs, 14.5% W across, centers at 69.5%, 87.5% and
    86.5%, 85%, each with a translucent dark offset shadow to the lower left; in front
    of the title.
12. Tagline: seven black hand-lettered capitals stacked one per line down the right
    edge, each 3.3% H tall.
13. Paper grain: light monochrome noise.

Three-ink print logic: red paper, black ink, white ink. The two extra reds are the
ground darkened (about 68% and 60% of its brightness), so they read as shadow and as
fruit without adding a hue.

## 6. Imagery

One illustration on transparency: a bird painted as a flat black shape with scratchy white
lines for feathers, diving at about 45 degrees, beak at the lower right. It is drawn, not
photographed, with no shading: that flatness is what lets it share a surface with the
lettering. Supply it as `illustration.png`, composed on a diagonal (or tilt it with
`transform rotate` as the build does). The build places it twice: once recolored flat
dark red and shifted (the shadow), once on top.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-cutout-drop-shadow**, hard variant, built as its own layer so that the title can
  sit between shadow and bird: the illustration is placed, filled flat with `fx overlay`,
  and moved by -45,105 px.
* **T-text-behind-subject**: lettering first, illustration after, cherries last.
* **T-arc-type** machinery (`text on-path`) on an open wavy path gives every letter its
  own tilt; a non-uniform `transform scale` then squeezes the letters to 60 to 70% width.
  A text stroke in the fill color (3 to 4% of the font size) fattens the line, and a 5 px
  white `fx outer-glow` in normal mode at 45% makes the edge fuzzy.
* **T-grunge** specks only, in tan, very sparse (`specks=260`, `blotches=12`).
* **T-paper-grain** amount 12, overlay 30%.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The bird: largest and darkest shape, on the main diagonal.
2. The eye slides down the bird to the cherries and lands inside the title.
3. Title initial and the rest of the title (white, cap height 12 to 18% H).
4. Author name (white, half the title's size), then the stacked tagline, the note last.

Cap heights: title 18 and 15 : author 9 : article 5 : tagline 3.3 : note 1.3, about
13 : 7 : 4 : 2.5 : 1.

## 9. Build recipe

`illustration.png` is your image here (flat, dark, on transparency). Set the words before anything else: the non-uniform scale rasterizes the two
on-path layers (warning PXW2003), so they cannot be retyped afterward.

```bash
pixelox render js scripts/grunge.js -o flecks.png --size 1080x1620 --seed 4 --param color='#b9824a' --param specks=260 --param blotches=12 --param scratches=0 --param clump=0.1 --param size=2
pixelox new -o cover.pxo --size 1080x1620 --fill '#ea3b3d'
pixelox comp place cover.pxo flecks.png --in-place --as Flecks --fit cover
pixelox layer opacity cover.pxo --in-place --layer Flecks --value 70
pixelox comp place cover.pxo illustration.png --in-place --as Shade --size 820x --at 47%,40% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate cover.pxo --in-place --layer Shade --degrees 38
pixelox fx overlay cover.pxo --in-place --layer Shade --color '#9e3b37'
pixelox transform move cover.pxo --in-place --layer Shade --by -45,105
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Author --content "RUTH VANE" --font Noteworthy --weight 700 --size 10% --tracking 60 --color '#fcf9f6' --stroke '#fcf9f6' --stroke-width 3%
pixelox text on-path cover.pxo --in-place --layer Author --d 'M 0 220 Q 260 300 520 215 T 1060 230'
pixelox transform scale cover.pxo --in-place --layer Author --size 560x205
pixelox transform move cover.pxo --in-place --layer Author --to 7.5%,5.5% --anchor tl
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Blurb --content $'INTERNATIONALLY\nBESTSELLING AUTHOR\nOF THE\nHOLLOW ROAD\nSERIES' --font Noteworthy --weight 700 --size 1.9% --leading 112% --align center --color '#fcf9f6' --at 83%,12.3% --anchor c
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Initial --content "S" --font Noteworthy --weight 700 --size 20% --color '#fcf9f6' --stroke '#fcf9f6' --stroke-width 2.5%
pixelox transform scale cover.pxo --in-place --layer Initial --size x300
pixelox transform rotate cover.pxo --in-place --layer Initial --degrees -8
pixelox transform move cover.pxo --in-place --layer Initial --to 4%,52% --anchor tl
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Title --content "KYLARKS" --font Noteworthy --weight 700 --size 17% --tracking 60 --color '#fcf9f6' --stroke '#fcf9f6' --stroke-width 4%
pixelox text on-path cover.pxo --in-place --layer Title --d 'M 0 200 Q 380 500 760 370 T 1480 190'
pixelox transform scale cover.pxo --in-place --layer Title --size 930x370
pixelox transform move cover.pxo --in-place --layer Title --to 8.5%,71.5% --anchor tl
pixelox fx outer-glow cover.pxo --in-place --layer Title --blur 5 --color '#ffffff' --blend normal --opacity 45
pixelox fx copy cover.pxo --in-place --from Title --to Initial,Author
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Article --content "THE" --font Noteworthy --weight 700 --size 7% --tracking 60 --color '#b5a6a4' --stroke '#b5a6a4' --stroke-width 3% --at 35%,65.5% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate cover.pxo --in-place --layer Article --degrees 15
pixelox comp place cover.pxo illustration.png --in-place --as Bird --size 820x --at 47%,40% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate cover.pxo --in-place --layer Bird --degrees 38
pixelox shape path cover.pxo --in-place --name Stems --d 'M 890 1168 Q 800 1220 768 1345 M 890 1168 Q 962 1210 972 1302' --fill none --stroke '#0a0a0a' --stroke-width 10 --cap round
pixelox shape ellipse cover.pxo --in-place --name Cherry1 --rect 672,1340,157x157 --fill '#901c1b'
pixelox fx drop-shadow cover.pxo --in-place --layer Cherry1 --offset -14,12 --blur 0 --color '#000000' --blend multiply --opacity 22
pixelox shape ellipse cover.pxo --in-place --name Cherry2 --rect 856,1299,157x157 --fill '#901c1b'
pixelox fx copy cover.pxo --in-place --from Cherry1 --to Cherry2
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Tag --content $'A\n \nN\nO\nV\nE\nL' --font Noteworthy --weight 700 --size 4.6% --leading 78% --align center --color '#111111' --stroke '#111111' --stroke-width 3% --at 91.5%,53% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate cover.pxo --in-place --layer Tag --degrees -4
pixelox layer add cover.pxo --in-place --name Grain --fill '#808080'
pixelox filter add-noise cover.pxo --in-place --layer Grain --amount 12 --monochrome
pixelox layer blend cover.pxo --in-place --layer Grain --mode overlay
pixelox layer opacity cover.pxo --in-place --layer Grain --value 30
pixelox export cover.pxo -o cover.png
```

Change first: the three strings (author, initial, rest of title), then the two wavy
paths (make each path longer than the text set on it: about 110 px per letter at
`--size 10%`, 190 px per letter at 17%), then the `transform scale` boxes that squeeze
them into place.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Ink swaps: mustard `#e3a82b` ground with shadow `#a87512`; teal `#1f8a8a` with shadow
  `#14595c`; cream `#efe6d2` ground with a red bird and black lettering. Keep the shadow
  at 60 to 70% of the ground's brightness.
* Other creatures and props: a fox with a key, a cat with a fish, a hand with a match.
  One flat creature, one small prop in a darker shade of the ground.
* Gig poster: the name becomes the band, the note the date and venue.
* Pairs with T-distress-type (wear clipped to the lettering in the ground color)
  and T-brush-swash behind the note.

## 11. Pitfalls

* A real font left untouched reads as "kids' menu". Squeeze the letters, put them on a
  wavy path, and rotate the initial; no two letters should share a baseline.
* A soft, blurred shadow. The shadow must be a hard flat shape, or the three-ink print
  idea is lost.
* Letters that fill in: stroke plus glow on a squeezed bold face closes the counters of
  A, R, and S. Keep the text stroke at or under 4% of the font size.
* The bird placed upright in the middle. The diagonal and the cropped-close wing tips are
  the composition; a centered, level illustration turns it into a field guide.
* Too many flecks or heavy grain make the red look dirty. Under 300 specks, grain under
  35% opacity.
