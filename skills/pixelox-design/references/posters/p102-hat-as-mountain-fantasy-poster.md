# p102: Hat-as-mountain fantasy screen print

## 1. Concept

An art-print poster for an epic fantasy film in three inks on black: an old wizard's head
in profile, facing right, fills the sheet, and his tall pointed hat is at the same time a
snow-covered mountain. A line of tiny travelers walks along the hat's brim as if along a
ridge; lower down, a gap in his flowing beard is a cave where a small creature crouches
over a point of light. The title sits under the beard in cream uncial-style capitals. It
works because one shape carries two readings at two scales (a face from afar, a landscape
with a story up close), and because the color is so restricted (plum-black, sage green,
cream) that the double image never becomes confusing.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait 2:3. Center axis at 50% W for all type; the image is asymmetric on purpose.
* Image zone 10 to 79% H. The hat/mountain is a long triangle: summit at 16% W, 10.4% H;
  brim running from 9.4% W, 50% H up to a sharp point at 94% W, 36% H. The brim line (a
  slope of about 14 degrees) is the main diagonal of the poster.
* Under the brim: face in profile at 65 to 80% W, 37.5 to 53% H (nose tip at 79% W, 45%
  H); hair and beard streaming down and left to 79% H, spanning 9 to 92% W.
* Travelers on the brim: 35 to 65% W, 39 to 42% H, each figure 2 to 2.5% H tall.
* Cave in the beard: 42.5 to 61% W, 61 to 72.5% H.
* Type: tagline at 7.9% H; title block 75.5 to 89% H, 34 to 66% W; subtitle at 92% H;
  credits at 95.5% H. The bottom of the beard overlaps the top of the title block.
* The black around the head is large: the left 9% and right 6% of the width, the top 10%
  and the bottom 10% are empty.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: black with a plum cast, `#11070c`, full canvas.
2. Head: profile of an old man with a long nose, heavy brow, and full beard, drawn in
   fine flowing lines; shadow side dark green `#1f231d`, strands mid green `#647354`,
   lit strands and cheek sage `#a49c71` to cream `#dec893`. The lower beard thins into
   separate locks that fade into the black.
3. Hat as mountain: the triangle above the brim shows a mountain face: a bright cream
   snow slope on the right two thirds, a dark ridge on the left, and pale mist filling the
   lower part just above the brim, so the brim's top edge is the lightest place.
4. Travelers: nine tiny black silhouettes (walkers with staffs and packs, one leading a
   pony) in single file on the brim, climbing to the right, against the pale mist.
5. Cave: a dark arch cut into the beard with stalactite teeth along its top; inside, a
   small bright glow in cream and a crouched figure on a rock in front of it.
6. Tagline: one line of tiny, widely tracked cream serif caps, centered at 7.9% H.
7. Title: two big words in cream `#f5e7a9` (cap heights 5.4% and 5.8% H), stacked and
   centered, with two tiny connecting words ("THE" above the first, "OF THE" tucked
   between the lines) and a thin green circle drawn around or in place of one round letter.
8. Subtitle: one line of small olive serif caps with italic connecting words, between two
   hairlines of fine dots; tiny credits below.
9. Screen-print grain.

## 6. Imagery

An illustration built on a visual pun: part of a portrait (a hat) is replaced by a
landscape of the same shape (a peak). To build it from photographs: (1) a profile
cutout with a strong beard, hair, or collar; (2) a mountain photo with one clean summit.
Clip the mountain into a triangle drawn where the hat should be (**T-double-exposure**,
with the window drawn as a `shape path` instead of taken from the subject), then map
both onto one ramp with **T-duotone**: `#11070c`, `#2b3a2c`, `#647354`, `#a49c71`,
`#dec893`. The pun works with other pairs: hair as a forest, a collar as cliffs, a
crown as a skyline, a pipe's smoke as a river.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-duotone**, five stops, applied to the head and to the mountain photo separately.
* Hat window: `shape path` triangle; photo clipped with `layer clip`; a third layer
  (cream mist, a linear gradient running up from the brim) clipped to the same triangle.
* Travelers: a dashed `shape line` (bodies) and a dotted round-cap line (heads) parallel
  to the brim, 12 px above it. For real silhouettes, place tiny cutouts with `fx overlay`.
* Cave: a dark arch `shape path` plus a small radial glow in screen mode.
* **T-photo-fade** to the ground from 68 to 80% H, so the beard dissolves before the
  title.
* **T-rules**: two dotted hairlines around the subtitle.
* **T-paper-grain**, amount 16, soft-light 45%.
* **T-shape-crop** in [techniques.md](../techniques.md) is the tested library version of the photo shown through a drawn shape (arch, panel, tiles, or letter); start from it when reusing this idea.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The bright cream triangle (the largest light shape): first read as a mountain.
2. Its lower edge, the brim, leads right to the nose and face: the image flips to a head.
3. The travelers on the brim; then down the beard to the glow in the cave.
4. The title under the cave, subtitle, tagline last.

Sizes: mountain 40% H tall; title caps 5.8% H; travelers 2.3% H; subtitle 1.1% H;
tagline 0.75% H.

## 9. Build recipe

`subject.png` (a profile cutout, placed so the top of its head tucks under the brim line)
and `photo.png` (a mountain with a bright slope) are your images here.

```bash
pixelox new -o saga.pxo --size 1080x1620 --fill '#11070c'
pixelox comp place saga.pxo subject.png --in-place --as Head --size x960 --at 56%,36% --anchor t
pixelox adjust gradient-map saga.pxo --in-place --layer Head --stops '#11070c@0,#2b3a2c@0.3,#647354@0.55,#a49c71@0.78,#dec893@1'
pixelox layer add saga.pxo --in-place --name Fade
pixelox fill gradient saga.pxo --in-place --layer Fade --from 0,66% --to 0,78% --stops '#11070c00@0,#11070cff@1'
pixelox shape path saga.pxo --in-place --name Hat --d 'M 176 168 L 1020 588 L 100 812 Z' --fill '#2b3a2c'
pixelox comp place saga.pxo photo.png --in-place --as Peak --size 1080x --at 0,40
pixelox layer clip saga.pxo --in-place --layer Peak
pixelox adjust gradient-map saga.pxo --in-place --layer Peak --stops '#11070c@0,#2b3a2c@0.3,#647354@0.55,#a49c71@0.78,#dec893@1'
pixelox layer add saga.pxo --in-place --name Mist
pixelox fill gradient saga.pxo --in-place --layer Mist --from 600,700 --to 520,470 --stops '#dec893f2@0,#dec89300@1'
pixelox layer clip saga.pxo --in-place --layer Mist
pixelox shape line saga.pxo --in-place --name Bodies --from 380,733 --to 700,655 --stroke '#0b0508' --stroke-width 22 --dash 7,25
pixelox shape line saga.pxo --in-place --name Heads --from 383.5,717 --to 703.5,639 --stroke '#0b0508' --stroke-width 10 --dash 0,32 --cap round
pixelox shape path saga.pxo --in-place --name Cave --d 'M 452 1150 C 452 1030 500 985 556 985 C 612 985 662 1030 662 1150 Z' --fill '#11070c'
pixelox layer add saga.pxo --in-place --name Glow
pixelox fill gradient saga.pxo --in-place --layer Glow --kind radial --from 556,1090 --to 556,1150 --stops '#f5e7a9ff@0,#dec89380@0.4,#dec89300@1'
pixelox layer blend saga.pxo --in-place --layer Glow --mode screen
pixelox text add saga.pxo --in-place --name Tagline --content "ONE ROAD OVER THE HIGH PASS." --font Baskerville --weight 400 --size 1.05% --tracking 250 --color '#dec893' --at 50%,7.9% --anchor c
pixelox text add saga.pxo --in-place --name The --content "THE" --font Baskerville --weight 400 --size 1.25% --tracking 150 --color '#f5e7a9' --at 42%,76% --anchor c
pixelox text add saga.pxo --in-place --name Word1 --content "SONG" --font Luminari --size 6% --tracking 20 --color '#f5e7a9'
pixelox transform move saga.pxo --in-place --layer Word1 --to 50%,76.7% --anchor t
pixelox text add saga.pxo --in-place --name Word2 --content "PEAKS" --font Luminari --size 6.4% --tracking 20 --color '#f5e7a9'
pixelox transform move saga.pxo --in-place --layer Word2 --to 50%,83.2% --anchor t
pixelox text add saga.pxo --in-place --name OfThe --content "OF THE" --font Baskerville --weight 400 --size 1% --tracking 150 --color '#f5e7a9' --at 50%,82% --anchor c
pixelox shape line saga.pxo --in-place --name Rule --from 30%,90.5% --to 70%,90.5% --stroke '#8a8f6a' --stroke-width 2 --dash 0,7 --cap round --opacity 70
pixelox text add saga.pxo --in-place --name Subtitle --content "PART ONE OF THE HIGH ROAD" --font Baskerville --weight 400 --size 1.5% --tracking 150 --color '#8a8f6a' --at 50%,92% --anchor c
pixelox text add saga.pxo --in-place --name Credits --content "A STUDIO NAME PICTURE   DIRECTED BY DIRECTOR NAME   PRINT EDITION 01" --font "Avenir Next Condensed" --weight 400 --size 0.6% --tracking 50 --color '#4a4f3e' --at 50%,95.5% --anchor c
pixelox layer add saga.pxo --in-place --name Grain --fill '#808080'
pixelox filter add-noise saga.pxo --in-place --layer Grain --amount 16 --monochrome
pixelox layer blend saga.pxo --in-place --layer Grain --mode soft-light
pixelox layer opacity saga.pxo --in-place --layer Grain --value 45
pixelox export saga.pxo -o saga.png
```

Change first: the two photos and the Hat triangle (its long lower edge must sit on the
subject's brow line); then move the traveler lines to stay 12 px above that edge; then
the three inks.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Other double images: a sleeping giant whose shoulder is a hill with a village; a
  woman's hair as a night sea with a ship; a helmet as a dome with a city on its rim; a
  wolf's back as a pine ridge.
* Ink sets on black: rust, sand, bone (desert western); navy, steel blue, ice (arctic);
  wine, rose, blush (gothic romance). Always three steps of one hue plus the tinted black.
* Reverse print: cream paper `#efe6c8`, inks dark green and plum; the title in plum.
* Gallery print: drop tagline and credits, keep title and an edition line.
* Pairs with T-silhouette (flat travelers), T-vignette, T-frame-border (thin cream
  keyline at 4%), and T-distress-type on the title for an old-print feel.

## 11. Pitfalls

* A window shape that does not belong to the figure: if the triangle does not sit where
  a hat would, the viewer sees a head with a photo stuck on it. Align its lower edge to
  the brow and let it overhang front and back like a brim.
* Using the photo's natural colors inside the window: the pun needs both images in the
  same inks. Map both with one ramp.
* Travelers too large or on a dark part of the edge: they must be tiny (about 2% H) and
  stand against the palest tone.
* A big bright title: it kills the slow discovery. Cream, under 6% H cap height, centered
  under the image.
* Pure black ground and gray-greens: the print goes dead. Tint the black toward plum and
  keep the lightest ink warm.
