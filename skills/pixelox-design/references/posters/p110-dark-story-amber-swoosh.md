# p110: Dark product story with amber swoosh

## 1. Concept

A vertical social story that sells one product: a pair of black headphones stands in the
middle of a warm near-black field, and a single thick amber band swings up from the lower
left corner, passes behind the product, and falls away to the right. Above it, a thin
signature-script phrase sits on a heavy one-word headline; a round discount badge and a
contact strip finish it. It works because the page has exactly one color accent, and that
accent is also the only curve: it points at the product and gives a dark, static layout a
sense of motion. Presented as a floating card on a white page; the page is not part of
the design.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait 9:16. Center axis at 50% W for the text block and the product; margins 9.4% W.
* Zones: top bar 5 to 7.5% H (logo left, three dots right); text block 13 to 33% H
  (script 13 to 20.6%, headline 22.5 to 27.5%, body 30 to 33%); empty 33 to 43% H;
  product 43.5 to 72% H; swoosh 56 to 85% H; empty 73 to 90% H on the left and center;
  contact strip 90.5 to 94.5% H.
* Off-axis elements: the badge at the left (10 to 26% W, 45 to 54% H), level with the
  top of the product; the swoosh, whose peak is right of center at 68% W.
* The top bar and the contact strip share the same left and right edges (9.4% and 90% W).

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: warm near-black `#312527`, full canvas.
2. Glow: a soft radial lift to `#554a4e` centered at 50% W, 30% H, fading out by the
   edges, so the text block and the top of the product sit in a pool of dim light.
3. Bottom shade: darker `#1c1314` from 70% H down.
4. Swoosh: one thick curved band, amber `#f5b502` at the left to orange-amber `#f6a30e`
   at the right. Its upper edge runs from 0% W, 79.8% H in an almost straight line up to
   55% W, 57% H, bends over a peak at 68% W, 55.9% H, and drops to 100% W, 69.5% H. The
   band is 5% H thick at the left edge, 8% at the peak, 11% at the right edge.
5. Floor shadow: a soft black ellipse, 34% W x 2% H, centered at 50% W, 71.5% H.
6. Product: black headphones, cutout, `30%,43.5%,40% x 28.5%`, in front of the swoosh:
   the band enters behind the left ear cup and leaves behind the right one.
7. Badge: light gray disc `#f0f0f0`, 15.7% W across, center 17.9% W, 49.3% H; inside it a
   tiny "save up to" line and a large "50%" in charcoal `#4a4a4a`.
8. Logo: white heavy caps, cap height 1.6% H, left at 9.4% W, 6.3% H. Three white dots
   (2.6% W across) at 79.5%, 84.5%, 89.5% W, 6.5% H.
9. Script phrase: 2 words, white, thin signature script, 46% W wide, 7.4% H tall with its
   loops, tilted up about 5 degrees, centered at 51% W, 16.9% H.
10. Headline: 1 word of 7 letters, white, heavy wide grotesque, cap height 5.1% H, 67% W
    wide, cap top at 22.5% H.
11. Body: 2 centered lines of about 45 characters, white, semibold, cap height 0.8% H,
    at 30.8% and 32.1% H.
12. Contact strip: a white disc (7% W) at 7.8% W, 92.3% H; right of it "More
    Information" (cap height 0.9% H) over a phone number (heavy, cap height 1.4% H), left
    at 14.5% W; at the right a black pill `#150f10` with a 1 px lighter edge,
    `71%,91.8%,19% x 2.6%`, with "ORDER NOW" in white.

Near-black plus one accent. The black is warm (it has red in it), so the amber looks
rich; on a neutral or blue-black the same amber turns greenish.

## 6. Imagery

One product cutout: over-ear headphones in matte black, three-quarter view, standing on
their ear cups, lit softly from above so the headband and the cup rims carry gray
highlights. Black product on a near-black ground is deliberate: the amber band behind it
is what draws its outline (the band passes exactly behind the cups, the darkest part).
Scale: 40% W wide, 28.5% H tall, centered. A real build can use any compact product
(speaker, shoe, watch, bottle, console controller); a dark product needs the bright band
behind it, a light product can sit in front of the dark ground alone.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-spotlight**, reduced: a radial glow behind the text (not behind the product), a
  darker bottom, and a feathered contact shadow painted through `select ellipse --feather`
  (a blurred shape layer would be clipped at its own bounds).
* Swoosh: one closed `shape path` (two cubic curves out along the upper edge, two back
  along the lower edge) with an `fx overlay` gradient left to right. It is added before
  the product so the product covers its middle.
* **T-script-overlap** pairing without the overlap: script 2% H above the block word,
  tilted -5 degrees, no shadow.
* **T-fit-width** on the headline (67% W) and the script (46% W).
* **T-pill-button**, filled, very small (2.6% H tall), black on near-black with a 1 px
  edge in the glow color so it stays visible: a footer detail, not the main call to
  action.
* No grain. Flat color, crisp vector edges, one soft glow.
* **T-swoosh-band** in [techniques.md](../techniques.md) is the tested library version of the curved color band; start from it when reusing this idea.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The headline word (largest, white on dark, upper third).
2. The script phrase above it, read as a lead-in.
3. Down the axis to the product, where the amber band catches the eye and sweeps it
   left to the badge figure and right off the edge.
4. The contact strip: phone number first (heavy), button second.

Cap-height ratio: headline 5.1 : badge figure 2.3 : logo 1.6 : phone 1.4 : body 0.8,
about 6.4 : 2.9 : 2 : 1.75 : 1.

## 9. Build recipe

`product.png` is your image here: a product cutout. For a wide product such as
headphones, replace `--size x550` with `--size 430x` (40% of the width). Replace the
`Logo` text with your mark (`comp place` with `--size 110x --at 9.4%,6.3% --anchor l`).

```bash
pixelox new -o story.pxo --size 1080x1920 --fill '#312527'
pixelox layer add story.pxo --in-place --name Glow
pixelox fill gradient story.pxo --in-place --layer Glow --kind radial --from 50%,30% --to 50%,75% --stops '#5a4f53ff@0,#31252700@1'
pixelox layer add story.pxo --in-place --name BottomShade
pixelox fill gradient story.pxo --in-place --layer BottomShade --from 0,68% --to 0,100% --stops '#1c131400@0,#1c1314cc@1'
pixelox shape path story.pxo --in-place --name Swoosh --d 'M 0 1532 C 250 1325 480 1130 620 1085 C 740 1050 900 1120 1080 1334 L 1080 1546 C 950 1330 820 1215 720 1215 C 600 1215 330 1340 0 1624 Z' --fill '#f5b014'
pixelox fx overlay story.pxo --in-place --layer Swoosh --stops '#f5b502@0,#f6a30e@1' --angle 0
pixelox layer add story.pxo --in-place --name FloorShadow
pixelox select ellipse story.pxo --in-place --rect 33%,70.5%,34%x2.2% --feather 12
pixelox fill solid story.pxo --in-place --layer FloorShadow --color '#0d090a' --mask @sel
pixelox select none story.pxo --in-place
pixelox comp place story.pxo product.png --in-place --as Product --size x550 --at 50%,72% --anchor b
pixelox shape ellipse story.pxo --in-place --name Badge --rect 108,862,170x170 --fill '#f0f0f0'
pixelox text add story.pxo --in-place --name BadgeNote --content "save up to" --font "Avenir Next Condensed" --weight 700 --size 0.95% --color '#4a4a4a' --at 17.9%,47.5% --anchor c
pixelox text add story.pxo --in-place --name BadgeFigure --content "40%" --font "DIN Condensed" --weight 700 --size 3.4% --color '#4a4a4a' --at 17.9%,50.6% --anchor c
pixelox text add story.pxo --in-place --name Logo --content "LOGO" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 900 --size 2.2% --color '#ffffff' --at 9.4%,6.3% --anchor l
pixelox shape ellipse story.pxo --in-place --name Dot1 --rect 845,111,28x28 --fill '#ffffff'
pixelox shape ellipse story.pxo --in-place --name Dot2 --rect 899,111,28x28 --fill '#ffffff'
pixelox shape ellipse story.pxo --in-place --name Dot3 --rect 953,111,28x28 --fill '#ffffff'
pixelox text add story.pxo --in-place --name Script --content "Pure Comfort" --font "Savoye LET" --size 9% --color '#ffffff'
pixelox transform scale story.pxo --in-place --layer Script --size 500x
pixelox transform rotate story.pxo --in-place --layer Script --degrees -5
pixelox transform move story.pxo --in-place --layer Script --to 51%,16.9% --anchor c
pixelox text add story.pxo --in-place --name Headline --content "HEADSET" --font "Arial Black" --size 7% --color '#ffffff'
pixelox transform scale story.pxo --in-place --layer Headline --size 725x
pixelox transform move story.pxo --in-place --layer Headline --to 51%,22.5% --anchor t
pixelox text add story.pxo --in-place --name Body --content $'Forty hours of battery, deep clean bass,\nand soft cushions made for all-day wear' --font "Avenir Next" --weight 600 --size 1.12% --leading 150% --align center --color '#ffffff' --at 50%,31.6% --anchor c
pixelox shape ellipse story.pxo --in-place --name ContactDisc --rect 46,1734,76x76 --fill '#ffffff'
pixelox text add story.pxo --in-place --name ContactLabel --content "More Information" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 500 --size 1.25% --tracking 20 --color '#ffffff' --at 14.5%,91.9% --anchor l
pixelox text add story.pxo --in-place --name Phone --content "00 555 010 2030" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 900 --size 1.9% --color '#ffffff' --at 14.5%,93.7% --anchor l
pixelox shape rect story.pxo --in-place --name Pill --rect 767,1763,205x50 --radius 25 --fill '#150f10' --stroke '#5a4f53' --stroke-width 1
pixelox text add story.pxo --in-place --name PillText --content "ORDER NOW" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 700 --size 1.5% --tracking 20 --color '#ffffff' --at 80.5%,93.1% --anchor c
pixelox export story.pxo -o story.png
```

Change first: the headline word (6 to 8 letters; `transform scale` keeps it at 67% W),
the script phrase, then the accent: recolor `Swoosh` and its two gradient stops together.
To reshape the band, move the peak (`620 1085` and `740 1050`) and keep the lower edge 90
to 210 px below the upper one.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Light twin: p113 is the same template on an off-white ground with a blue two-tone wave
  in place of the amber band and charcoal type. Build both for a two-color product range.
* Accent swaps on the dark ground: lime `#b6e000`, coral `#ff5a4d`, cyan `#22c8e8`. Keep
  the ground warm for warm accents and switch it to blue-black `#141a24` for cool ones.
* Two bands: add a thinner band (2% H) in white at 30% opacity, 3% H below the main one,
  for a racing-stripe feel.
* Feed post 4:5: remove the empty band at 73 to 90% H, keep the contact strip at 92% H.
* Pairs with T-cutout-drop-shadow (glow variant in the accent color behind a dark
  product), T-price-burst in place of the round badge, and T-paper-grain at amount 8.

## 11. Pitfalls

* A neutral or cold black ground under a warm accent. Use a black with red in it
  (`#312527`), or the amber looks dirty.
* A thin, even band. The swoosh must be thick (5 to 11% H) and grow thicker toward one
  end; a hairline arc reads as a stray guide line.
* The product in front of nothing. A black product needs the band (or a glow) directly
  behind its darkest part, or its outline is lost.
* A bold or brush script. The script has to be thin and loose against the heaviest sans
  you have; two heavy faces stacked make the block shout twice.
* Body copy wider than the headline. Keep both body lines inside the headline's width
  (67% W) and break them by hand.
