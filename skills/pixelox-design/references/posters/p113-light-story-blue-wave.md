# p113: Light product story with blue wave

## 1. Concept

A vertical social story for one product on a clean two-field page: the upper 60% is
off-white with a barely visible sunburst and charcoal type, the lower 40% is a blue
field whose top edge is a rising wave with a lighter cyan lip. White headphones sit
exactly on the boundary, half on white and half on blue. It works because the wave does
three jobs at once: it divides text from contact details, it gives a white product a
dark surface to stand out against, and its upward sweep to the right adds energy to a
quiet layout. Presented as a floating card on a white page; the page is not part of the
design.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait 9:16. Center axis at 50% W for the text block and the product; margins 9.4% W.
* Two fields. The boundary (top of the cyan lip) starts at 59.8% H on the left edge,
  stays almost level to 45% W, then climbs to 46.9% H at the right edge. The deep blue
  begins 6 to 9% H below it (71% H at the far left, 65% H at the center, 56% H at the
  right).
* Zones: top bar 5 to 7.5% H (logo left, three dots right); text block 13 to 33% H
  (script 13 to 20.4%, headline 22.5 to 27.5%, body 30 to 33%); empty 33 to 41% H;
  product 41.5 to 65.5% H, straddling the boundary; empty blue 67 to 90% H; contact strip
  91 to 94.5% H.
* Off-axis: the discount figure at the left (12 to 24% W, 47.4 to 50.6% H), with no
  badge shape around it.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: off-white `#f1f0f2`, full canvas.
2. Sunburst: about 40 thin rays of uneven width from a center at 50% W, 48% H (behind
   the product), only 3% darker than the ground (`#e9e8ea`): felt more than seen.
3. Cyan lip: a wave shape filled `#24c0e2`, from the boundary line down.
4. Deep blue field: a second wave shape 6 to 9% H lower, gradient from `#2496d0` at the
   upper left to `#2366bb` at the lower right, running to the bottom edge.
5. Product shadow: a soft dark-blue ellipse `#16506e`, 32% W x 2.6% H, centered at 52% W,
   65.5% H, lying on the blue just under the product.
6. Product: white headphones, cutout, `33%,41.5%,35% x 24%`, tilted about 10 degrees
   clockwise.
7. Discount: a tiny "save up to" line over a large "50%" pattern, charcoal, centered at
   18.1% W, 47.6% and 49.5% H.
8. Logo: charcoal heavy caps, cap height 1.5% H, left at 9.4% W, 6.3% H. Three charcoal
   dots (2.6% W across) at 79.5%, 84.5%, 89.5% W, 6.5% H.
9. Script phrase: 2 words, charcoal, thin signature script, 45% W wide, 7.4% H tall with
   its loops, tilted up about 5 degrees, centered at 51% W, 16.8% H.
10. Headline: 1 word of 7 letters, charcoal `#555457`, heavy wide grotesque, cap height
    5.1% H, 67% W wide, cap top at 22.5% H.
11. Body: 2 centered lines of about 45 characters, charcoal, semibold, cap height 0.8% H,
    at 30.8% and 32.1% H.
12. Contact strip on the blue: a white disc (6.5% W) at 9.9% W, 92.6% H; right of it
    "More Information" (cap height 0.9% H) over a phone number (heavy, cap height 1.3%
    H), white, left at 16.4% W; at the right a charcoal pill `#23262d`,
    `70.6%,91.8%,19.1% x 2.6%`, with "ORDER NOW" in white.

## 6. Imagery

One product cutout: white over-ear headphones, three-quarter view, resting on one ear
cup and tilted about 10 degrees, lit softly from the upper left with pale gray shading.
A white product on an off-white ground would vanish, so it is placed where the blue
field begins: the lower two fifths of the product stand against cyan and blue, and a
soft dark-blue shadow on the wave anchors it. Scale: 35% W wide, 24% H tall. Any light
or pastel product works (earbuds case, sneaker, skincare jar, mug); a dark product
should move up so that most of it sits on the white field.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-sunburst**, extremely faint: `uneven=0.8`, 40 rays, ray color `#dcdbe0` at alpha
  0.32 on the `#f1f0f2` ground, center behind the product.
* Wave: two closed `shape path` layers (cyan, then blue 6 to 9% H lower) so the lip is a
  band of even thickness that widens at both ends; `fx overlay` gradient on the blue at
  315 degrees (upper left to lower right). **T-waves** (`scripts/waves.js`, `count=2`,
  `chaos=0.3`) gives the same look when the exact curve does not matter.
* Product shadow: a feathered ellipse painted through `select ellipse --feather` on its
  own layer under the product (a blurred shape layer would be clipped at its bounds).
* **T-script-overlap** pairing without the overlap: script 2% H above the block word,
  tilted -5 degrees.
* **T-fit-width** on the headline (67% W) and the script (45% W).
* **T-pill-button**, filled charcoal, 2.6% H tall, in the contact strip.
* No grain, no vignette: the surface is clean and bright.
* **T-swoosh-band** in [techniques.md](../techniques.md) is the tested library version of the curved color band; start from it when reusing this idea.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The headline word (largest and darkest on the light field).
2. The script phrase above it.
3. Down to the product on the boundary; the wave carries the eye up to the right and
   back along its lower edge.
4. The discount figure at the left, level with the product.
5. The contact strip on the blue: phone number, then the button.

Cap-height ratio: headline 5.1 : discount figure 2.3 : logo 1.5 : phone 1.3 : body 0.8,
about 6.4 : 2.9 : 1.9 : 1.6 : 1.

## 9. Build recipe

`product.png` is your image here: a product cutout. For a wide product such as
headphones, replace `--size x480` with `--size 380x` (35% of the width). Replace the
`Logo` text with your mark (`comp place` with `--size 110x --at 9.4%,6.3% --anchor l`).

```bash
pixelox render js scripts/rays.js -o rays.png --size 1080x1920 --seed 6 --param color='#dcdbe0' --param cx=0.5 --param cy=0.48 --param count=40 --param alpha=0.32 --param fade=0.6 --param uneven=0.8
pixelox new -o story.pxo --size 1080x1920 --fill '#f1f0f2'
pixelox comp place story.pxo rays.png --in-place --as Rays --fit cover
pixelox shape path story.pxo --in-place --name Lip --d 'M 0 1148 C 200 1165 420 1190 560 1150 C 720 1060 950 950 1080 896 L 1080 1920 L 0 1920 Z' --fill '#24c0e2'
pixelox shape path story.pxo --in-place --name Field --d 'M 0 1375 C 70 1325 190 1292 330 1275 C 570 1248 850 1160 1080 1072 L 1080 1920 L 0 1920 Z' --fill '#2488cb'
pixelox fx overlay story.pxo --in-place --layer Field --stops '#2496d0@0,#2366bb@1' --angle 315
pixelox layer add story.pxo --in-place --name ProductShadow
pixelox select ellipse story.pxo --in-place --rect 36%,64.2%,32%x2.6% --feather 12
pixelox fill solid story.pxo --in-place --layer ProductShadow --color '#16506e' --mask @sel
pixelox select none story.pxo --in-place
pixelox comp place story.pxo product.png --in-place --as Product --size x480 --at 50.5%,65.5% --anchor b
pixelox transform rotate story.pxo --in-place --layer Product --degrees 10
pixelox text add story.pxo --in-place --name DiscountNote --content "save up to" --font "Avenir Next Condensed" --weight 700 --size 0.9% --color '#3a393b' --at 18.1%,47.5% --anchor c
pixelox text add story.pxo --in-place --name DiscountFigure --content "40%" --font "DIN Condensed" --weight 700 --size 3.4% --color '#3a393b' --at 18.1%,49.9% --anchor c
pixelox text add story.pxo --in-place --name Logo --content "LOGO" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 900 --size 2.1% --color '#555457' --at 9.4%,6.3% --anchor l
pixelox shape ellipse story.pxo --in-place --name Dot1 --rect 845,111,28x28 --fill '#555457'
pixelox shape ellipse story.pxo --in-place --name Dot2 --rect 899,111,28x28 --fill '#555457'
pixelox shape ellipse story.pxo --in-place --name Dot3 --rect 953,111,28x28 --fill '#555457'
pixelox text add story.pxo --in-place --name Script --content "Feel Lighter" --font "Savoye LET" --size 9% --color '#555457'
pixelox transform scale story.pxo --in-place --layer Script --size 490x
pixelox transform rotate story.pxo --in-place --layer Script --degrees -5
pixelox transform move story.pxo --in-place --layer Script --to 51%,16.8% --anchor c
pixelox text add story.pxo --in-place --name Headline --content "EARCUPS" --font "Arial Black" --size 7% --color '#555457'
pixelox transform scale story.pxo --in-place --layer Headline --size 725x
pixelox transform move story.pxo --in-place --layer Headline --to 51%,22.5% --anchor t
pixelox text add story.pxo --in-place --name Body --content $'Feather-weight frame, clear open sound,\nand a fold-flat hinge for every commute' --font "Avenir Next" --weight 600 --size 1.12% --leading 150% --align center --color '#6a696c' --at 50%,31.6% --anchor c
pixelox shape ellipse story.pxo --in-place --name ContactDisc --rect 72,1743,70x70 --fill '#ffffff'
pixelox text add story.pxo --in-place --name ContactLabel --content "More Information" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 500 --size 1.25% --tracking 20 --color '#ffffff' --at 16.4%,91.6% --anchor l
pixelox text add story.pxo --in-place --name Phone --content "00 555 010 2030" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 900 --size 1.8% --color '#ffffff' --at 16.4%,93.5% --anchor l
pixelox shape rect story.pxo --in-place --name Pill --rect 762,1762,207x50 --radius 25 --fill '#23262d'
pixelox text add story.pxo --in-place --name PillText --content "ORDER NOW" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 700 --size 1.5% --tracking 20 --color '#ffffff' --at 80.2%,93.1% --anchor c
pixelox export story.pxo -o story.png
```

Change first: the headline word (6 to 8 letters), the script phrase, then the two blues
(`Lip` fill and the `Field` gradient stops; keep the lip lighter and greener than the
field). To move the boundary, shift every y value in both paths by the same amount and
move the product and its shadow with it.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Dark twin: p110 is the same template on a warm near-black ground with one amber band
  in place of the wave and white type. Use the pair for light and dark product colors.
* Other waves on the same off-white: coral `#ff6b5e` over crimson `#c2264b`; lime
  `#b5e61d` over forest `#1e7a46`; lilac `#b79cff` over violet `#5a3fd6`. The lip is
  always the lighter, more saturated step.
* Flip the wave so it climbs to the left and move the discount figure to the right.
* Feed post 4:5: boundary at 62% H, contact strip at 92% H, drop the body lines.
* Pairs with T-pattern (a faded dot field in the upper corner in place of the sunburst),
  T-cutout-drop-shadow (soft studio shadow) when the product sits fully on the white, and
  T-price-burst for a louder discount.

## 11. Pitfalls

* A white product placed fully on the white field. It must cross onto the blue by at
  least a third of its height, and it needs the shadow on the wave.
* A visible sunburst. If the rays can be counted at a glance they are too dark; keep
  them within 3 to 5% of the ground's lightness.
* A wave with a flat lip. The cyan band should be thicker at both ends than in the
  middle, and the boundary should rise at least 12% H from one side to the other.
* Black type. Pure black on off-white is harsher than the soft blues can carry; use
  charcoal `#555457` and keep white for the blue field only.
* Contact details on the boundary. Everything in the contact strip sits well inside the
  deep blue (below 90% H), never on the cyan lip.
