# p044: Blue sky monument with white title

## 1. Concept

A church-event flyer built on one low-angle photograph: a stone statue with arms spread
wide, seen from below against a clear blue sky, fills the bottom third, and a white title
block (condensed caps, a script word, a tracked sub-line, a short paragraph) hangs in the
sky above it. The idea is the open-armed shape under an open sky: the statue's horizontal
arms underline the whole text column like a wide base, and the only two colors are sky blue
and white, warmed by the tan stone.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait 2:3. One vertical axis at 50% W. Side margins 6% W for the footer, 9% W for the
  paragraph, 18% W for the title block.
* Zones: presenter line at 4% H; title block 18 to 42% H; paragraph 51 to 63% H; statue
  69 to 100% H; footer line at 96.5% H, printed over the statue's plinth.
* Inside the centered title block the date hangs from the headline's left edge (18.4% W);
  the script word and the sub-line are centered 1.5% W right of the axis (52.5% W).
* The statue's arms span 2 to 98% W at 72 to 78% H: a near-full-width horizontal bar that
  closes the composition. Head on the axis, top at 69% H.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Photo, full bleed. Sky: palest at the top center (`#cfdbed`, sun haze), mid blue
   `#9aaeca` to `#a3bddf` through the middle, deepest `#3e6eb0` to `#527fbc` at the lower
   left and right; a few thin white cloud streaks.
2. Statue in the photo (not a separate layer): a robed figure in tan stone or pale bronze
   (`#decbba` lit, `#b7a595` mid, `#38302e` deep folds), viewed from below so the robe
   widens toward the bottom: a triangle from the head at 50%,69% to a 60% W base at 92% H,
   standing on a rough stone plinth that fills the bottom 8% H.
3. Presenter line: tracked white caps, centered, 4% H.
4. Date: white, left edge 18.4% W, cap top 18% H.
5. Headline word: white condensed caps, 18.4 to 84% W, cap top 20.5% H, baseline 29.9% H.
6. Script word: white formal script, 23.8 to 81% W, its top swash touching the headline's
   baseline at 29.5% H, descending to 39% H.
7. Sub-line: widely tracked white caps, centered at 52.5% W, 40.3% H.
8. Paragraph: four centered white lines, mixed case, first cap top at 51.5% H, line pitch
   3% H, the last line short; widest line 82% W.
9. Footer line: address, web address, phone, separated by spaced hyphens; small tracked
   white caps, centered, 96.5% H.

Monochrome blue plus white, with one warm neutral. Blue and tan are near-complements,
which is why the statue stands out without any outline or glow.

## 5. Text elements

| Role | Content pattern | Case | Font class (look-alike / local) | Weight | Cap height | Tracking | Leading | Align | Color | Position |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Presenter | "`[ORG]` PRESENTS", 3 words | caps | condensed gothic (Bebas Neue, Oswald 500 / DIN Condensed) | 700 | 1.9% | 150 | - | center | `#ffffff` | 50%, 4.1% |
| Date | DD.MM.YYYY | - | same | 700 | 2.0% | 100 | - | left | `#ffffff` | 18.4%, cap top 18% |
| Headline | one word, 9 letters | caps | same | 700 | 9.4% | 0 | - | fitted to 65.6% W | `#ffffff` | 18.4%, cap top 20.5% |
| Script | one word, 7 to 8 letters | title | formal high-contrast script (Great Vibes, Allura / Snell Roundhand) | heavy cut | capital 8% | 0 | - | center | `#ffffff` | 52.5%, top 29.5% |
| Sub-line | "SUMMER EVENT"-type, 2 words | caps | condensed gothic | 700 | 1.95% | 300 | - | center | `#ffffff` | 52.5%, 40.3% |
| Paragraph | 4 lines, 38 to 40 characters each, last line under 20 | sentence | condensed gothic with lowercase (Oswald 500, Pathway Gothic One / DIN Condensed) | 700 | 2.1% | 120 | 100 to 105% | center | `#ffffff` | 50%, top 51.5% |
| Footer | address - web - phone on one line | caps | condensed gothic | 700 | 1.3% | 150 | - | center | `#ffffff` | 50%, 96.5% |

Two families: a condensed gothic for everything, and one script word. All white, no
shadows. The paragraph is set bold, tracked 120, and as large as the date (cap height
2.1% H) so that white-on-blue text keeps enough stroke to survive; its lines are packed
at a leading of about 100%, so it reads as one soft block.

## 6. Imagery

A single photo taken from the foot of a monument looking up: the figure's arms reach both
edges, the robe flares toward the camera, the sky behind is cloudless and gets darker away
from the sun. Natural color, no grade. What a replacement needs: a subject shot from below
or straight on that is **wide and low** (outstretched arms, a bridge, a gate, a row of
trees, a roofline), a plain sky covering the top 65%, and a tone darker than white behind
the title. Anchor the subject to the bottom edge and let it bleed off the bottom; do not
show its base floating.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-photo-fade**, reversed: a sky-blue field (`#8fa9d2`) is solid above 40% H and clears
  by 70% H, so the title always sits on calm, even blue whatever the photo does up there.
* Sun haze: a soft white radial glow centered on the top edge (50% W, 0% H), reaching
  down to 50% H, 65% strong at its center; it gives the sky its pale-top, deep-bottom fall-off.
* **T-script-overlap**, quiet variant: script in the same white as the block word, level,
  no shadow, just touching the block word's baseline.
* **T-fit-width** on the headline word (65.6% W).
* No grain, no vignette. The picture stays clean and airy.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The headline word (largest white shape).
2. The script word and sub-line directly under it.
3. The paragraph, a quieter block of the same width as the statue's body.
4. The statue: its arms catch the eye at the bottom and send it back up the axis.
5. Footer and presenter lines.

Cap-height ratio: headline 9.4 : script capital 8 : paragraph 2.1 : date, presenter,
sub-line 2 : footer 1.3, about 7 : 6 : 1.6 : 1.5 : 1.

## 9. Build recipe

`photo.png` is your image here: a photo whose subject fills its lower third under a plain
sky. It is anchored to the bottom edge at full width; the top of the canvas is filled by
the fade color.

```bash
pixelox new -o skyfest.pxo --size 1080x1620 --fill '#8fa9d2'
pixelox comp place skyfest.pxo photo.png --in-place --as Photo --size 1080x --at 0,100% --anchor bl
pixelox layer add skyfest.pxo --in-place --name SkyFade
pixelox fill gradient skyfest.pxo --in-place --layer SkyFade --from 0,70% --to 0,40% --stops '#8fa9d200@0,#8fa9d2ff@1'
pixelox layer add skyfest.pxo --in-place --name Haze
pixelox fill gradient skyfest.pxo --in-place --layer Haze --kind radial --from 50%,0 --to 50%,50% --stops '#ffffffa6@0,#ffffff00@1'
pixelox layer add skyfest.pxo --in-place --name Deep
pixelox fill gradient skyfest.pxo --in-place --layer Deep --from 0,45% --to 0,100% --stops '#3e6eb000@0,#3e6eb059@1'
pixelox text add skyfest.pxo --in-place --name Presenter --content "YOUR CHURCH PRESENTS" --font "DIN Condensed" --weight 700 --size 2.6% --tracking 150 --color '#ffffff' --at 50%,4.3% --anchor c
pixelox text add skyfest.pxo --in-place --name Date --content "22.08.2031" --font "DIN Condensed" --weight 700 --size 2.75% --tracking 100 --color '#ffffff'
pixelox transform move skyfest.pxo --in-place --layer Date --to 18.4%,17.7% --anchor tl
pixelox text add skyfest.pxo --in-place --name Headline --content "RIVERSIDE" --font "DIN Condensed" --weight 700 --size 10% --color '#ffffff'
pixelox transform scale skyfest.pxo --in-place --layer Headline --size 708x
pixelox transform move skyfest.pxo --in-place --layer Headline --to 18.4%,20.5% --anchor tl
pixelox text add skyfest.pxo --in-place --name Script --content "Revival" --font "Snell Roundhand" --weight 900 --size 12% --color '#ffffff'
pixelox transform scale skyfest.pxo --in-place --layer Script --size 620x
pixelox transform move skyfest.pxo --in-place --layer Script --to 52.5%,28.8% --anchor t
pixelox text add skyfest.pxo --in-place --name Sub --content "SUMMER EVENT" --font "DIN Condensed" --weight 700 --size 2.7% --tracking 300 --color '#ffffff'
pixelox transform move skyfest.pxo --in-place --layer Sub --to 52.5%,40.2% --anchor t
pixelox text add skyfest.pxo --in-place --name Body --content $'Three days of music, teaching, and food\nfor every age. Bring a friend, a chair,\nand an open heart. Gates open at four;\nfirst song at five.' --font "DIN Condensed" --weight 700 --size 3% --tracking 130 --leading 104% --align center --color '#ffffff'
pixelox transform move skyfest.pxo --in-place --layer Body --to 50%,51.5% --anchor t
pixelox text add skyfest.pxo --in-place --name Footer --content "12 EXAMPLE ROAD, YOUR CITY  -  WWW.EXAMPLE.COM  -  PHONE 555 0100" --font "DIN Condensed" --weight 700 --size 1.8% --tracking 150 --color '#ffffff' --at 50%,96.7% --anchor c
pixelox export skyfest.pxo -o skyfest.png
```

Change first: the fade color. Sample your photo's sky where it meets the fade
(`pixelox analyze stats photo.png --rect 540,300,9x9 --json`) and use that hex in `new` and
in both SkyFade stops, so the join is invisible. Then the headline (7 to 10 letters), the
script word, and the paragraph (break the lines by hand at 38 to 40 characters).

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Twin: p043 is the same title block in dark maroon on a white winter photo, without the
  paragraph. Use p043's dark type when the sky is paler than about `#d5dde8`; white type
  needs a sky at least as deep as `#9aaeca`.
* Sunset version: fade color `#e8a070`, deep layer `#7a2f4a`, type white; the paragraph
  gains a `fx drop-shadow` (offset 0,2, blur 8, opacity 30).
* Night version: fade `#0f1d3d`, haze removed, T-bokeh or `particles.js` stars in the top
  half, type white.
* Drop the paragraph and move the statue up to 60% H for a cleaner teaser; or replace the
  paragraph with a three-column T-info-bar at 54 to 62% H.
* Suits open-air services, pilgrimages, memorial days, travel and city-break ads, museum
  and heritage posters.

## 11. Pitfalls

* White type on the palest part of the sky. Near the sun the sky reaches `#cfdbed`, and a
  1.9% H white line there almost disappears (the presenter line of the original nearly
  does). Keep the haze under 65% strength, or move the glow to a top corner.
* A subject that is tall and narrow (a tower, a standing figure with arms down): it cuts
  the page in two and collides with the paragraph. This layout wants a wide, low subject.
* A paragraph in regular weight or tracked 0: at this size white-on-blue text thins out.
  Use weight 700, tracking 100 to 140, and no more than four lines.
* A visible seam where the fade meets the photo's own sky: the fade color does not match.
  Sample it from the photo rather than choosing a "nice blue".
* Letting the footer run over a light, detailed part of the subject. Keep it on the darker
  plinth or ground, or add a 25% dark gradient over the bottom 6% H.
