# p065: Engraved portrait on green type panel

## 1. Concept

A Sunday-service flyer printed in three inks on cream: a speaker's portrait, redrawn as a
sepia banknote engraving, sits in a bottle-green panel whose surface is woven from rows of
one tiny repeated phrase; below it a fat green name and a brown script weekday form the
title, and a single row of small print closes the page. It works because every part is
flat and limited to green, brown, and cream, so the photo stops being a photo and the
whole sheet reads as one piece of letterpress.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait 4:5. Margins 7.5% W left and right, 4.5% H top, 3% H bottom.
* One left hang line at 7.4% W for the panel, the block word, the script, and the time.
* Zones: picture panel 4.5 to 63.2% H (x 7.9 to 92.1% W); title 65.6 to 89.5% H, using only
  the left 64% of the width; logo ring alone in the right third at 73.6 to 81% H; info row
  91 to 96.6% H in three columns (7 to 17% W, 23.5 to 73% W, 80 to 94% W).
* A vertical line of small print runs up the left margin outside the panel (x 6% W, 9 to
  61% H).

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: cream `#fdf6da`, flat.
2. Panel: `7.9%,4.5%,84.2% x 58.7%`, green `#065d16`, square corners.
3. Panel texture: about 46 rows of one phrase (3 words run together, then a plus sign)
   repeated edge to edge in lighter green `#2f8f40`; cap height 0.5% H, row pitch 1.25% H.
   From a distance it is a fine horizontal weave; up close it is a message.
4. Portrait: head, shoulders, and a hand holding a microphone, cut out, drawn in engraved
   lines and dots: dark brown `#361d05` shadows, tan `#ab9471` mid-tones, cream highlights.
   Hair touches the panel top at 4.5% H; face centered at 48% W; shoulders span 15 to 91% W
   at the panel's bottom edge, which crops the figure in a straight line at 63.2% H.
5. Vertical print: one dark brown line rotated -90 degrees (reads bottom to top), x 6% W,
   from 61% up to 9% H, with a small red video icon inside it.
6. Block word: 4 letters, green `#05510d`, x 7.4 to 64.3% W, cap top 65.6% H, baseline
   76.5% H (cap height 10.9% H); extra-wide heavy letters.
7. Script word: a weekday in dark brown `#361d05`, x 6.7 to 60% W, capital top 72% H,
   baseline 83.4% H, descender to 89.5% H. It overlaps the bottom third of the block word.
8. Logo: a thin brown ring, 9.4% W across, centered at 80.3% W, 77.3% H, with a small mark.
9. Info row, left: the time in heavy brown, a time-zone note under it.
10. Info row, middle: two tan hairlines at 91% and 96.6% H from 23.5 to 73% W; between them
    the venue (brown, bold) and the address (green, letterspaced).
11. Info row, right: a three-line centered stack: a small brown line, the brand word in
    heavy green, a brown line.

## 6. Imagery

A waist-up portrait of a smiling man in glasses and a blazer over a patterned shirt,
holding a microphone at chest height, turned slightly to the left, lit from the front
left. It has been redrawn as an engraving: contour-following hatch lines and stipple dots
in one dark brown ink on cream, like the portrait on a banknote or an old newspaper
masthead. The build gets the same family of look from a photo with **T-halftone-portrait**
set to a fine line screen (`--shape line --cell 4`), which keeps the two-ink logic; a true
hand engraving needs an illustrator. The logo is a one-color line mark in a ring.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-halftone-portrait**, line-screen variant: black and white, levels 15 to 225, then
  `filter halftone --shape line --cell 4 --angle 30` with brown ink and cream paper. The
  result is clipped to the panel so the panel's bottom edge crops the figure.
* Micro-type weave: one row of text is set on its own transparent strip, as wide as the
  canvas and as tall as one row (1080 x 17 px), exported, and tiled down the panel as a
  pattern overlay (`fx overlay --kind pattern`). The tile repeats from the canvas origin,
  so the strip's height is the row pitch. It is a repeat pattern in the sense of
  **T-pattern**, made of type.
* Block word: **T-stretch-type**, widened a little and fitted to its width (`--scale-x
  102.7% --fit-width 616`); it stays live text.
* **T-script-overlap**, quiet version: the script sits in front of the block word's lower
  third with no rotation, no shadow, and no accent color; the dark brown on green carries
  the overlap by itself.
* **T-vertical-type** for the margin line; **T-rules** for the two hairlines around the
  venue.
* No gradients, no shadows, no grain: flat inks only.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The portrait's face: the lightest area inside the darkest block.
2. The green block word directly under the panel, on the same left edge.
3. The script weekday that hooks into it.
4. Along the info row: time, venue, the green brand word at the right.
5. The logo ring, then the vertical print and the woven phrase, found only up close.

Cap-height ratio: block word 10.9 : time and brand 2.3 : venue 1.6 : small print 1.0 :
weave 0.5, about 11 : 2.3 : 1.6 : 1 : 0.5.

## 9. Build recipe

`subject.png` (a cutout portrait, waist up, with clear light and shadow) and `logo.png`
(a dark one-color round mark) are your images here.

```bash
pixelox new -o row.pxo --size 1080x17 --fill transparent
pixelox text add row.pxo --in-place --name Line --content "GROWINGINGRACE + GROWINGINGRACE + GROWINGINGRACE + GROWINGINGRACE + GROWINGINGRACE + GROWINGINGRACE + GROWINGINGRACE + GROWINGINGRACE +" --font @geometric-sans --weight 600 --fit-width 890 --tracking 120 --color '#2f8f40' --at 95,3 --anchor-to ink
pixelox export row.pxo -o row.png
pixelox new -o sunday.pxo --size 1080x1350 --fill '#fdf6da'
pixelox shape rect sunday.pxo --in-place --name Panel --rect 7.9%,4.5%,84.2%x58.7% --fill '#065d16'
pixelox fx overlay sunday.pxo --in-place --layer Panel --kind pattern --from row.png
pixelox comp place sunday.pxo subject.png --in-place --as Portrait --size x930 --at 50%,63.2% --anchor b
pixelox adjust bw sunday.pxo --in-place --layer Portrait
pixelox adjust levels sunday.pxo --in-place --layer Portrait --in-black 15 --in-white 225
pixelox filter halftone sunday.pxo --in-place --layer Portrait --cell 4 --shape line --angle 30 --ink '#361d05' --paper '#f3e2bd'
pixelox layer clip sunday.pxo --in-place --layer Portrait
pixelox text add sunday.pxo --in-place --name Margin --content "Streaming live on @yourchannel  /  Also on the church app" --font @geometric-sans --weight 500 --size 1.15% --color '#4a3320' --at 50%,50% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate sunday.pxo --in-place --layer Margin --degrees -90
pixelox transform move sunday.pxo --in-place --layer Margin --to 6%,61% --anchor b
pixelox text add sunday.pxo --in-place --name Block --content "HOPE" --font @heavy-wide --scale-x 102.7% --fit-width 616 --color '#05510d' --at 7.4%,65.6% --anchor-to ink
pixelox text add sunday.pxo --in-place --name Script --content "Sunday" --font @brush --fit-width 590 --color '#361d05' --at 6.7%,72.5% --anchor-to ink
pixelox fx stroke sunday.pxo --in-place --layer Script --width 3 --color '#361d05' --position outside
pixelox comp place sunday.pxo logo.png --in-place --as Logo --size 100x --at 80.3%,77.3% --anchor c
pixelox text add sunday.pxo --in-place --name Time --content "8AM" --font @heavy-wide --size 3.1% --color '#361d05' --at 11.8%,93.4% --anchor c
pixelox text add sunday.pxo --in-place --name Zone --content "(GMT)" --font @geometric-sans --weight 500 --size 1.35% --color '#361d05' --at 11.8%,95.7% --anchor c
pixelox shape line sunday.pxo --in-place --name RuleTop --from 23.5%,91% --to 73%,91% --stroke '#b9ad90' --stroke-width 1
pixelox shape line sunday.pxo --in-place --name RuleBottom --from 23.5%,96.6% --to 73%,96.6% --stroke '#b9ad90' --stroke-width 1
pixelox text add sunday.pxo --in-place --name Venue --content "MAIN AUDITORIUM" --font @geometric-sans --weight 700 --size 2.2% --tracking 20 --color '#361d05' --at 48.3%,93.2% --anchor c
pixelox text add sunday.pxo --in-place --name Address --content "12 EXAMPLE ROAD, YOUR DISTRICT, YOUR CITY" --font @geometric-sans --weight 600 --size 1.5% --tracking 150 --color '#0d6c21' --at 48.3%,95.2% --anchor c
pixelox text add sunday.pxo --in-place --name Note1 --content "& ACROSS ALL" --font @geometric-sans --weight 500 --size 1.4% --color '#361d05' --at 87%,91.7% --anchor c
pixelox text add sunday.pxo --in-place --name Note2 --content "HOPE" --font @heavy-wide --size 3.1% --color '#05510d' --at 87%,93.8% --anchor c
pixelox text add sunday.pxo --in-place --name Note3 --content "LOCATIONS" --font @geometric-sans --weight 500 --size 1.6% --color '#361d05' --at 87%,96% --anchor c
pixelox export sunday.pxo -o sunday.png
```

Change first: the phrase in the weave (keep it short, without spaces, 8 repeats to fill
the row), the block word, and the script word. The strip height in `--size 1080x17` is the
row pitch: a taller strip spaces the rows out. The `3` in `--at 95,3` slides all rows up
or down: with another panel, change it until no row is cut by the panel's top or bottom
edge.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Other two-ink pairs on cream: oxblood `#6b1016` with navy `#14213d`; royal blue
  `#1c3faa` with black; mustard `#c98a00` with dark green. On a dark sheet: cream and gold
  on `#14213d`.
* Replace the weave phrase per series (a verse reference, the sermon title, the year) and
  keep everything else: an instant campaign system.
* A round or arched panel (T-geo-shapes) instead of the rectangle; the figure's head may
  then break out over the panel's top edge.
* Speaker announcements, podcast covers, author events, coffee and craft labels,
  anniversary portraits. Square post: panel 5 to 62% H, title only, no info row.
* Pairs with T-paper-grain (amount 18) for a printed feel,
  T-stencil-poster instead of the line screen for a bolder portrait, and T-arc-type around
  the logo ring.

## 11. Pitfalls

* A full-color or plain grayscale photo in the panel breaks the print illusion. The
  portrait must be reduced to brown ink on cream.
* A line screen that is too coarse (cell above 7 px on a 1080 px canvas) turns the face
  into stripes; too fine (under 3 px) turns it back into a gray photo. A face needs 60 or
  more lines from chin to hairline.
* A weave that is too bright competes with the portrait. Keep its green within 15%
  lightness of the panel green and its cap height under 0.6% H.
* The script in green or the block word in brown: the overlap disappears. The two title
  words must be in the two different inks.
* Centering the title under the panel. The empty right third beside the title, holding
  only the logo ring, is what gives the layout its air.
* Leaving the tip layer in the stack: a clipped layer clips to the nearest unclipped layer
  below it, so a stray text row between the panel and the portrait hides the portrait.
  Remove it after the export.
