# p056: Red-border news cover with seated portrait

## 1. Concept

A news-weekly cover: a seated portrait on a warm peach studio backdrop, framed by a solid
red border with a white keyline, a four-letter white serif masthead that the sitter's head
overlaps, and one stacked block of serif cover lines at the lower left. It works because
the frame and masthead are a fixed, instantly recognized shell, and everything inside it
is one photograph plus one text block: the cover makes a single statement ("this person,
this honor") instead of selling ten stories.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait 3:4. Red border 4.1% W on every side (equal in pixels, so 3.1% H top and
  bottom); a white keyline 0.6% W wide sits directly on its inner edge.
* Picture area: `4.6%,3.5%,90.8% x 93%`. Inside it, one text margin at 8.2% W: every
  cover line hangs from that left edge.
* Masthead zone 6 to 22% H, centered on 49.5% W, 69% W wide.
* Figure: fills 9 to 97% H; head centered at 57% W, 21% H (right of the canvas center),
  body leaning left so the dark jacket runs under the text block.
* Cover-line block: 8.2 to 40% W, 49 to 79% H. The right half of the cover below the
  masthead carries no type at all.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Backdrop: studio seamless in peach, lit from the upper right: pale `#f4d9c6` at the
   top right, `#e9946b` across the middle, falling to terracotta `#8a3320` in the lower
   left corner.
2. Masthead: `[MASTHEAD]`, 4 serif capitals, white, cap height 15.4% H, 69% W wide
   (15 to 84% W), cap top at 6.2% H, letters set wide apart.
3. Portrait cutout: a woman seated at three-quarter length, top of head at 9% H, covering
   most of the masthead's third letter and the edge of the second. Dark oversized jacket
   `#131615` with soft gray highlights; her left shoulder reaches 15% W, her knee and a
   black boot fill the lower right corner to 93% W.
4. Headline: 3 short lines of white bold serif caps, cap height 3.9% H, left at 8.2% W,
   cap tops at 49.4%, 54.1%, 59% H; the longest line is 32% W wide.
5. Short rule: amber `#e0913a`, 2.6% W long, 0.3% H thick, at 64.9% H.
6. Name line: italic serif caps, pale peach `#f0c9ae`, cap height 2% H, at 67.6% H.
7. Second short rule, same as the first, at 70.5% H.
8. Deck: 4 lines of small bold serif caps, cap height 1.15% H, tops from 72% to 78% H;
   the first words in amber `#f0a53c`, the rest white.
9. Border: red `#f22827`, 4.1% W, over the picture's edges (it crops the figure).
10. Keyline: white, 0.6% W, on the border's inner edge.
11. Two tiny lines inside the border: the issue date at the top left (4.4% W, 1.6% H) and
    a web address at the bottom right (95.6% W, 98.4% H), dark red-brown `#3a0808`, cap
    height 0.7% H.

## 6. Imagery

A studio portrait at three-quarter length: a woman seated and turned slightly to her
right, hair pulled back tight, chin resting on the fingertips of one raised hand, the
other forearm lying across a raised knee; an oversized dark leather blazer with strong
shoulders, a black over-the-knee boot at the lower right. She looks straight into the
lens with a calm, level expression. Soft key light from the upper right puts a glow on the
backdrop behind her head and gray highlights on the leather. For a build: a cutout
with dark clothing (the white type sits on it), placed right of center so the head covers
one masthead letter. No grade, no outline, no shadow: the photo stays natural.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-frame-border**: the border is drawn last as an unfilled `shape rect` on the canvas
  edge with a stroke of twice the border width (88 px; half of it falls outside the
  canvas), so it crops the photo cleanly; the keyline is a second unfilled rect.
* **T-text-behind-subject**, light version: the head hides about one letter in four.
* **T-rules**: two short amber accent bars that divide headline, name, and deck.
* Backdrop gradient: `fx overlay` on the backdrop rect at 40 degrees, four stops from
  terracotta to pale peach; no texture.
* Tall masthead: `transform scale --size 745x222` sets width and cap height at once (the
  text is rasterized, warning PXW2003).
* **T-stretch-type** in [techniques.md](../techniques.md) is the tested library version of the stretched, slanted, or fattened lettering; start from it when reusing this idea.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The face: center-right, the lightest warm area against dark hair and jacket, and the
   masthead points at it by disappearing behind it.
2. The masthead (largest type, white on peach).
3. Down the dark jacket to the white headline at the lower left.
4. The amber rule, the name, the deck; border fine print last.

Cap heights: masthead 15.4 : headline 3.9 : name 2 : deck 1.15, about 13 : 3.4 : 1.7 : 1.

## 9. Build recipe

`subject.png` is your image here: a cutout of a seated or half-length figure in dark
clothing. With a full seated figure, raise `--size x1300` until the head top sits at 9% H
and slide `--at` until the head covers one masthead letter.

```bash
pixelox new -o cover.pxo --size 1080x1440 --fill '#f22827'
pixelox shape rect cover.pxo --in-place --name Backdrop --rect 44,44,992x1352 --fill '#e9946b'
pixelox fx overlay cover.pxo --in-place --layer Backdrop --stops '#6e2414@0,#c8623e@0.35,#eba77f@0.65,#f4d9c6@1' --angle 40
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Masthead --content "NOTE" --font "Times New Roman" --weight 400 --size 20% --tracking 60 --color '#ffffff'
pixelox transform scale cover.pxo --in-place --layer Masthead --size 745x222
pixelox transform move cover.pxo --in-place --layer Masthead --to 49.5%,6.2% --anchor t
pixelox comp place cover.pxo subject.png --in-place --as Subject --size x1300 --at 57%,1396 --anchor b
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Headline --content $'VOICES\nOF A NEW\nDECADE' --font "Times New Roman" --weight 700 --size 5.5% --leading 90% --color '#ffffff' --at 8.2%,48.6% --anchor tl
pixelox shape line cover.pxo --in-place --name Rule1 --from 8.2%,64.9% --to 10.8%,64.9% --stroke '#e0913a' --stroke-width 4
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Name --content "ADA MORGAN" --font "Times New Roman" --weight 400 --style italic --size 2.8% --tracking 40 --color '#f0c9ae' --at 8.2%,67.6% --anchor l
pixelox shape line cover.pxo --in-place --name Rule2 --from 8.2%,70.5% --to 10.8%,70.5% --stroke '#e0913a' --stroke-width 4
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name PlusLine --content "PLUS 11 MORE" --font "Times New Roman" --weight 700 --size 1.6% --tracking 60 --color '#f0a53c' --at 8.2%,72.6% --anchor l
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Deck --content $'PEOPLE RESHAPING\nHOW WE WORK\nAND LIVE' --font "Times New Roman" --weight 700 --size 1.6% --tracking 60 --leading 125% --color '#ffffff' --at 8.2%,73.6% --anchor tl
pixelox shape rect cover.pxo --in-place --name Border --rect 0,0,1080x1440 --fill none --stroke '#f22827' --stroke-width 88
pixelox shape rect cover.pxo --in-place --name Keyline --rect 47,47,986x1346 --fill none --stroke '#ffffff' --stroke-width 6
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Date --content "MARCH 10, 2031" --font "Avenir Next Condensed" --weight 700 --size 1% --tracking 80 --color '#3a0808' --at 4.4%,1.6% --anchor l
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Site --content "example.com" --font "Avenir Next Condensed" --weight 700 --size 1% --tracking 80 --color '#3a0808' --at 95.6%,98.4% --anchor r
pixelox export cover.pxo -o cover.png
```

Change first: the masthead word (3 to 5 letters; keep `745x222`), the headline lines, the
name. Then the backdrop stops: keep the darkest stop under the text block and the
lightest behind the head. If the headline falls on a light part of your photo, darken the
first two stops or move the subject left.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Border and backdrop swaps that keep the "frame next to backdrop" rule: cobalt border
  `#1c4ed8` with a pale blue-gray backdrop; black border with a warm gray backdrop;
  forest `#0f5c3a` with sage `#b9c8a8`. Keep masthead and headline white.
* Black-and-white portrait (T-mono-on-color on a flat `#d9d9d9` backdrop) with the red
  border as the only color.
* Uses beyond news: staff "person of the month" posts, award announcements, alumni and
  speaker profiles, birthday tributes. As a 4:5 social post keep the border at 4% W and
  drop the deck.
* Add one right-side cover line at 60 to 92% W, 70% H, right-aligned, when there is a
  second story; never more than two blocks.
* Pairs with T-photo-fade (a dark ramp from the bottom, behind the text block) when the
  subject wears light clothing, and T-paper-grain at amount 10.

## 11. Pitfalls

* The head hides the masthead. Cover one letter, at most one and a half; the first and
  last letters must be fully visible, and the cap top must clear the head by sight.
* A border of uneven thickness. Use the same pixel inset on all four sides (44 px here)
  and draw it last so it also trims the photo.
* White cover lines on pale backdrop. The block must sit on dark clothing or on the dark
  corner of the gradient; check contrast before fixing sizes.
* Centering the cover lines. They hang from one left edge at 8.2% W; the rules start on
  the same edge.
* A sans masthead or mixed families. The authority of this look comes from one serif
  family throughout; the sans is only for the 0.7% H fine print.
