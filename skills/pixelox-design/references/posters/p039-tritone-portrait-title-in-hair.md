# p039: Tritone portrait with title in the hair

## 1. Concept

A novel cover in the colors of a sound-system flyer: a woman's head with a huge round afro
is printed in red and orange ink on a solid yellow sheet, the ink turning green toward the
bottom edge, and the title is hand-cut black lettering set inside the hair, bent and
tapered so it follows the hair's round outline. It works because the hair is used as a
ready-made title panel (the biggest calm shape on the page), because the three inks are a
well-known flag triad, and because the lettering is warped like the picture, so type and
portrait feel printed in one pass.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait, about 1 : 1.6; built here at 2:3. No margins for the art: the figure runs off
  the bottom, and the hair almost touches both sides (9 to 93.5% W).
* Zones: review quotes top left 3 to 20% H; prize sticker top right 2.5 to 17.5% H; hair
  disc 28 to 71% H; title inside the hair 30 to 61% H, left of center (14 to 66.5% W);
  author under it 60 to 74% H at 17 to 41% W; face 53 to 71% H at 40 to 68% W; shoulders
  and jacket 78 to 100% H.
* The color of the dark ink changes on a horizontal line: red above 80% H, green below
  87% H, blending between.
* Text margin for the quotes: 5.7% W.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: saturated yellow `#f6ec18`, full canvas.
2. Portrait: head and shoulders of a woman facing the camera, chin slightly raised, large
   hoop earrings, denim jacket. Hair: a near-perfect disc 84% W across centered at 51% W,
   49% H. The photo is reduced to one ink on the yellow: darkest parts red `#dc2113`,
   mid-tones orange `#e56114`, highlights the bare yellow of the sheet. Edges of the hair
   are dry-brushed and ragged, with scratches of yellow showing through.
3. Green shift: below 80% H the same shadows are printed in green `#398f42` (jacket, lower
   shoulders), with an olive overlap zone between.
4. Title line 1: 4 black hand-cut capitals, cap height 14% H, on a baseline that rises
   about 4% H from left to right; 21 to 66.5% W, 30 to 46% H.
5. Title line 2: 4 black capitals that taper: the first letter is 17% H tall, the last 8%
   H; the top edge rises to the right, the bottom edge rises faster, so the line is a
   wedge pointing at the face; 14 to 66.5% W, 43.5 to 61% H.
6. Author: 2 lines of smaller black hand-cut caps (cap height 5% H), tilted about 6
   degrees counter-clockwise, 17 to 41% W, 60 to 74% H, overlapping the cheek and the
   left earring.
7. Print wear: specks and nicks of bare yellow in the black letters and the red ink.
8. Quotes, top left: two blocks, each a one-word quote in bold red caps with quotation
   marks, and under it a reviewer's name in regular red caps on two lines.
9. Sticker, top right: a lime-green disc 24.7% W across centered at 84% W, 10% H, with
   three stacked white label strips carrying a prize name in black, a year in lime on
   white, and one word in black below.
10. Grain.

The title's letters look cut with a knife from black paper: slightly uneven strokes,
blunt corners, small nicks. A plain heavy condensed face plus warping and wear gets close.

## 6. Imagery

One portrait photograph, frontal, lit from the front left, with a large round hairstyle
(or a hat, a halo of flowers, a headwrap: any big simple mass above the face). Treatment:
**T-duotone** with three stops that end in the ground color, so highlights vanish into the
sheet and the figure looks screen-printed: `#b01f12` at 0, `#e2401c` at 0.35, `#f08a1a` at
0.65, `#f6ec18` at 1. Raise the contrast first (`adjust levels --in-black 25 --in-white
215`). The green zone is a second ink: a green layer faded in from 76 to 90% H, set to
`hue` blend and clipped to the portrait. If the subject has no large hair, paint the title
panel: a lumpy red disc from `splatter.js` (few drops, no arms) behind the head, as the
recipe does for the stand-in.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-duotone** (three stops ending in the ground color) on the cutout.
* Ink change: `layer add`, `fill gradient` with green from alpha 0 to 1, `layer blend
  --mode hue`, `layer clip`.
* **T-splatter** as a painted disc (`radius=0.44`, `drops=25`, `arms=0`) for the hair mass.
* Warped type: each title line is set flat and pushed into a four-point shape with
  `transform perspective --quad` (the corners are top-left, top-right, bottom-right,
  bottom-left). A true flag or arc warp needs `transform warp` with a points file.
* **T-distress-type**, shared: one grunge layer in the ground yellow above everything
  except the small type, 45%.
* **T-label-box** for the sticker strips.
* **T-paper-grain**, amount 14, overlay 30%.
* **T-warp-type** in [techniques.md](../techniques.md) is the tested library version of the growing, rising, or wedge-warped headline; start from it when reusing this idea.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The red disc of hair on yellow (largest contrast of color), then immediately the black
   title inside it.
2. The wedge of title line 2 points down-right to the face and the eyes.
3. The author's name beside the cheek.
4. Up to the sticker, then the quotes.

Cap heights: title 14 to 17 : author 5 : quote word 1.9 : sources 1.4, about 10 : 3.3 :
1.3 : 1.

## 9. Build recipe

`subject.png` is your image here: a frontal head-and-shoulders cutout. With real big
hair, delete the Hair layer and scale the cutout until the hair spans 84% W.

```bash
pixelox render js scripts/splatter.js -o hair.png --size 960x960 --seed 6 --param color='#dc2113' --param radius=0.44 --param drops=25 --param reach=1.08 --param arms=0
pixelox render js scripts/grunge.js -o wear.png --size 1080x1620 --seed 14 --param color='#f6ec18' --param specks=6000 --param blotches=18 --param scratches=50 --param clump=0.5
pixelox new -o cover.pxo --size 1080x1620 --fill '#f6ec18'
pixelox comp place cover.pxo hair.png --in-place --as Hair --at 51%,49% --anchor c
pixelox comp place cover.pxo subject.png --in-place --as Portrait --size x920 --at 56%,101% --anchor b
pixelox adjust bw cover.pxo --in-place --layer Portrait
pixelox adjust levels cover.pxo --in-place --layer Portrait --in-black 25 --in-white 215
pixelox adjust gradient-map cover.pxo --in-place --layer Portrait --stops '#b01f12@0,#e2401c@0.35,#f08a1a@0.65,#f6ec18@1'
pixelox layer add cover.pxo --in-place --name GreenInk
pixelox fill gradient cover.pxo --in-place --layer GreenInk --from 0,76% --to 0,90% --stops '#2f8f3a00@0,#2f8f3aff@1'
pixelox layer blend cover.pxo --in-place --layer GreenInk --mode hue
pixelox layer clip cover.pxo --in-place --layer GreenInk
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Title1 --content "LOUD" --font Impact --size 16% --color '#1a1b1a'
pixelox transform perspective cover.pxo --in-place --layer Title1 --quad '235,544 694,492 718,694 229,740'
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Title2 --content "TIDE" --font Impact --size 16% --color '#1a1b1a'
pixelox transform perspective cover.pxo --in-place --layer Title2 --quad '155,752 718,700 724,822 155,983'
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Author --content $'AUTHOR\nNAME' --font Impact --size 6.2% --tracking 20 --leading 110% --color '#1a1b1a' --at 18%,67% --anchor l
pixelox transform rotate cover.pxo --in-place --layer Author --degrees -6
pixelox comp place cover.pxo wear.png --in-place --as Wear --fit cover
pixelox layer opacity cover.pxo --in-place --layer Wear --value 45
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Quote1 --content "'LUMINOUS.'" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 800 --size 2.2% --tracking 20 --color '#d21a1a' --at 5.7%,4.9% --anchor l
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Source1 --content $'FIRST REVIEWER\nNAME' --font "Avenir Next" --weight 500 --size 1.65% --tracking 40 --leading 140% --color '#d21a1a' --at 6.9%,8.6% --anchor l
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Quote2 --content "'ELECTRIC.'" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 800 --size 2.2% --tracking 20 --color '#d21a1a' --at 5.7%,13.6% --anchor l
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Source2 --content $'SECOND REVIEWER\nNAME' --font "Avenir Next" --weight 500 --size 1.65% --tracking 40 --leading 140% --color '#d21a1a' --at 6.9%,17.3% --anchor l
pixelox shape ellipse cover.pxo --in-place --name Sticker --rect 774,29,266x266 --fill '#c3d421'
pixelox shape rect cover.pxo --in-place --name Strip --rect 842,62,132x152 --fill '#ffffff'
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Prize --content $'Readers\nPrize for\nFiction' --font "Gill Sans" --weight 600 --size 2% --leading 118% --color '#1a1b1a' --at 848,118 --anchor l
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Year --content "2031" --font "Gill Sans" --weight 400 --size 2% --color '#a9b81c' --at 848,194 --anchor l
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Status --content "Shortlisted" --font "Gill Sans" --weight 400 --size 2.5% --color '#1a1b1a' --at 907,244 --anchor c
pixelox layer add cover.pxo --in-place --name Grain --fill '#808080'
pixelox filter add-noise cover.pxo --in-place --layer Grain --amount 14 --monochrome
pixelox layer blend cover.pxo --in-place --layer Grain --mode overlay
pixelox layer opacity cover.pxo --in-place --layer Grain --value 30
pixelox export cover.pxo -o cover.png
```

Change first: the two title words (3 to 5 letters each) and their quads: keep the left
edge of line 2 about 1.7 times as tall as its right edge. Then the three inks.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Other triads on a flat ground: cyan sheet with navy and magenta inks turning violet;
  cream sheet with black and rust inks turning teal; pink sheet with purple and orange.
  Always: last gradient-map stop = the sheet color.
* Title in other "ready-made panels": inside a hat, a coat, an umbrella, a moon, a speech
  balloon held by the subject.
* Gig poster or festival flyer: replace quotes with a date and venue block, the sticker
  with a ticket-price burst (T-price-burst).
* Square album cover: crop to 24 to 80% H; drop quotes and sticker.
* Pairs with T-halftone-portrait (coarse dots instead of flat inks), T-grunge, and
  T-arc-type (a subtitle on an arc along the top of the hair).

## 11. Pitfalls

* Ending the gradient map in white instead of the sheet color: the face gets a white mask
  and the screen-print illusion is gone.
* Title set straight and flat on the hair: it looks stuck on. Tilt and taper each line so
  its edges run roughly parallel to the outline of the mass it sits in.
* Title over the face. Keep it in the hair, ending at the hairline, with only the author
  allowed to touch the cheek.
* The green shift as a hard horizontal cut. Blend it over at least 10% H, and clip it to
  the portrait so the yellow ground stays pure.
* A second black area (black jacket, black sticker): black belongs to the lettering alone.
