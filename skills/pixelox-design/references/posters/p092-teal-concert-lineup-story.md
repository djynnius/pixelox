# p092: Teal concert story with artist line-up

## 1. Concept

A gospel-concert announcement in story format: the headlining singer, arm raised, stands
in a pool of cyan stage light at the top; five guest artists form a shoulder-to-shoulder
row beneath him; their bodies fade into navy, where an elegant serif title lockup and two
lines of condensed names sit. It works because it stacks the three questions of a concert
poster in reading order (what event, who, where) and gives each its own typeface, while a
strict two-color scheme (teal and navy, with peach-cream type) holds six different
photographs together.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait 9:16, center axis at 50% W. Text margins 6% W; the two event-name lines are
  justified to 6 to 95.5% W.
* Zones: presenter line 3% H; event name 5.5 to 13.2% H; lead artist 10 to 45% H; side
  tiles (date at the left, scan code at the right) 19 to 34% H; guest row, heads at 36 to
  46% H, bodies fading out by 63% H; title lockup 54.5 to 73% H; line-up 75 to 84% H;
  venue plate, address, and ticket line 85 to 96% H.
* Shown as a phone screen capture that crops the sides of a wider flyer: the date tile,
  the scan tile, the second line of names, and the ticket line run off the left and right
  edges. Positions below pull the two tiles inside the margins (4 to 19% W and 81 to
  96% W).

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: navy-black `#060a1c` (bottom 40% of the canvas is this color, flat).
2. Stage glow: a cyan pool `#07adbd` centered at 50% W, 23% H, falling to teal `#085d6f`
   at about 40% W from the center and to the ground by 62% H; the top strip (0 to 4% H) is
   dark teal `#022437`.
3. Light beams: thin vertical shafts and dotted LED columns in pale cyan, 20 to 30%
   opacity, between 15 and 45% H on both sides of the lead artist.
4. Presenter line: gray-white, centered, 3% H.
5. Event name line 1 (a word and a number): peach-cream `#f2d5c4`, x 6.3 to 95.5% W, cap
   top 5.5% H, cap height 3.5% H. Line 2 (two words): pale ice `#e6f3f5`, x 5.7 to 95.8% W,
   cap top 10.3% H, cap height 3% H. Both in an extra-wide heavy face.
6. Date tile: navy `#081220` rectangle, 15% W x 13.5% H, top at 19.5% H, at the left edge;
   inside, centered: weekday and month (small, white), the day with its ordinal (large,
   peach), the time (white).
7. Scan tile: white rounded square, 15% W, top at 19.5% H, at the right edge, holding a
   scan code; a 2-word white caption under it at 29% H.
8. Lead artist cutout: a bearded man in a dark tee looking up and to the left, right
   arm raised with one finger pointing at the sky (hand at 5 to 15% W, 10 to 18% H); head
   at 42 to 70% W, 16.5 to 33% H; chest down to 45% H, behind the guest row.
9. Guest row: five waist-up cutouts side by side, overlapping at the shoulders, centers at
   about 10, 29, 50, 72, 91% W; the middle one is 8% larger and in front. Clothing light
   and bright (white shirt, gray dress, pink jacket, white suit with red tie, navy suit).
10. Fade: ground color from transparent at 52% H to solid at 63% H.
11. Title lockup, centered: a thin script article ("The") at 54.5 to 59.5% H; serif
    capitals line 1 (5 letters), x 24.5 to 78.5% W, cap top 60% H; line 2 (6 letters), x 18
    to 84.5% W, cap top 67.4% H; cap height 5.5% H each; cream `#f1e6dc`. A tiny script
    "of" sits inside the counter of the round letter in line 2.
12. "FEATURING": small, very wide-tracked, 75.3% H.
13. Names: two lines of tall condensed capitals in peach `#f0d2c3`, three names a line,
    separated by small white brush-drawn crosses; centers at 78.3 and 82.2% H; line 2
    is wider than the frame.
14. Venue plate: a 2 px gray-blue outline pill, `12%,85.8%,77.5% x 4%`, with a round gold
    emblem (11% W) overlapping its left end and the venue name inside.
15. Address at 91.8% H; ticket line at 94.8% H (white lead-in, cyan web addresses).

Cool analogous ground (navy to cyan) with a warm, pale accent. The peach is close to the
artists' skin tones, which is why the type and the people look like they belong together.

script for the show's title, condensed gothic for the names, a plain sans for logistics.
That is the upper limit; it holds only because each voice keeps to its own band.

## 6. Imagery

Six portrait cutouts from different shoots. The lead artist is the largest (head 17% H
tall) and the only one with a gesture; the five guests are waist-up publicity shots, all
smiling toward the camera, scaled so their heads are the same size (10% H) and lined up on
one eye line at 41% H. Each guest overlaps the next by about a fifth of its width, outer
ones behind, the center one in front. Everyone is graded slightly cool and contrasty so
the teal light seems to fall on them; no outlines or drop shadows. A round metallic
emblem (the host's seal) anchors the venue plate. The scan code is a real QR image on a
white tile; the recipe draws a checker as a stand-in.

## 7. Effects and textures

* Stage glow: one radial gradient layer, cyan to teal to transparent, centered behind the
  lead artist's head (the dark-ground form of T-spotlight, built here with `fill gradient`).
* **T-sunburst**, light-shaft variant: `rays.js` with the source above the canvas
  (`cy=-0.15`, `spread=80`, `uneven=0.8`, `count=44`) in pale cyan, screen mode.
* **T-photo-fade**: ground navy, 52 to 63% H, over all six cutouts; the title lockup
  starts inside the ramp so its article overlaps the guests' chests.
* **T-label-box** for the date tile; **T-rules** for the outlined venue pill.
* **T-script-overlap**, miniature: two script words (the article above, the connective
  inside a letter) soften the serif lockup; neither is rotated or colored.
* Extended event type: `transform scale` with an unequal size stretches Arial Black to
  145% width (this rasterizes the layer, warning PXW2003).
* **T-stretch-type** in [techniques.md](../techniques.md) is the tested library version of the stretched, slanted, or fattened lettering; start from it when reusing this idea.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The lead artist's face in the brightest part of the glow, then up his arm.
2. The event name at the top, where the finger points.
3. Down through the row of guest faces to the serif title lockup.
4. The names, then the venue plate; date tile and scan tile are found on the way.

Cap-height ratio: title lockup 5.5 : event name 3.5 and 3 : day 3.3 : names 2.4 :
presenter 1.3 : small print 1.0 to 1.2. The steps are small (about 1.5x each); what
separates the levels here is typeface and color, not size.

## 9. Build recipe

`subject.png` (lead artist), `subject2.png` (a guest artist), and `logo.png` (a round
emblem) are your images here. The recipe alternates the two cutouts across the guest row
to show the layout: place five different guest cutouts in their place, and your own scan
code image instead of `code.png`.

```bash
pixelox render js scripts/rays.js -o beams.png --size 1080x1920 --param color='#9af0ff' --param cx=0.5 --param cy=-0.15 --param count=44 --param spread=80 --param uneven=0.8 --param alpha=0.3 --param fade=0.8
pixelox render js scripts/pattern.js -o code.png --size 130x130 --param kind=checker --param color='#0a0f1c' --param cell=13
pixelox new -o concert.pxo --size 1080x1920 --fill '#060a1c'
pixelox layer add concert.pxo --in-place --name Glow
pixelox fill gradient concert.pxo --in-place --layer Glow --kind radial --from 50%,23% --to 50%,62% --stops '#08b2c6ff@0,#0a7f93e6@0.35,#07405acc@0.62,#060a1c00@1'
pixelox comp place concert.pxo beams.png --in-place --as Beams --fit cover
pixelox layer blend concert.pxo --in-place --layer Beams --mode screen
pixelox text add concert.pxo --in-place --name Presenter --content "Your Name and Friends presents" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 500 --size 1.8% --color '#b9c6c9' --at 50%,3% --anchor c
pixelox text add concert.pxo --in-place --name Title1 --content "JUBILEE 75" --font "Arial Black" --size 4.6% --color '#f2d5c4' --at 50%,7.25% --anchor c
pixelox transform scale concert.pxo --in-place --layer Title1 --size 963x67
pixelox text add concert.pxo --in-place --name Title2 --content "PRAISE CONCERT" --font "Arial Black" --size 4% --color '#e6f3f5' --at 50%,11.7% --anchor c
pixelox transform scale concert.pxo --in-place --layer Title2 --size 973x58

pixelox text add concert.pxo --in-place --name Hour --content "6PM" --font "Arial Black" --size 1.9% --color '#ffffff' --at 11.5%,31% --anchor c
```

Change first: the two event lines (keep each between 10 and 14 characters so the stretch
stays near 145%), then the two title words. After changing the second title word, move the
`Of` layer onto the center of its round letter (read the build, then set `--at`). Size
every guest cutout so the heads match (`--size x600` assumes the same framing for all).

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Other light colors on the same navy: violet `#8a4dff` with a magenta rim; amber `#ffb347`
  with a deep red rim (then make the accent type pale blue instead of peach).
* Three guests instead of five: centers at 22, 50, 78% W, each 15% larger.
* No lead artist: enlarge the guest row (heads 13% H), move it up to 20 to 50% H, and let
  the glow sit behind the middle guest.
* Feed 4:5 version: drop the address and ticket lines, keep one line of names.
* Tours, award nights, comedy line-ups, conference speaker panels, sports fixtures.
* Pairs with T-bokeh in the glow, T-lens-flare at the pointing hand, T-color-grade (cold
  variant) over all cutouts, and T-gradient-type (peach to cream) on the names.

## 11. Pitfalls

* Guest cutouts with different head sizes or eye lines make the row look like a ransom
  note. Scale by head height, not by image height, and align the eyes.
* Six photos in their original colors on a teal ground look pasted. Give all of them the
  same cool grade and let the fade take their lower edges.
* The glow centered on the canvas instead of behind the lead's head: the head loses its
  halo and the top text sits on the brightest area. Keep the center at 23% H.
* A fifth typeface, or names set in the title's serif: the bands blur. One face per band.
* Names in pure white compete with the title lockup. Keep them in the peach accent and
  one step smaller than the title.
* Stretching type by more than 150% distorts the round letters. For a wider line, add
  letters or tracking instead.
