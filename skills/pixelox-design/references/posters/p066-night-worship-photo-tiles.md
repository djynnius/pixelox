# p066: Black and amber night flyer with photo tiles

## 1. Concept

An evening worship-concert flyer on black: three upright grayscale photo tiles stand side
by side like church windows, two performers in black and white are cut out back to back in
front of them, two white doves fly across the tile edges, and below, a white serif word
with a gold script word under it gives the title. It works because color is rationed: the
pictures are all gray, so the amber glow at the top, the gold script, the amber date, and
the amber footer band are the only color and tie the sheet together from top to bottom.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait 3:4. Margins 10% W for the tiles, 13.5% W for the text. Center axis at 50% W.
* Three tile columns: 10 to 35.5%, 37.3 to 62.7%, 64.5 to 90% W (each 25.5% W, gaps 1.8% W).
  The center tile starts higher (12.5% H) than the side tiles (17.8% H).
* Zones: logo block 1.5 to 9.5% H; picture zone 12.5 to 55% H, fading to black from 43% H;
  title 55 to 76% H; details 80 to 93% H in two columns (13.4% W and 61.5% W); footer band
  95.5 to 100% H.
* Symmetry is strict in the top half (logo, pills, tiles, the two names at 18% and 81.5% W)
  and relaxed in the details, which are left-hung in two columns.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: near-black `#090707`. Behind everything, barely visible, a dark photo of a
   church nave with pointed arches (10 to 15% opacity).
2. Top glow: amber `#b64f03` at the top edge (strongest in the two corners), through brown
   `#744611` at 11% H, gone by 24% H. A small red-amber glow also sits in the bottom-left
   corner at 85 to 95% H.
3. Side tiles: grayscale photos, `10%,17.8%,25.5% x 38%` and `64.5%,17.8%,25.5% x 38%`
   (a worshipper raising a book; a dim interior with flowers), mid to dark gray.
4. Center tile: `37.3%,12.5%,25.4% x 43.5%`, a bright cloudy sky, nearly white at the top:
   the light source of the picture, directly behind the two heads.
5. Performers: two cutouts in black and white, back to back on the axis, heads at 24.5 to
   40% H; a man in sunglasses with a guitar neck rising at 25% W, a woman singing into a
   hand-held microphone toward the right. Together they span 23 to 78% W and cover the
   inner edges of all three tiles. A warm brown haze `#82593c` at low opacity sits between
   them and the tiles at 33 to 50% H.
6. Fade: ground color from transparent at 43% H to solid at 56.5% H, over tiles and bodies.
7. Doves: two white birds with spread wings, natural photo cutouts. One at 19 to 40% W,
   10.5 to 24% H, crossing the top-left corner of the left tile; one at 79.5 to 95.5% W,
   23 to 33% H, crossing the right edge of the right tile.
8. Logo: an amber disc with a small building mark, 6% W across, at 50% W, 3.7% H; brand
   name in white bold caps under it at 8.3% H.
9. Pills: two amber outlines, `10%,6.2%,12% x 3%` and `78%,6.2%,12% x 3%`, fully rounded,
   each holding four tiny amber sparkles in a row.
10. Sparkles: four-point stars; white at 72% W, 2.5% H and at 86% W, 74.5% H; amber at
    7.5% W, 28.5% H; six tiny amber ones in two columns at 8.3% and 91.5% W beside the
    details (82.5, 86.7, 91% H).
11. Performer names: white, two lines each, centered at 18% W and 81.5% W, 48 to 52.5% H.
12. Headline: one 5-letter word, white serif capitals, x 15 to 86% W, cap top 55.2% H,
    baseline 67.1% H (cap height 11.8% H).
13. Script word: gold `#e3a54c`, x 26.4 to 73.6% W, 65 to 76% H, its top loops overlapping
    the feet of the headline's middle letters.
14. Details left: date in amber at 82.6% H; two white lines at 87.8 and 91% H.
15. Details right: a short amber rule (61.5 to 67.5% W, 80.7% H) over a 5-line white blurb.
16. Footer band: `0,95.5%,100% x 4.5%`, amber `#f8a119`, slightly redder `#fc881c` at the
    left end, with a dark web address centered in it.

Three voices: a didone for the solemn word, a formal script for the warm word, and one
geometric sans for all facts. The date is the only fact in color and in bold.

## 6. Imagery

Six pictures, all but the doves in black and white. Three background photos cropped to
tall tiles (a worshipper, a bright sky, a dim interior): choose one light picture for the
center and two darker ones for the sides. Two performer cutouts, waist up, posed back to
back so each faces outward; high contrast, faces partly in shadow. Two doves in flight,
kept pure white, the brightest things on the sheet after the headline. In the build one
landscape photo is shared by the two side tiles, a second photo fills the center tile, and
one bird image is placed twice at different sizes; a dark illustration is filled white so
it reads as a silhouette. The fade (T-photo-fade) removes the lower edge of every picture
at once, so no cutout needs a clean bottom.

## 7. Effects and textures

* Tiles: a photo placed over a rectangle and clipped to it (`layer clip`), then
  `adjust bw`. One path with two sub-rectangles makes both side tiles.
* **T-photo-fade**: ground color, 43 to 56.5% H, above tiles and performers, below the
  names and the headline. It is the gradient-layer form, not a mask, because one layer
  fades all five pictures at once.
* Top glow: a linear gradient layer, amber with 95% alpha at the top edge, brown with 60%
  at 10% H, transparent at 24% H.
* **T-fit-width** for the headline (71% W); **T-script-overlap** in its centered, upright
  form: the script is 0.68 of the headline's width, not rotated, placed so its ascenders
  cross the headline's baseline.
* **T-rules**: the short amber rule above the blurb; the pill outlines.
* Sparkles are `shape star` with 4 points and a thin waist (inner radius a quarter of the
  outer).
* **T-shape-crop** in [techniques.md](../techniques.md) is the tested library version of the photo shown through a drawn shape (arch, panel, tiles, or letter); start from it when reusing this idea.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The headline (white, cap height 11.8% H, the largest light shape).
2. Up to the two faces against the bright center tile, then out along the doves.
3. Back down through the gold script to the amber date.
4. Hours and place, the blurb, the footer address; logo and pills last.

Cap-height ratio: headline 11.8 : date 2.7 : names and brand 1.6 : details 1.35 : blurb
1.0, about 12 : 2.7 : 1.6 : 1.35 : 1.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Accent swaps on the same black: ice blue `#7fc8ff` with a navy glow `#0b2f6b` (a winter
  or night-vigil version); rose `#ff8fa3` with wine `#6b0f2a`; emerald `#3ddc97` with deep
  green `#0b3d2e`. Keep the photos gray.
* One performer: a single cutout on the axis in front of the center tile; move both names
  into one line under it.
* Five narrow tiles (each 14% W) for a choir or band line-up, stepped in height like organ
  pipes, tallest in the middle.
* Jazz night, gospel concert, candlelight carols, gala dinner, memorial service. A 4:5
  version drops the blurb and puts the date and place on one centered line.
* Pairs with T-bokeh behind the tiles, T-light-leak instead of the linear glow,
  T-paper-grain (amount 12) over everything, and T-frame-border as a thin gold
  keyline when there are no doves to break the edges.

## 11. Pitfalls

* Leaving the photos in color: skin tones and stage light fight the amber, and the three
  tiles stop reading as one set. Convert every photo, then let the accent be the only hue.
* Three tiles of the same brightness: the faces need the light tile directly behind them.
  Light center, dark sides.
* Performers that end in a hard horizontal cut. The fade must reach solid black above the
  headline's cap line (56% H) and start below the chins.
* The script placed fully below the headline reads as a caption. Let its tallest strokes
  overlap the headline's feet by 2% H, and keep it narrower than the headline.
* Doves (or any ornament) sitting neatly inside a tile look pasted. Each one must cross a
  tile edge onto the black.
* Too many sparkles. The original uses about a dozen, most of them tiny; three large ones
  are the limit.
