# p104: Inflated yellow shapes club-night poster

## 1. Concept

A club-night poster made of six glossy, balloon-like yellow shapes (a hook, a four-lobed
splat, a squiggle, an S-curve, a teardrop, and an eye with lashes) floating on black with
violet smoke, and a two-line white headline low on the sheet. It works because of one
hard complementary contrast, yellow against violet, and one soft trick: every shape looks
inflated, so a flat doodle vocabulary gains weight and light. The eye in the middle gives
the abstract shapes a face to gather around. In the source it was shown as a sheet
floating on black with a violet glow around it; that surround is not part of the design.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait A-series (1 : 1.414). Small text keeps 6.5% W margins left and right; the
  yellow shapes ignore the margins and bleed off all four edges.
* Three horizontal zones on a center axis: information 4 to 29% H, the eye 32 to 58% H,
  the headline 69.5 to 91.5% H.
* The top zone is a small three-part layout: two centered lines across the top (5.8% and
  9.3% H), then a left-aligned stack of four words at 6.5% W and a right-aligned stack of
  three date lines ending at 94% W, both from 11 to 29% H.
* The headline block is 58.5% W wide (21 to 79.5% W), both lines fitted to that width, so
  it is centered although each line is set from the left.
* One shape per corner, and each one passes behind a text block: hook behind the word
  stack, splat behind the date, S-curve behind the headline, squiggle beside it. Text and
  shapes interlock; nothing sits on empty ground alone except the eye.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: near-black `#030206` with large soft clouds of violet (`#0b0722` rising to
   `#4a36b0`), covering about half the area; the upper middle and the lower left are the
   brightest.
2. Light points: about 40 small soft violet dots (`#8a7be0`, `#b0a4ff`), 1.5 to 4.5% W,
   scattered, screen mode.
3. Hook: a yellow tube 10.5% W thick that enters from the top edge at 12% W, swings down
   to 23% H and up again to a round end at 42% W, 16% H; a short capsule of the same
   thickness hangs from the top edge at 28% W down to 9% H.
4. Squiggle: the same tube on the left edge, x 0 to 17% W, from 55 to 94% H, three bends.
5. S-curve: a thicker tube (16% W) entering from the right edge at 67% H, turning at 73% W
   and leaving through the bottom edge at 46% W.
6. Splat: a filled four-lobed blob, box `66%,0%,34% x 28%`, cut by the top and right edges.
   Teardrop: a filled drop, `82%,32%,6% x 10%`.
7. Eye: a lens-shaped outline, tube 4.8% W thick, box `23%,36%,52% x 22%`, with four
   lashes (short capsules, 4 to 5% H long) fanning from its upper edge.
8. All yellow parts: base `#f6d21f`, with a soft bevel that lights the upper left of every
   tube in pale yellow `#fffbd0` and shades the lower right in ochre `#a86e00`.
9. Pupil: a glossy sphere, diameter 20% W, center 49% W, 47% H, light gray `#e6e6e6` in the
   middle to near-black at the rim.
10. Presenter line and web address, centered, at 5.8% and 9.3% H.
11. Word stack (left) and date stack (right), white with a hard black shadow 3 px down-right.
12. Headline, two lines, white, hard black shadow 6 px down-right, in front of the S-curve.

## 4. Palette

| Hex | Role | Area |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `#030206` | ground | 30% |
| `#3a2a8a` | violet smoke (from `#0b0722` to `#4a36b0`), light points | 35% |
| `#f6d21f` | all shapes; highlight `#fffbd0`, shade `#a86e00` | 25% |
| `#ffffff` | all type | 8% |
| `#5a5a5a` | pupil (gray sphere) | 2% |

A complementary pair, yellow and violet, on black. The violet is kept dark (lightness
under 45%) and blurred; the yellow is light, saturated, and sharp. White appears only as
type, and the one neutral gray object, the pupil, becomes the focal point because it is the
only thing that is neither yellow nor violet.

## 5. Text elements

| Role | Content pattern | Case | Font class (look-alike / local) | Weight | Cap height | Tracking | Leading | Align | Color | Position |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Presenter | `[VENUE]` + "PRESENTS", 3 words | caps | neo-grotesque (Roboto / Helvetica Neue) | 700 | 1.6% | 800 | - | center | `#ffffff` | 50%, 5.8% |
| Web address | one URL | caps | same | 400 | 1.5% | 100 | - | center | `#9c94c4` | 50%, 9.3% |
| Word stack | 4-word tag line, one word per line, 2 to 5 letters | caps | same, light | 300 | 2.7% | 90 | 124% | left | `#ffffff` | 6.5%, top 12% |
| Date stack | "DD/", "MM/", "YYYY" on three lines | digits | same, light | 300 | 2.7% | 90 | 124% | right | `#ffffff` | 94%, top 11% |
| Headline 1 | 1 word, 5 letters | caps | humanist bold sans (Open Sans Bold, Noto Sans Bold / Helvetica Neue) | 700 | 9% | 0 | - | fitted, 58.5% W | `#ffffff` | 21%, top 69.5% |
| Headline 2 | 1 word, 5 letters | caps | same | 700 | 9% | 0 | - | fitted, 58.5% W | `#ffffff` | 21%, top 82.5% |

One sans family in three weights. The presenter line is tracked to 800 so that three small
words span 80% of the width; the stacks are light and thin so they can lie across the fat
yellow shapes without fighting them, and the hard offset shadow is what keeps thin white
letters readable on yellow.

## 6. Imagery

No photographs. The picture is built from thick round-capped strokes and two filled blobs,
all in one yellow, made to look inflated with a wide soft bevel. The vocabulary is
doodle-like on purpose: curl, splat, wave, drop, eye. The eye is the only figurative
element: lens outline, four lashes, and a gray sphere as the pupil with its highlight in
the middle. For other subjects keep the rule "one figurative sign in the middle, abstract
relatives in the corners": a mouth with drops, a hand with stars, a sun with rays.

## 7. Effects and textures

* Smoke ground: **T-smoke** reduced to one opaque `fill noise --as-layer` cloud layer with
  three stops (the ground color, `#0b0722`, `#4a36b0`; `--octaves 3`, `--warp 36`,
  `--bias=-20`). The dark middle stop keeps half the sheet black; without it the violet
  floods.
* Light points: **T-bokeh**, near layer only (`particles.js`, `shape=glow`, 38 dots),
  screen mode.
* Inflated shapes (no library technique): `shape path` with `--fill none`, a stroke of 52
  to 170 px, `--cap round --join round`; then `fx bevel --style inner --depth 160 --size 40
  --soften 10` with a pale yellow highlight and an ochre shadow, copied to the other shape
  layers with `fx copy`. Several open sub-paths in one `--d` give several tubes in one
  layer (they must share a stroke width).
* Pupil: `fx overlay --style radial` on a circle, light center to dark rim.
* **T-fit-width** on both headline lines; **T-block-shadow** without the stroke
  (`fx drop-shadow --blur 0 --blend normal --opacity 100`) on headline and stacks.
* **T-geo-shapes** thinking applies to placement: the shapes share two tube widths and
  touch or leave the edges; none floats free except the teardrop.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The eye (center, the only face-like sign, with the only gray).
2. The headline: largest type, white on the darkest and the yellowest areas at once.
3. Up the left edge: squiggle, word stack on the hook.
4. Across the top lines to the date stack on the splat.

Cap heights: headline 9 : stacks 2.7 : presenter 1.6, about 5.6 : 1.7 : 1. Yellow area to
type area is roughly 3 : 1; the shapes are the loud part, the type is the clear part.

## 9. Build recipe

No images needed. Path data is in pixels for a 1080 x 1528 canvas.

```bash
pixelox render js scripts/particles.js -o stars.png --size 1080x1528 --seed 3 --param shape=glow --param colors='#8a7be0,#6a55d8,#b0a4ff' --param count=38 --param min=1.5 --param max=4.5 --param amin=0.35 --param amax=0.9
pixelox new -o night.pxo --size 1080x1528 --fill '#030206'
pixelox fill noise night.pxo --in-place --as-layer --name Haze --seed 7 --scale 42% --octaves 3 --warp 36 --bias=-20 --contrast 10 --stops '#030206,#0b0722,#4a36b0'
pixelox comp place night.pxo stars.png --in-place --as Stars --fit cover
pixelox layer blend night.pxo --in-place --layer Stars --mode screen
pixelox shape path night.pxo --in-place --name Ribbons --d 'M 135 -60 C 80 210 290 390 455 245 M 300 -80 L 300 95 M 100 880 C 230 950 -50 1030 50 1120 C 140 1200 -20 1250 60 1330 C 90 1370 100 1390 115 1410' --fill none --stroke '#f6d21f' --stroke-width 112 --cap round --join round
pixelox shape path night.pxo --in-place --name BigS --d 'M 1140 1030 C 900 900 780 1010 880 1200 C 940 1330 700 1380 560 1420 C 480 1450 470 1500 500 1580' --fill none --stroke '#f6d21f' --stroke-width 170 --cap round --join round
pixelox shape path night.pxo --in-place --name Blobs --d 'M 720 110 C 760 40 860 60 900 130 C 930 60 980 -20 1100 -20 L 1100 420 C 1040 330 1000 300 960 310 C 950 420 870 470 800 400 C 740 340 800 260 840 230 C 760 230 690 180 720 110 Z M 918 495 C 890 560 880 640 918 645 C 956 640 946 560 918 495 Z' --fill '#f6d21f'
pixelox shape path night.pxo --in-place --name Eye --d 'M 250 718 Q 530 432 810 718 Q 530 1012 250 718 Z M 330 630 L 300 580 M 470 570 L 455 512 M 610 570 L 628 512 M 738 630 L 772 588' --fill none --stroke '#f6d21f' --stroke-width 52 --cap round --join round
pixelox fx bevel night.pxo --in-place --layer Ribbons --style inner --depth 160 --size 40 --soften 10 --angle 120 --altitude 45 --highlight '#fffbd0' --shadow '#a86e00' --opacity 80
pixelox fx copy night.pxo --in-place --from Ribbons --to BigS,Blobs,Eye
pixelox shape ellipse night.pxo --in-place --name Pupil --rect 422,606,216x216 --fill '#5a5a5a'
pixelox fx overlay night.pxo --in-place --layer Pupil --style radial --stops '#e6e6e6@0,#6a6a6a@0.45,#161616@1'
pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name Presenter --content "YOUR VENUE PRESENTS" --font @neo-grotesque --weight 700 --size 2.2% --tracking 800 --color '#ffffff' --at 51%,5.8% --anchor c
pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name Site --content "WWW.EXAMPLE.COM" --font @neo-grotesque --weight 400 --size 2.1% --tracking 100 --color '#9c94c4' --at 50%,9.3% --anchor c
pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name Tagline --content $'THE\nSHAPE\nOF\nNOISE' --font @neo-grotesque --weight 300 --size 3.75% --leading 124% --tracking 90 --color '#ffffff' --at 6.5%,12% --anchor tl --anchor-to ink
pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name Date --content $'21/\n06/\n2031' --font @neo-grotesque --weight 300 --size 3.75% --leading 124% --tracking 90 --align right --color '#ffffff' --at 94%,11% --anchor tr --anchor-to ink
pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name H1 --content "DISCO" --font @neo-grotesque --weight 700 --fit-width 632 --color '#ffffff' --at 21%,69.5% --anchor-to ink
pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name H2 --content "NIGHT" --font @neo-grotesque --weight 700 --fit-width 632 --color '#ffffff' --at 21%,82.5% --anchor-to ink
pixelox fx drop-shadow night.pxo --in-place --layer H1 --offset 6,6 --blur 0 --color '#000000' --blend normal --opacity 100
pixelox fx copy night.pxo --in-place --from H1 --to H2
pixelox fx drop-shadow night.pxo --in-place --layer Tagline --offset 3,3 --blur 0 --color '#000000' --blend normal --opacity 100
pixelox fx copy night.pxo --in-place --from Tagline --to Date,Presenter
pixelox export night.pxo -o night.png
```

Change first: the two headline words (4 to 6 letters each, so the fitted lines keep a
similar height), then the central sign (replace the `Eye` path and the pupil), then the
two colors (change `--seed` on the `fill noise` line if a bright cloud lands behind small
text). To redraw a tube, write its centerline as `M` plus one or two `C` curves; the
stroke width does the rest. Keep the bevel `--size` at a third to a half of the thinnest
stroke width.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Other complementary pairs: hot pink `#ff4fa3` shapes on deep green smoke (`#04140c` to
  `#0f6a48`); mint `#7dffc8` on wine (`#12030a` to `#8a1d4f`); orange `#ff8a1f` on navy.
  Change the bevel's highlight and shadow to a tint and a shade of the new shape color.
* Light version: ground `#f2efe8`, no smoke, shapes in two colors, type black, shadows off.
* Chrome shapes instead of candy: base `#b8bcc6`, highlight `#ffffff`, shadow `#30343c`,
  `--depth 300`.
* Suits club nights, festivals, kids' and family events (with a friendlier sign than an
  eye), psychedelic or retro-futurist themes, playlist covers (crop square around the eye).
* Pairs with T-arc-type (a line of text around the eye), T-paper-grain at low strength,
  and T-outline-type for a second headline line.

## 11. Pitfalls

* Flat yellow shapes with no bevel look like clip-art cut-outs. The soft, wide bevel
  (`--size 40 --soften 10`) is the style; a narrow hard bevel (size under 15) looks like a
  plastic button instead of a balloon.
* Too many shapes or more than two tube widths. Six shapes, two widths, one color; add a
  seventh only by removing one.
* Violet smoke that covers everything: yellow then has no black to sit on and the sheet
  goes flat purple. Keep the dark middle stop and a negative `--bias`, and check that about
  half the ground is black.
* Thin white type on yellow without the hard shadow is unreadable. Keep the 3 px offset
  shadow, or move the small text onto dark ground.
* Shapes that stop short of the edge. Each corner shape must run off the sheet; a shape
  that ends inside the margins looks like a sticker and shrinks the poster.
