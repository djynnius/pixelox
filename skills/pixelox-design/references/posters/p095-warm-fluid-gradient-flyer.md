# p095: Warm fluid gradient flyer with framed title

## 1. Concept

A summer music-event flyer with no picture at all: the ground is a stack of flowing,
overlapping color bands that run from orange at the top through red and pink to a violet
corner, and all the type is white, with the two-word title held in a thin white keyline
frame in the middle. It works because the background does the emotional work (heat,
movement) while the type stays cool and orderly: one color, one family, one central column.
The frame is what turns a loose pile of text lines into a poster.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait, close to US-letter proportion (1 : 1.29); build it as 4:5.
* One central column, 57% W wide, from 21.5% to 78.5% W. Its left edge carries the day
  number, the month, the tag line, the frame, and the body rule; its right edge carries the
  frame and the end of the weekday block. Side margins are 21.5% W each.
* The side margins are not empty: they are decoration gutters. Left gutter: a small dot grid
  (top) and one line of sideways text at 5% W. Right gutter: a larger dot grid crossing the
  frame's corner and a thin vertical rule at 95% W.
* Vertical zones: date band 8.5 to 24% H; frame 32.5 to 67.5% H (35% H tall, so the frame
  is 1.3 : 1); body 72.5 to 84% H; web address at 95% H.
* Gaps: 8.5% H between date band and frame, 5% H between frame and body. Inside the frame
  the four lines are centered on 50% W and spaced evenly: 38%, 42 to 47.5%, 51 to 56.5%,
  61.5% H.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Base gradient, full canvas, diagonal from the top right to the bottom left: orange
   `#fba31a`, `#f68a22` at 30%, red-orange `#f0603a` at 50%, pink `#e8406a` at 70%,
   magenta `#c9408a` at 85%, blue-violet `#5c53a2` at the end.
2. Fluid bands: 6 to 8 large shapes with smooth S-curved edges, each a flat step along the
   same color run and each 70% opaque, so the base gradient shades them from inside. Their
   edges run diagonally (about 30 degrees off horizontal), and one or two edges cross the
   frame. No outlines, no shadows.
3. Violet corner: a soft radial patch of `#4a55a7` in the bottom left, radius 46% W.
4. Dot grids, white, dot 0.5% W, pitch 2.6% W: top left `5.5%,8%,8% x 23%` (3 columns),
   right `70%,24%,23% x 14.5%` (9 columns, overlapping the frame's top right corner),
   bottom `29.5%,91.5%,28.5% x 6%` (11 columns).
5. Day number at the column's left edge, top at 8.5% H; month under it at 18.8% H; a small
   tag line at 23.4% H.
6. Thin horizontal rule from 42.5 to 62% W at 12% H, joining the number to the weekday.
7. Weekday (12.2% H) and opening hours (15.4% H), left-aligned at 65% W.
8. Frame: white keyline `21.5%,32.5%,57% x 35%`, stroke 0.45% W, no fill.
9. Inside the frame, centered: kicker, title line 1, title line 2, kind of event.
10. Body rule: vertical, at 21.5% W from 72.5 to 89.5% H. Body text, five lines, indented
    to 24.7% W, top at 72.5% H. A "MORE INFO" label at 83.2% H followed by three small
    round outline icons (social links), 2.4% W each.
11. Sideways line: lowercase, widely tracked, rotated -90, at 5% W from 76.5% up to 44% H.
12. Right rule: vertical, at 95% W from 49 to 81% H.
13. Web address, right-aligned to 94% W at 95% H.

cool accent in one corner; it makes the orange look hotter.

One family, three weights. The contrast is between bold and tight (title, month) and

are a third of its cap height and five times its letter spacing, so they read as a frame
inside the frame.

## 6. Imagery

None. The background is the image: a diagonal gradient under translucent wave bands
(**T-waves** in fill mode, rendered on a 2000 px square and rotated 32 degrees so the band
edges run diagonally and the square still covers the canvas). There are no photographs
or logos. The only icons are three small round glyphs after the "MORE INFO" label, drawn
with `shape icon` (`globe`, `message-circle`, `at-sign`: neutral stand-ins, because brand
logos are not in the icon set).

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-waves**: `count=8`, `amp=0.11`, `freq=0.9`, `chaos=0.9`, `alpha=0.7`, eight colors
  listed in the order of the base gradient so every band is one step further along the
  run. `top=0.3` and `bottom=0.84` keep all eight edges inside the visible area after
  rotation.
* Base gradient with `fill gradient` from `85%,0` to `15%,100%`; because the bands are 70%
  opaque, it shows through and gives each band a soft inner gradient.
* Corner: radial `fill gradient` from `0,100%`, alpha `f2` to `00`.
* **T-pattern**: three small dot renders (`cell=28`, `weight=0.17`), each sized to an odd
  number of cells so no dot is cut at the edge.
* **T-frame-border**, keyline variant: 5 px stroke, no fill.
* **T-rules**: one short horizontal connector, two vertical rules. **T-vertical-type** for
  the sideways line.
* **T-icon-row**, contact variant: three 26 px `shape icon` glyphs in white after the
  "MORE INFO" label.
* No grain and no shadows. Add T-paper-grain at low strength only if the gradient bands.
* **T-fluid-ribbons** in [techniques.md](../techniques.md) is the tested library version of the flowing gradient ribbons (`scripts/ribbons.js` draws them directly); start from it when reusing this idea.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The framed title: largest bold type, dead center, boxed.
2. The day number, top left: taller than the title (7.5% against 5.5%) but in regular
   weight, so it reads second.
3. Along the thin rule to the weekday and hours.
4. Down through the frame to the body text; web address last.

Cap heights: number 7.5 : title 5.5 : month 2.6 : weekday 2.1 : kicker 1.6 : body 1.1, about
7 : 5 : 2.4 : 1.9 : 1.5 : 1. Two display sizes that differ in weight, then a clear 2x drop.

## 9. Build recipe

No images needed. Everything is generated.

```bash
pixelox render js scripts/waves.js -o flow.png --size 2000x2000 --seed 9 --param colors='#f7922a,#f47a2a,#f05e36,#ec4a55,#e8406a,#e03c75,#d6317a,#c9408a' --param count=8 --param top=0.3 --param bottom=0.84 --param amp=0.11 --param freq=0.9 --param chaos=0.9 --param alpha=0.7
pixelox render js scripts/pattern.js -o dots1.png --size 84x308 --param kind=dots --param color='#ffffff' --param cell=28 --param weight=0.17
pixelox render js scripts/pattern.js -o dots2.png --size 252x196 --param kind=dots --param color='#ffffff' --param cell=28 --param weight=0.17
pixelox render js scripts/pattern.js -o dots3.png --size 308x84 --param kind=dots --param color='#ffffff' --param cell=28 --param weight=0.17
pixelox new -o flyer.pxo --size 1080x1350 --fill '#f99a1c'
pixelox layer add flyer.pxo --in-place --name Base
pixelox fill gradient flyer.pxo --in-place --layer Base --from 85%,0 --to 15%,100% --stops '#fba31a@0,#f68a22@0.3,#f0603a@0.5,#e8406a@0.7,#c9408a@0.85,#5c53a2@1'
pixelox comp place flyer.pxo flow.png --in-place --as Flow --size 2000x --at 50%,50% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate flyer.pxo --in-place --layer Flow --degrees 32
pixelox layer add flyer.pxo --in-place --name Corner
pixelox fill gradient flyer.pxo --in-place --layer Corner --kind radial --from 0,100% --to 46%,100% --stops '#4a55a7f2@0,#5c53a2b3@0.45,#5c53a200@1'
pixelox comp place flyer.pxo dots1.png --in-place --as DotsTL --size 84x --at 5.5%,8% --anchor tl
pixelox comp place flyer.pxo dots2.png --in-place --as DotsR --size 252x --at 70%,24% --anchor tl
pixelox comp place flyer.pxo dots3.png --in-place --as DotsB --size 308x --at 29.5%,91.5% --anchor tl
pixelox text add flyer.pxo --in-place --name Day --content "14" --font @geometric-sans --weight 400 --size 10.4% --color '#ffffff' --at 21.5%,8.5% --anchor tl --anchor-to ink
pixelox text add flyer.pxo --in-place --name Month --content "JUNE" --font @geometric-sans --weight 700 --size 3.6% --color '#ffffff' --at 21.5%,18.8% --anchor tl --anchor-to ink
pixelox text add flyer.pxo --in-place --name Tag --content "Open air on the river stage" --font @geometric-sans --weight 700 --size 1.6% --tracking 60 --color '#ffffff' --at 21.5%,23.4% --anchor l
pixelox shape line flyer.pxo --in-place --name RuleTop --from 42.5%,12% --to 62%,12% --stroke '#ffffff' --stroke-width 2
pixelox text add flyer.pxo --in-place --name Weekday --content "SATURDAY" --font @geometric-sans --weight 400 --size 2.9% --tracking 60 --color '#ffffff' --at 65%,12.2% --anchor l
pixelox text add flyer.pxo --in-place --name Hours --content "18.00 - 02.00" --font @geometric-sans --weight 600 --size 1.9% --color '#ffffff' --at 65%,15.4% --anchor l
pixelox shape rect flyer.pxo --in-place --name Frame --rect 21.5%,32.5%,57%x35% --fill none --stroke '#ffffff' --stroke-width 5
pixelox text add flyer.pxo --in-place --name Kicker --content "LIVE" --font @geometric-sans --weight 400 --size 2.2% --tracking 520 --color '#ffffff' --at 50%,38% --anchor c --anchor-to ink
pixelox text add flyer.pxo --in-place --name H1 --content "GOLDEN" --font @geometric-sans --weight 700 --size 7.6% --tracking 40 --color '#ffffff' --at 50%,42% --anchor t --anchor-to ink
pixelox text add flyer.pxo --in-place --name H2 --content "HOURS" --font @geometric-sans --weight 700 --size 7.6% --tracking 40 --color '#ffffff' --at 50%,51% --anchor t --anchor-to ink
pixelox text add flyer.pxo --in-place --name Kind --content "FESTIVAL" --font @geometric-sans --weight 400 --size 2.2% --tracking 520 --color '#ffffff' --at 50%,61.5% --anchor c --anchor-to ink
pixelox shape line flyer.pxo --in-place --name RuleBody --from 21.5%,72.5% --to 21.5%,89.5% --stroke '#ffffff' --stroke-width 3
pixelox text add flyer.pxo --in-place --name Body --content $'Six bands and four DJs across two stages, from the first\nwarm-up set at sunset to the last record after midnight.\nFood trucks, a lakeside bar, and free water all night.\nGates open one hour before the first act. Bring a jacket:\nit gets cool once the sun is down.' --font @geometric-sans --weight 600 --size 1.5% --leading 118% --color '#ffffff' --at 24.7%,72.2% --anchor tl
pixelox text add flyer.pxo --in-place --name More --content "MORE INFO :" --font @geometric-sans --weight 400 --size 1.5% --tracking 160 --color '#ffffff' --at 24.7%,83.2% --anchor l
pixelox shape icon flyer.pxo globe --in-place --name Icon1 --size 26 --at 41.5%,83.2% --anchor c --color '#ffffff'
pixelox shape icon flyer.pxo message-circle --in-place --name Icon2 --size 26 --at 44.9%,83.2% --anchor c --color '#ffffff'
pixelox shape icon flyer.pxo at-sign --in-place --name Icon3 --size 26 --at 48.3%,83.2% --anchor c --color '#ffffff'
pixelox text add flyer.pxo --in-place --name SideLine --content "sound from dusk to dawn" --font @geometric-sans --weight 600 --size 1.6% --tracking 300 --color '#ffffff' --at 50%,50% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate flyer.pxo --in-place --layer SideLine --degrees -90
pixelox transform move flyer.pxo --in-place --layer SideLine --to 5%,76.5% --anchor bl
pixelox shape line flyer.pxo --in-place --name RuleRight --from 95%,49% --to 95%,81% --stroke '#ffffff' --stroke-width 3
pixelox text add flyer.pxo --in-place --name Site --content "www.example.com" --font @geometric-sans --weight 600 --size 1.6% --tracking 160 --color '#ffffff' --at 94%,95.1% --anchor r
pixelox export flyer.pxo -o flyer.png
```

Change first: the two title words (5 to 7 letters each; the longer one must stay under 47%
W, which leaves 5% W of air inside the frame on both sides), then the eight band colors,
then `--seed` on the waves render until no band edge runs through the middle of a word.
The tracked center lines use `--anchor-to ink`: wide tracking adds space after the last
letter, and anchoring by the ink puts the visible letters on the 50% W axis.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Cool run: `#19c3d6,#1ba3d8,#2a7fe0,#3d5fe0,#5a4ad6,#7a3fc8,#9a36b4,#b8329c` with a base
  from `#22d3c5` to `#3a2a8c`: night swim, winter festival, tech meetup.
* Two-color calm version: 4 bands, `alpha=0.35`, one hue in four tints (for example
  `#0b6e4f` to `#9fe3b5`): wellness, corporate summer party.
* Replace the keyline with a solid white card (T-label-box logic: card white, title in the
  pink `#e03c75`) when the title must survive small print sizes.
* Square post: keep frame and title, move the date into the frame's top edge as one line,
  drop body and gutters.
* Pairs with T-gradient-type on the title (white to pale yellow), T-pill-button in place
  of the "MORE INFO" row, and T-paper-grain against banding.

## 11. Pitfalls

* Bands with low alpha in mixed order: the stack averages into one muddy orange and the
  base gradient disappears. List the band colors in the same order as the gradient and keep
  `alpha` at 0.6 to 0.8.
* Placing the rotated wave square without `--size 2000x`: it is fitted to the canvas, and
  its straight edges show as diagonal cuts in the corners.
* A band edge with high contrast running through the title: white type flickers between
  two grounds. Re-seed, or lower `amp`, until the title sits on one or two close colors.
* Colored or gray type "for variety". Everything in front is white; the hierarchy comes
  from size, weight, and tracking.
* Dot grids cut at their edges (half dots): render each grid at an odd multiple of the
  cell size (84, 252, 308 px for a 28 px cell).
* Filling the side gutters with content. They hold only dots, one rule, and one sideways
  line; the 21.5% margins are what make the central column look deliberate.
