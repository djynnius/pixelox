# p061: Teal announcement with thin headline

## 1. Concept

A service-time announcement on a teal-to-navy gradient: a two-line headline in hairline-thin
wide capitals fills the width, the one fact that changed (the time) sits under it in fat
yellow letters, and two hosts, cut out at chest height, rise from the bottom edge with a
hand-painted yellow tag beside them. It works by contrast of weight, not size: the headline
is the largest thing but the lightest, so the heavy yellow time, although less than half as
tall, is what the eye lands on.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait 3:4. Center axis at 50% W for the top block; margins 9% W for the headline, 11%
  W for the time and its sub-line.
* Zones: logo badge 6 to 15.6% H; kicker 19% H; headline 22.3 to 44.2% H; time 48.4 to
  52.9% H; sub-line 54.5% H; hosts 57.8 to 100% H; painted tag 85.5 to 96.8% H at the left.
* The headline lines are justified to 82.4% W (8.8 to 91.2% W). The time is 76% W wide and
  starts at 11% W; the sub-line hangs from the same 11% W edge. So the top is centered and
  the time block is left-hung, 2% W inside the headline's edge.
* The two hosts overlap: the front one centered at 39.5% W, the back one at 63% W and 2% H
  lower; together they span 23 to 89% W, leaving the lower-left corner for the tag.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: teal `#004b60` at the top, `#01334c` at 64% H, deep navy `#030f49` at the bottom
   edge; a smooth vertical blend with a faint blotchy texture.
2. Logo badge: a white ring (13% W across, stroke 0.8% W) with a white emblem and a tiny
   name inside, centered at 50% W, 10.8% H.
3. Kicker: 2 words in yellow bold caps, centered at 19% H, 50% W wide.
4. Headline line 1: 6 letters, white, hairline strokes (0.7% W thick), cap top 22.3% H, cap
   height 10.5% H.
5. Headline line 2: 7 letters, same, cap top 34.1% H, cap height 10.1% H. The gap between
   the lines is 1.3% H.
6. Time: digits, "AM", and a time zone in brackets, in fat wide yellow caps `#fbf62a`, cap
   height 4.5% H, 11 to 87% W, with a small near-black shadow directly underneath.
7. Sub-line: 4 words in light white caps, left at 11% W, 54.5% H, 50% W wide.
8. Back host: a woman with long hair in a dark floral top, facing the camera, head top at
   60% H, centered at 63% W, running off the bottom edge.
9. Front host: a man in tinted glasses, a blue suit, and a magenta tie, turned slightly to
   the right, head top at 57.8% H, centered at 39.5% W, shoulders from 23 to 68% W, in front
   of her left shoulder.
10. Painted tag: a rough amber-yellow patch `#f8bd36` with ragged, dabbed edges,
    `8.8%,85.5%,23.7% x 11.3%`, in front of the man's sleeve.
11. Tag text: three short black bold lines, left at 13.5% W, at 88.6, 91.2, and 93.8% H.
12. Grain: fine noise over everything.

Cool analogous ground (teal to navy) with yellow as the single warm accent, used three
times in falling order: time, kicker, tag. The tag is a warmer yellow so it reads as paint,
not as type.

## 5. Text elements

| Role | Content pattern | Case | Font class (look-alike / local) | Weight | Cap height | Tracking | Leading | Align | Color | Position |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kicker | "SPECIAL ANNOUNCEMENT"-type, 2 words | caps | neo-grotesque (Inter, Arimo / Helvetica Neue) | 700 | 2.1% | 0 | - | center | `#f5e336` | 50%, 19% |
| Headline 1 | "SUNDAY"-type word, 6 letters | caps | thin wide sans (Montserrat 200, Raleway 200 / Avenir Next) | 275 | 10.5% | 100 to 200 | - | justified to 82.4% W | `#ffffff` | 8.8%, cap top 22.3% |
| Headline 2 | "SERVICE"-type word, 7 letters | caps | same | 275 | 10.1% | 100 to 200 | - | justified to 82.4% W | `#ffffff` | 8.8%, cap top 34.1% |
| Time | "10AM" + zone in brackets | caps | heavy extended grotesque (Archivo Black, Krona One / Arial Black) | 900 | 4.5% | 0 | - | left | `#fbf62a` | 11%, cap top 48.4% |
| Sub-line | 4 words: where it applies | caps | geometric sans (Montserrat 400 / Avenir Next) | 400 | 1.7% | 20 | - | left | `#ffffff` | 11%, 54.5% |
| Tag | 3 lines, 1 to 2 words each | caps | neo-grotesque (Inter / Helvetica Neue) | 700 | 1.9% | 0 | 135% | left | `#141414` | 13.5%, 91.2% |

Three weights of sans and nothing else: ultra-light for the headline, black for the time,
bold for labels. The weight contrast (about 1 : 8 in stroke thickness between headline and
time) does the job a second typeface usually does.

## 6. Imagery

Two studio portraits cut out at chest height, in natural color with soft, even light:
polished, friendly, looking at the camera. They overlap by about a third so they read as a
pair, the taller in front. Their dark clothes melt into the navy at the bottom, so no fade
or shadow is needed; the faces sit entirely below the text block and nothing crosses them.
One portrait works as well (center it at 50% W); three need a narrower headline.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-fit-width** on both headline lines (one width, 82.4% W). Because the face is thin,
  the lines are widened with tracking, not with weight.
* **T-block-shadow**, softened: the time carries a near-black shadow offset 3,7 px with a
  4 px blur, enough to lift the yellow off the teal without an outline.
* **T-brush-swash** / **T-label-box**: the tag is a painted patch, not a rectangle; the
  recipe uses `torn.js` with all four edges torn in amber, tilted -3 degrees.
* Ground: one linear gradient layer over a flat teal fill.
* **T-paper-grain**: `filter grain --as-layer`, amount 8, which also hides banding in the
  gradient.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The yellow time (heaviest, brightest, at the center of the page).
2. Up to the white headline (largest, but light).
3. Down to the two faces.
4. The yellow tag at the lower left (yellow again), then the kicker and the logo.

Cap heights: headline 10.5 : time 4.5 : kicker 2.1 : tag 1.9 : sub-line 1.7, about
6 : 2.6 : 1.2 : 1.1 : 1.

## 9. Build recipe

`subject.png` (front host), `subject2.png` (back host), and `logo.png` are your images
here. With one host, drop the `Back` line and place the other at 50% W.

```bash
pixelox render js scripts/torn.js -o tag.png --size 310x176 --seed 5 --param color='#f8bd36' --param rim=none --param edges=tblr --param rough=0.45 --param detail=0.9
pixelox new -o notice.pxo --size 1080x1440 --fill '#004b60'
pixelox layer add notice.pxo --in-place --name Depth
pixelox fill gradient notice.pxo --in-place --layer Depth --from 0,22% --to 0,100% --stops '#004b6000@0,#01334cff@0.55,#030f49ff@1'
pixelox comp place notice.pxo logo.png --in-place --as Logo --size 140x --at 50%,10.8% --anchor c
pixelox text add notice.pxo --in-place --name Kicker --content "SERVICE TIME CHANGE" --font @neo-grotesque --weight 700 --size 2.9% --color '#f5e336' --at 50%,19% --anchor c
pixelox text add notice.pxo --in-place --name H1 --content "FAMILY" --font @thin-sans --fit-width 890 --tracking 180 --color '#ffffff' --at 50%,22.3% --anchor t --anchor-to ink
pixelox text add notice.pxo --in-place --name H2 --content "SERVICE" --font @thin-sans --fit-width 890 --tracking 180 --color '#ffffff' --at 50%,34.3% --anchor t --anchor-to ink
pixelox text add notice.pxo --in-place --name Time --content "9:30AM (GMT)" --font @heavy-wide --fit-width 770 --color '#fbf62a' --at 11%,48.4% --anchor-to ink
pixelox fx drop-shadow notice.pxo --in-place --layer Time --offset 3,7 --blur 4 --color '#06080c' --opacity 85
pixelox text add notice.pxo --in-place --name Where --content "IN ALL OUR CITY CAMPUSES" --font @geometric-sans --weight 400 --size 2.35% --tracking 20 --color '#ffffff' --at 11%,55.8% --anchor l
pixelox comp place notice.pxo subject2.png --in-place --as Back --size x580 --at 64%,100% --anchor b
pixelox comp place notice.pxo subject.png --in-place --as Front --size x620 --at 40%,100% --anchor b
pixelox comp place notice.pxo tag.png --in-place --as Tag --at 22.5%,91.1% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate notice.pxo --in-place --layer Tag --degrees -3
pixelox text add notice.pxo --in-place --name TagText --content $'THIS WEEK\nONE SERVICE\nONLY' --font @neo-grotesque --weight 700 --size 2.4% --leading 135% --color '#141414' --at 12.4%,91.2% --anchor l
pixelox filter grain notice.pxo --in-place --as-layer --name Grain --amount 8
pixelox export notice.pxo -o notice.png
```

Change first: the two headline words (5 to 8 letters each; they are fitted to one width,
so a shorter word comes out taller), then the time string (it is fitted to 71% W: a short
string such as "9AM" becomes very tall, so replace its `--fit-width 770` with `--size 7%`),
then the tag lines (keep each under 12 characters, or widen `tag.png`).

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Warm version: ground `#5b1d05` to `#2a0a12`, time and kicker `#ffd23c`, tag `#ffffff`
  with dark red text. Evening version: `#1b1040` to `#07061a` with a mint `#7dffc9` time.
* No photo: move the time block down to 60% H, enlarge it to cap height 7% H, and put the
  tag at the center under it as a button-like patch.
* Venue change, guest announcement, holiday opening hours, school or clinic notices,
  webinar reminder. Square: logo, one headline line, time, one host at the right.
* Story (9:16): keep the top block, let the hosts fill 55 to 100% H.
* Pairs with T-pill-button in place of the painted tag for a cleaner brand, T-light-leak in
  a top corner, and T-rules (a hairline rule under the kicker).

## 11. Pitfalls

* Setting the thin headline small. Hairline type survives only when large (cap height 9%
  H or more) and on a dark, calm ground; on a photo or below 6% H it disappears.
* Making the headline bold "to be safe": the weight contrast with the time is the whole
  design. If the headline must be heavier, make the time lighter or smaller instead.
* Yellow text without the shadow on mid teal vibrates. Keep the small dark shadow, or
  darken the ground behind the time.
* Faces under the text block. The hosts start below 57% H; if a head reaches the sub-line,
  reduce the cutouts, do not move the text.
* A perfectly rectangular yellow box for the tag looks like a warning label. The rough
  edge and the slight tilt are what make it a friendly aside.
