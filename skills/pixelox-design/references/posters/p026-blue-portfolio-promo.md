# p026: Blue portfolio promo with seated figure

## 1. Concept

A self-promotion card for a designer: a lavender-to-blue gradient, a four-line centered
headline in two blues with two tilted sticker words stuck onto it, and below it a smiling
man lounging in a blue seat beside an oversized, tilted phone that shows his work. A
glossy "Swipe" pill and two contact rows close the page. It works because everything is
one hue: background, clothing, seat, ribbon, buttons, and type are all blue, so a busy
pile of objects (person, phone, ribbon, icon tiles, stickers) reads as one calm scene,
with navy and white as the only contrast.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait A-series (1 : 1.414). Margins 6% W; center axis at 50% W for all type.
* Zones: social handle 6 to 9% H (top right); headline 10 to 36% H; sub-copy 37 to 41% H;
  hero scene 40 to 95% H; button 79.5 to 86% H (in front of the scene); contact rows 87 to
  97% H.
* The hero scene is two masses side by side: the seated figure on the left (11 to 63% W)
  and the phone on the right (55 to 88% W), overlapping by 8% W, the phone in front.
* Headline lines differ in width on purpose (13%, 47%, 76%, 29% W): a diamond-like stack
  whose widest line is the third.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: vertical gradient, lavender-white `#d6d9f9` at the top, `#c0c9fa` at 30% H,
   `#a6b6f3` at 50% H, `#7a99f5` at 75% H, bright blue `#4b78f5` at the bottom. A paler
   patch `#e3e4fa` at the top center.
2. Ribbon: a thick glossy blue band (6% W wide) that loops in from the left edge at 61 to
   71% H and around the right side at 54 to 78% H, behind figure and phone.
3. Seated figure (cutout): a young man in a royal-blue hoodie and black trousers,
   reclining in a rounded indigo `#3a3c8b` seat, face turned up to the right, smiling;
   box `11%,45%,52% x 50%`.
4. Phone: a black-framed handset 33% W wide and 44% H tall, rotated 9 degrees clockwise,
   centered at 71.5% W, 64.5% H; white screen with a three-line bold heading, a small blue
   pill, and gray rows; soft shadow to the lower right.
5. Two icon tiles: white rounded squares 13% W wide (corner radius 25%), tilted -10 and
   +10 degrees, at 12% W, 52.5% H and 89.5% W, 66% H, each with one blue line icon and a
   soft blue-gray shadow.
6. Headline, four centered lines: line 1 small navy (cap height 2.4% H), line 2 navy (7%
   H), line 3 royal blue (7% H, the widest), line 4 navy (4.4% H).
7. Sticker A: a royal-blue rounded box with a white word, rotated -8 degrees, overlapping
   the start of line 2 at 14 to 30% W, 17 to 21.5% H.
8. Sticker B: a white rounded box with a blue word, rotated +8 degrees, hanging off the
   end of line 3 at 73.5 to 90% W, 28.5 to 33.5% H, with a soft shadow.
9. Sub-copy: two centered lines of small navy text at 37.9 and 39.6% H.
10. Social handle: two blue discs (4% W), each with a small white glyph, and a handle in
    navy, right-aligned to 94% W at 7.6% H.
11. Button: glossy indigo pill `29%,79.5%,40% x 6.5%` with a small tap icon and a white
    bold word.
12. Contact rows: two blue rounded squares (5.5% W) at 24% W, a white mail icon in the
    first and a white phone icon in the second, with white text to their right, at 89.5%
    and 94.3% H.

## 6. Imagery

* One studio cutout of a relaxed, seated person with the seat included, lit softly from
  the front, clothing in the palette's blue. The pose (leaning back, legs crossed, looking
  up and away) sets the easy tone. See "Getting a cutout" in techniques.md.
* The phone is a render with the designer's own interface on screen. In the recipe it is
  drawn from shapes (a black rounded rectangle, a white screen, a notch pill, a heading, a
  button), merged and rotated; replace the screen with a real screenshot by placing it
  and clipping it to the Screen shape before the merge.
* The ribbon and the icon tiles are soft 3D shapes; here they are flat shapes with a
  gradient and a shadow.

## 7. Effects and textures

* Ground: `fill gradient` with five stops, top to bottom.
* Ribbon: one `shape path` of two open curves, stroke 64 px, round caps, with
  `fx overlay` (`#2143c9` to `#5f86f8`, angle 90) and `fx bevel` for a soft tube edge.
* **T-label-box**: the two stickers; box and word are each rotated by the same angle
  (`transform rotate --degrees`), about their own centers, which coincide.
* **T-cutout-drop-shadow**, soft variant, on phone and tiles (blue-gray shadow color
  `#24368f`, never black).
* **T-pill-button**: filled pill, radius = half the height, left-to-right gradient
  `#2f39c4` to `#6a4fe0`, white inner glow at 35% for the glossy top.
* **T-icon-row**: every small icon is one `shape icon` line from the bundled outline set:
  `pen-tool` and `heart` on the tiles (72 px, turned with their tile by `--rotation`),
  `at-sign` and `share-2` on the social discs (24 px, white), `pointer` on the button
  (48 px), `mail` and `phone` on the contact squares (36 px). The set has no brand logos:
  for a real network mark, place your own logo file on the disc.
* **T-geo-shapes** for the discs and the rounded squares behind the icons (one `shape
  path` each).
* **T-device-mockup** in [techniques.md](../techniques.md) is the tested library version of the phone mockup; start from it when reusing this idea.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. Headline line 3 (the widest, the only colored line), then line 2 above it.
2. The face (it looks up toward the headline), then the phone screen beside it.
3. The stickers, as a second, joking voice.
4. The button, then the contact rows; the handle at the top right last.

Cap heights: 7 : 4.4 : 2.4 : 1.9 : 1.5 : 1.1, about 6.4 : 4 : 2.2 : 1.7 : 1.4 : 1.

## 9. Build recipe

`subject.png` is your image here: a cutout of a person (ideally seated, with the seat),
dressed in or graded toward the palette's blue. Canvas 1080x1528.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* One-hue swaps: all greens (`#e3f6e8 / #7ed69a / #1f9d55 / #0b2a18`), all oranges
  (`#fff0e0 / #ffb46b / #f26a1b / #2a1205`), all violets. Dress or grade the subject into
  the hue (`adjust hsl --hue` on the clothing color range).
* Product version: replace the person with a product cutout on a pedestal ellipse and
  keep the phone, stickers, and button for an app or shop launch.
* Square post: headline lines 2 and 3 only, one sticker, subject cropped at the waist.
* Story 9:16: same stack with more air; move the button to 86% H and drop the handle row.
* Pairs with T-gradient-mesh for a softer ground, T-text-behind-subject (enlarge line 3
  and let the head overlap it), and T-info-bar in place of the two contact rows.

## 11. Pitfalls

* A second hue anywhere (a red shirt, an orange button) breaks the one-color scene; the
  eye then goes to that object first. Grade it into the palette or remove it.
* Stickers tilted more than 12 degrees or larger than a third of the big line look like
  errors; 6 to 10 degrees and 25 to 30% of the headline's cap height is the range.
* Rotating a box and its word separately only works if the word is centered on the box:
  both then turn about the same center. Left-aligned words drift out of the box.
* Black shadows on a blue scene look dirty. Use a dark blue shadow at 30 to 40%.
* The button under the subject. It must sit in front of the scene (added after the
  subject and phone) or it reads as part of the photo.
