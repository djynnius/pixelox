# p058: Amber motion-blur summit with giant acronym

## 1. Concept

A story-format announcement for a leadership conference: a panning photo of a bird in
flight, streaked into amber and olive by motion blur, carries a four-letter cream acronym
whose letters lean forward and grow from left to right until the last one is three times
the size of the first and plunges down the page. It works because the type itself moves
(lean, growth, a drop below the baseline) in the same direction as the blur, so speed is
felt before a word is read, while all the facts stay small, straight, and calm.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait 9:16 (story). Margins 6.3% W. A center axis for the top block and the bottom
  third; one left hang line at 6.3% W for the date.
* Zones: social handles 2 to 4% H; title block 11 to 19% H; giant acronym 24 to 77% H;
  date 63 to 72% H at the left, under the short letters; venue line 78% H; four feature
  columns 83 to 89% H; closing line 93% H.
* The acronym's first three letters stand on a common baseline at 50% H and step up in
  height (16, 19, and 27% H); the fourth letter is 49% H tall and 30% W wide, its top at
  27.6% H and its bottom at 77% H, hugging the right edge (68 to 97.5% W).
* Light runs diagonally: the top-left corner is pale cream, the bottom-right dark olive.
  Dark text sits in the light corner, cream text in the dark one.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground photo: a panned shot, everything smeared into streaks rising about 12 degrees to
   the right; mapped to amber. Top-left `#fee7be`, left middle `#f2ad39`, right edge and
   bottom `#1c210a`, brown streaks `#7d4313` near the bottom.
2. Social row: three small two-line blocks (platform name, handle) with dot icons, dark,
   starting at 6.3%, 23.5%, and 41% W, 2.4 to 3.4% H.
3. Organizer: 4 letters, thin, very widely spaced, dark, centered at 11.7% H.
4. Event title: 3 words in condensed bold caps, dark, 18.5 to 82% W, cap top 13.5% H.
5. Three one-word values in tiny spaced caps at 18.6% H, split by two short vertical
   rules at 36% and 57% W.
6. Acronym letters 1 to 3: cream `#fbeccc`, heavy condensed italic, leaning 12 degrees;
   letter 1 at 3 to 25% W (34 to 50% H), letter 2 at 26 to 47% W (31 to 50% H), letter 3
   at 48 to 66% W (24 to 51% H).
7. Acronym letter 4: the same face, 68 to 97.5% W, 27.6 to 77% H. Its lower left edge
   swings under the third letter.
8. Four one-word labels in small dark bold caps, one over each letter, at 30.1% H, centered
   on 19%, 38%, 60%, and 83.5% W; tiny hand-drawn doodles (a crown, a squiggle, a cross)
   beside three of them.
9. Slogan inside the fourth letter: a row of four small stars and three short dark lines,
   each line indented further than the one above, 56 to 75% W, 52 to 58% H.
10. Bird cutout: wings raised, itself slightly blurred, amber toned, 37 to 67% W, 57 to
    72% H, in front of the lower edge of the fourth letter.
11. Cream 8-point sparkle at 14.5% W, 50.5% H.
12. Date block at 6.3% W: month in small spaced caps at 63.8% H; day range and year in two
    heavy wide lines, 65.3 to 71.4% H, 33% W wide, near-white.
13. Venue: outline pin icon at 28% W and one spaced line to 75.5% W, 78% H, cream.
14. Feature row: four outline icons (5.5% W) at 84.3% H over two-line cream labels at 86.9
    and 88.4% H, centered on 15%, 38%, 61.7%, and 85% W, split by three 1 px vertical
    rules at 26%, 50%, and 73.5% W.
15. Closing line: tiny spaced caps at 93.1% H between two short horizontal rules (6.3 to
    13% W and 82.5 to 93% W).

## 6. Imagery

One action photograph: a seabird with wings raised, shot with a slow shutter while
panning, so the water and sky behind it are horizontal streaks. For a build, any opaque
photo becomes the streaked ground (`filter motion-blur`, 350 to 450 px, then an amber
gradient map: **T-duotone** with four stops), and the moving subject is a separate cutout
placed in front of the letters (**T-motion-streak**, **T-text-behind-subject**). Good
subjects: a bird, a runner, a dancer, a cyclist, a wave. The subject is small (30% W) and
sits below the center, half over the dark ground and half over the cream letter.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-motion-streak** on the ground: blur distance 35% of the width at an angle of 168
  degrees (streaks rise to the right).
* **T-duotone**, four stops: `#10160a`, `#6b3f0f`, `#e2a23a`, `#fff0cf`.
* Diagonal light: a cream linear gradient from the top-left corner to transparent at
  the center, and an olive one from the center to the bottom-right corner.
* Growing letters: the first three letters are one text layer bent with
  `transform perspective` (left edge 16% H tall, right edge 27% H, tops shifted right for
  the lean); that layer is rasterized (warning PXW2003). The fourth letter is its own live
  text layer: **T-stretch-type** makes it tall and narrow (`--scale-x 60.9% --fit-width
  385`) and `--style italic` leans it.
* **T-rules**: hairline dividers between values and between feature columns; two short
  rules around the closing line.
* **T-paper-grain**: `filter grain --as-layer`, amount 8, to hide banding in the blur.
* **T-warp-type** (`--warp rise --bend 35 --h-distort 70`) grows a word as live text, but
  it also narrows the first letter. The perspective quad keeps the three letters equally
  wide, as in the original, so the recipe keeps it.
* **T-fit-width**: the title line and the last letter are set to exact widths with
  `--fit-width`.
* **T-icon-row**: `shape icon` draws the four outline icons over the feature labels (72 px,
  `--stroke-width 3`), the `map-pin` before the venue, and three 24 px glyphs before the
  handles. The bundled set has no social-network logos: `at-sign`, `globe`, and
  `message-circle` stand in.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The fourth letter (a cream shape half the height of the page), entered from the top
   right and followed down its curve.
2. The bird at the end of that curve.
3. Left along the baseline to the date, the brightest small text.
4. Back up to the title block; then the venue, the four features, the closing line.

Cap heights: last letter 49 : first letters 16 to 27 : title 3.1 : date 2.5 : labels 1.1,
about 45 : 20 : 2.8 : 2.3 : 1.

## 9. Build recipe

`photo.png` (any warm photo; it is blurred beyond recognition) and `subject.png` (a cutout
of something in motion) are your images here.

```bash
pixelox new -o summit.pxo --size 1080x1920 --fill '#49330c'
pixelox comp place summit.pxo photo.png --in-place --as Pan --fit cover
pixelox filter motion-blur summit.pxo --in-place --layer Pan --angle 168 --distance 400
pixelox adjust gradient-map summit.pxo --in-place --layer Pan --stops '#10160a@0,#6b3f0f@0.3,#e89a25@0.62,#fff0cf@1'
pixelox layer add summit.pxo --in-place --name Light
pixelox fill gradient summit.pxo --in-place --layer Light --from 0,0 --to 85%,60% --stops '#fde8c0e6@0,#f6cd9080@0.35,#f2ad3900@0.8'
pixelox layer add summit.pxo --in-place --name Shade
pixelox fill gradient summit.pxo --in-place --layer Shade --from 35%,50% --to 100%,100% --stops '#1c210a00@0,#141a08e6@1'
pixelox text add summit.pxo --in-place --name Word --content "ACT" --font @condensed-heavy --size 20% --tracking -30 --color '#fbeccc'
pixelox transform perspective summit.pxo --in-place --layer Word --quad '86,653 722,461 622,979 32,960'
pixelox text add summit.pxo --in-place --name Last --content "S" --font @condensed-heavy --style italic --scale-x 60.9% --fit-width 385 --color '#fbeccc' --at 99%,27.6% --anchor tr --anchor-to ink
pixelox comp place summit.pxo subject.png --in-place --as Mover --size 330x --at 50%,66.5% --anchor c
pixelox adjust gradient-map summit.pxo --in-place --layer Mover --stops '#1a1206@0,#8a4f12@0.45,#f0b860@0.8,#fff3d6@1'
pixelox shape icon summit.pxo at-sign --in-place --name Social1 --size 24 --at 6.3%,2.9% --anchor l --color '#3a2a10'
pixelox text add summit.pxo --in-place --name Handle1 --content "@yourhandle" --font @neo-grotesque --weight 500 --size 0.85% --color '#3a2a10' --at 9.2%,2.9% --anchor l
```

Change first: the acronym (three letters in `Word`, the last one in `Last`; a round last
letter such as S, C, G, or O gives the sweeping curve, a straight one such as T or L gives
a pillar), then the four label words, then the date, then the four feature labels and
their icons (`pixelox shape icons --search WORD` finds a name). The `--quad` corners are
top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left of the three-letter group.

The slogan is set in cream on the dark ground beside the letters. When your letters leave
a wide cream area (a long foot, a fat bowl), move it inside in dark olive `#1c210a`, as the
reference does.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Cool version: map `#04121f`, `#0f3f66`, `#3aa0d8`, `#e6f6ff` for a night or ocean
  feel; letters `#eaf6ff`.
* Dawn version: `#1a0b1f`, `#7a1f4a`, `#f08a4b`, `#ffe9c9`, letters cream; suits a prayer
  conference or a new-year launch.
* A whole word instead of an acronym (5 to 6 letters): grow only the last letter and keep
  the rest at 16% H.
* Sport launch, marathon, graduation, youth camp, startup summit. Landscape: letters on
  the left 60%, date and features in a column on the right.
* Pairs with T-light-leak in the light corner, T-pill-button for a "REGISTER" call, and
  T-gradient-type (cream to gold) on the last letter.

## 11. Pitfalls

* Blur and letters pointing different ways. The streaks, the italic lean, and the growth
  of the letters must all run toward the same corner (here up and to the right).
* The big letter covering the facts. It owns the right 30% W from 27 to 77% H; keep the
  date at the left and everything else above 20% or below 78% H.
* A sharp, full-color subject on the blurred amber ground looks pasted. Map it to the same
  ramp and blur it by a few pixels.
* Too many small elements at the same size (labels, slogan, values, features). Keep them
  at one cap height (about 1.1% H) and one color per background, so they read as a system.
* Pure white letters: they burn out against the cream corner. Use cream `#fbeccc` and let
  the date alone be near-white.
