# p041: Teal cloudscape worship-night post

## 1. Concept

A square invitation to a worship evening, set entirely in one teal on a bright sky: a
narrow stacked title (a thin script word over two tall condensed words) stands in the
middle of a glowing cloudscape like a column, with a round date badge to its right and a
quiet column of details under it. It works because everything is the same hue (the sky,
the type, the badge), so the page feels like air and light, and the only warm mark on it
(two orange brush strokes for "part two") becomes a small, sure accent.

## 2. Format and grid

* Square 1:1. Center axis. The content lives in a narrow column from 35 to 65% W; the
  outer 35% on each side is empty sky.
* Zones: logo and presenter 6 to 16% H; "presents" script 18.5 to 22% H; title stack 24 to
  70% H; tagline 70.8% H; theme row 73.5 to 77.5% H; verse between two rules 79 to 89% H;
  contact block 91 to 96% H.
* Two satellites break the column: the date badge at 75% W, 36.5% H, and a small round
  monogram badge at 26.5% W, 69.5% H, placed diagonally opposite each other.
* The two display words are left-aligned at 36% W; the details below are centered.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Sky: soft cumulus clouds, teal `#4b91a1` at the edges to pale `#9dc7c7`, cloud tops
   near-white; clouds gather in a band at 38 to 52% H and again at 64 to 72% H.
2. Glow: cream-white `#f1f6ee` radial light at 50% W, 36% H, radius 38% W, behind the
   title, with a faint halo ring.
3. Vignette: deep teal `#176585` at the corners (70% at the bottom corners, 45% at the top).
4. Logo: a teal outline mark in a pill shape, 4.6% W wide, 7.4% H tall, centered, top at
   5.8% H; a two-line presenter name in tiny bold teal caps under it (14.1 and 15.4% H).
5. "Presents": thin monoline script, teal, centered at 20.3% H, 27% W wide.
6. Script word: 5 letters with a large looping capital, thin monoline teal script, x 35 to
   59% W, y 24 to 37% H; its tail hangs into the first display word.
7. Display word 1: 3 letters, tall condensed rounded caps, x 36 to 60% W, cap top 37% H,
   baseline 53.5% H (cap height 16.5% H). Filled with dark-to-mid teal and a faceted
   (low-poly triangle) pattern; cloud highlights show through the letters.
8. A small superscript "'S" in the same face (cap height 5.5% H) right of word 1, at
   60.5 to 64.5% W, 43 to 49% H.
9. Display word 2: 5 letters, same face in a unicase style, x 36.7 to 64.8% W, top 54.3% H,
   baseline 69.6% H (height 15.3% H), same fill.
10. Roman numeral: two short orange `#f29a2e` brush strokes, x 66 to 68.5% W, y 63.5 to
    70.5% H (2% W clear of the last letter), right of the last letter.
11. Date badge: solid teal `#2a7f96` disc, 10.6% W, centered at 75.1% W, 36.4% H; a thin
    teal ring, 14.4% W, open at the lower left; the word "DATE" in tiny tracked caps
    riding the ring at 10 o'clock; inside the disc three white bold lines (weekday, day
    and month, year).
12. Monogram badge: teal disc 6% W with a white ring and a white script monogram, at
    26.6% W, 69.6% H.
13. Tagline: 4 single words divided by thin bars, tiny bold tracked caps, dark teal,
    centered at 70.8% H.
14. Theme row: an outlined white pill (7% W x 3% H) reading "THEME" at 36.7% W, then a
    2-line bold caps theme in dark teal `#1d5a6e`, left edge at 45% W.
15. Rule, 3-line verse in white italic (one phrase bold), rule: 79%, 81.8 to 86%, 88.9% H;
    rules run from 35 to 65% W, 1 px white.
16. Contact: four tiny dark round icons at 91.3% H, phone number at 93.5% H, handle at
    95.1% H, dark teal.

## 6. Imagery

No people and no product: a sky is the picture. It is a daylight cloudscape recolored to
teal (shadows teal, highlights cream), brightest in the middle so the title is backlit.
Clouds also show inside the display letters, as if the letters were windows, together
with a low-poly triangle texture that gives them a faceted, cut-glass look. The logo and
the monogram badge are small line marks in the same teal. The recipe generates the sky;
a real cloud photo treated with T-duotone (`#256d83` to `#f1f6ee`) is a direct upgrade.

## 7. Effects and textures

* Generated sky: one `fill noise --as-layer` line (clouds, three teal stops, `--scale
  42%`, `--stretch 0.7`, `--bias=-8`) under a cream radial glow, then **T-vignette** in deep
  teal instead of black.
* **T-image-in-type**: a faceted pattern (a checker turned 45 degrees, white, 14%) is
  clipped into each display word over a vertical teal gradient (`fx overlay`, dark at the
  bottom).
* **T-script-overlap**: the thin script word sits on top of the first display word and
  overlaps its upper-left corner; same color, so it reads as one lockup.
* **T-arc-type** for the badge label on its ring; the ring is an open arc drawn with
  `shape path`.
* **T-pill-button** (outlined, small) as the "THEME" label; **T-rules** for the two
  hairlines around the verse.
* **T-icon-row**, contact variant: four 24 px `shape icon` glyphs (`phone`, `globe`,
  `at-sign`, `message-circle`) in dark teal, 30 px apart, above the contact lines. The set
  has no social-network logos; these neutral glyphs stand in for them.
* The orange numeral is two round-capped line segments, slightly tilted, 10 px wide.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The two tall display words in the bright center (darkest shapes on the lightest area).
2. The script word above them (thin, but large and attached).
3. The date badge, the only solid shape off the axis; then the orange strokes.
4. Theme row, verse, contact, top to bottom down the column.

Heights: display words 16 : script capital 9 : badge date 1.7 : theme 1.6 : verse 1.4 :
smallest labels 0.9, about 18 : 10 : 1.9 : 1.8 : 1.5 : 1.

## 9. Build recipe

`logo.png` is your image here (a small dark or teal line mark). Everything else is
generated. Change the sky's `--seed` until the brightest clouds sit behind the title and
the lower third is calm.

```bash
pixelox render js scripts/pattern.js -o facets.png --size 1080x1080 --param kind=checker --param color='#ffffff' --param cell=34 --param angle=45 --param alpha=0.14
pixelox new -o night.pxo --size 1080x1080 --fill '#71aeb5'
pixelox fill noise night.pxo --in-place --as-layer --name Sky --seed 4 --scale 42% --octaves 5 --warp 18 --stretch 0.7 --angle 90 --contrast 12 --bias=-8 --stops '#5aa3b3,#b5d6d6,#f6faf2'
pixelox layer add night.pxo --in-place --name Glow
pixelox fill gradient night.pxo --in-place --layer Glow --kind radial --from 50%,36% --to 50%,86% --stops '#f1f6eeff@0,#f1f6eed9@0.4,#f1f6ee00@1'
pixelox layer add night.pxo --in-place --name Vignette
pixelox fill gradient night.pxo --in-place --layer Vignette --kind radial --from 50%,38% --to 50%,112% --stops '#17658500@0.45,#176585cc@1'
pixelox comp place night.pxo logo.png --in-place --as Logo --size 62x --at 50%,5.8% --anchor t
pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name Presenter --content $'RIVERSIDE\nPRAISE TEAM' --font @geometric-sans --weight 700 --size 1.25% --tracking 40 --leading 115% --align center --color '#256d83' --at 50%,14.8% --anchor c
pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name Presents --content "Presents" --font @script-formal --weight 500 --size 5% --color '#256d83' --at 50%,20.3% --anchor c
pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name D1 --content "HIS" --font "DIN Condensed" --weight 700 --size 22.6% --color '#256d83' --at 36%,37% --anchor tl --anchor-to ink
pixelox fx overlay night.pxo --in-place --layer D1 --stops '#1b5f78@0,#3f93a8@1' --angle 90
pixelox comp place night.pxo facets.png --in-place --as Facets1 --fit cover
pixelox layer clip night.pxo --in-place --layer Facets1
pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name D2 --content "CALL" --font "DIN Condensed" --weight 700 --size 21% --color '#256d83' --at 36.7%,54.3% --anchor tl --anchor-to ink
pixelox fx copy night.pxo --in-place --from D1 --to D2
pixelox comp place night.pxo facets.png --in-place --as Facets2 --fit cover
pixelox layer clip night.pxo --in-place --layer Facets2
pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name Script --content "Hear" --font @script-formal --weight 500 --size 12.5% --color '#256d83' --at 35%,27.5% --anchor tl --anchor-to ink

pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name BadgeLabel --content "DATE" --font @geometric-sans --weight 600 --size 11px --tracking 400 --color '#256d83'
pixelox text on-path night.pxo --in-place --layer BadgeLabel --d 'M 729 393 A 82 82 0 0 1 811 311' --side outside
pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name BadgeDay --content "SUNDAY" --font @geometric-sans --weight 700 --size 1.5% --tracking 20 --color '#ffffff' --at 75.1%,33.5% --anchor c
pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name BadgeDate --content $'14. 09\n2031' --font @geometric-sans --weight 700 --size 2.3% --leading 110% --align center --color '#ffffff' --at 75.1%,37.7% --anchor c

pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name MonoText --content "Hv" --font @script-formal --weight 700 --size 3% --color '#ffffff' --at 26.6%,69.6% --anchor c
pixelox text add night.pxo --in-place --name Tagline --content "SONG   /   SCRIPTURE   /   PRAYER   /   STILLNESS" --font @geometric-sans --weight 700 --size 1.2% --tracking 80 --color '#1d5a6e' --at 50%,71.6% --anchor c

pixelox shape icon night.pxo globe --in-place --name Icon2 --size 24 --at 525,91.3% --anchor c --color '#1d3f4d' --stroke-width 2.5
pixelox shape icon night.pxo at-sign --in-place --name Icon3 --size 24 --at 555,91.3% --anchor c --color '#1d3f4d' --stroke-width 2.5
pixelox shape icon night.pxo message-circle --in-place --name Icon4 --size 24 --at 585,91.3% --anchor c --color '#1d3f4d' --stroke-width 2.5

```

Change first: the three title words (a short script word, then a 3-letter and a 4-letter
display word; DIN Condensed is wider than the ideal face, so a 5-letter word needs
`--size 17%` to stay inside the column), then the date. To change the
hue, replace every teal with the same lightness in another color and keep the cream glow.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Dawn version: pink-peach sky `#e9a9a0 / #f6d5c4 / #fff6ee`, plum type `#6a2a4a`, the
  accent strokes in gold. Night version: navy sky `#0d1b3a / #27407a / #9db7e8`, white
  title, badge in gold `#e3b23c`.
* A real cloud photo with T-duotone instead of the generated sky; or T-sunburst light
  shafts (`uneven=0.8`, `cy=-0.1`, blurred) falling onto the title.
* Clip the sky itself into the display words (repeat the `fill noise --as-layer` line
  above each word and `layer clip` it; the same `--seed` lines up with the sky) for a
  stronger window effect.
* Portrait 4:5: stretch the empty sky above the logo and below the contact block; do not
  widen the column.
* Suits prayer meetings, retreats, choir evenings, memorial services, wellness and
  meditation events. Pairs with A-centered-title layouts for book covers.

## 11. Pitfalls

* Widening the title to fill the square. The narrow column with empty sky on both sides
  is the look; keep the stack under 32% W.
* A script that is too heavy: a bold script beside tall heavy caps fights them. Use the
  lightest script weight and let it be large instead.
* Dark teal text on the mid-teal edges disappears. All small dark text must sit inside the
  glow (the middle 40% W); text outside it is white.
* The orange accent growing: more than one orange element (an orange badge, orange rules)
  breaks the single-hue calm. One mark, under 1% of the area.
* Clouds with hard contrast behind the verse make it unreadable. Keep the lower third
  soft and mid-toned (another `--seed`, or a lower `--contrast`); busy clouds belong
  behind the title.
* Icons larger than the contact text. At 24 px they match the 15 px text; keep them one
  size and one color.
