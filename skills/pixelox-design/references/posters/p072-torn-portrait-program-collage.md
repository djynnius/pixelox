# p072: Torn portrait collage with program text

## 1. Concept

A convention poster made like a pinned-up paste-up: a big color portrait on a gray
backdrop with the left half of the face ripped away and replaced by white paper carrying
fragments of giant black letters; around it, black-and-white photo strips, an orange
schedule panel, columns of tiny program text, a piece of tape, and a torn strip of dark
landscape along the bottom that holds the title. It works because the poster shows its
own subject (cutting and juxtaposing), and because the busy scraps are held by a strict
underlying grid and by only two accent colors, orange and acid yellow.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait A-series (1 : 1.414). No outer margin: scraps bleed off every edge. Text keeps
  2.5% W from the edges.
* Three columns: left 0 to 35% W (schedule, small photos, orange panel); center 35 to 73%
  W (the portrait's face); right 73 to 98% W (speaker list, set on the portrait's gray
  backdrop).
* Rows: photo strip 0 to 12% H; portrait 12 to 82% H; torn bottom band 80 to 100% H, its
  ragged white upper edge rising from 84% H at the left to 79% H at the right.
* The tear in the face runs vertically at 37 to 52% W, so the portrait's remaining half
  sits exactly on the center column.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Paper: pale warm gray `#e9e8e6`.
2. Photo strip, top: a gray photo panel at 28 to 64% W, 0 to 8% H; a second pale panel at
   68 to 96% W, 0 to 7.5% H; a black bar `#161616` under both, 28 to 96% W, 8 to 12.5% H.
3. Portrait backdrop: studio gray `#b9b5b8`, `35%,12.3%,65% x 69.7%`.
4. Portrait (clipped to the backdrop): a young man's head and bare shoulders in natural
   color, windswept dark hair, round glasses, head filling 37 to 62% W, 13 to 62% H.
5. Torn white scrap over the left half of the face: off-white paper about 35% W by 46% H,
   right edge ragged, tilted -4 degrees, soft shadow. On it, clipped to it: two or three
   lower-case letters from the logotype in a heavy grotesque, each about 20% H tall,
   rotated about -28 degrees and cropped by the scrap's edges.
6. Orange panel `#e2611c`: `0,28%,31% x 32%`, with a bold caps heading at its upper right
   and a grid of narrow white boxes holding tiny rotated text.
7. Small black-and-white portrait photo at 0 to 28% W, 20.5 to 27.5% H; a second one at
   0 to 25% W, 72 to 85% H with a black block to its right (25 to 37% W).
8. Schedule block: bold caps heading at 2.5% W, 12.6% H; two tiny columns under it.
9. Tape: a translucent mustard strip `#a69b5d`, 17% W by 3.4% H, tilted -3 degrees, at
   60 to 77% W, 10.5 to 14% H, bridging the photo strip and the portrait.
10. Speaker list: a bold caps heading rotated to read upward at 73% W, 13 to 29% H; two
    columns of tiny text (bold name, 4 to 6 lines each) at 75.5% and 87.5% W, 13 to 71% H.
11. Bottom band: a dark, desaturated landscape texture (`#3d4442`, bluish highlights) with
    a torn top edge and a 1 to 2% H white paper rim along the tear.
12. Title: two bold lines in acid yellow `#d9e021` at 25% W, 89% and 91.3% H; a tiny white
    paragraph under them.
13. Logotype: one word broken into three stacked 3-letter syllables, bold rounded lower
    case, orange `#e8813a`, right edge at 98% W, 88 to 98.5% H.

## 6. Imagery

* Main portrait: head and bare shoulders, three-quarter view facing left, strong flash,
  on a seamless gray backdrop. Only its right half and the shoulders remain visible.
* Two or three small black-and-white portraits (contact-sheet size) and a strip of
  anonymous photo panels with black bars.
* Bottom: a dark close-up of rock or earth, desaturated toward teal-gray
  (**T-duotone**, `#161a1a` to `#3f4a4a` to `#9aa5a3`), torn along its top.

Everything is physically "cut": edges are either razor straight (photo panels) or torn
with a white fiber rim (the face scrap, the bottom band).

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-torn-collage**: `torn.js` with `edges=r` for the face scrap, `edges=t` for the
  bottom band. Tearing a photo: place a torn strip, place the photo above it, `layer
  clip`. A second white strip of the same seed placed 16 px higher, underneath, gives the
  white paper rim.
* Letters on the scrap: one text layer, rotated, placed right above the scrap and
  clipped to it, so the scrap's edge crops the letters.
* **T-vertical-type** for the rotated section heading.
* **T-label-box**: orange panel, black bars, white text boxes; **T-rules** in spirit:
  the bars act as heavy rules between photo panels.
* Tape: a mustard rectangle at 75% opacity, tilted, drawn over the junction it "holds".
* Short soft shadow on every torn scrap (offset 3,6, blur 12, 35%).
* **T-torn-photo** in [techniques.md](../techniques.md) is the tested library version of the torn photo edge with a white rim; start from it when reusing this idea.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The eye in the remaining half of the face, then the tear and the black letter shapes
   beside it.
2. The orange panel at the left (largest color field).
3. Down the torn white edge to the yellow title and the orange logotype.
4. Program text in the left and right columns, read up close.

It is a two-distance poster: from afar a ripped face and three color accents; at arm's
length a full program.

## 9. Build recipe

`subject.png` (a portrait cutout; it is clipped into the gray backdrop), `subject2.png`
(a second portrait for the small black-and-white photo), and `photo.png` (any textured
landscape or surface for the bottom band) are your images here. Canvas 1080x1528.

```bash
pixelox render js scripts/torn.js -o scrap.png --size 400x720 --seed 4 --param color='#f1f0ed' --param edges=r --param rough=0.9
pixelox render js scripts/torn.js -o tear.png --size 1160x420 --seed 8 --param color='#ffffff' --param edges=t --param rough=0.8
pixelox new -o paste.pxo --size 1080x1528 --fill '#e9e8e6'
pixelox shape path paste.pxo --in-place --name Panels --d 'M 302 0 H 691 V 122 H 302 Z M 734 0 H 1037 V 115 H 734 Z' --fill '#a4a4a5'
pixelox shape path paste.pxo --in-place --name Bars --d 'M 302 122 H 1037 V 191 H 302 Z M 270 1070 H 400 V 1284 H 270 Z' --fill '#161616'
pixelox shape rect paste.pxo --in-place --name Backdrop --rect 35%,12.3%,65%x69.7% --fill '#b9b5b8'
pixelox comp place paste.pxo subject.png --in-place --as Portrait --size x1000 --at 54%,21.3% --anchor t
pixelox layer clip paste.pxo --in-place --layer Portrait
pixelox comp place paste.pxo scrap.png --in-place --as Scrap --at 35.5%,47% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate paste.pxo --in-place --layer Scrap --degrees=-4
pixelox fx drop-shadow paste.pxo --in-place --layer Scrap --offset 3,6 --blur 12 --opacity 35
pixelox text add paste.pxo --in-place --name Letters --content $'ix\ncu' --font "Helvetica Neue" --weight 700 --size 21% --tracking=-40 --leading 80% --color '#161616' --at 36%,47% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate paste.pxo --in-place --layer Letters --degrees=-28
pixelox layer clip paste.pxo --in-place --layer Letters
pixelox shape rect paste.pxo --in-place --name Orange --rect 0,28%,31%x32% --fill '#e2611c'
pixelox shape path paste.pxo --in-place --name Boxes --d 'M 8 540 H 70 V 660 H 8 Z M 82 540 H 140 V 660 H 82 Z M 152 540 H 322 V 660 H 152 Z M 8 690 H 120 V 820 H 8 Z M 132 690 H 200 V 820 H 132 Z M 212 690 H 322 V 820 H 212 Z' --fill '#f1f0ed'
pixelox text add paste.pxo --in-place --name Workshops --content "WORKSHOPS" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 800 --size 1.55% --tracking 20 --color '#161616' --at 30%,34% --anchor r
pixelox shape rect paste.pxo --in-place --name SmallPhoto --rect 0,20.5%,28%x7% --fill '#8f8f90'
pixelox comp place paste.pxo subject2.png --in-place --as SmallFace --size 300x --at 14%,20.2% --anchor t
pixelox layer clip paste.pxo --in-place --layer SmallFace
pixelox adjust bw paste.pxo --in-place --layer SmallFace
pixelox text add paste.pxo --in-place --name Schedule --content "SCHEDULE" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 800 --size 1.55% --tracking 20 --color '#161616' --at 2.5%,12.6% --anchor l
pixelox text add paste.pxo --in-place --name Times --content $'10:00  Doors and coffee\n11:00  Opening talk\n12:30  Paper swap\n14:00  Studio sessions\n16:30  Open review\n18:00  Closing party' --font "Helvetica Neue" --weight 500 --size 0.75% --leading 150% --color '#161616' --at 2.5%,13.8% --anchor tl
pixelox shape rect paste.pxo --in-place --name Tape --rect 60%,10.6%,17%x3.4% --fill '#a69b5d' --opacity 75
pixelox transform rotate paste.pxo --in-place --layer Tape --degrees=-3
pixelox text add paste.pxo --in-place --name Speakers --content "FEATURED SPEAKERS" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 800 --size 1.55% --tracking 20 --color '#161616' --at 50%,50% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate paste.pxo --in-place --layer Speakers --degrees=-90
pixelox transform move paste.pxo --in-place --layer Speakers --to 71.5%,29% --anchor bl
pixelox text add paste.pxo --in-place --name Bios1 --content $'First Speaker\nCuts old magazines\ninto wall pieces and\nteaches weekend\nclasses in a shared\nstudio.\n\nSecond Speaker\nWorks with maps,\ntickets, and letters\nto tell family\nstories in layers.\n\nThird Speaker\nPrints, tears, and\npastes posters for\nbands and theaters.' --font "Helvetica Neue" --weight 400 --size 0.75% --leading 140% --color '#3a3a3a' --at 75.5%,13% --anchor tl
pixelox text add paste.pxo --in-place --name Bios2 --content $'Fourth Speaker\nBuilds animated\ncollages from scans\nand shows them on\ncity walls.\n\nFifth Speaker\nCollects packaging\nand turns it into\nportraits of its\nmakers.\n\nSixth Speaker\nRuns a zine library\nand a monthly\ncut-and-paste night.' --font "Helvetica Neue" --weight 400 --size 0.75% --leading 140% --color '#3a3a3a' --at 87.5%,13% --anchor tl
pixelox comp place paste.pxo tear.png --in-place --as Rim --at 50%,100% --anchor b
pixelox transform move paste.pxo --in-place --layer Rim --by 0,60
pixelox comp place paste.pxo tear.png --in-place --as BandShape --at 50%,100% --anchor b
pixelox transform move paste.pxo --in-place --layer BandShape --by 0,78
pixelox comp place paste.pxo photo.png --in-place --as Rock --size 1160x --at 50%,100% --anchor b
pixelox layer clip paste.pxo --in-place --layer Rock
pixelox adjust gradient-map paste.pxo --in-place --layer Rock --stops '#161a1a@0,#3f4a4a@0.5,#9aa5a3@1'
pixelox text add paste.pxo --in-place --name Title --content $'Paper Cuts Weekend\nMarch 14, 2031' --font "Avenir Next" --weight 700 --size 1.95% --leading 125% --color '#d9e021' --at 25%,90.2% --anchor l
pixelox text add paste.pxo --in-place --name Note --content $'Two days of talks, tables, and scissors for people\nwho make pictures out of other pictures. Bring a\nstack of old paper and something to trade.' --font "Helvetica Neue" --weight 400 --size 0.75% --leading 140% --color '#ffffff' --at 25%,93.6% --anchor tl
pixelox text add paste.pxo --in-place --name Logotype --content $'Cut\nand\nmix' --font "Avenir Next" --weight 700 --size 3.6% --tracking 20 --leading 92% --color '#e8813a' --at 98%,93.3% --anchor r

```

Change first: the portrait (set `--size` and `--at` so the head is 25 to 35% W wide and
the scrap's torn edge, at about 52% W, crosses the middle of the face), then the letters on the scrap (take them from your logotype),
then the program text. Keep every scrap either dead straight or clearly torn.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Accent swaps: replace orange and acid yellow with red `#e2231a` and cyan `#19c8e6`, or
  cobalt `#1f4fd6` and pink `#ff7ab8`. Keep the rest gray and black.
* Make the portrait black and white (`adjust bw` on the Portrait layer) and let the orange
  panel be the only color.
* Swap the torn letters for a second photo (a mouth, an eye, a landscape) clipped to the
  scrap: a classic face mash-up.
* Works for design conferences, zine fairs, theater seasons, album art, magazine openers.
* Pairs with T-halftone-portrait (halftone the visible half of the face), T-grunge and
  T-paper-grain over everything, and T-glitch slices as "cuts".

## 11. Pitfalls

* Tearing through the eye that remains: the portrait loses its focus. The tear should pass
  between the eyes or down the nose; one whole eye must stay.
* Scraps all at 0 degrees with no shadows look like a web layout. Tilt the torn pieces 2 to
  5 degrees and give them a short shadow; keep the printed panels square.
* Program text set too large "to be readable": the contrast between the huge face and
  the tiny text is the look. Keep it at 0.5 to 0.6% H cap height and in narrow columns
  (22 to 26 characters).
* More than two accent colors turns the collage into a mess; the photos already bring
  variety.
* Forgetting the white rim on torn photo edges: without it the tear reads as a jagged
  digital mask.
