# p024: Sunset-sky worship concert flyer

## 1. Concept

A church-concert flyer built as one glowing sunset: a singer with a guitar stands in a
peach cloudscape, a backlit crowd fills the bottom third, and the hot stage light between
them carries a one-word headline in near-black letters with a white brush-script subtitle
slashed across its lower edge. It works because the whole page is a single top-to-bottom
ramp from pale cream to dark maroon, so the dark headline lands exactly where the picture
is brightest and the white small print exactly where it is darkest.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait, about 2:3. Center axis; text margins 12% W.
* Zones: web address at 6.7% H; singer 11 to 58% H; headline 59.5 to 71.5% H; script line
  71 to 79% H (overlapping the headline's lower edge); credits block 84 to 92% H; the
  bottom 8% H is empty dark maroon.
* Two small square boxes float beside the singer on a diagonal: date box upper right
  (x 75.5 to 88% W, y 18.3 to 26.5% H), start-time box lower left (x 9.5 to 22% W, y 39.5
  to 47.5% H).
* A bundle of thin white diagonal light streaks (rising to the right at about 42 degrees)
  crosses the middle of the page behind the singer.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Sky: cumulus clouds at sunset, full width, 0 to 66% H; pale peach `#eac9aa` to cream
   `#fbf2e6`, with brown-mauve cloud shadows `#a57c6a` at the lower left and right.
2. Sun: a white radial glow at 74% W, 40% H, radius 30% W, right of the singer's shoulder.
3. Halftone dots: white dots, pitch 1.6% W, fading downward, over the top 25% H at 30%.
4. Crowd photo: silhouettes with raised hands in front of a stage, 60 to 100% H, mapped
   to maroon, orange, and cream.
5. Light streaks: about ten thin white lines (3 px) and three wide soft bars (16 px, 30%),
   all at the same angle, between x 15 and 100% W, y 15 and 65% H.
6. Singer cutout: a man in a gray sweater singing into a stand microphone and playing an
   acoustic guitar, x 22 to 80% W, head top at 11% H, cut off by the haze at 57% H.
7. Birds: seven tiny dark chevrons at x 70 to 92% W, y 41 to 56% H.
8. Haze band: a horizontal band of light, transparent at 52% H, cream-white at 63% H,
   orange at 70% H, transparent at 80% H. It hides the singer's lower edge and the seam
   between sky and crowd, and it is the stage light.
9. Headline: one 5-letter word, near-black brown `#2c140c`, x 18.5 to 81% W, cap top
   59.5% H, baseline 71.5% H (cap height 12% H). The lower middle of the word is lit
   orange by a small glow in front of it.
10. Script line: 3 words, white brush script, x 15 to 83% W, tilted up 5 degrees, center
    at 75% H, with a dark soft shadow.
11. Bottom shade: dark maroon `#421a18`, transparent at 72% H to 95% opaque at 100% H.
12. Date box and start box: each a white 2 px square outline with a tan `#c9a27a` filled
    square of the same size shifted 1% W left and 0.8% H down behind it (60% opacity);
    small bold label above a large number, dark brown.
13. Web address: tracked caps, plum-brown `#5a2a4a`, centered at 6.7% H.
14. Credits block, centered: lead-in in thin white caps at 84.2% H; performer names in
    orange `#f5a23c` separated by thin rules at 87% H; venue line in white bold at 89.3% H;
    a last regular line at 91.3% H.

Monochrome warm: every color is an orange-brown at a different lightness. White is used
for light and for text on the dark third; there is no cool color at all.

One geometric sans in four weights plus one brush script. The headline is the only heavy
element; everything else is small and wide-tracked, so it reads as caption, not as noise.

## 6. Imagery

Three photographs composited into one scene. (1) A dramatic cloudscape at golden hour,
light coming from the right. (2) A cutout musician, waist up, three-quarter view, singing
into a microphone, graded slightly warm; the low sun sits behind his strumming hand so
the right side of the guitar is blown out to white. (3) A festival crowd from behind,
hands up, against stage lights, used almost as a silhouette and tinted maroon to orange
(**T-duotone**, three stops). The recipe generates the sky and the light; you supply the
singer and the crowd. Any performer cutout works: a choir row, a preacher, a pianist.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-photo-fade**, used twice with light instead of a flat ground: the haze band (cream
  to orange) dissolves the singer's lower edge and the top of the crowd photo; the bottom
  shade clears a dark field for the credits.
* **T-duotone** on the crowd: `#2a100f` to `#c8643a` at 0.55 to `#fff1c8`.
* Light streaks: one `shape path` with ten line segments at the same angle, white, 3 px,
  85%; a second path of three 16 px bars at 30%. They suggest sun rays without a flare.
* **T-pattern**: white halftone dots, `cell=18`, `fade=down`, over the top quarter.
* **T-script-overlap**: white script over the lower edge of the dark headline, tilted -5
  degrees, shadow offset 0,5 blur 12 at 60%.
* **T-label-box**: outline box plus an offset tan box behind it.
* **T-vignette**, warm: `#3a140e` at the corners, 45%.
* **T-light-streaks** in [techniques.md](../techniques.md) is the tested library version of the defocused light streaks; start from it when reusing this idea.
* **T-haze-band** in [techniques.md](../techniques.md) is the tested library version of the glow band that hides the lower edges of the cutouts and photo seams; start from it when reusing this idea.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The headline: darkest shape on the brightest part of the page, cap height 12% H.
2. The singer's face directly above it, then along the guitar neck to the sun.
3. The script line (white on dark, tilted, touching the headline).
4. The two boxes (when), then the orange performer line and the venue.

Cap heights: headline 12 : script 4.2 : box numbers 3 : credits 1.3 : lead-in 1.1, about
11 : 4 : 2.7 : 1.2 : 1.

## 9. Build recipe

`subject.png` (performer cutout) and `photo.png` (a backlit crowd or audience photo) are
your images here. The sky is generated; replace `sky.png` with a real cloud photo if you
have one.

```bash
pixelox render js scripts/clouds.js -o sky.png --size 1080x1100 --seed 12 --param lo='#8f6654' --param mid='#e6c2a2' --param hi='#fffaf0' --param scale=2.2 --param octaves=5 --param warp=0.5 --param contrast=2.1
pixelox render js scripts/pattern.js -o dots.png --size 1080x420 --param kind=dots --param color='#ffffff' --param cell=18 --param weight=0.4 --param fade=down --param alpha=0.3
pixelox new -o concert.pxo --size 1080x1620 --fill '#441f1e'
pixelox comp place concert.pxo photo.png --in-place --as Crowd --size 1080x --at 0,100% --anchor bl
pixelox adjust gradient-map concert.pxo --in-place --layer Crowd --stops '#2a100f@0,#c8643a@0.55,#fff1c8@1'
pixelox comp place concert.pxo sky.png --in-place --as Sky --at 0,0
pixelox layer add concert.pxo --in-place --name Sun
pixelox fill gradient concert.pxo --in-place --layer Sun --kind radial --from 74%,40% --to 74%,62% --stops '#ffffffff@0,#fff4e0cc@0.35,#fff4e000@1'
pixelox comp place concert.pxo dots.png --in-place --as Dots --at 0,0
pixelox shape path concert.pxo --in-place --name Streaks --d 'M 180 930 L 520 624 M 240 990 L 760 522 M 330 960 L 640 681 M 420 1010 L 900 578 M 520 990 L 980 576 M 600 1040 L 1060 626 M 560 420 L 760 240 M 700 820 L 1080 478 M 120 640 L 300 478 M 800 330 L 1040 114' --fill none --stroke '#ffffff' --stroke-width 3 --opacity 85
pixelox shape path concert.pxo --in-place --name Bars --d 'M 300 1000 L 820 532 M 460 1040 L 1000 554 M 640 1000 L 1080 604' --fill none --stroke '#ffffff' --stroke-width 16 --opacity 30
pixelox comp place concert.pxo subject.png --in-place --as Singer --size x790 --at 51%,59.5% --anchor b
pixelox shape path concert.pxo --in-place --name Birds --d 'M 770 690 l 12 -8 l 10 8 M 850 665 l 12 -8 l 10 8 M 905 705 l 10 -7 l 9 7 M 940 760 l 12 -8 l 10 8 M 830 800 l 10 -7 l 9 7 M 985 730 l 9 -6 l 8 6 M 880 880 l 12 -8 l 10 8' --fill none --stroke '#3a1c14' --stroke-width 4
pixelox layer add concert.pxo --in-place --name Haze
pixelox fill gradient concert.pxo --in-place --layer Haze --from 0,52% --to 0,80% --stops '#fff6e000@0,#fff6e0f5@0.4,#f39a4ae6@0.66,#f39a4a00@1'
pixelox layer add concert.pxo --in-place --name Shade
pixelox fill gradient concert.pxo --in-place --layer Shade --from 0,72% --to 0,100% --stops '#421a1800@0,#421a18f2@1'
pixelox text add concert.pxo --in-place --name Headline --content "LIGHT" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 900 --size 16.4% --tracking -30 --color '#2c140c'
pixelox transform move concert.pxo --in-place --layer Headline --to 50%,59.5% --anchor t
pixelox layer add concert.pxo --in-place --name Lit
pixelox fill gradient concert.pxo --in-place --layer Lit --kind radial --from 52%,71% --to 52%,78% --stops '#ffb45ecc@0,#ffb45e00@1'
pixelox layer blend concert.pxo --in-place --layer Lit --mode screen
pixelox text add concert.pxo --in-place --name Script --content "The Harvest Concert" --font "Brush Script MT" --size 7.6% --color '#ffffff'
pixelox transform scale concert.pxo --in-place --layer Script --size 730x
pixelox transform rotate concert.pxo --in-place --layer Script --degrees -5
pixelox transform move concert.pxo --in-place --layer Script --to 49%,75% --anchor c
pixelox fx drop-shadow concert.pxo --in-place --layer Script --offset 0,5 --blur 12 --color '#2c140c' --opacity 60
pixelox shape rect concert.pxo --in-place --name DateBack --rect 74.3%,19.1%,12.5%x8.2% --fill '#c9a27a' --opacity 60
pixelox shape rect concert.pxo --in-place --name DateBox --rect 75.5%,18.3%,12.5%x8.2% --fill none --stroke '#ffffff' --stroke-width 2
pixelox text add concert.pxo --in-place --name DateLabel --content "MAY" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 700 --size 1.7% --tracking 40 --color '#3a1c14' --at 81.7%,20.7% --anchor c
pixelox text add concert.pxo --in-place --name DateNum --content "17" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 700 --size 4.2% --color '#3a1c14' --at 81.7%,23.6% --anchor c
pixelox shape rect concert.pxo --in-place --name StartBack --rect 8.3%,40.3%,12.5%x8.2% --fill '#c9a27a' --opacity 60
pixelox shape rect concert.pxo --in-place --name StartBox --rect 9.5%,39.5%,12.5%x8.2% --fill none --stroke '#ffffff' --stroke-width 2
pixelox text add concert.pxo --in-place --name StartLabel --content "START" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 700 --size 1.7% --tracking 40 --color '#3a1c14' --at 15.7%,41.9% --anchor c
pixelox text add concert.pxo --in-place --name StartNum --content "6" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 700 --size 4.2% --color '#3a1c14' --at 14%,44.8% --anchor c
pixelox text add concert.pxo --in-place --name StartPm --content "PM" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 700 --size 1.7% --color '#3a1c14' --at 17.6%,44.3% --anchor c
pixelox text add concert.pxo --in-place --name Site --content "WWW.EXAMPLE.COM" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 500 --size 1.5% --tracking 200 --color '#5a2a4a' --at 50%,6.7% --anchor c
pixelox text add concert.pxo --in-place --name LeadIn --content "FEATURING:" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 400 --size 1.5% --tracking 60 --color '#ffffff' --at 50%,84.2% --anchor c
pixelox text add concert.pxo --in-place --name Performers --content "HOPE CHOIR     SIS. JANE ROWE     THE LEVITES" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 500 --size 1.8% --tracking 20 --color '#f5a23c' --at 50%,87% --anchor c
pixelox text add concert.pxo --in-place --name Venue --content "VENUE NAME, 12 EXAMPLE ROAD, YOUR CITY" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 700 --size 1.8% --tracking 20 --color '#ffffff' --at 50%,89.3% --anchor c
pixelox text add concert.pxo --in-place --name Last --content "FREE ENTRY  DOORS OPEN 5PM" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 500 --size 1.5% --tracking 20 --color '#ffffff' --at 50%,91.3% --anchor c
pixelox layer add concert.pxo --in-place --name Vignette
pixelox fill gradient concert.pxo --in-place --layer Vignette --kind radial --from 50%,42% --to 50%,115% --stops '#3a140e00@0.5,#3a140e73@1'
pixelox export concert.pxo -o concert.png
```

Change first: the headline word (4 to 6 letters; at `--size 16.4%` the cap height is
12% H, and a word wider than 70% W should be fitted with `transform scale --size 700x`), the script line, and the two box values. For a cooler evening, change the three
sky colors and the haze stops together, not one at a time.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Dawn version: sky `#7fa6c9 / #f3c9b0 / #fff4e6`, haze `#fff1d0` to `#ff9e7a`, bottom
  shade navy `#14203a`, headline navy `#101a33`. Night version: sky `#141a3a / #3a2a66 /
  #c9a8ff`, haze magenta, headline white with the script in gold.
* No performer: enlarge the headline to 80% W at 45 to 60% H and let the streaks and the
  sun carry the upper half; works for conference and prayer-night themes.
* Replace the sun with **T-lens-flare** at the same point, or add **T-sunburst** shafts
  (`uneven=0.8`, `spread=70`) falling from the top right.
* Square post: singer 5 to 60% H, headline 58 to 76% H, one credit line; drop the boxes
  into the top corners.
* Suits gospel concerts, worship nights, open-air crusades, harvest festivals, and album
  covers. Pairs with T-bokeh over the crowd and T-color-grade (warm vintage) as a final
  pass.

## 11. Pitfalls

* A visible seam where the sky ends and the crowd begins. The haze band must be opaque
  (alpha `f5`) at the seam; move the band, not the photos.
* A headline in pure black looks cut out of a different picture. Use a brown-black from
  the palette and let the glow layer light its lower edge.
* Script too thin or too small on the busy crowd: keep its cap height at 4% H or more and
  keep the shadow.
* Cool-colored subject (blue shirt, daylight white balance) against the warm sky looks
  pasted. Warm it first with `adjust color-balance --highlights 10,0,-12`.
* Streaks at mixed angles read as scratches. Every streak has the same slope; only length
  and thickness vary.
