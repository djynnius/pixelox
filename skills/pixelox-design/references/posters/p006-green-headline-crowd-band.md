# p006: Green block headline over crowd band

## 1. Concept

A weekly-service poster on off-white paper: a two-line dark green headline in a heavy
condensed face fills the upper half edge to edge, a wide crowd photo is cropped to a band
across the lower half, and a minister in black, cut out, stands in front of both so that
his head covers part of the second headline line. It works because it has only three big
shapes (green block, photo band, black figure) on a lot of clean paper, and the figure
stitches the other two together.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait A-series (1 : 1.414). Margins 10% W left and right, 8% H top and bottom.
* Two hang lines: everything small is left-aligned at 10% W or right-aligned at 90% W. The
  headline is justified between the same two lines (80% W wide).
* Zones: top line at 8% H; headline 12 to 47.5% H; paper gap 47.5 to 52.5% H; photo band
  52.5 to 85% H, full bleed left and right; footer line at 91 to 92% H.
* The figure stands on the vertical axis (center at 51% W), head top at 36% H, running off
  the bottom edge. It crosses the headline, the gap, the band, and the footer zone.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: off-white paper `#f4f4f4` with a faint gray mottle (lighter and darker patches,
   2 to 3% contrast) and fine grain.
2. Top-left line: presenter, two surnames joined by "&", navy bold caps, left at 10% W,
   8.1% H.
3. Top-right line: numeric date, a thin vertical divider, and the time, navy bold, right
   edge at 90% W, 8.1% H.
4. Headline line 1: one word of 6 letters, dark green `#024422`, from 10.6% to 89.7% W,
   cap top 11.8% H, baseline 29.9% H (cap height 18% H).
5. Headline line 2: one word of 7 letters, same green and width, cap top 31.4% H, baseline
   47.5% H (cap height 16% H). The gap between the lines is 1.5% H: almost touching.
6. Photo band: `0,52.5%,100% x 32.5%`, a worship crowd seen from behind with raised hands,
   out of focus at the top, cool and slightly desaturated.
7. Band slit: a paper-colored horizontal stripe, 1.3% H thick, at 61.4% H, cutting the band
   into a short strip (9% H) and a tall strip (22% H). The figure passes in front of it.
8. Figure cutout: a man in a black suit and clerical collar, turned three-quarters to the
   right, hands clasped over a small black book at 80% H. Shoulders span 27 to 80% W.
9. Footer left: a navy map-pin icon (3% W) at 10% W and a two-line address, 90.5 and 92% H.
10. Footer right: three navy dots in a row, each 2.7% W, ending at 90% W, centered at 92% H.

## 4. Palette

| Hex | Role | Area |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `#f4f4f4` | paper ground | 40% |
| `#024422` | headline green | 20% |
| `#171d24` | the figure's black suit, photo shadows | 20% |
| `#40454d` | cool gray mid-tones of the crowd photo | 15% |
| `#081b3a` | navy small text, pin, dots | 5% |

One strong hue (bottle green) on neutral paper, with navy standing in for black in the
small text. The photo is pulled toward gray-blue so it does not add a second accent color.

## 5. Text elements

| Role | Content pattern | Case | Font class (look-alike / local) | Weight | Cap height | Tracking | Leading | Align | Color | Position |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Presenter | `[NAME] & [NAME]`, 2 surnames | caps | geometric sans (Montserrat / Avenir Next) | 700 | 1.0% | 20 | - | left | `#081b3a` | 10%, 8.1% |
| Date and time | DD.MM.YY, divider, "10AM" | caps | same | 700 | 1.2% | 0 | - | right | `#081b3a` | 90%, 8.1% |
| Headline 1 | "SUNDAY"-type word, 6 letters | caps | ultra-condensed heavy grotesque (Anton, Archivo Black condensed / Impact) | heavy | 18% | -10 | - | justified to 80% W | `#024422` | 10.6%, cap top 11.8% |
| Headline 2 | "WORSHIP"-type word, 7 letters | caps | same | heavy | 16% | -10 | - | justified to 80% W | `#024422` | 10.6%, cap top 31.4% |
| Address | street / city, 2 lines | title | geometric sans (Montserrat / Avenir Next) | 700 | 0.95% | 0 | 125% | left | `#081b3a` | 14%, 91.2% |

Two families: one heavy condensed display face, one geometric sans at a single weight.
The small type is tiny (1% H) against an 18% H headline: an 18 : 1 jump with nothing in
between, which is what makes the layout feel editorial instead of flyer-like.

## 6. Imagery

Two pictures. (1) A wide photo of a congregation from behind, many raised arms, warm stage
light far away; it is cropped to a letterbox band, desaturated about 30% and cooled so the
skin tones go gray-brown and the shadows blue-black (T-color-grade, cold variant). (2) A
studio cutout of a man in black clerical dress, lit softly from the right, looking off to
the right; clean edge, no outline, no shadow. His black suit is the darkest and largest
shape on the page and sits on the axis. Any single dark-clothed figure works; a figure in
white would vanish on the paper.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-fit-width**: both headline lines are scaled to the same 79% W, so the block is a
  rectangle; the 6-letter line comes out taller than the 7-letter line.
* **T-text-behind-subject**: the cutout is placed after the headline and the band. The head
  covers the middle two letters of line 2 only; the first three and last two stay clear.
* Band crop: the photo is clipped to a rectangle (`layer clip`), then a paper-colored
  stripe is drawn over it for the slit.
* **T-color-grade** on the band photo: vibrance -30, shadows toward blue.
* **T-paper-grain**, amount 10, soft-light 35%, plus a pale cloud mottle in multiply at 12%
  for the paper feel.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The green headline (largest, most saturated, top).
2. The face, which interrupts line 2, then down the black figure to the hands and book.
3. The crowd band behind him (context: what kind of gathering).
4. Top line (who, when), then the footer address.

Cap-height ratio: headline 18 and 16 : date 1.2 : names and address 1.0, about 17 : 1.2 : 1.

## 9. Build recipe

`subject.png` (a cutout figure in dark clothing) and `photo.png` (a wide crowd or
congregation photo) are your images here.

```bash
pixelox render js scripts/clouds.js -o mottle.png --size 1080x1528 --seed 21 --param lo='#b9b9b9' --param hi='#ffffff' --param scale=4 --param octaves=6 --param warp=0.4
pixelox new -o service.pxo --size 1080x1528 --fill '#f4f4f4'
pixelox comp place service.pxo mottle.png --in-place --as Mottle --fit cover
pixelox layer blend service.pxo --in-place --layer Mottle --mode multiply
pixelox layer opacity service.pxo --in-place --layer Mottle --value 12
pixelox text add service.pxo --in-place --name Presenter --content "ROWAN & HALE" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 700 --size 1.4% --tracking 20 --color '#081b3a' --at 10%,8.1% --anchor l
pixelox text add service.pxo --in-place --name When --content "06.09.31    10AM" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 700 --size 1.65% --color '#081b3a' --at 90%,8.1% --anchor r
pixelox shape line service.pxo --in-place --name WhenRule --from 82.9%,7.4% --to 82.9%,8.8% --stroke '#081b3a' --stroke-width 2
pixelox text add service.pxo --in-place --name H1 --content "EASTER" --font Impact --size 10% --tracking -10 --color '#024422'
pixelox transform scale service.pxo --in-place --layer H1 --size 854x
pixelox transform move service.pxo --in-place --layer H1 --to 10.6%,11.8% --anchor tl
pixelox text add service.pxo --in-place --name H2 --content "SERVICE" --font Impact --size 10% --tracking -10 --color '#024422'
pixelox transform scale service.pxo --in-place --layer H2 --size 854x
pixelox transform move service.pxo --in-place --layer H2 --to 10.6%,31.6% --anchor tl
pixelox shape rect service.pxo --in-place --name Band --rect 0,52.5%,100%x32.5% --fill '#40454d'
pixelox comp place service.pxo photo.png --in-place --as Crowd --size 1080x --at 50%,68.7% --anchor c
pixelox layer clip service.pxo --in-place --layer Crowd
pixelox adjust vibrance service.pxo --in-place --layer Crowd --vibrance -30
pixelox adjust color-balance service.pxo --in-place --layer Crowd --shadows=-8,0,14
pixelox adjust levels service.pxo --in-place --layer Crowd --gamma 0.85
pixelox shape rect service.pxo --in-place --name Slit --rect 0,61.4%,100%x1.3% --fill '#f4f4f4'
pixelox comp place service.pxo subject.png --in-place --as Figure --size x980 --at 51%,100% --anchor b
pixelox shape ellipse service.pxo --in-place --name PinHead --rect 10%,89.9%,2.8%x2% --fill '#081b3a'
pixelox shape polygon service.pxo --in-place --name PinTip --center 11.4%,91.9% --radius 13 --sides 3 --rotation 90 --fill '#081b3a'
pixelox shape ellipse service.pxo --in-place --name PinHole --rect 10.9%,90.5%,1%x0.7% --fill '#f4f4f4'
pixelox text add service.pxo --in-place --name Address --content $'12 Example Road,\nYour City 00000' --font "Avenir Next" --weight 700 --size 1.3% --leading 125% --color '#081b3a' --at 14%,91.2% --anchor l
pixelox shape ellipse service.pxo --in-place --name Dot1 --rect 80.6%,91%,2.7%x1.9% --fill '#081b3a'
pixelox shape ellipse service.pxo --in-place --name Dot2 --rect 84%,91%,2.7%x1.9% --fill '#081b3a'
pixelox shape ellipse service.pxo --in-place --name Dot3 --rect 87.3%,91%,2.7%x1.9% --fill '#081b3a'
pixelox layer add service.pxo --in-place --name Grain --fill '#808080'
pixelox filter add-noise service.pxo --in-place --layer Grain --amount 10 --monochrome
pixelox layer blend service.pxo --in-place --layer Grain --mode soft-light
pixelox layer opacity service.pxo --in-place --layer Grain --value 35
pixelox export service.pxo -o service.png
```

Change first: the two headline words (5 to 7 letters each; the shorter word goes on top
because it comes out taller), then the green. If the head hides too much of line 2, move
the figure down with a smaller `--size x900` or shift it sideways by 5% W.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Color swaps for the headline with the same paper and navy: oxblood `#6b1016`, royal blue
  `#12308f`, burnt orange `#c2410c`. One hue only.
* Dark version: ground `#101412`, headline `#e9e4d4`, band photo left in warm color, small
  text `#c9c4b4`; the figure then needs light clothing.
* Three slits instead of one (at 61%, 70%, 79% H) turn the band into blinds, a stronger
  graphic.
* Conference speaker, graduation, memorial, choir concert, sermon-series cover. Square
  post: one headline line at 20% H, band 55 to 85% H, figure cropped at the chest.
* Pairs with T-duotone on the band (green to paper) for a one-ink look, and T-rules for a
  hairline under the top line.

## 11. Pitfalls

* Headline lines with very different letter counts (4 and 9): fitted to one width, one
  becomes a giant and the other a ribbon. Keep the two words within 2 letters of each
  other.
* The head lands on the first or last letter of line 2 and the word stops reading. Keep
  the head inside the middle third of the width.
* A colorful band photo competes with the green. Desaturate it and cool it; the photo is
  texture, not a second hero.
* Forgetting the paper gap between headline and band (5% H). When the band touches the
  type, the layout loses its three clear shapes.
* Pure white ground with pure black text looks like a document. The off-white, the mottle,
  and the navy are what make it feel printed.
