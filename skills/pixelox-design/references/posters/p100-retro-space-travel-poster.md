# p100: Retro space-travel sun poster

## 1. Concept

A tongue-in-cheek travel poster for a holiday on a scorched planet, drawn in the flat
vector style of 1930s tourism prints: an astronaut lounges in a striped deck chair with a
bottle in hand, seen from behind, facing an enormous white-hot sun that fills the width of
a charcoal sky; a needle rocket stands on the horizon; drinks cool in a crater. The
headline is a pun in three sizes: small rounded caps, one giant slanted word in yellow
with a red 3D edge, small caps again; the punchline sits inside the sun in icy blue, one
word of it dripping with icicles. It works because the joke is staged like a serious
landscape: one huge circle, one small back-view figure, and type whose colors (hot yellow
and red, cold blue) act out the words.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait 2:3 (close to it: 1 : 1.48), with a white paper border 2.2% W wide on all
  sides; the scene lives inside it.
* Horizon at 65.4% H. Above: sky with the sun (78% W across, centered at 50% W, 49% H, so
  its lower quarter is below the horizon and hidden). Below: gray ground with black
  rivers of shadow, sweeping toward the viewer.
* Headline zone 5 to 33% H, slanted: lines rise about 4 to 6 degrees to the right.
  Line 1 starts at 11.6% W; the giant word spans 9 to 93% W; line 3 is tucked under the
  giant word's right half (64 to 93% W).
* Punchline inside the sun, right of center: 41 to 79% W, 35 to 43% H, set level.
* Figure and chair left of center: 21.6 to 55% W, 50 to 81% H. Rocket right: 67.5 to 76%
  W, 44 to 67% H. Foreground crater with bottles: 23 to 50% W, 78.5 to 86.5% H.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Paper: off-white `#fcfdf7`, full canvas (shows as the border).
2. Sky panel: inside the border, a gradient from black `#030303` at the top to charcoal
   `#444441` around the sun and gray `#63645f` at the horizon.
3. Sun: a disc with a radial fade, white at the center to warm yellow `#fed882` at the
   rim, a 3 px red outline, two small orange sunspots near the upper left of the disc, and
   three small red prominences licking off the rim.
4. Ground: light gray `#bac1c1` and mid gray `#919496` plains below the horizon, crossed by
   wide black curving channels and blobs (cast shadows and lava rivers), with a ragged
   black ridge line along the horizon.
5. Rocket: a tall black needle on three legs, standing on the horizon in front of the sun.
6. Astronaut: seen from behind, gray suit with white highlights and red shoulder stripes,
   round helmet, raising a bottle with an orange drink in the right hand.
7. Deck chair: a sling of vertical stripes in blue `#18629d` and khaki `#b7ac60` on a thin
   blue frame, between the viewer and the astronaut's body.
8. Crater cooler: a small crater filled with pale blue ice holding four brown bottles.
9. Headline line 1: 2 short words, pale yellow rounded caps, slanted.
10. Giant word: 7 tall condensed caps in pale yellow `#fbed8d`, with a solid red `#dd3128`
    extrusion 9% of the cap height deep toward the lower right, slanted 6 degrees.
11. Headline line 3: 2 short words and an ellipsis, pale yellow rounded caps.
12. Punchline: 2 lines of rounded caps in mid blue `#1f5fa8`; one 4-letter word in the
    first line is larger, light blue `#8fd0f0` with a dark blue outline and icicles
    hanging from the bottom of each letter.
13. Tiny signature bottom left and a copyright line bottom right in the border.

## 6. Imagery

A flat vector illustration: no gradients except the sky and the sun, hard-edged shapes in
5 or 6 grays, black shadows drawn as shapes. The viewer stands behind the figure, which
is small (25% H tall with the chair) against a disc three times its height: the classic
"tiny tourist, giant sight" composition of travel posters. In a build, the sky, sun,
ground, rocket, and chair are shapes; the figure is a cutout flattened with `filter
cutout` so it joins the vector look. A back view is ideal; anything seated or standing
calmly works.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-frame-border**: the sky panel is a rectangle inset 24 px on every side.
* Sky and sun: `fx overlay` gradients on shapes (linear on the panel, `--style radial` on
  the disc), plus `fx stroke` in red on the disc (**T-spotlight** logic: the light is a
  drawn object).
* Ground shadows: a black-and-white `fill noise --as-layer` cloud layer (`--stretch 0.5`,
  `multiply`), cut to pure black and white with `adjust threshold --level 118` and clipped
  to the ground rectangle, which leaves hard-edged black pools on the gray.
* **T-geo-shapes**: rocket, horizon ridge, and chair sling are `shape path` layers; the
  sling carries `pattern.js` stripes clipped to it.
* **T-extrude-type** on the giant word: `fx extrude --depth 37 --angle 315` with red
  sides (`--color '#dd3128'`), added before the word is rotated. The text stays live.
* **T-outline-type** logic on the icy word (light fill, dark outline) plus three tapered
  `paint stroke` drips for icicles.
* No grain: the print is clean.
* **T-fit-width**: the giant word is set to 880 px (81.5% W) with `--fit-width`.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The giant yellow word with its red edge.
2. The white sun directly below it, and the blue punchline inside the sun.
3. The figure in the chair (the eye follows the figure's gaze back to the sun).
4. Rocket, crater, small headline lines.

## 9. Build recipe

`subject.png` is your image here: the tourist, ideally seen from behind. Everything else
is drawn.

```bash
pixelox render js scripts/pattern.js -o stripes.png --size 1080x1620 --param kind=stripes --param color='#18629d' --param cell=46 --param weight=0.5 --param angle=7
pixelox new -o travel.pxo --size 1080x1620 --fill '#fcfdf7'
pixelox shape rect travel.pxo --in-place --name Sky --rect 24,24,1032x1572 --fill '#030303'
pixelox fx overlay travel.pxo --in-place --layer Sky --stops '#63645f@0,#63645f@0.34,#444441@0.6,#030303@1' --angle 90
pixelox shape ellipse travel.pxo --in-place --name Sun --rect 119,374,842x842 --fill '#fed882'
pixelox fx overlay travel.pxo --in-place --layer Sun --style radial --stops '#ffffff@0,#fffbe6@0.45,#fed882@1'
pixelox fx stroke travel.pxo --in-place --layer Sun --width 3 --color '#dd3128' --position outside
pixelox shape rect travel.pxo --in-place --name Ground --rect 24,1060,1032x536 --fill '#aeb3b1'
pixelox fill noise travel.pxo --in-place --as-layer --name Shadows --seed 17 --scale 33% --octaves 3 --warp 48 --stretch 0.5 --angle 90 --stops '#ffffff,#000000' --blend multiply
pixelox adjust threshold travel.pxo --in-place --layer Shadows --level 118
pixelox layer clip travel.pxo --in-place --layer Shadows
pixelox shape path travel.pxo --in-place --name Ridge --d 'M 24 1056 L 130 1048 L 210 1058 L 330 1050 L 470 1060 L 610 1052 L 700 1062 L 860 1050 L 980 1058 L 1056 1052 L 1056 1076 L 24 1080 Z' --fill '#030303'
pixelox shape path travel.pxo --in-place --name Rocket --d 'M 775 715 C 790 800 800 900 804 1000 L 832 1085 L 800 1058 L 750 1058 L 718 1085 L 746 1000 C 750 900 760 800 775 715 Z' --fill '#030303'
pixelox comp place travel.pxo subject.png --in-place --as Tourist --size x400 --at 38%,74% --anchor b
pixelox adjust bw travel.pxo --in-place --layer Tourist
pixelox filter cutout travel.pxo --in-place --layer Tourist --levels 5 --simplicity 60
pixelox shape path travel.pxo --in-place --name Sling --d 'M 237 1010 L 520 990 L 420 1296 L 330 1304 Z' --fill '#b7ac60'
pixelox comp place travel.pxo stripes.png --in-place --as Stripes --fit cover
pixelox layer clip travel.pxo --in-place --layer Stripes
pixelox text add travel.pxo --in-place --name Line1 --content "WHERE THE" --font @rounded-heavy --size 3.8% --tracking 60 --color '#fbed8d' --at 11.6%,7.6% --anchor l
pixelox transform rotate travel.pxo --in-place --layer Line1 --degrees -4
pixelox text add travel.pxo --in-place --name Giant --content "DAYLIGHT" --font @condensed-heavy --fit-width 880 --tracking 10 --color '#fbed8d' --at 50.9%,20.7% --anchor c --anchor-to ink
pixelox fx extrude travel.pxo --in-place --layer Giant --depth 37 --angle 315 --color '#dd3128'
pixelox transform rotate travel.pxo --in-place --layer Giant --degrees -6
pixelox text add travel.pxo --in-place --name Line3 --content "LASTS" --font @rounded-heavy --size 3.4% --tracking 80 --color '#fbed8d' --at 95%,30.6% --anchor r
pixelox transform rotate travel.pxo --in-place --layer Line3 --degrees -4
pixelox text add travel.pxo --in-place --name Punch1 --content "BRING" --font @rounded-heavy --size 3.4% --tracking 60 --color '#1f5fa8' --at 51.5%,37.8% --anchor r
pixelox text add travel.pxo --in-place --name Icy --content "ICE" --font @rounded-heavy --size 5.6% --tracking 40 --color '#8fd0f0' --stroke '#1f5fa8' --stroke-width 3 --at 53.5%,37.4% --anchor l
pixelox layer add travel.pxo --in-place --name Icicles
pixelox paint stroke travel.pxo --in-place --layer Icicles --points '598,640 598,690; 650,640 650,676; 706,640 706,698' --size 16 --hardness 90 --spacing 5 --taper 0,100% --color '#8fd0f0'
pixelox text add travel.pxo --in-place --name Punch2 --content "AND A CHAIR!" --font @rounded-heavy --size 4% --tracking 60 --color '#1f5fa8' --at 59%,43.6% --anchor c
pixelox export travel.pxo -o travel.png
```

Change first: the copy (keep the giant word at 7 to 8 letters), the two type color groups
(hot colors for the headline, cold for the punchline), then the scene shapes. Change
`--seed` on the `fill noise` line for another pattern of ground shadows.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Other destinations with the same staging: a giant ringed planet and a picnic; a huge
  moon over a campsite; a volcano and a sun umbrella; an iceberg and a swimmer. One
  enormous circle or triangle, one small back-view figure, one vehicle.
* Daylight palette: sky `#1e6fb8` to `#9fd3ef`, sun `#ffd23c`, ground sand `#e9c98f`,
  headline white with a navy extrusion.
* Event poster: replace the punchline with date and place in the rounded caps; keep the
  giant word for the event's name.
* Landscape: sun at the right third, headline stacked at the left over the sky.
* Pairs with T-sunburst (flat rays behind the sun), T-arc-type (a line of type following
  the sun's rim), T-paper-grain for an aged print.

## 11. Pitfalls

* A photographic figure in a vector scene: flatten it (`filter cutout`, 4 to 6 levels)
  and strip its color, or it will look like a sticker.
* Extrusion in a darker shade of the letter color instead of a contrasting one: the 3D
  edge vanishes on the dark sky. Yellow letters, red edge.
* Headline set level: it loses its showcard swing. Rotate each headline line 4 to 6
  degrees counter-clockwise; keep the punchline level for contrast.
* A sun with a gradient to orange or with rays: it becomes a sunset. Here the sun is a
  white disc with a thin yellow rim, and its heat is told by the black shadows.
* Too many small jokes (bottles, signs, footprints) at equal size: they compete with the
  figure. Two props at most, each under 8% H.
