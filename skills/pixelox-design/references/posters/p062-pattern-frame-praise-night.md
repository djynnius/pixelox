# p062: Wax-print frame with gold headline panel

## 1. Concept

A themed-service flyer: a near-black panel sits inside a border of bold wax-print pattern
(green, red, and gold arcs, stripes, and zigzags), and on the panel a chunky two-line
headline in yellow-to-orange gradient letters is crossed by a white signature-style script
word, with a golden saxophone beside it and two laughing hosts stepping in from the lower
left. It works because the loud pattern is kept to a thin frame: it sets the cultural tone
in 15% of the area, and the black panel gives the gold type and the faces a calm stage.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait 4:5. The panel is inset 7% W at the left, 5.5% W at the right, and 5.2% H at the
  top; it runs off the bottom edge, so the frame is a three-sided arch.
* Inside the panel: a center axis at 50% W for the logo and headline (upper half), then a
  split lower half: hosts in the left 57% W, a right-aligned info column from 47 to 89.5% W.
* Zones: logo 9.7 to 18.3% H; headline 23 to 48% H; script 41 to 52% H, overlapping the
  second headline line; hosts 50 to 100% H; info bar 61.1 to 64.3% H; time and venue 66.7 to
  74.6% H; second-venue note 78 to 83% H; streaming lines 93 to 95.5% H.
* The front host breaks the frame: his shoulder and arm cover the left border strip from
  62% H down, which ties panel and pattern together.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Pattern ground, full canvas (only the border strips show): dark green `#31530a` and
   deep red `#740603` fields with gold `#cfa14a` concentric arcs (ring spacing 4% W), black
   outlines, olive stripes on the right strip, zigzag bars at the top right.
2. Panel: `7%,5.2%,87.5% x 94.8%`, near-black `#16130e` with a faint woven texture.
3. Logo: a white ring badge, 10.8% W across, centered at 50% W, 14% H.
4. Headline line 1: one word of 6 letters, 16.5 to 80% W, cap top 23.1% H, cap height 12%
   H; fill runs from yellow `#f2c40e` at the top to orange `#d9741a` at the bottom, with a
   dark brown outline (0.7% W) and a hard brown-black shadow offset down-right. The letters
   are slightly rough and the line tilts 3 degrees up to the right.
5. Headline line 2: "&" plus one word of 4 letters, 18.5 to 77.5% W, cap top 36.2% H, cap
   height 11.5% H, same treatment.
6. Illustration: a golden saxophone, 15% W by 28% H, at 69 to 84% W, 24 to 52% H, tucked
   against the right end of both headline lines.
7. Script word: one word in thin white monoline script with a looped capital, 38.5 to 74%
   W, 41.4 to 52% H, rising 8 degrees, lying over the lower right of line 2 and the
   saxophone's bell.
8. Back host: a woman with long straight hair in a dark sequined dress, singing, head top
   54.5% H, centered at 35% W, running off the bottom.
9. Front host: a man in a maroon tunic, laughing, fists raised at chest height, head top
   50% H, centered at 15% W, cut by the left and bottom edges.
10. Info bar: `47.2%,61.1%,42.3% x 3.2%`, rust brown `#6a300f`, with one line of white
    caps: a bold 4-word invitation, a double vertical stroke, and the date in regular weight.
11. Time: white condensed bold, cap height 4% H, centered at 58% W, 69.5% H, with the time
    zone in small brackets below at 73% H.
12. Divider: a 1 px light vertical line at 65.2% W from 66.7 to 74.6% H.
13. Venue: name in white condensed bold caps at 67.5% W, 68.6% H; a two-line address in
    small light caps below.
14. Second-venue note: two right-aligned lines ending at 89.2% W: a light, widely spaced
    line at 79.2% H and a bold line at 82% H.
15. Streaming lines: two tiny right-aligned lines ending at 89.5% W, 93.4 and 94.9% H.
16. Grain over everything.

## 6. Imagery

* Two studio cutouts in warm, low-key light, both caught mid-song or mid-laugh; clothing
  in maroon and black-gold so they belong to the palette. They overlap by a third, enter
  from the lower left, and are cropped by the canvas edge, not by the panel.
* One illustration with transparency: a brass instrument in gold, upright, placed as an
  ornament at the end of the headline. Any single object that names the theme works (a
  drum, a microphone, a wheat sheaf, a crown).
* The pattern is drawn, not photographed: flat colors and black outlines in the style of
  printed cloth. The recipe approximates it with ring and stripe patterns (**T-pattern**)
  over red and green fields; a scan or vector of real cloth can replace the three pattern
  layers.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-frame-border**, as a panel: the pattern covers the canvas, the dark panel is drawn
  over it, inset 7% W, 5.5% W, and 5.2% H, open at the bottom.
* **T-pattern**: gold rings centered near the top-left corner (`cell=46`, `weight=0.42`),
  dark horizontal stripes over the right half (`cell=30`), olive diagonal stripes on top.
* **T-gradient-type** on the headline (`fx overlay`, bottom orange to top yellow), plus
  **T-block-shadow**: `fx stroke` 7 px outside in `#3a1a08`, and a hard shadow offset 7,9.
  The styles are copied to line 2 with `fx copy`. The letters are made chunkier with a
  12 px text stroke in the fill color (`text add --stroke`); the gradient covers it.
  Each line is fitted with `--fit-width`, placed by the center of its letters (`--anchor c
  --anchor-to ink`), and then rotated -3 degrees about that center.
* **T-script-overlap**: the script is about as tall as the headline caps, rotated -8
  degrees, placed over the lower right corner of line 2, with a soft shadow.
* **T-label-box**: the rust bar behind the invitation line, with a left-to-right gradient.
* **T-paper-grain**: `filter grain --as-layer`, amount 12, for the woven look of the panel.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The gold headline (brightest, largest, upper middle of the black panel).
2. The white script and the saxophone at its right end.
3. Down-left to the two faces.
4. Across to the rust bar (date), then time and venue below it, the note, the fine print.
5. The frame is seen all the time but never read.

Cap heights: headline 12 : time 4 : venue 2.2 : note 1.7 : bar 1.3 : fine print 0.9, about
13 : 4.4 : 2.4 : 1.9 : 1.4 : 1.

## 9. Build recipe

or object on transparency, ideally taller than wide), and `logo.png` are your images here.

Change first: the two headline words and the script word; then the three pattern colors
(`RedField`, the canvas fill, and the ring color) to match your theme; then the hosts.
With one host, center the cutout at 24% W.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Other cloth palettes: indigo `#1a2a6c`, white, and sky `#7fb2e5` (adire); black, white,
  and red (mud cloth, use `kind=checker` and `kind=grid`); pink, orange, and teal for a
  carnival night.
* Other frames, same panel: a tartan for a highland or harvest event (two stripe layers
  at 0 and 90 degrees), T-waves bands for a beach service, T-sunburst rays in two golds.
* Light panel: cream `#f3ead7` with dark brown text and a red-to-maroon headline.
* Cultural day, heritage month, wedding thanksgiving, jazz night, food festival. Square:
  panel inset 6% all round, headline and bar only, hosts dropped.
* Pairs with T-arc-type (a round seal with the date in place of the bar) and
  T-cutout-drop-shadow (soft) under the hosts when the panel is light.

## 11. Pitfalls

* Letting the pattern show through too much. Over 20% of the area it fights the faces and
  the type; keep the strips at 5 to 8% of the width.
* Headline colors outside the pattern's palette. The gold of the letters must be the gold
  of the cloth, or the frame looks pasted on.
* Script too thin to survive on the gradient letters: give it a soft shadow and keep its
  strokes at least 3 px wide at 1080 px, or use a bolder script.
* Hosts fully inside the panel look boxed in. Let the front one overlap the frame and run
  off the canvas edge.
* Six info items at six sizes. Group them as the reference does: one bar, one time and
  venue pair with a divider, one two-line note, one two-line footer.
