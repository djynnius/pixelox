# p052: Collage of overlapping vintage magazine covers

## 1. Concept

A square editorial image made of five magazine covers laid over each other like prints on
a table: one upright black-and-white fashion cover in the middle, and four tilted covers
that enter from the corners and are cut off by the canvas. It works because the middle
cover is pure black and white (a tall didone masthead, a hat, a veil, a black coat on
white paper) and each neighbor adds exactly one color: a red border, cream paper, a yellow
border. The crops make the viewer finish the hidden mastheads in their head.

## 2. Format and grid

* Square. No margins: every cover bleeds off at least one edge.
* Middle cover: upright, `13.5%,0%,70.5% x 100%` (it is taller than the canvas, so its
  masthead top and its lower edge are cut). Its own center axis is at 49% W.
* Four corner covers, each showing 15 to 33% W of itself:
  left cover tilted 11 degrees counter-clockwise, visible 0 to 33% W, 0 to 85% H;
  upper right cover upright, 84 to 100% W, 0 to 68.5% H;
  lower right cover tilted 6 degrees counter-clockwise, 82 to 100% W, 68 to 100% H;
  lower left cover upright, 0 to 13% W, 82 to 100% H.
* The tilted covers lean the same way (counter-clockwise); nothing is parallel to the
  middle cover except the two small upright ones.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: near-black `#0b0d0c` (it only shows as the lower right cover).
2. Middle cover paper: off-white `#f5f5f4`, `13.5%,0%,70.5% x 100%`.
3. Middle masthead: 5 capital letters, black, tall condensed didone, 64% W wide
   (20 to 84% W), cap height about 21% H with the top 2% cut by the canvas edge, baseline
   at 19.5% H.
4. Portrait (black and white, high contrast, on white): a wide flat-brimmed hat with its
   crown overlapping the masthead's lower edge (brim 23 to 80% W at 26 to 32% H), a face
   behind a dotted net veil centered at 49% W, 42% H, a dark patterned scarf flying to the
   right, a white scarf falling down the center, and a black coat that fills 55 to 100% H.
5. Cover lines: a 2-line bold italic serif pair at 73 to 84% W, 37 to 41.5% H, and one
   more line at 44.5% H; cap height 1.5% H; near-black.
6. Dateline: month and year over a price, 2 centered lines of bold serif caps, cap height
   1.3% H, at 75% W, 95 to 98% H.
7. Lower left cover: yellow `#e9b21e` border around a slate `#5c6367` picture field with
   large serif letters in cream and red; only a 13% W x 18% H corner is visible.
8. Upper right cover: cream paper `#dcd8c8`, a black brush-script masthead cut off after
   two letters, and a painted tan-and-red oval (22% W) as its illustration.
9. Lower right cover: black, white light serif masthead cut off after two letters (cap
   height 4.5% H), a tiny price line above it, a hairline of white along its left edge.
10. Left cover (on top of everything): red `#e01f26` border 2.6% W thick, black field, a
    white keyline 0.5% W inside the border, a white light serif masthead (cap height 13% H,
    only its last one or two letters visible), a tracked small-caps word under it, and a
    giant bold red serif question in lowercase (x-height 9% H) whose last three characters
    and question mark are visible at 45 to 62% H.

## 4. Palette

Black and white plus one red: the oldest editorial scheme there is. Cream and yellow are
small and sit at the far edges, so they read as "other magazines", not as accents of the
main cover.

## 6. Imagery

One photograph, on the middle cover: a studio fashion portrait of a woman from the hips
up, facing the camera, in a black wide-brimmed disc hat, a dotted net veil over her whole
face, a white scarf knotted at the throat, and a black coat with full sleeves, hands on
hips. It is printed as hard black and white on white paper with almost no gray
(**T-mono-on-color** with a white ground: `adjust bw`, then `adjust levels --in-black 45
--in-white 190`). The picture is built from three big shapes: the horizontal black bar of
the hat brim, the white triangle of the scarf, and the black mass of the coat. Pick a
portrait with one strong horizontal (a hat, a fringe, sunglasses) and dark clothing.
The other covers need no photos: flat fields, a border, big cropped type, one painted oval.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-text-behind-subject**: the masthead is placed first, the portrait after it, so the
  hat crown overlaps the letters' feet by 1 to 5% H.
* **T-frame-border** for the left cover (red border, black field, white keyline) and the
  lower left cover (yellow border).
* Tilted covers: each one is built upright from 4 to 7 layers, baked into one layer with
  `layer merge`, rotated with `transform rotate --around` one of its corners, and pushed
  partly off the canvas with `transform move --by`.
* Tall masthead, **T-stretch-type**: `--scale-x 61.7%` with `--fit-width 690` makes the
  didone 1.6x as tall as its natural proportions (690 x 236 px) and keeps the text live.
  `--at 52%,0-18` starts it 18 px above the top edge, so the letters' tops are cut.
* No shadows between the covers, no grain: the scans lie flat. Depth comes only from
  overlap and the tilt.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The veiled face under the hat brim: the only detailed area, in the middle, framed by
   the two largest black shapes.
2. Up to the black masthead.
3. Left to the red border and the red question, the only saturated color.
4. Around the corners: script masthead, the oval, the lower right masthead, the yellow
   corner; cover lines and the dateline last.

Cap heights: masthead 21 : left masthead 13 : red question 9 : script 5.5 : cover lines
1.5, about 14 : 9 : 6 : 3.7 : 1. Type is either huge and cropped or tiny; nothing is in
between.

## 9. Build recipe

`subject.png` is your image here: a cutout portrait, ideally with a hat or another strong
dark shape at the top. The dateline is set in paper white because it lands on the dark
clothing of a bust; with a photo that leaves white paper there, use `#1a1a1a`.

```bash
pixelox new -o collage.pxo --size 1080x1080 --fill '#0b0d0c'
pixelox shape rect collage.pxo --in-place --name Paper --rect 146,0,762x1080 --fill '#f5f5f4'
pixelox text add collage.pxo --in-place --name Masthead --content "REVUE" --font @didone --weight 700 --scale-x 61.7% --fit-width 690 --tracking -10 --color '#0a0a0a' --at 52%,0-18 --anchor t --anchor-to ink
pixelox comp place collage.pxo subject.png --in-place --as Subject --size x900 --at 49%,100% --anchor b
pixelox adjust bw collage.pxo --in-place --layer Subject
pixelox adjust levels collage.pxo --in-place --layer Subject --in-black 45 --in-white 190 --gamma 0.85
pixelox text add collage.pxo --in-place --name Lines --content $'The Return of\nthe Veil' --font @oldstyle --weight 700 --style italic --size 2.3% --leading 135% --align right --color '#1a1a1a' --at 83%,37% --anchor tr
pixelox text add collage.pxo --in-place --name Lines2 --content "Paris Notebook" --font @oldstyle --weight 700 --style italic --size 2.3% --color '#1a1a1a' --at 83%,44.5% --anchor tr
pixelox text add collage.pxo --in-place --name Dateline --content $'MAY 1952\nPRICE 50c' --font "Bodoni 72" --weight 700 --size 1.9% --tracking 20 --leading 130% --align center --color '#f5f5f4' --at 71%,96% --anchor c
pixelox shape rect collage.pxo --in-place --name YellowCover --rect 0,82%,13.3%x18% --fill '#e9b21e'
pixelox shape rect collage.pxo --in-place --name YellowInner --rect 0,86%,11.5%x14% --fill '#5c6367'
pixelox text add collage.pxo --in-place --name YellowLetter --content "C" --font "Bodoni 72" --weight 700 --size 12% --color '#d8402e' --at 3%,91%
pixelox shape rect collage.pxo --in-place --name CreamCover --rect 84%,0,16%x68.5% --fill '#dcd8c8'
pixelox text add collage.pxo --in-place --name Script --content "Soiree" --font "Brush Script MT" --size 11% --color '#141414' --at 85.5%,0.5%
pixelox shape ellipse collage.pxo --in-place --name Blob --rect 87%,13%,22%x26% --fill '#c9a36a' --stroke '#d9553a' --stroke-width 16
pixelox transform rotate collage.pxo --in-place --layer Blob --degrees -24
pixelox shape rect collage.pxo --in-place --name BlackCover --rect 700,600,330x440 --fill '#0b0d0c'
pixelox shape line collage.pxo --in-place --name BlackEdge --from 701,600 --to 701,1040 --stroke '#f2f2f2' --stroke-width 2
pixelox text add collage.pxo --in-place --name BlackPrice --content "PRICE \$2.50" --font @oldstyle --weight 400 --size 1.3% --tracking 60 --color '#f2f2f2' --at 760,640
pixelox text add collage.pxo --in-place --name BlackMast --content "NORTH" --font @oldstyle --weight 400 --size 7% --color '#f2f2f2' --at 792,665
pixelox layer merge collage.pxo --in-place --layers BlackCover,BlackEdge,BlackPrice,BlackMast --name CoverBR
pixelox transform rotate collage.pxo --in-place --layer CoverBR --degrees -6 --around 700,600
pixelox transform move collage.pxo --in-place --layer CoverBR --by 186,135
pixelox shape rect collage.pxo --in-place --name RedBorder --rect 100,90,640x900 --fill '#e01f26'
pixelox shape rect collage.pxo --in-place --name RedField --rect 128,118,584x844 --fill '#000000'
pixelox shape rect collage.pxo --in-place --name RedKeyline --rect 142,132,556x816 --fill none --stroke '#ffffff' --stroke-width 5
pixelox text add collage.pxo --in-place --name RedMast --content "LEDGER" --font @oldstyle --weight 400 --size 17% --color '#ffffff' --at 680,150 --anchor tr
pixelox text add collage.pxo --in-place --name RedSub --content "MAGAZINE" --font @oldstyle --weight 400 --size 2% --tracking 250 --color '#ffffff' --at 676,340 --anchor tr
pixelox text add collage.pxo --in-place --name RedQuestion --content "what now?" --font Georgia --weight 700 --size 17% --tracking -10 --color '#e01f26' --at 690,740 --anchor r
pixelox text add collage.pxo --in-place --name RedIssue --content "VOL. 12  NO. 14" --font @geometric-sans --weight 500 --size 1.3% --tracking 80 --color '#1a0506' --at 690,976 --anchor r
pixelox layer merge collage.pxo --in-place --layers RedBorder,RedField,RedKeyline,RedMast,RedSub,RedQuestion,RedIssue --name CoverL
pixelox transform rotate collage.pxo --in-place --layer CoverL --degrees -11 --around 740,990
pixelox transform move collage.pxo --in-place --layer CoverL --by -384,-142
pixelox export collage.pxo -o collage.png
```

How the tilted covers are placed: build the cover upright anywhere inside the canvas
(`layer merge` drops what lies outside it), rotate it around the corner that will stay
visible (`--around 740,990` is the red cover's lower right corner), then move it by the
distance between that corner and its target (here to 356,848: `--by -384,-142`).
Change first: the middle masthead and portrait, then the red cover's question, then the
tilt angles (keep them between 5 and 12 degrees).

## 10. Variations and pairings

* One accent, other hue: swap the red border for cobalt `#1f4fd8` or emerald `#0f8a5f`
  and keep everything else black, white, and cream.
* A "moodboard" for a brand: replace the corner covers with product shots, swatches of
  flat color, and a cropped logo, all tilted 5 to 12 degrees around one upright hero.
* Use only the middle cover (layers 2 to 6) as a stand-alone fashion cover in 3:4.
* Portrait 4:5: keep the middle cover at 70% W and let the corner covers show a little
  more of their height.
* Pairs with T-paper-grain (amount 12) for a scanned feel, T-halftone-portrait on one of
  the corner covers, and T-torn-collage scraps under the covers.

## 11. Pitfalls

* All covers upright: it becomes a grid, not a pile. Tilt at least two, all in the same
  direction, by clearly different angles (6 and 11 degrees).
* Showing whole neighbor covers. Each corner cover shows a third or less of itself; when a
  full masthead is readable it competes with the middle one.
* Gray photo. The portrait must snap to black and white; raise `--in-black` and lower
  `--in-white` until the coat is solid black and the paper is clean.
* More than one saturated color of the same size. Red owns 9% of the area; yellow must
  stay under 4%.
* Drop shadows under the covers. They turn a flat editorial collage into a desk mockup;
  leave them out or keep them under 15% opacity.
