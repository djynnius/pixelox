# p036: Black exhibition poster with color shapes

## 1. Concept

An art-exhibition poster on solid black: a three-letter word in fat white capitals turned
on its side stands like a pillar on the left, and beside it a tight column of flat,
full-saturation shapes (half ring, bar, disc, arch, triangle, small ring) stacks to the
same height. Dates and details sit below in white condensed type, and a red bar closes
the page. It works because type and shapes form one block with a single outline, the
word being simply the first "shape" in the column, and because six pure colors on black
need no further decoration. Shown as a hanging sheet with a shadow; the mockup is not
part of the design.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait, close to A-series. Side margins 10% W. The page is built on a square unit of
  21% W (the outer radius of the half ring).
* Zones: artist line 6 to 9% H; picture block 13.5 to 66% H; details 70 to 85% H; red bar
  89 to 93.6% H; 6.4% H of black below the bar.
* The picture block is 67% W wide (16.5 to 83.8% W) and has two columns: the vertical
  word (16.5 to 39.3% W) and the shape column (41.9 to 83.8% W), separated by a 2.6% W
  gap. Both run from 13.5 to 66% H exactly.
* The details row repeats the split: date (10 to 38% W), a vertical rule at 41.3% W, then
  the event type and entry line (45 to 90% W). The rule lines up with the gap above it.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: black `#000000`.
2. Artist line: white condensed caps, centered at 50% W, 7.7% H.
3. Vertical word: three white letters in a heavy wide sans, rotated 90 degrees
   counter-clockwise (reads bottom to top), box `16.5%,13.5%,22.8% x 52.5%`. The letter
   height (now horizontal) is 22.8% W.
4. Yellow half ring `#fec200`: the lower half of a ring (a "U"), outer radius 21% W,
   inner radius 10% W, flat top at 14% H, spanning 41.9 to 83.8% W.
5. Pink bar `#ff5280`: `41.9%,27.2%,6.1% x 26.7%`, hanging from the half ring's left arm.
6. Green block `#04a404`: `60.6%,31.2%,19.8% x 12.4%`, a rectangle whose right side is cut
   by a concave arc.
7. Red ring `#f40504`: outer diameter 7.3% W, stroke 1.4% W, at 79.9% W, 35.7% H, in the
   bite of the green block.
8. Orange disc `#fd6200`: diameter 13.4% W, centered at 54.7% W, 46.4% H, overlapping the
   pink bar's right edge.
9. White triangle: right-angled, pointing left, right edge on the column edge (83.8% W)
   from 45.3 to 57.6% H, apex at 76% W.
10. Blue arch `#41d2fb`: the upper half of a ring, outer radius 17.6% W, inner 8.8% W,
    flat base at 65.6% H, spanning 41.9 to 77% W.
11. Date: two lines of white condensed caps, fitted to one width (10 to 38% W), cap tops
    70% and 79.1% H.
12. Vertical rule: white, 2 px, at 41.3% W from 70.3 to 84.6% H.
13. Event type: one word of white condensed caps at 45.1% W, cap top 70.3% H, 45% W wide;
    below it "FREE ENTRY" at cap top 80.7% H, 30% W wide.
14. Red bar: `0,89%,100% x 4.6%`, `#ee1615`, edge to edge, with a centered white line.

## 6. Imagery

No photograph. The picture is a column of seven flat shapes that share edges: the half
ring's arms sit on the bar and the green block, the disc overlaps the bar, the arch
closes the bottom. The shapes loosely suggest letters or a totem without spelling
anything.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-geo-shapes**: half rings as `shape path` (outer arc, line in, inner arc back, `Z`);
  `A r r 0 0 0` from left to right draws the lower half, `0 0 1` the upper half. The
  concave bite in the green block is an arc with sweep 0 drawn downward.
* **T-vertical-type**: add the word, `transform rotate --degrees -90`, then
  `transform scale --size x803` sets its length to the column height, and `transform move
  --anchor bl` puts its corner on the grid.
* **T-fit-width**: the two date lines fitted to 302 px; the event word to 485 px.
* **T-rules**: one vertical rule between the two detail columns.
* **T-info-bar**, reduced to a full-bleed strip with one centered line.
* No texture at all; edges are razor sharp.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The shape column and the vertical word together (one block, half the page).
2. The date at the lower left (largest horizontal type), across the rule to the event
   word and the entry line.
3. The red bar with the contact line.
4. The artist line at the top.

## 9. Build recipe

Nothing to supply. Canvas 1080x1528.

```bash

pixelox text add show.pxo --in-place --name Artist --content "ARTIST NAME" --font "DIN Condensed" --weight 700 --size 4% --tracking 20 --color '#ffffff' --at 50%,7.9% --anchor c
pixelox text add show.pxo --in-place --name Word --content "NOW" --font "Avenir Next" --weight 900 --size 20% --color '#ffffff' --at 50%,50% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate show.pxo --in-place --layer Word --degrees=-90
pixelox transform scale show.pxo --in-place --layer Word --size x803
pixelox transform move show.pxo --in-place --layer Word --to 178,1007 --anchor bl
pixelox shape path show.pxo --in-place --name HalfRing --d 'M 453 214 A 226 226 0 0 0 905 214 L 789 214 A 110 110 0 0 1 569 214 Z' --fill '#fec200'

pixelox shape path show.pxo --in-place --name Triangle --d 'M 905 692 L 905 880 L 820 786 Z' --fill '#ffffff'
```

Change first: the vertical word (3 to 4 letters; the scale step sets its length, so a
longer word gets thinner letters), then the dates and event word, then the six colors.
To rearrange the shapes, keep every size a multiple of the unit (226 px here) or its
half, and keep each shape touching two others.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* White ground `#f4f1ea` with black type and the same six colors (a museum look); swap
  the white triangle to black.
* Fewer colors: yellow, red, blue plus black and white for a primary-color version.
* Replace the shape column with one photo cropped to the same 42% W x 52% H box
  (`layer clip` to a rectangle) and keep everything else.
* Landscape: vertical word at the far left, shapes in a horizontal row, details in a
  column at the right.
* Suits gallery openings, design fairs, school art shows, jazz and film programs. Pairs
  with T-pattern (dots inside one shape), T-paper-grain for a printed feel, and
  A-vertical-type when two words flank the shapes.

## 11. Pitfalls

* Shapes scattered with gaps between them read as clip art. They must touch or overlap,
  and the column's outer edges must be straight.
* The vertical word and the column at different heights. Fit the word's length to the
  column exactly (same top and bottom).
* Tints or outlines on the shapes weaken the effect; use flat fills at full saturation.
* Condensed detail type set too small against the giant word: keep the date at a cap
  height of at least 5% H so it survives next to a 23% W letter height.
* Reading direction: rotate -90 so the word reads upward from the bottom; a +90 rotation
  on the left edge feels like a book spine and fights the shapes.
