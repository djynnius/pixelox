# p042: Ice-blue double-exposure fantasy cover

## 1. Concept

A young-adult fantasy cover in a single blue: the profile bust of a woman, facing right,
is a window onto a palace front with a glowing doorway at the top of a long staircase;
bare flowering branches grow out of her crown like antlers, a small dragon flies past her
forehead, and her raised hand enters from the right edge. The title is broken into three
voices that sit around and on the figure. It works because the silhouette gives a hard,
simple outer shape while the inside is deep and detailed, and because the whole sheet is
one hue from near-white mist to navy, so six separate photographs read as one image.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait 2:3 (close to it: 1 : 1.56). Loose center axis at 49% W: the doorway, the
  staircase, the big title word, the ornament, and the author line are all centered on it.
* The figure fills the sheet: crown at 19% H, face profile 74 to 81% W between 33 and 49%
  H, body 18 to 81% W running off the bottom. Branches reach the top edge and the left
  edge (2 to 65% W, 3 to 37% H).
* Text zones: small title words left of the figure at 8 to 29% W, 49 to 54% H (in the
  mist); a curved word right of the chin at 78 to 90% W, 43.5 to 60% H; the big word on
  the chest at 25 to 73% W, 75 to 82% H; ornament at 85.4% H; author at 92% H; credit
  95% H.
* The doorway glow (49% W, 58% H) is the center of everything: the eye is pulled there
  first and the big word sits one stair-flight below it.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: pale ice blue `#dff1f9` with faint ghost tree trunks and soft mist, slightly
   whiter toward the edges.
2. Figure silhouette: woman's head, long loose hair, neck, and shoulders in profile facing
   right. Solid enough to read as a shape; long wisps of hair break the left edge.
3. Scene inside the figure: an ornate two-story palace facade (balcony with arches at 37
   to 46% H) above a wide stone staircase flanked by lion statues, climbing to a tall
   arched door that glows pale cyan `#bfe6ff`; a tiny dark robed figure stands in the door.
   Tones run from navy `#142038` at the body's edges to mid blue `#3b6991` on the stone.
4. Branches: dark-blue twigs with small pale blossoms growing up and left from the crown,
   semi-transparent where they cross the mist.
5. Dragon: a small winged silhouette in slate blue `#3b5f8f`, 73 to 93.5% W, 14 to 23% H.
6. Hand: a raised open hand and forearm in navy at the right edge, 78 to 100% W, 51 to 69%
   H, fingers pointing up toward the curved word.
7. Mist: near-white fog creeping in from the bottom-left and bottom-right corners over the
   figure's edges.
8. Title, small words: 2 lines of bold serif caps in medium blue, right-aligned to 29% W.
9. Title, curved word: 6 serif caps stepped down a curve shaped like a closing bracket,
   each letter nearly upright (tilted less than 30 degrees) and shrinking from 2.4% H at
   the top to 1.8% H at the bottom.
10. Title, big word: 3 thin lowercase-style letters in silver-white with a soft blue-white
    glow, cap height 7.5% H, on the dark chest.
11. Ornament: a hairline scroll with a small four-point star flare at its center.
12. Author: tracked serif caps in pale blue; credit line: smaller tracked sans caps in
    mid blue.
13. Grain.

The title is read as "small words, curved word, big word": three sizes, three positions,
one sentence. Only the last word is display-sized.

## 6. Imagery

Two photographs and three small cutouts. (1) A profile portrait with long hair on a plain
backdrop, cut out. (2) An architectural scene with strong central perspective and one
light: stairs to a doorway, a nave, a forest path, a street at night. The scene is clipped
inside the figure with **T-double-exposure** so the door falls on the chest and the roof
line on the skull. (3) Twigs, a flying creature, a hand: drawn or cut out, placed as dark
shapes. Everything is then mapped onto one blue ramp (**T-duotone** as an adjustment
layer): `#0b1424` to `#233f61` to `#3b6991` to `#b6d7ec` to `#f2fbff`.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-double-exposure**: subject converted to black and white and pushed down to a dark
  silhouette (`adjust levels --gamma 0.75 --out-white 100`), scene clipped to it in
  `screen`, so only the scene's lights lift out of the dark shape.
* **T-duotone** as `adjust gradient-map --as-layer` above figure and ground, five stops.
* Door glow: small radial gradient, `#eaf7ff` to `#9fd4ff` at alpha 0, screen.
* Branches: `paint stroke --d` with five curves, `--taper 0,90%`, so each stroke thins to
  a point; blossoms from `particles.js` (`shape=dot`, `focus` on the upper left).
* Ground haze: one `fill noise --as-layer` line in pale blue to white, `--stretch 2
  --angle 90` so the clouds stand upright like ghost trunks, at 45%.
* **T-smoke** logic for the mist: pale blobs at the bottom corners, blurred 60.
* **T-arc-type** for the curved word (`text on-path`, half circle on the right side).
* **T-gradient-type** (silver: white to blue-gray) and the tight glow of **T-neon-glow**
  on the big word.
* **T-paper-grain**: `filter grain --as-layer --amount 8`, last.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The glowing doorway inside the dark figure.
2. Down the stairs to the big word.
3. Out to the face and the curved word at the chin, across to the small words on the
   left; the sentence is assembled.
4. Up into the branches and the dragon; last, the author at the foot.

Cap heights: big word 7.5 : curved word 2.4 : small words 1.8 : author 1.5 : credit 0.9.

## 9. Build recipe

`subject.png` (a profile cutout) and `photo.png` (the scene with one bright focal point)
are your images here. Move the Door glow onto your scene's light, and start the branch
curves at the top of your subject's head.

```bash
pixelox render js scripts/particles.js -o blossoms.png --size 1080x1620 --seed 5 --param shape=dot --param colors='#d9ecf8,#9cc3e0,#ffffff' --param count=60 --param min=0.4 --param max=1.3 --param focus='0.3,0.16,0.3'
pixelox render js scripts/blobs.js -o mist.png --size 1080x1620 --param bg=transparent --param colors='#eef8fd' --param at='0,0.97;1,0.92;0.04,0.72;0.98,0.72' --param size=0.4 --param alpha=0.95
pixelox new -o cover.pxo --size 1080x1620 --fill '#dff1f9'
pixelox fill noise cover.pxo --in-place --as-layer --name Haze --seed 7 --scale 40% --warp 32 --stretch 2 --angle 90 --stops '#b6d7ec,#ffffff' --opacity 45
pixelox comp place cover.pxo subject.png --in-place --as Figure --size x1400 --at 50%,100% --anchor b
pixelox adjust bw cover.pxo --in-place --layer Figure
pixelox adjust levels cover.pxo --in-place --layer Figure --gamma 0.75 --out-white 100
pixelox comp place cover.pxo photo.png --in-place --as Scene --size 1080x1620 --at 0,0
pixelox layer clip cover.pxo --in-place --layer Scene
pixelox layer blend cover.pxo --in-place --layer Scene --mode screen
pixelox adjust gradient-map cover.pxo --in-place --stops '#0b1424@0,#233f61@0.3,#3b6991@0.55,#b6d7ec@0.86,#f2fbff@1' --as-layer --name Blue
pixelox layer add cover.pxo --in-place --name Door
pixelox fill gradient cover.pxo --in-place --layer Door --kind radial --from 49%,58% --to 49%,67% --stops '#eaf7ffff@0,#9fd4ff99@0.4,#9fd4ff00@1'
pixelox layer blend cover.pxo --in-place --layer Door --mode screen
pixelox layer add cover.pxo --in-place --name Branches
pixelox paint stroke cover.pxo --in-place --layer Branches --d 'M 500 380 C 420 300 380 200 300 60 M 470 400 C 360 360 250 300 110 270 M 530 370 C 520 250 570 160 660 50 M 450 420 C 330 440 200 420 40 500 M 392 262 C 330 250 290 190 215 170' --size 14 --hardness 85 --spacing 4 --taper 0,90% --color '#233f61'
pixelox comp place cover.pxo blossoms.png --in-place --as Blossoms --fit cover
pixelox shape path cover.pxo --in-place --name Creature --d 'M 790 300 Q 850 215 912 288 Q 960 228 1012 250 Q 985 292 992 342 Q 950 300 916 320 Q 890 362 850 330 Q 822 318 790 300 Z' --fill '#3b5f8f' --opacity 85
pixelox comp place cover.pxo mist.png --in-place --as Mist --fit cover
pixelox filter gaussian cover.pxo --in-place --layer Mist --sigma 60
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name SmallWords --content $'CROWNS\nOF THE' --font @oldstyle --weight 700 --size 2.5% --tracking 60 --leading 150% --align right --color '#3f72b8' --at 29%,51.5% --anchor r
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Curved --content "SILENT" --font @oldstyle --weight 400 --size 3.2% --tracking 420 --color '#3f72b8'
pixelox text on-path cover.pxo --in-place --layer Curved --d 'M 850 700 A 130 130 0 0 1 850 960' --side outside
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name BigWord --content "sea" --font @didone --weight 400 --size 15% --tracking 120 --color '#f2f8ff' --at 49%,78.5% --anchor c --anchor-to ink
pixelox fx overlay cover.pxo --in-place --layer BigWord --stops '#aebfd6@0,#f2f8ff@0.6,#ffffff@1' --angle 90
pixelox fx outer-glow cover.pxo --in-place --layer BigWord --blur 26 --color '#bfe6ff' --opacity 70
pixelox shape line cover.pxo --in-place --name Ornament --from 33%,85.4% --to 65%,85.4% --stroke '#cfe6f7' --stroke-width 2 --opacity 80
pixelox shape star cover.pxo --in-place --name Star --center 49%,85.4% --points 4 --outer 26 --inner 4 --fill '#ffffff'
pixelox fx outer-glow cover.pxo --in-place --layer Star --blur 22 --color '#bfe6ff' --opacity 90
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Author --content "A . AUTHOR" --font @oldstyle --weight 400 --size 2.1% --tracking 250 --color '#a9c4dc' --at 49%,92% --anchor c
pixelox text add cover.pxo --in-place --name Credit --content "COVER STUDIO" --font @geometric-sans --weight 500 --size 1.25% --tracking 300 --color '#5f86b0' --at 49%,95% --anchor c
pixelox filter grain cover.pxo --in-place --as-layer --name Grain --amount 8
pixelox export cover.pxo -o cover.png
```

Change first: the two photos; then the blue ramp (keep five steps from near-black to
near-white in one hue); then the three title parts. For the upright stepped letters of
the original, set each letter of the curved word as its own layer instead of `text
on-path`.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Hue swaps for genre: ember (near-black brown to orange to cream) for desert or dragon
  stories; forest green to pale mint for fae; plum to rose for romance; graphite to
  white for crime.
* Other silhouettes as windows: a wolf, a crown, a ship, a keyhole, a tree. The scene
  inside should have one central vanishing point.
* Film poster: move the big word to 88% H, add a credit block under it, drop the author.
* Square album cover: crop to 15 to 82% H.
* Pairs with T-silhouette (flat creature shapes), T-lens-flare on the door, T-vignette in
  navy at the bottom, and T-photo-fade if the body should dissolve into mist.

## 11. Pitfalls

* The scene's bright area lands on the face: the profile disappears into the mist. Keep
  the face and the back of the head dark; put the light on the chest.
* Two hues (a warm door in a blue picture) can work, but then the title must take the
  warm color too; half-measures look like a mistake.
* All three title parts at similar sizes: it becomes a scattered sentence. One part is
  4 times bigger than the others.
* Small title words in the dark figure, or the big word on the pale mist: each part needs
  the ground that contrasts with it (blue type on mist, white type on navy).
* Branches as thick as the neck. They start at 14 px and taper to nothing, and they are
  one step lighter than the figure's navy.
