# p054: Two-speaker flyer with gold glow

## 1. Concept

A service flyer for two speakers: two men in gray suits, cut out, stand shoulder to
shoulder in front of a column of golden light on a charcoal ground, each with a small
three-line name plate beside him, and below them a two-word headline stacks a wide white
word on top of a tall orange-gradient word. It works because the page has one warm zone
(the light behind the heads, the orange word, the gold details) inside a cold dark frame,
and because the two headline words differ in everything (width, weight, color) yet lock
together by overlapping. Presented in the source as a mockup on a blurred concert photo
with seller badges; only the flat flyer is described. Its bottom edge is hidden there;
the proportions read as about 3:4.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait 3:4. Center axis at 50% W. Margins 3.5% W at the top corners (badge), 13% W for
  the headline, 5% W for the name plates.
* Zones: presenter line 3.7% H; start-time badge in the top-left corner, 1.6 to 12% H;
  speakers 10 to 60% H; pill 58.8 to 63.2% H; headline 65 to 84% H; three contact rows
  87.5, 90, and 93.6% H.
* The two figures overlap in the middle: the left one stands behind and lower (head top
  13% H, face on 30% W), the right one in front and higher (head top 10% H, face on 65% W).
  Their bodies make an inverted V that points down at the pill.
* Name plates sit in the outer gutters at different heights: right plate at 79 to 95% W,
  27.5 to 34% H (beside the right man's chin); left plate at 5 to 19% W, 42.5 to 49% H
  (beside the left man's shoulder). Diagonal placement, mirrored by the badge at top left.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: charcoal `#1e1e23`, slightly bluer (`#252b36`) toward the top and at the left
   and right edges around 55% H (`#44586e`), warmer brown-black (`#312c28`) at the bottom
   left. A ghosted crowd photo (raised hands, blue-gray, about 12% opacity) fills the top
   third behind everything.
2. Gold light column: a soft glow centered at 50% W, 28% H, about 30% W wide and 45% H
   tall, core `#d5aa70`, edges `#84622d`, crossed by vertical bright bars (stage-light or
   organ-pipe look) 1.2% W wide every 2.8% W.
3. Hairline circle: 1 px light gray-blue ring, center 48% W, 45% H, radius 44.7% W; only
   its upper arc shows, passing over the heads' level and behind the figures.
4. Left figure (behind): waist-up cutout, body turned to the right, head 16% W wide.
5. Right figure (front): waist-up cutout, facing the camera, one hand at his jacket button;
   overlaps the left figure's shoulder. Both: gray suit `#6c6d6f` to `#a4a3a2`, white
   shirt, dark tie, glasses.
6. Bottom shade: ground color, transparent at 42% H, solid by 57% H; it swallows the
   figures at hip level.
7. Dust: fine white specks over the whole page, densest in the corners, 10 to 60% opaque.
8. Presenter line: small tracked light-gray caps, centered, 3.7% H.
9. Badge: gold ring (stroke 0.45% W) with a thin white segment on its upper left,
   14.6% W across, center 10.5% W, 6.8% H; inside, "STARTS" small and the hour large, white.
10. Name plates: tiny gray role label, then title (white), first name (gold), surname
    (white), each plate centered on its own axis (87% W and 12% W).
11. Pill: `27.2%,58.8%,45.6% x 4.4%`, dark fill, 2 px gold outline, cream caps inside.
12. Headline word 2 (lower, drawn first): tall condensed caps, 13.3 to 86.9% W, cap top
    69.8% H, baseline 83.9% H, gradient from orange `#f97c1c` (left) to peach-gold `#fdc56d`
    (right).
13. Headline word 1 (upper, on top): wide extended white caps, 22.5 to 78% W, cap top
    65.2% H, baseline 70.8% H, with a soft black shadow that darkens the top of word 2.
14. Contact rows: address (gray, regular), web and email (white, bold, tracked), then
    three small gold social badges (a disc, a "LIVE" tag, a play button), 37 to 62.5% W.

ultra-condensed heavy face (orange word), and a neutral geometric sans for the small rows.

5.6% H; the orange word is 74% W wide at 14.1% H, 2.5 times taller.

## 6. Imagery

Two studio cutouts of men in matching gray suits (in the source it is one model in two
poses), waist-up, evenly lit from the front, sharp edges, no outline. Natural color, but
the wardrobe is neutral so they stay inside the palette. Size them so each head is 16 to
17% W wide; stagger the head tops by 3% H and let the front figure hide the rear figure's
near shoulder. The gold column behind the heads acts as a halo and separates dark hair
from the dark ground. A faint crowd photo in the top third is optional texture. If the
two people are photographed in different light, grade both first with `adjust bw` at
40% or T-color-grade so they match.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-spotlight**: a radial gold glow behind the heads (center 50%, 28% H; radius 26% H)
  instead of a product stage light. In the original the glow is a column, taller than
  wide; the round version is the one-command build.
* **T-pattern** stripes: vertical light bars (`kind=stripes`, `cell=30`, `weight=0.4`),
  blurred, clipped to the glow with `layer clip` so they fade out with it, in screen mode.
* **T-cutout-drop-shadow**, soft variant, on the front figure only (offset 0,10, blur 30,
  50%) to detach it from the rear figure.
* **T-gradient-type** on headline word 2: `fx overlay` left to right, `#f97c1c` to
  `#fdc56d`. **T-fit-width** on both headline words.
* **T-pill-button**, outlined variant with a dark fill, in gold.
* Dust: `particles.js` dots in screen mode. Bottom shade: a gradient layer in the ground
  color (`Shade`, 42 to 57% H). It is a layer, not a mask, because it has to cover both
  cutouts and the glow at once.
* **T-icon-row**, badge variant: the social row is three gold badges with dark `shape
  icon` glyphs: `at-sign` on a disc, the "LIVE" tag, and `play` (filled) on a rounded
  rectangle. The set has no social-network logos; the neutral glyph stands in for one.
* **T-haze-band** in [techniques.md](../techniques.md) is the tested library version of the glow band that hides the lower edges of the cutouts and photo seams; start from it when reusing this idea.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The orange word (largest, warmest), read together with the white word sitting on it.
2. Up to the two faces in the gold light.
3. Out to the name plates (right first: it is higher and beside the nearer face).
4. The start-time badge at top left.
5. Pill, then contact rows; presenter line last.

Cap-height ratio: orange word 14.1 : white word 5.6 : pill and badge time 2 : presenter
1.4 : name plates and contact 1.2 : labels 0.6, about 12 : 4.7 : 1.7 : 1.2 : 1 : 0.5.

## 9. Build recipe

here: waist-up cutouts. With real waist-up photos use `--size 620x` and `--size 560x`
so the heads come out 16 to 17% W wide; the sizes below suit the bust-shaped stand-ins.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Accent swaps (glow, ring, first names, lower word): electric blue `#2f8bff` to `#8fd0ff`
  on the same charcoal; magenta `#e0218a` to `#ff9ac8` on plum `#1a0f1e`; emerald
  `#0fa968` to `#a6f0c6` on `#0e1a16`. Keep the upper word white.
* Three speakers: add a third cutout in the middle, in front, head top at 8% H; shrink the
  outer two to 85% and put all three name plates in one row at 55% H instead of the pill.
* Conference panel, debate night, podcast live show, awards host announcement.
* 4:5 social crop: keep everything, cut the bottom social row, move contact rows to 91
  and 94% H.
* Pairs with T-smoke (blue-gray haze at the left and right edges), T-bokeh in gold behind
  the figures instead of bars, T-lens-flare on the ring, and T-vignette as a last layer.

## 11. Pitfalls

* Speakers in bright or mixed clothing: the page gets a second and third accent. Ask for
  neutral clothes, or desaturate each figure about 50% with `adjust vibrance`.
* Both figures at the same height and size looks like a passport sheet. Stagger by 3% H,
  overlap the shoulders by 5 to 8% W, and turn the rear figure toward the front one.
* The gradient running top to bottom on the lower word: it then fights the white word's
  shadow. Run it left to right (`--angle 0`), deep orange on the left.
* Setting both headline words in the same face. The lockup depends on wide-over-narrow;
  with two condensed words it becomes an ordinary two-line stack.
* Name plates over the suits or the glow: they vanish. Keep them in the dark gutters, at
  least 2% W away from the cutouts.
