# p051: Gradient spheres abstract event flyer

## 1. Concept

A club or art-night flyer built from four giant gradient discs (two warm, two cool) that
drift in from the edges of a deep purple page, with a one-word title broken across two
lines in wide white capitals at the center and small white details pinned to the corners.
It works because each disc is a simple two- or three-color linear gradient that reads as
a lit sphere, the warm and cool pairs sit on opposite diagonals, and every piece of type
is white, so the color never competes with the message.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait, 7:9 in the original; build at 4:5. Margins 8% W left, 8 to 14% W right, 5% H
  top and bottom.
* The title block sits on the optical center: 23 to 77% W, 37 to 63% H. A short rule
  above it (right half) and one below it (left half) bracket it diagonally.
* Details are distributed to four corners: date top left, web address and day/time top
  right, a vertical caption bottom left, "more info" and icons bottom right. A
  right-aligned paragraph sits on the lower warm disc.
* Discs: warm top left (center 13.6% W, 6.7% H, radius 25% W); cool top right (center
  74% W, 28% H, radius 33% W); cool left (center -3% W, 71% H, radius 23.6% W); warm
  bottom (center 65% W, 84.5% H, radius 30.7% W). Warm and cool alternate around the
  title; none of the four is fully inside the page.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: deep purple `#3a1c5b`.
2. Tone-on-tone blobs: large soft-edged organic shapes one step lighter and bluer
   (`#392970`), covering about half the ground, so the purple is not flat.
3. Disc A (top left): magenta `#ec2781` at its upper left to yellow `#f9d884` at its
   lower right.
4. Disc B (top right, the largest): pale aqua `#8bd1d3` at the lower left through cyan
   `#28c4f4` to deep blue `#0061a6` at the upper right.
5. Disc C (left edge): pale teal `#7dc6cd` to blue `#188dc4`.
6. Disc D (bottom): purple-magenta `#a73268` at the lower left through red-pink `#ce385e`
   and orange `#f6921f` to yellow `#fcc02c` at the upper right.
7. Satellites: five to eight small discs, 0.6 to 3% W in radius, in the same warm and cool
   gradients, scattered in the gaps (for example orange at 44% W, 12.8% H; teal at 96% W,
   58.5% H; teal at 11.4% W, 94.4% H).
8. Dotted lines: a vertical run of nine small white dots at 92.3% W from 9.4 to 40% H; a
   horizontal run of nine at 94.7% H from 12.9 to 52% W.
9. Date, top left: big light numerals at 7.9% W, cap top 6.1% H; a thin white line from
   28 to 53% W at 8.9% H; the month in bold caps at 17.2% H; a tiny tracked line at
   20.6% H.
10. Top right: tracked web address ending at 92.4% W, 5.1% H; day name ending at 86% W,
    22.4% H; time range under it at 27% H.
11. Rule above the title: 49.7 to 76.4% W at 32.7% H. Rule below: 23.6 to 50.3% W at
    67.3% H. Both white, 3 px.
12. Title: two lines of four letters, white, 53.6% W wide, cap tops 36.7% and 52% H.
13. Paragraph: five right-aligned lines ending at 84% W, 74 to 83% H, on disc D.
14. Vertical caption: tiny tracked lower-case text reading upward at 6% W, 49 to 82% H.
15. Bottom right: "MORE INFO :" at 62.5% W, 94.4% H, followed by three small outlined
    circles (social icons).

## 6. Imagery

No photograph. Four discs with linear gradients act as planets or stage lights. Each disc
is lit from one side: the light end of its gradient faces the page center for the cool
discs and faces away from it for the warm ones, which keeps the title area mid-toned.
Small satellite discs repeat the same gradients at 5 to 10% of the size.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-geo-shapes**: discs are `shape path` circles (two arcs), so their centers can lie
  off the canvas. Gradient: `fx overlay --stops ... --angle N` (0 = left to right, 90 =
  bottom to top, 45 = lower left to upper right, 315 = upper left to lower right).
* Ground blobs: one `fill noise --as-layer` line with `--scale 62%`, `--octaves 2`,
  `--contrast 51` between the two purples gives large two-tone organic shapes with soft
  edges.
* **T-rules**: two 3 px rules, one thin line after the day number, and dotted runs made
  with `shape line --dash 0,46 --cap round` (the stroke width is the dot size).
* **T-vertical-type** for the caption on the left edge.
* **T-icon-row**, contact variant: three 24 px white `shape icon` glyphs (`at-sign`,
  `globe`, `message-circle`) after "MORE INFO :". The set has no social-network logos;
  these neutral glyphs stand in for them.
* No grain, no shadows; the gradients carry the depth.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The title (largest, pure white, in the gap between the blue and orange discs).
2. The big blue disc, then the date at the top left (large numerals).
3. Day and time on the blue disc at the top right.
4. The paragraph on the orange disc; corner details last.

The two rules point from the title toward the day/time block (upper right) and toward the
vertical caption (lower left), spreading attention along one diagonal.

Change first: the title word (split an 8-letter word 4 + 4, or use two 4 to 5 letter

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Palette swaps: lime-to-teal against violet-to-pink on navy `#0e1433`; gold-to-red
  against teal on black; pastel discs (`#ffd6e0`, `#c9f0ff`, `#fff2b3`) on white with
  dark type.
* Add depth: `fx inner-shadow` in a darker shade on the unlit side of each disc, or a
  blurred copy of a disc behind it as a glow (`filter gaussian --sigma 40`, `screen`).
* One hero disc: enlarge disc B to radius 45% W behind the title and reduce the others
  to satellites.
* Square post: title, discs B and D, date only.
* Suits DJ nights, science fairs, product teasers, festival line-ups. Pairs with
  T-gradient-mesh for the ground, T-paper-grain for a printed finish, and T-pill-button
  for tickets.

## 11. Pitfalls

* Radial "ball" gradients with a white highlight look like 1990s clip art. Use linear
  gradients across the disc; the round outline alone makes it a sphere.
* Mixing warm and cool inside one disc (orange to blue) gives a muddy middle. One
  temperature per disc.
* All four discs fully on the page turns them into polka dots. Let every large disc be
  cut by at least one edge.
* Title on the brightest part of a disc: white on yellow or pale aqua disappears. Keep
  the title over purple and the darker ends of the gradients, or add
  `fx drop-shadow --offset 0,4 --blur 18 --opacity 35`.
* Small text without tracking looks cramped at 1% H; track it 100 to 250 and keep lines
  short.
