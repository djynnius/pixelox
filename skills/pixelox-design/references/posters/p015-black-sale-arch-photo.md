# p015: Black sale flyer with arch photo

## 1. Concept

A sale flyer split into a wide black text column and a narrow white strip ruled with thin
vertical lines; a round-topped arch holding a warm fashion photo rises from the lower
right and bridges the two fields. The two-line headline is set in a heavy condensed face,
the first line off-white, the second in signal red. It works because black, white, and
one red carry the whole message, and the only soft, colorful thing (the photo) is held
inside a hard geometric shape.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait A-series (1 : 1.414). The canvas is split vertically at 75.3% W: black on the
  left, white on the right.
* Every text element hangs from one left edge at 10.2% W. Nothing is centered. The text
  column is 10.2 to 67% W wide at most.
* Zones in the text column: brand 8% H; headline 14 to 43% H; red rule 45.5% H; offer
  56 to 66% H; thin rule 70% H; note 74.5% H; button 82.5 to 88% H; web address 90.5% H.
* The arch occupies `54.4%,45%,45.6% x 55%` and overlaps the black column by 21% W, so
  the offer block and the button sit beside its curved edge.
* The band from 46 to 55% H is empty: it separates "what" (headline) from "how much".

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: near-black `#151513`, full canvas.
2. White strip: `75.3%,0%,24.7% x 100%`, pure white.
3. Pinstripes: five 2 px near-black vertical lines on the strip at 79.4, 83.5, 87.6, 91.7,
   and 95.8% W, running from the top edge down behind the arch.
4. Red triangle: flat red `#fc1c2e` with corners at 100%,37%; 87%,45.3%; 100%,50%. It
   points left out of the right edge, just above the arch, behind the pinstripes.
5. Arch: a round-topped door shape (radius 39% W) pushed off the right edge so only its
   left 58% shows: vertical left edge at 54.4% W from the bottom up to 72.6% H, then a
   quarter circle up to the top at 45% H. Thin near-black outline, 3 px.
6. Photo, clipped to the arch: a hand holding a dark brown leather tote by tan handles in
   front of a flat beige wall `#e6bb90`; white trousers and a heeled sandal at the bottom.
7. Brand: tracked bold caps, off-white, cap height 1.5% H, left 10.2% W, at 8.4% H.
8. Headline line 1: 5 letters, off-white `#f4f2ed`, cap height 14.2% H, 53% W wide, top at
   14% H.
9. Headline line 2: 6 letters, red, cap height 12.8% H, same width, top at 30.2% H (gap
   2% H).
10. Red rule: 22.6% W long, 0.45% H thick, at 45.5% H, left-aligned.
11. Offer: "80% OFF" pattern, off-white, condensed bold, cap height 6.8% H, 40% W wide, top
    at 56% H.
12. Item line: 2 words, white, bold geometric sans, cap height 2% H, top at 64.4% H.
13. Thin rule: red, 2 px, 10.2 to 37% W, at 70% H.
14. Note: 2 to 3 words, off-white, regular, cap height 1.4% H, at 74.5% H.
15. Button: red pill `10.2%,82.5%,30.8% x 5.3%`; label "SHOP NOW" in near-black tracked
    bold caps, cap height 1.6% H.
16. Web address: off-white, regular, cap height 1.4% H, at 90.5% H.

Two families: one condensed heavy face for the three loud lines, one geometric sans for
everything small. Both headline lines are fitted to the same width, so the 5-letter line
comes out taller than the 6-letter line.

## 6. Imagery

One lifestyle photo, used opaque and cropped by the arch: a close crop of a person
carrying a structured leather handbag at knee height against a plain beige wall, soft
daylight, low contrast, warm tones only. The product sits in the upper half of the arch;
legs and shoes fill the lower half. No face. The photo is not graded. What matters is a
plain, warm, light background: it has to separate from both the black column and the
white strip. Any product-in-hand or product-on-plain-backdrop photo works.

## 7. Effects and textures

* Arch crop: a `shape path` (vertical edge, quarter-circle arc, off-canvas right side),
  the photo placed above it and held in with `layer clip`; a second copy of the path with
  no fill and a 3 px stroke draws the outline.
* **T-rules**: the pinstripes on the strip (2 px, every 4.1% W), the thick red rule under
  the headline, and the thin red rule above the note.
* **T-fit-width** on both headline lines (576 px on a 1080 px canvas) and on the offer.
* **T-pill-button**, filled, accent red, dark label.
* Completely flat: no shadows, gradients, or grain.
* **T-shape-crop** in [techniques.md](../techniques.md) is the tested library version of the photo shown through a drawn shape (arch, panel, tiles, or letter); start from it when reusing this idea.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. Headline line 1 (largest, off-white on black, top left).
2. Headline line 2 in red directly below; the red rule underlines the pair.
3. The photo in the arch (the only image, and the only warm color).
4. Back left to the offer, the item line, and down to the red button.

Cap heights: headline 14.2 and 12.8 : offer 6.8 : item 2 : small text 1.4 to 1.6, about
9 : 4.5 : 1.4 : 1. Each level is at least twice the next.

## 9. Build recipe

`photo.png` is your image here (a product or lifestyle photo with a plain light
background; it is scaled to the arch height and cropped by the arch).

```bash
pixelox new -o sale.pxo --size 1080x1528 --fill '#151513'
pixelox shape rect sale.pxo --in-place --name Strip --rect 75.3%,0,24.7%x100% --fill '#ffffff'
pixelox shape path sale.pxo --in-place --name Triangle --d 'M 1080 565 L 940 692 L 1080 764 Z' --fill '#fc1c2e'
pixelox shape line sale.pxo --in-place --name Pin1 --from 79.4%,0 --to 79.4%,60% --stroke '#151513' --stroke-width 2
pixelox shape line sale.pxo --in-place --name Pin2 --from 83.5%,0 --to 83.5%,60% --stroke '#151513' --stroke-width 2
pixelox shape line sale.pxo --in-place --name Pin3 --from 87.6%,0 --to 87.6%,60% --stroke '#151513' --stroke-width 2
pixelox shape line sale.pxo --in-place --name Pin4 --from 91.7%,0 --to 91.7%,60% --stroke '#151513' --stroke-width 2
pixelox shape line sale.pxo --in-place --name Pin5 --from 95.8%,0 --to 95.8%,60% --stroke '#151513' --stroke-width 2
pixelox shape path sale.pxo --in-place --name Arch --d 'M 588 1528 L 588 1110 A 422 422 0 0 1 1080 694 L 1080 1528 Z' --fill '#e6bb90'
pixelox comp place sale.pxo photo.png --in-place --as Photo --size x860 --at 77%,100% --anchor b
pixelox layer clip sale.pxo --in-place --layer Photo
pixelox shape path sale.pxo --in-place --name ArchLine --d 'M 588 1528 L 588 1110 A 422 422 0 0 1 1080 694' --fill none --stroke '#151513' --stroke-width 3
pixelox text add sale.pxo --in-place --name Brand --content "NORTHLANE" --font @geometric-sans --weight 700 --size 2.1% --tracking 200 --color '#f4f2ed' --at 10.2%,8.4% --anchor l
pixelox text add sale.pxo --in-place --name H1 --content "CYBER" --font @condensed-heavy --fit-width 576 --color '#f4f2ed' --at 10.8%,14% --anchor-to ink
pixelox text add sale.pxo --in-place --name H2 --content "MONDAY" --font @condensed-heavy --fit-width 576 --color '#fc1c2e' --at 10.8%,30.2% --anchor-to ink
pixelox shape rect sale.pxo --in-place --name RedRule --rect 10.6%,45.3%,22.6%x0.45% --fill '#fc1c2e'
pixelox text add sale.pxo --in-place --name Offer --content "70% OFF" --font @condensed-heavy --fit-width 432 --color '#f4f2ed' --at 10.2%,56% --anchor-to ink
pixelox text add sale.pxo --in-place --name Item --content "Leather Tote" --font @geometric-sans --weight 700 --size 2.8% --color '#ffffff' --at 10.2%,64.4% --anchor tl --anchor-to ink
pixelox shape line sale.pxo --in-place --name ThinRule --from 10.2%,70% --to 37%,70% --stroke '#fc1c2e' --stroke-width 2
pixelox text add sale.pxo --in-place --name Note --content "This weekend only" --font @geometric-sans --weight 400 --size 2% --color '#f4f2ed' --at 10.2%,74.5% --anchor l
pixelox shape rect sale.pxo --in-place --name Pill --rect 10.2%,82.5%,30.8%x5.3% --radius 40 --fill '#fc1c2e'
pixelox text add sale.pxo --in-place --name PillText --content "SHOP NOW" --font @geometric-sans --weight 700 --size 2.2% --tracking 120 --color '#151513' --at 25.8%,85.2% --anchor c --anchor-to ink
pixelox text add sale.pxo --in-place --name Site --content "www.example.com" --font @geometric-sans --weight 400 --size 1.95% --color '#f4f2ed' --at 10.2%,90.5% --anchor l
pixelox export sale.pxo -o sale.png
```

Change first: the two headline words (4 to 6 letters each; both are fitted to 576 px, so
a shorter word becomes taller), the offer, then the photo. Keep `Arch` and `ArchLine` on
the same path data. `@condensed-heavy` (Impact on macOS) is wider than the ideal face, so
the caps come out near 12.7% and 10.5% H; a registered Anton or Oswald 700 at the same
`--fit-width` gives the 14% and 13% of the description.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Swap the accent only: `#ffd400` on black for a clearance sale, `#00c26e` for a "green"
  or outlet event, `#ff5fa2` for beauty. Keep the off-white and the black.
* Light version: ground `#f4f2ed`, strip `#151513` with off-white pinstripes, headline
  line 1 in near-black, line 2 in red.
* Mirror it (strip and arch on the left, text hung from 33% W) when the photo's subject
  faces right.
* Square post: split at 62% W, keep the headline, the offer, and the button; drop the
  note and the web address.
* Pairs with T-price-burst over the arch's edge (a second offer), T-duotone on the photo
  (black to red to off-white) for a stricter three-color version, and T-paper-grain at
  amount 10.

## 11. Pitfalls

* A photo with a dark or busy background: the arch disappears into the black column.
  Choose a light, plain backdrop or lighten it first with `adjust levels`.
* Centering the button or the web address under the headline. Everything shares the left
  edge at 10.2% W; one centered item breaks the column.
* Red body text on black. Small red type vibrates and is hard to read; red is for the
  large second line, rules, shapes, and the button only.
* Pinstripes too thick or too many. Five 2 px lines; at 4 px they turn into a barcode and
  compete with the headline.
* Headline lines of very different lengths (3 letters against 8): fitted to one width,
  the short line becomes enormous. Keep both lines within one letter of each other.
