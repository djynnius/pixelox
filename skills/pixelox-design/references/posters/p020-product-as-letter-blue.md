# p020: Product replaces a letter on sky blue

## 1. Concept

A smart-home ad on a flat sky-blue wall: a five-letter greeting is set huge in thin white
letters across the middle, and the product (a slim, pill-shaped doorbell) stands in the
row of letters in place of the fourth one, exactly as tall as the capitals, while a hand
comes up from the bottom and presses it. The headline is the product name, the picture,
and the demonstration at once. Everything else (two-line intro, one paragraph, logo) is
small, white, and hung from one left margin.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait, about 3:4. One left margin at 7.1% W for every text block; nothing is
  centered. Right margin 9.4% W (the big word ends at 90.6% W).
* Horizontal bands: intro lines 18 to 24% H; big word 40 to 60% H (the vertical middle of
  the page); paragraph 66.5 to 75% H; links 76.6% H; logo 85.8 to 91.4% H; fine print
  97% H.
* The big word spans the full measure, 7.2 to 90.6% W. Letter slots: letters one to
  three occupy 7.2 to 56.2% W, the product 62.6 to 70.8% W, the last letter 74.8 to
  90.6% W.
* The upper 17% is empty wall; so is everything right of 45% W above and below the word,
  except the hand (58 to 88% W, 56 to 100% H).

## 3. Layer stack

1. Wall: sky blue `#7fbfe2`, a touch darker at the lower left (`#71b5d8`) and lighter at
   the upper right (`#8ac4e9`). It is a photographed painted wall, so the tone drifts
   softly; there is no texture to speak of.
2. Intro line 1: product name and category, 4 words, white, bold, cap height 2.1% H, top
   at 17.9% H, left 7.1% W, 46% W wide.
3. Intro line 2: a 3-word promise ending in a period, white, light weight, same size, top
   at 21.6% H.
4. Big word, first part: three letters (a capital and two lowercase), white, thin
   monoline geometric sans, cap height 19.9% H, stroke 1.7% W, baseline at 59.8% H.
5. Product: a tall pill-shaped device with a dark face `#3a3c42`, a white rim, a camera
   lens at the top and a ring button at the bottom; `62.6%,39.8%,8.2% x 20%`, standing on
   the baseline, top level with the cap line. A soft shadow falls to its lower left.
6. Big word, last letter: one round lowercase letter, 74.8 to 90.6% W, x-height 14% H.
7. Hand: a right hand entering from the bottom edge, wrist at 68 to 85% W, index finger
   pointing up and pressing the product's button at 66% W, 57% H. It casts a soft dark
   blue shadow on the wall to its left.
8. Paragraph: 6 lines of small white text, left 7.1% W, 34% W wide, top at 66.5% H, line
   pitch 1.34% H.
9. Links line: two short bold items separated by a thin vertical rule, at 76.6% H.
10. Logo: a 4-letter lowercase wordmark in bold rounded letters, white,
    `7.1%,85.8%,17% x 5.6%`.
11. Fine print: one line of very small white text at 97.2% H, 7.1 to 67% W.

## 6. Imagery

Two cutouts on a flat wall. The product is shot straight on, evenly lit, with no
perspective, so that it can pass for a letter: its width (8.2% W) is close to the width
of a lowercase "l" plus its side bearings, and its height equals the cap height. The
hand is a natural, well-lit right hand with the index finger extended upward; the wrist
leaves through the bottom edge. Both get a soft directional shadow
(**T-cutout-drop-shadow**, soft variant) falling to the lower left, which is what makes
the flat color read as a real wall. Any tall, narrow product can play the letter: a
toothbrush, a bottle, a remote, a lipstick, a thermometer, a pen.

## 7. Effects and textures

* Wall drift: one diagonal linear gradient layer over the flat fill, `#71b5d8` at the
  lower left to `#8dc7eb` at the upper right.
* **T-object-as-letter**: the word is set in two text layers with a gap, and the product
  is scaled to the cap height and stood on the baseline in the gap.
* Stroke weight: the stand-in face at weight 275 is slightly too thin, so `fx stroke` in
  white (2 px, outside) thickens it to about 1.7% W.
* **T-cutout-drop-shadow** soft shadows: product `--offset -14,12 --blur 26 --opacity 35`;
  hand `--offset -50,14 --blur 44 --opacity 45`; shadow color `#1d4a60`, not black.
* **T-rules**: a 1 px vertical divider between the two link items.
* No grain, no vignette.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The big white word, left to right, which runs straight into the product.
2. The product and the fingertip on its button (dark object, warm hand, on the word's
   baseline).
3. Up to the two intro lines, which name what was just seen.
4. Down the left margin: paragraph, links, logo.

Cap heights: big word 19.9 : intro 2.1 : paragraph 0.95 : fine print 0.6, about
21 : 2.2 : 1 : 0.6. A nearly 10x jump between the word and everything else is the whole
hierarchy.

## 9. Build recipe

`product.png` is your image here (a tall, narrow product, front view) and `subject.png`
is a hand or arm cutout that reaches up to it; the stand-in is a bust, placed where the
hand would be.

```bash
pixelox new -o letter.pxo --size 1080x1440 --fill '#7fbfe2'
pixelox layer add letter.pxo --in-place --name Drift
pixelox fill gradient letter.pxo --in-place --layer Drift --from 0,100% --to 100%,0 --stops '#71b5d8ff@0,#8dc7ebff@1'
pixelox text add letter.pxo --in-place --name Intro1 --content "Luma Style hair straightener" --font @geometric-sans --weight 700 --size 2.75% --color '#ffffff'
pixelox transform move letter.pxo --in-place --layer Intro1 --to 7.1%,17.9% --anchor tl
pixelox text add letter.pxo --in-place --name Intro2 --content "Smooth in one pass." --font @geometric-sans --weight 400 --size 2.75% --color '#ffffff'
pixelox transform move letter.pxo --in-place --layer Intro2 --to 7.1%,21.6% --anchor tl
pixelox text add letter.pxo --in-place --name WordA --content "Sty" --font @thin-sans --size 27.8% --tracking 80 --color '#ffffff'
pixelox transform move letter.pxo --in-place --layer WordA --to 7.2%,39.9% --anchor tl
pixelox fx stroke letter.pxo --in-place --layer WordA --width 2 --color '#ffffff' --position outside
pixelox text add letter.pxo --in-place --name WordB --content "e" --font @thin-sans --size 27.8% --color '#ffffff'
pixelox transform move letter.pxo --in-place --layer WordB --to 90.6%,60% --anchor br
pixelox fx copy letter.pxo --in-place --from WordA --to WordB
pixelox comp place letter.pxo product.png --in-place --as Product --size x288 --at 66.7%,59.9% --anchor b
pixelox fx drop-shadow letter.pxo --in-place --layer Product --offset=-14,12 --blur 26 --color '#1d4a60' --opacity 35
pixelox comp place letter.pxo subject.png --in-place --as Hand --size x640 --at 74%,100% --anchor b
pixelox fx drop-shadow letter.pxo --in-place --layer Hand --offset=-50,14 --blur 44 --color '#1d4a60' --opacity 45
pixelox text add letter.pxo --in-place --name Para --content $'Ready in fifteen seconds, with plates that float\nso they never pull. Style reads the heat sixty\ntimes a second and holds it steady from root\nto tip. It switches itself off when you set it\ndown, and one charge covers a week of\nmornings, at home or away.' --font @geometric-sans --weight 400 --size 1.3% --leading 107% --color '#ffffff'
pixelox transform move letter.pxo --in-place --layer Para --to 7.1%,66.5% --anchor tl
pixelox text add letter.pxo --in-place --name Link1 --content "example.com" --font @geometric-sans --weight 700 --size 1.3% --color '#ffffff' --at 7.1%,76.6% --anchor l
pixelox shape line letter.pxo --in-place --name LinkRule --from 19.9%,76% --to 19.9%,77.2% --stroke '#ffffff' --stroke-width 1
pixelox text add letter.pxo --in-place --name Link2 --content "In stores" --font @geometric-sans --weight 700 --size 1.3% --color '#ffffff' --at 21.4%,76.6% --anchor l
pixelox text add letter.pxo --in-place --name Wordmark --content "luma" --font @rounded-heavy --fit-width 190 --tracking -20 --color '#ffffff' --at 7.1%,85.8% --anchor-to ink
pixelox text add letter.pxo --in-place --name Fine --content "Heat mat and travel pouch included. Battery life depends on the temperature setting and hair length." --font @geometric-sans --weight 400 --size 0.85% --color '#ffffff' --at 7.1%,97.2% --anchor l
pixelox export letter.pxo -o letter.png
```

Change first: the word. Pick one whose straight-stroke letter (l, i, I, 1) can be the
product, split it into the part before and the part after that letter, and move
`Product` into the gap; then swap the wall color. The first part is placed by its top
left (cap line at 39.9% H) because it may contain a descender; the last letter and the
product stand on the baseline at 60% H. Tune `--tracking` on the first part until the
gap before the product is about 4 to 5% W. A word without descenders (no g, j, p, q, y)
keeps the band under the baseline clear for the paragraph, as in the original.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Other walls: blush `#f3c6c0` with a white word for beauty; sage `#b9cdb5` for wellness;
  sand `#e7d7bf` with a dark brown word for home goods; near-black `#101214` with the
  product lit from the side for audio.
* The product as "i" (add a dot above it with `shape ellipse`), as "1" in a price or a
  date, or as the "o" for round products (a speaker, a watch face, a plate).
* Landscape banner: the word across 80% of the width, intro lines above its first
  letter, logo at the lower right.
* No hand: keep only the product and its shadow; the design still works.
* Pairs with T-pill-button (replace the links line), T-paper-grain at amount 8 to give
  the wall a painted feel, and T-photo-fade when a real wall photo is used as the ground.

## 11. Pitfalls

* A product that is taller or shorter than the capitals stops reading as a letter. Scale
  it to the cap height exactly and stand it on the baseline.
* A gap that is too wide. The space on each side of the product should match the spacing
  between the other letters (about 4% W here), or the word falls into two words.
* A bold face for the big word. Heavy letters at 20% H swallow the product; the thin
  monoline weight is what lets a small dark object win.
* Black shadows on a colored wall look dirty. Use a darker, less saturated version of the
  wall color (`#1d4a60` on this blue).
* Centering the intro or the paragraph under the word. All text hangs from 7.1% W; the
  empty right half is where the hand lives.
