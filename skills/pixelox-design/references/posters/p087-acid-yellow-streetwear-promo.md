# p087: Acid-yellow streetwear promo with photo-filled letters

## 1. Concept

A streetwear brand promo in two colors, acid yellow and black: three giant capital
letters (the brand's initials) stand across the page as windows onto a night city, a
seated man in black clothes and sunglasses sits in front of them, and a white dry-brush
wordmark with a yellow script word is painted over his legs on a ragged black band. It
works because the yellow is so loud that everything else can be black: the letters, the
clothes, the brush marks, and the small slogans all merge into one dark mass that the
yellow cuts into shapes.

## 2. Format and grid

* Portrait 4:5. No quiet margin: brush marks touch every edge; small type keeps 2.5 to
  4.5% W from the edges.
* Giant letters: 9 to 95% W, 12.5 to 77% H (cap height about 63% H), tight enough to
  touch. The man sits on the center axis (53% W) and hides most of the middle letter.
* Four corner blocks around the hero: logo top left (4 to 27% W, 1.5 to 13.5% H), slogan
  top right (76 to 95% W, 2 to 9.5% H), slogan mid left (2 to 12% W, 25.5 to 36% H), claim
  and tag lower right (80 to 97% W, 69 to 84% H).
* Lockup: lower left to center, 10 to 73% W, 68 to 93% H, tilted 7 to 9 degrees upward.
* Footer on the black band: 92 to 98.5% H.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: acid yellow `#e8fb01`, with sparse black specks and a few hairline scratches.
2. Black dry-brush marks at the edges: a vertical smear at the left edge (0 to 8% W,
   40 to 75% H) and short dabs at the right edge near 20 to 30% H.
3. Giant letters: 3 heavy condensed capitals, filled with a city-skyline photo mapped to
   near-black `#070803`, olive `#2c3212`, and yellow-green `#93a318`; the triangular
   counters and the gaps show the yellow ground.
4. Man cutout: seated with one knee up and a forearm resting on it, black hoodie with a
   white chest print, glossy black jacket, dark sunglasses, wristwatch; head top at 2.5% H,
   widest at 10 to 88% W, running into the black band at 85% H. Hard, crisp, high-clarity
   grade.
5. Black band: a wide dry-brush stroke across the bottom, ragged top edge at 80 to 88% H
   (higher on the left), solid black below; it swallows the man's legs.
6. Brush wordmark: `[BRAND]`, 3 capitals in white dry-brush italic lettering with streaky
   gaps, 10 to 59% W, 68.5 to 88.5% H, tilted 9 degrees upward.
7. Script word: one mixed-case word of 9 letters in yellow brush script, 28 to 63% W,
   82 to 93% H, same tilt, overlapping the wordmark's feet; a yellow brush underline
   below it (38 to 73% W).
8. Logo: a small outlined crown over 3 black extended capitals (cap height 4.4% H) and a
   3-word tagline separated by dots (cap height 0.7% H).
9. Slogans: black bold italic condensed caps, cap height 2.2% H: 2 lines at the top right
   and 3 one-word lines at the left, each with a short black brush flick under it.
10. Claim: 3 lines of black heavy italic caps (cap height 2.5% H) at 80% W, 69 to 77% H,
    then a slanted black tag `83%,76.5%,14% x 7.5%` holding `[BRAND]` in yellow.
11. Footer: a thin white vertical rule at 3.4% W with 3 one-word lines of widely tracked
    white light caps beside it; at the right, 3 white social discs (3.2% W each) and a
    white handle.

## 6. Imagery

* **Subject**: a young man photographed slightly from below, seated with his right knee
  raised and his right forearm hanging over it, left hand on the other knee; short
  textured hair, rectangular sunglasses, neutral mouth. All-black outfit so he reads as
  one silhouette with the letters; the only light areas are face, hands, and the white
  chest print. Cut out, then sharpened hard (`adjust clarity --amount 40`).
* **City**: a skyline of towers under a stormy sky, shown only inside the giant letters
  (**T-image-in-type**) and recolored to black, olive, and yellow-green (**T-duotone**
  with a dark top stop, so the letters stay darker than the ground).
* Brush marks, specks, band, and underline are generated, not photographed.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-text-behind-subject** with **T-image-in-type**: letters, then the city clipped to
  them, then the man. The letters are narrowed with `--scale-x 58.4%` and fitted with
  `--fit-width 930` (**T-stretch-type**), which makes them about 860 px tall; they stay
  live text.
* **T-brush-swash** four times, all from `scripts/brush.js`: the black bottom band
  (placed with an explicit `--size 1500x`, centered on the bottom edge so half of it
  hangs off the canvas, rotated 3 degrees), the left edge smear (rotated 82 degrees), the
  yellow underline, and the wordmark's fill.
* Dry-brush lettering: the wordmark is set in near-black `#1a1a1a`, and a white swash
  with `bristles=80`, `dry=0.8`, `passes=1` is clipped to it, so the letters look painted
  with a half-dry brush.
* **T-grunge**, dirt layer only: black specks and scratches at 70% on the yellow.
* **T-script-overlap**: yellow script over the feet of the white wordmark, both tilted.
* **T-label-box**: the slanted black tag with yellow text.
* Social discs: one `shape line` with `--dash 0,50 --cap round` draws the three white
  discs; a black `shape icon` sits on each (**T-icon-row**: `share-2`, `at-sign`,
  `message-circle`, 20 px, `--stroke-width 2.5`). The icon set has no brand logos; place
  the user's own social logos as images if they are needed.
* **T-fit-width**: the giant letters and the brush wordmark are set to exact widths with
  `--fit-width`; the wordmark is placed by its center (`--anchor c --anchor-to ink`) and
  then rotated in place.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The face with sunglasses, top center, where the dark figure meets the yellow.
2. The giant letters behind him, read as a shape before they are read as a name.
3. Down the arm to the white brush wordmark and the yellow script.
4. The black tag at the right, then the corner slogans, logo, and footer.

Cap heights: giant letters 63 : wordmark 13 : script 7 : logo 4.4 : tag 3.8 : slogans 2.2
: footer 1, about 63 : 13 : 7 : 4.4 : 3.8 : 2.2 : 1. The brand appears four times at four
sizes; repetition, not explanation, is the message.

## 9. Build recipe

`subject.png` (cutout of the person) and `photo.png` (a skyline or any high-contrast
texture) are your images here. For a seated full figure keep `--size x1150`; the head
should break the top of the letters.

```bash
pixelox render js scripts/brush.js -o band.png --size 1400x440 --seed 4 --param color='#050505' --param bristles=170 --param passes=3 --param rough=0.8 --param dry=0.3
pixelox render js scripts/brush.js -o edge.png --size 520x230 --seed 11 --param color='#050505' --param bristles=70 --param passes=2 --param rough=0.9 --param dry=0.6
pixelox render js scripts/grunge.js -o specks.png --size 1080x1350 --seed 6 --param color='#050505' --param specks=5000 --param blotches=70 --param scratches=50 --param clump=0.7
pixelox render js scripts/brush.js -o drybrush.png --size 640x330 --seed 5 --param color='#f4f4f4' --param bristles=80 --param passes=1 --param rough=0.9 --param dry=0.8
pixelox render js scripts/brush.js -o swoosh.png --size 470x34 --seed 3 --param color='#e8fb01' --param bristles=30 --param passes=2 --param rough=0.9
pixelox new -o promo.pxo --size 1080x1350 --fill '#e8fb01'
pixelox comp place promo.pxo specks.png --in-place --as Specks --fit cover
pixelox layer opacity promo.pxo --in-place --layer Specks --value 70
pixelox comp place promo.pxo edge.png --in-place --as EdgeL --at 0%,58% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate promo.pxo --in-place --layer EdgeL --degrees 82
pixelox text add promo.pxo --in-place --name Letters --content "VYB" --font @condensed-heavy --scale-x 58.4% --fit-width 930 --tracking -40 --color '#11100a' --at 52%,12.5% --anchor t --anchor-to ink
pixelox comp place promo.pxo photo.png --in-place --as City --fit cover
pixelox adjust gradient-map promo.pxo --in-place --layer City --stops '#070803@0,#2c3212@0.5,#93a318@1'
pixelox layer clip promo.pxo --in-place --layer City
pixelox comp place promo.pxo subject.png --in-place --as Subject --size x1150 --at 53%,88% --anchor b
pixelox adjust clarity promo.pxo --in-place --layer Subject --amount 40
pixelox comp place promo.pxo band.png --in-place --as Band --size 1500x --at 50%,100% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate promo.pxo --in-place --layer Band --degrees 3
pixelox text add promo.pxo --in-place --name BrushWord --content "VYB" --font @condensed --weight 900 --style italic --fit-width 500 --color '#1a1a1a' --at 34.5%,78% --anchor c --anchor-to ink

pixelox layer clip promo.pxo --in-place --layer DryFill
pixelox text add promo.pxo --in-place --name Script --content "Movement" --font "Brush Script MT" --size 12% --color '#e8fb01' --at 46%,87.5% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate promo.pxo --in-place --layer Script --degrees -7
pixelox comp place promo.pxo swoosh.png --in-place --as Swoosh --at 54%,92.5% --anchor c
pixelox transform rotate promo.pxo --in-place --layer Swoosh --degrees -7
pixelox shape path promo.pxo --in-place --name Crown --d 'M 128 62 L 122 30 L 142 46 L 162 22 L 182 46 L 202 30 L 196 62 Z' --fill none --stroke '#050505' --stroke-width 5
pixelox text add promo.pxo --in-place --name Logo --content "VYB" --font @heavy-wide --size 6.4% --tracking -20 --color '#050505' --at 15.8%,8.2% --anchor c
pixelox text add promo.pxo --in-place --name Tagline --content "STREET  .  STUDIO  .  STAGE" --font @geometric-sans --weight 700 --size 0.95% --tracking 120 --color '#050505' --at 15.8%,12.3% --anchor c

pixelox text add promo.pxo --in-place --name Claim --content $'STAND\nOUT LOUD\nWITH' --font @condensed --weight 900 --style italic --size 3.4% --leading 100% --color '#050505' --at 80.4%,71.2% --anchor l
pixelox shape path promo.pxo --in-place --name Tag --d 'M 908 1036 L 1054 1022 L 1046 1124 L 900 1138 Z' --fill '#050505'
```

Note the explicit `--size 1500x` on the band: without `--size`, `comp place` shrinks an
image that is larger than the canvas to fit inside it. Change first: the three letters
(letters with open counters such as A, R, K, V show more city), the script word, then
the accent color. Keep the `--stops` top color darker than the ground or the letters
dissolve.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Other fluorescent grounds with black: hot orange `#ff5a00`, electric cyan `#00e5ff`,
  magenta `#ff1f8e`. Remap the city's top stop to a darker shade of the same hue.
* Inverted: black ground, yellow brush band, letters filled with a yellow-toned photo,
  white slogans.
* Sport and gym promos, sneaker drops, DJ nights, barbershop and tattoo flyers; a
  product cutout (shoe, cap, can) can replace the person.
* Square: drop the left slogan and the claim, keep letters, figure, and lockup.
* Pairs with T-splatter (yellow drops on the black band), T-glitch on the letters, and
  T-final-grade with clarity 30 for the hard print look.

## 11. Pitfalls

* A third color. Red or blue accents turn it into a generic sale flyer; every graphic
  element is yellow, black, or white.
* Letters as bright as the ground. If the photo fill is light the giant letters vanish;
  map highlights no brighter than `#93a318`.
* Light clothing on the subject. The figure must merge with the letters and the band;
  darken the cutout (`adjust levels --gamma 0.8`) or choose another photo.
* Clean edges. Without the brush band, edge smears, and specks the yellow looks like an
  empty slide; keep at least three rough black marks touching the edges.
* An upright, tidy lockup. Wordmark and script are tilted 7 to 9 degrees and overlap
  each other and the legs; set level and apart they lose all energy.
