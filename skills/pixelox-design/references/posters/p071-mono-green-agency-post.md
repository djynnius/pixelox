# p071: Black-and-white agency post with green accents

## 1. Concept

A square social post for a business agency: a black-and-white cutout of a smiling man
working on a laptop stands in front of a charcoal-and-white split ground, with discs and
pills in one acid green and a four-line headline stack at the right. It works because
everything is gray except one color, so the green does three jobs at once (it marks the
key word, the buttons, and the circle behind the head) and a stock photo of any person
drops into the system once it is turned to black and white. Shown inside a dark preview
frame; the frame is not part of the design.

## 2. Format and grid

* Square. Margins 4.6% on every side for the corner elements.
* Split ground: charcoal on the left 35% and along the bottom; a white field covering the
  right two thirds of the top 65%, with its left edge slanted about 15 degrees.
* The subject occupies the left and center (0 to 72% W, 7 to 100% H); his laptop reaches
  the right edge at 65 to 95% H.
* Right column, 51 to 93% W: headline stack 19 to 42% H; services card 51 to 71% H;
  contact pill 91 to 95% H.
* Left column: logo 5 to 9% H; idea badge (a dark disc) at 11 to 28% W, 44 to 61% H, on
  the subject's shoulder; phone pill 4.6 to 36% W, 89 to 95% H.

## 3. Layer stack

1. Ground: charcoal `#343434`.
2. White field: a polygon with corners near 36%,0; 100%,0; 100%,65%; 30%,72%; 28%,28%.
3. White disc (radius 12.4% W) at 17% W, 36% H with a green `#51db02` disc (radius 11.3%
   W) set 2.6% W right and 1.5% H lower inside it: a white crescent stays visible on the
   upper left. Behind the subject's head and shoulder.
4. Green disc (radius 13.9% W) at 55.5% W, 56.5% H and a dark disc `#3a3a3a` (radius 13%
   W) offset 2.8% W right and 1.9% H lower on top of it: a green crescent on the upper
   left. Behind the subject's arm.
5. Two "node" dots: charcoal discs (radius 1.6% W) with a 0.75% W white outline at 11.3%
   W, 35.5% H and 49.4% W, 42.2% H.
6. Subject cutout, black and white, high contrast, soft shadow to the right.
7. Headline stack (right): a tracked small line; a small tracked word reversed out of a
   black bar; a large light word in black; a heavy word in green, the widest line, set in
   front of the subject's shoulder; a tracked small line.
8. Idea badge: dark disc `#3a3a3a` with three centered white lines (small, large thin,
   small).
9. Services card: a white tab and a green tab side by side (4.2% H tall), and under them
   a dark rounded panel with four numbered white lines.
10. Phone pill: green, fully round, with a bold dark-green phone number and a tracked web
    address under it.
11. Contact pill: dark, small, with a white arrow and a green bold label.
12. Logo text (two short white lines, top left); four small green discs with a tiny gray
    caption (top right).

## 6. Imagery

One cutout: a young man with curly hair and glasses, smiling down at a laptop he holds
open in front of him, in a plain short-sleeved shirt; waist-up, turned slightly right. Treated with
**T-mono-on-color**: black and white, levels pushed (`--in-black 20 --in-white 225`), so
skin is light gray and hair near black. The laptop crosses the lower right and overlaps
the white field's bottom edge, which hides that edge. A soft shadow falls to his right on
the white field.

## 7. Effects and textures

* **T-mono-on-color** on the subject; **T-cutout-drop-shadow** (soft: offset 18,10, blur
  40, 35%).
* **T-geo-shapes**: disc pairs. A crescent is two discs of nearly the same size, the top
  one offset 2 to 3% W toward the lower right.
* **T-label-box**: the black bar behind one tracked word; the white and green tabs; the
  dark list panel (radius 16 px).
* **T-pill-button**: the green phone pill and the dark contact pill with `shape arrow`.
* Wide tracking (600 to 800) on the small lines of the headline stack makes them as wide
  as the big words without making them louder.
* No grain; flat fills only.

## 8. Hierarchy and eye path

1. The face (largest light shape, framed by the green disc).
2. The green heavy word beside it, then the light word above it.
3. Down to the services card (green tab), then the green phone pill at the lower left.
4. Badge on the shoulder, contact pill, logo, socials.

Green appears at four points that form a Z across the square: disc, key word, tab, phone
pill.

## 9. Build recipe

`subject.png` is your image here: a waist-up cutout of a person (any color photo; the
recipe converts it). Canvas 1080x1080.


Change first: the subject (keep the head inside or just right of the green disc), then
the two big words (8 to 9 letters each), then the accent color in every `#51db02`.
Recolor the two dark greens used for text on green (`#1e3b0a`, `#2f5f12`) to a dark shade
of the new accent.

## 10. Variations and pairings

* Accent swaps: orange `#ff6a13`, yellow `#ffd400` (then text on the accent is black),
  cyan `#19c8e6`, magenta `#ff2e88`. Everything else stays gray.
* Light version: ground `#f2f2f2`, field `#ffffff`, dark discs become `#d8d8d8`, type
  dark.
* Story 9:16: stack it. Headline in the top third, subject in the middle, card and pills
  in the bottom third.
* Other uses: real-estate agent, coach, clinic, course launch, hiring post.
* Pairs with T-halftone-portrait on the subject, T-pattern (a small dot grid in a
  corner), and T-outline-type for a ghost word behind the head.

## 11. Pitfalls

* A color photo of the person: skin tones add a second hue and the accent stops working.
  Always convert to black and white and raise contrast.
* Using the accent for large fields (a green ground): it must stay near 10% of the area.
* Green text on white at light weights is unreadable; the green word must be the heavy
  one (weight 800 to 900), and small green text only goes on dark pills.
* Too many callouts at equal size. Here there are five (badge, card, two pills, socials);
  each is under 9% of the area and they sit on the edges, never on the face.
* The white field's edges cut through the head. Keep the slanted edge behind the body or
  outside the silhouette.
